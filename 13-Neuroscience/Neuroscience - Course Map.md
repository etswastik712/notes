> [!interactive]+ Interactive: The Hodgkin–Huxley neuron: threshold, all-or-none spikes and repetitive firing
> The squid-axon membrane model: C dV/dt = I − g_Na m³h (V − E_Na) − g_K n⁴ (V − E_K) − g_L (V − E_L), with three gating variables m, h, n relaxing toward voltage-dependent targets.
>
> ![[ns_hodgkin_huxley.png|720]]
>
> **Try:** Rest is −65 mV. A kick of 6 mV decays, 7 mV fires a spike peaking near +37 mV; 15 mV peaks at +40 mV, hardly higher. Constant current: no spikes below about 6 µA/cm², then a steady train (≈ 60 Hz at 7, 87 Hz at 20). Block Na⁺ (TTX) and no spike is possible; block K⁺ (TEA) and the spike will not repolarise properly.
