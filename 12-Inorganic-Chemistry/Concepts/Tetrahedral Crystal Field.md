# Tetrahedral Crystal Field

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A pp.480–481, Tables 20.4–20.5; Huheey pp.401–403.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Four ligands on alternate cube corners: $e$ below $t_2$, and the gap is only $\frac49$ of the octahedral one.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $e=-\frac35\Delta_T=-2.67\,Dq$, $t_2=+\frac25\Delta_T=+1.78\,Dq$, $\Delta_T=\frac49\Delta_O$ (angular sum: octahedron $+\frac{12}5$, tetrahedron $-\frac{16}{15}$). The $t_2$ lobes sit $35.3^\circ$ from a ligand, the $e$ lobes $54.7^\circ$. Always high spin.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Observed $\Delta_T$: $[\mathrm{CoCl_4}]^{2-}$ 3300, $[\mathrm{CoBr_4}]^{2-}$ 2900, $[\mathrm{CoI_4}]^{2-}$ 2700 cm$^{-1}$.
