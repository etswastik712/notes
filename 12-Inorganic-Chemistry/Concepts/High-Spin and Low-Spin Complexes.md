# High-Spin and Low-Spin Complexes

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A pp.476–477; Huheey pp.399–402.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> In $d^4$–$d^7$ octahedral complexes the electrons either avoid $e_g$ and pair (low spin) or avoid pairing and enter $e_g$ (high spin).

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Low spin iff $\Delta_O>P$; for all of $d^4$–$d^7$ the energy difference vanishes at $\Delta_O=P$. 3$d$ ions: high spin with weak-field ligands ($\mathrm{F^-}$), low spin with strong ($\mathrm{CN^-}$). $4d$/$5d$: almost always low spin. $d^1$–$d^3$, $d^8$–$d^{10}$: no choice. Tetrahedral: always high spin.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Distinguish by magnetism: $d^6$ HS $\mu=4.90\,\mu_B$, LS 0. A switch with temperature is **spin crossover**.
