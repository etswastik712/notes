# Frontier Orbitals

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=84|slides 84–85]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Reactions between molecules are governed by the highest filled and lowest empty orbitals: the nearer they are in energy, the stronger the interaction.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Hard species: low HOMO, high LUMO, large gap (electrostatic bonding); soft species: high HOMO, low LUMO, small gap (covalent bonding). Stabilisation $\sim\beta^2/(E_{\rm LUMO}-E_{\rm HOMO})$.

CO: HOMO and LUMO on C. NH$_3\to$BF$_3$: N lone pair into B $2p_z$.

Used in: [[CH06 Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]] §5, [[CH04 Acid-Base Concepts]] §5. Related: [[Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]] · [[Lewis Acids and Bases]].
