# Iminoborane

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=113|slide 113]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The B–N analogue of an alkyne, which does not survive as a monomer but cyclo-oligomerises.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{RB{\equiv}NR'}$ trimerises to the borazine ring $(\mathrm{RBNR'})_3$; analogues of cyclobutadiene and cyclooctatetraene also occur.

The trimer is the commonest outcome.

Used in: [[CH08 Main Group Rings and Polymers]] §3. Related: [[Inorganic Heterocycle]].
