# Polyphosphazene

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=119|slide 119]]; Tutorial 4 Q10.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A polymer with a P=N backbone whose properties are tuned by the two groups on each phosphorus.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $(\mathrm{NPCl_2})_3\xrightarrow{210-250\,°C}[\mathrm{NPCl_2}]_n\xrightarrow{\mathrm{RONa,RNH_2,R_2NH,RM}}[\mathrm{N{=}PR^1R^2}]_n$. $\mathrm{OCH_2CF_3}$ polymer: repeat mass 243.0 g/mol (×2.10 vs NPCl$_2$).

Very low glass transition temperature (flexible P–N–P backbone); used as elastomers and biomaterials.

Used in: [[CH08 Main Group Rings and Polymers]] §5. Related: [[Cyclophosphazene]].
