# Reduction of a Representation

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** [[CH03 Molecular Orbital Theory Polyatomics]] §3 (not on the slides).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Count how many of the ligand orbitals stay put under each symmetry operation, then ask how that pattern decomposes into the standard symmetry types.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $n_i=\tfrac1h\sum_R\chi_\Gamma(R)\chi_i(R)$. $\mathrm{NH_3}$: $\Gamma=(3,0,1)\Rightarrow a_1+e$. $\mathrm{H_2O}$: $(2,0,2,0)\Rightarrow a_1+b_1$.

The number of LGOs of each type equals the number of central-atom orbitals available to match them.
