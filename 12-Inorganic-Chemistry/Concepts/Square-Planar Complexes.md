# Square-Planar Complexes

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A p.481, Fig. 20.10; Huheey pp.403–405, Fig. 11.12, Table 11.5.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Take the two trans ligands off an octahedron: $d_{x^2-y^2}$ shoots up and the rest fall.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Order (Dq): $d_{x^2-y^2}+12.28\gg d_{xy}+2.28>d_{z^2}-4.28>d_{xz,yz}-5.14$. Low-spin $d^8$ leaves $d_{x^2-y^2}$ empty: favoured for $4d^8$/$5d^8$ ($\mathrm{Pd^{II}}$, $\mathrm{Pt^{II}}$, $\mathrm{Au^{III}}$, $\mathrm{Rh^I}$, $\mathrm{Ir^I}$) and $3d^8$ with $\mathrm{CN^-}$ ($[\mathrm{Ni(CN)_4}]^{2-}$). Rule of thumb $\Delta_{SP}\approx1.3\Delta_O$.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> $3d^8$ with halides: tetrahedral, paramagnetic ($[\mathrm{NiCl_4}]^{2-}$).
