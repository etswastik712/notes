# Faraday Constant

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** S&A §5.2.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The charge of one mole of electrons: the conversion between joules and volts.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $F=96485$ C/mol; $RT/F=25.69$ mV at 298 K; $(RT/F)\ln10=59.16$ mV.

$\Delta G=-\nu FE$: 1 V × 1 electron = 96.5 kJ/mol.

Used in: [[CH09 Redox]] §2, §5. Related: [[Standard Reduction Potential]].
