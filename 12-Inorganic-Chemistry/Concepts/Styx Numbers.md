# Styx Numbers

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** [[CH07 Main Group Cages and Clusters]] §2 (extra).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A four-number bookkeeping of the bridge, 3-centre, 2-centre and BH$_2$ units in a borane.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> For $\mathrm B_p\mathrm H_{p+q}$: $s$ = B–H–B, $t$ = B–B–B, $y$ = B–B, $x$ = BH$_2$; $s+x=q$, $t+y=p-q/2$. $\mathrm{B_2H_6}$ 2002, $\mathrm{B_4H_{10}}$ 4012, $\mathrm{B_5H_9}$ 4120, $\mathrm{B_{10}H_{14}}$ 4620.

Counts bonds; Wade's rules predict shape.

Used in: [[CH07 Main Group Cages and Clusters]] §2. Related: [[Three-Centre Two-Electron Bond]] · [[Wade's Rules]].
