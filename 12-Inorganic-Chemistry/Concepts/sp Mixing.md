# sp Mixing

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** S&A Ch.2 ([[Shriver and Atkins Inorganic Chemistry 5th Ed.pdf#page=72|PDF p.72]]); the slide shows the two orderings ([[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=35|slide 35]]).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> When the 2s and 2p are close in energy the two $\sigma_g$ orbitals mix and push each other apart, so the $\pi$ pair drops below $3\sigma_g$.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Li$_2$ to N$_2$: $1\pi_u<3\sigma_g$; O$_2$, F$_2$: $3\sigma_g<1\pi_u$. The 2s–2p gap is 8.8 eV (C), 12.4 (N), 16.5 (O), 21.5 (F).

Required by experiment: $\mathrm{B_2}$ is paramagnetic (two unpaired $\pi_u$ electrons) and $\mathrm{C_2}$ is diamagnetic ($\pi_u^4$).

Used in: [[CH02 Molecular Orbital Theory Diatomics]] §4. Related: [[Bond Order]] · [[Molecular Orbital Theory]].
