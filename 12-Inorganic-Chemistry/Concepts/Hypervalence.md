# Hypervalence

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=23|slide 23]]; the MO description of $\mathrm{SF_6}$ is in CH03.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> More bonds than the octet allows: but not necessarily more than eight electrons in the valence shell of an orbital model.

> [!key] Definition
> **Hypervalence:** a main-group atom with more than 8 electrons in a Lewis structure: P in $\mathrm{PCl_5}$ (10), S in $\mathrm{SF_6}$ (12). Traditional explanation: low-lying $d$ orbitals. The lecture: the absence of a $2d$ shell is **not the only** reason it is rare in period 2 (packing more than four atoms round a small atom is hard), and **MO theory describes hypervalent compounds without $d$ orbitals**.

## MO view (preview, CH03)
For $\mathrm{SF_6}$ the six F ligand $\sigma$ orbitals form $a_{1g}+e_g+t_{1u}$; S $3s$ and $3p$ match $a_{1g}$ and $t_{1u}$; $e_g$ is non-bonding; the 12 electrons fill $a_{1g}$, $t_{1u}$ (bonding) and $e_g$ (non-bonding) with all antibonding orbitals empty. (The slide labels the sets $1a_1$, $1t_1$, $1e$; the standard $O_h$ labels are used here.)
