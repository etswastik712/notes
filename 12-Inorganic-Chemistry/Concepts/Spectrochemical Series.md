# Spectrochemical Series

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A p.475; Huheey pp.404–408.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Ligands ordered by the energy of the first $d$–$d$ band they produce: weak-field on the left, strong-field on the right.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{I^-<Br^-<S^{2-}<SCN^-<Cl^-<ONO^-<N_3^-<F^-<OH^-<C_2O_4^{2-}<O^{2-}<H_2O<NCS^-<MeCN<py<NH_3<en<bpy<phen<NO_2^-<PPh_3<CN^-<CO}$. Same order for every metal. Jørgensen: $\Delta_O=f\,g$ ($f$: 0.72 $\mathrm{Br^-}$ … 1.00 $\mathrm{H_2O}$ … 1.70 $\mathrm{CN^-}$), good to ≈7 % except $\mathrm{CN^-}$.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Anionic ligands are mostly low and $\mathrm{OH^-}<\mathrm{H_2O}$: the series is **not** electrostatic ($\sigma$ donation and $\pi$ effects matter; ligand-field theory).
