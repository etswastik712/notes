# Ligand Group Orbitals

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=48|slides 48–52]] (H₂O).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Combine the ligand orbitals into the patterns that have the same symmetry as a central-atom orbital; then they can overlap with it.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{H_2O}$: $\mathrm{H_a+H_b}$ is $a_1$, $\mathrm{H_a-H_b}$ is $b_1$; $\mathrm{NH_3}$: $a_1+e$; $\mathrm{SF_6}$: $a_{1g}+e_g+t_{1u}$; $\mathrm{CH_4}$: $a_1+t_2$.

An LGO with no central-atom partner is non-bonding (the $e_g$ of SF$_6$; the O $2p_\perp$ of water).

Used in: [[CH03 Molecular Orbital Theory Polyatomics]] §3–§6. Related: [[Reduction of a Representation]] · [[Symmetry Labels a b e t]].
