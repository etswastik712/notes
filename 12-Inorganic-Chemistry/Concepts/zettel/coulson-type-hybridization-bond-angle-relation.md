# Coulson-Type Hybridization Bond Angle Relation

A mathematical relationship equating the inter-orbital bond angle between equivalent s-p hybrid orbitals to their fractional s and p characters.

$$\cos \theta = \frac{S}{S - 1} = \frac{P - 1}{P}$$
