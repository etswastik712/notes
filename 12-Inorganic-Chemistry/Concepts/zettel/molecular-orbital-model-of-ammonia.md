# Molecular Orbital Model of Ammonia

The molecular orbital representation of ammonia where nitrogen 2s and 2pz orbitals mix with symmetry-adapted hydrogen 1s combinations to yield 1a1, 2a1, and 3a1 orbitals, while nitrogen 2px and 2py orbitals combine with matching hydrogen combinations to form degenerate 1e pairs. The HOMO (2a1) is largely localized on the nitrogen atom and corresponds to the lone pair.
