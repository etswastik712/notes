# Three-Center Two-Electron Bond in Diborane

A bonding interaction in diborane where two boron atomic orbitals and one bridging hydrogen 1s orbital combine to produce three molecular orbitals: one bonding, one non-bonding, and one antibonding. Two valence electrons occupy the bonding orbital, forming a bridge that stabilizes the electron-deficient dimer.
