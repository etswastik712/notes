# Aqua Acid

A class of Brønsted acid where the acidic proton resides directly on a coordinated water molecule attached to a central metal ion. Its strength increases with higher positive charge, smaller ionic radius, and increasing covalency from enhanced orbital overlap.

$$[\text{Fe}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_6]^{3+} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow [\text{Fe}(\text{OH}_2)_5(\text{OH})]^{2+} + \text{H}_3\text{O}^+$$

## Links
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/hydroxo-acid]] — Aqua acids carry acidic protons on intact coordinated water ligands, whereas hydroxo acids possess protons on hydroxyl groups lacking adjacent oxo groups
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/oxo-acid]] — Aqua acids lose a proton from a coordinated water ligand rather than a hydroxyl group bonded to a center bearing an oxo moiety
