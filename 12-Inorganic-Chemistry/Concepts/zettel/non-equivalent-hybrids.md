# Non-Equivalent Hybrids

A hybridization scheme where orbitals on a central atom partition into distinct subsets possessing unequal fractional s and p character, subject to the conservation rule that total s character sums to 1 and total p character sums to 3.

$$\sum P = 3$$

$$\sum S = 1$$

$$\cos \theta = \frac{S}{S - 1}$$
