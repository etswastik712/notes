# Antibonding Molecular Orbital

A molecular orbital resulting from destructive interference between adjacent atomic orbitals, characterized by a nodal surface between the nuclei and an energy higher than the separated atomic orbitals.

$$\psi_- = \chi_A - \chi_B$$
