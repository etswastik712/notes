# Electron Pair Repulsion Hierarchy

The relative order of electrostatic repulsion magnitude among electron pairs, where lone pairs exert stronger repulsive force than bonding pairs because lone pairs are held closer to the central nucleus.

$$\text{l.p./l.p.} > \text{l.p./b.p.} > \text{b.p./b.p.}$$
