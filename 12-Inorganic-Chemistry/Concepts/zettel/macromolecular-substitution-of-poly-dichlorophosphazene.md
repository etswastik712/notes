# Macromolecular Substitution of Poly(dichlorophosphazene)

The post-polymerization nucleophilic replacement of chlorine atoms along the poly(dichlorophosphazene) backbone using alkoxides, primary/secondary amines, or organometallic reagents to tailor physical and chemical material properties.
