# Oxo Acid

A Brønsted acid containing an acidic proton on a hydroxyl group with one or more oxo groups attached to the same central atom, typically found where the central element is in a high oxidation state. Its acidity is modulated by the inherent electronegativity and inductive effects of the central atom and its substituents.
