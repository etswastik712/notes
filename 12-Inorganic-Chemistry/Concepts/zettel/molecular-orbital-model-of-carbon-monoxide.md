# Molecular Orbital Model of Carbon Monoxide

The molecular orbital electronic structure of CO wherein the 3sigma HOMO is largely non-bonding and localized on carbon (C 2s character) while the 2pi LUMO is localized on carbon (C 2p character), enabling metal coordination via simultaneous sigma-donation and pi-back-donation.
