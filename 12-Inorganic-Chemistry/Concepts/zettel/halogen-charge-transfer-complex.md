# Halogen Charge-Transfer Complex

A molecular adduct formed when a dihalogen acts as a Lewis acid by accepting electron pair density from a solvent or halide donor into its low-lying sigma* antibonding molecular orbital, producing solvent-dependent color shifts and charge-transfer absorption bands.

$$[\text{I}_3]^-$$
