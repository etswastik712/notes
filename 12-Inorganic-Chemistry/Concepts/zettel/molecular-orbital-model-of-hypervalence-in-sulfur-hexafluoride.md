# Molecular Orbital Model of Hypervalence in Sulfur Hexafluoride

An explanation of hypervalent bonding in octahedral SF6 using symmetry-adapted ligand group orbitals constructed from fluorine atomic orbitals rather than sulfur d orbitals. Twelve valence electrons occupy one bonding a1g set, three bonding t1u sets, and two non-bonding eg sets, leaving all antibonding orbitals unoccupied and stabilizing the molecule.
