# Construction of Lewis Structures

A systematic procedure to assemble molecular dot diagrams by summing valence electrons, placing the least electronegative element at the center, establishing single bonds, and distributing remaining electron pairs to satisfy the octet rule.

## Links
- needs:: [[zettel/octet-rule]] — Electron pairs are iteratively distributed to peripheral and central atoms specifically until each atom attains an octet
- used-in:: [[zettel/formal-charge]] — Formal charge evaluation is applied across constructed candidate Lewis structures to identify the lowest-energy arrangement
