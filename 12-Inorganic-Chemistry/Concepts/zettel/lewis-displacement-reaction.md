# Lewis Displacement Reaction

A reaction category wherein one Lewis base displaces another Lewis base from an acid center, or one Lewis acid displaces another Lewis acid from a base center. Brønsted proton transfers represent a specific case where a base displaces another base from H+.

$$\text{HS}^- \text{(aq)} + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{S}^{2-} \text{(aq)} + \text{H}_3\text{O}^+ \text{(aq)}$$
