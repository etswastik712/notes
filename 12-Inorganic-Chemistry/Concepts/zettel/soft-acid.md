# Soft Acid

A Lewis acid containing more polarizable acceptor centers that forms complexes with pronounced covalent character. It exhibits a halide binding preference of fluoride < chloride < bromide < iodide.

$$\text{F}^- < \text{Cl}^- < \text{Br}^- < \text{I}^-$$

$$\text{R}_2\text{O} \ll \text{R}_2\text{S}$$

$$\text{R}_3\text{N} \ll \text{R}_3\text{P}$$
