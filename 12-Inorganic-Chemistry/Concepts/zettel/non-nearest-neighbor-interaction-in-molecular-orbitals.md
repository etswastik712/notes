# Non-Nearest Neighbor Interaction in Molecular Orbitals

The interaction between atoms that are not directly adjacent in a polyatomic molecule, producing weak bonding or weak antibonding character based on orbital phase alignment. In linear or cyclic systems, these secondary overlaps shift molecular orbital energy levels relative to isolated nearest-neighbor bonds.
