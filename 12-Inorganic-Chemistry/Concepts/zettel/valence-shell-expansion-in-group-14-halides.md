# Valence Shell Expansion in Group 14 Halides

A mechanism of Lewis acidity wherein central atoms such as silicon or tin expand their valence shell to accommodate incoming electron pairs, converting tetrahalides into hypervalent six-coordinate complexes.

$$[\text{SiF}_6]^{2-}$$

$$\text{SiF}_4 + 2\text{HF} \rightarrow [\text{SiF}_6]^{2-} + 2\text{H}^+$$

$$\text{SiI}_4 < \text{SiBr}_4 < \text{SiCl}_4 < \text{SiF}_4$$
