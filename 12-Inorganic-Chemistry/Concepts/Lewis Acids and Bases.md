# Lewis Acids and Bases

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=62|slides 62–64, 70–71]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The acid is the electron-pair acceptor, the base the electron-pair donor; they join by a dative bond into an adduct.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Five types of acceptor: incomplete octet, cation, octet rearrangement ($\mathrm{CO_2}$), shell expansion ($\mathrm{SiF_4}$), $\pi^*/\sigma^*$ ($\mathrm{I_2}$). Orbital picture: base HOMO → acid LUMO.

Includes all coordination compounds, all proton transfers and adduct formation; it is the widest definition and the one used in organic mechanisms.
