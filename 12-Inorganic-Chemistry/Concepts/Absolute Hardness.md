# Absolute Hardness

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** [[CH06 Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]] §6 (Parr and Pearson 1983; Pearson 1988).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Hardness measures how reluctant a species is to change its number of electrons: half the gap between ionising it and adding one.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\eta=(I-A)/2$, $\chi=(I+A)/2$ (Mulliken), $S=1/2\eta$; cation: $\eta=(I_{n+1}-I_n)/2$. $\mathrm{Al^{3+}}$ 45.8, $\mathrm{Na^+}$ 21.1, $\mathrm{Ag^+}$ 7.0, $\mathrm{Au^+}$ 5.6 eV; F 7.0, Cl 4.7, Br 4.2, I 3.7 eV.

$\Delta N=(\chi_A-\chi_B)/2(\eta_A+\eta_B)$ and $\Delta E=-(\chi_A-\chi_B)^2/4(\eta_A+\eta_B)$ rank interactions: $\mathrm{Ag^+}$ with I: −1.42 eV, with F: −0.30 eV.

Used in: [[CH06 Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]] §6. Related: [[Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]] · [[Frontier Orbitals]].
