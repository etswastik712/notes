# Irving-Williams Series

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A p.483, Fig. 20.14.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The stability of complexes of the divalent first-row ions follows one order for almost every ligand.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{Ba^{2+}<Sr^{2+}<Ca^{2+}<Mg^{2+}<Mn^{2+}<Fe^{2+}<Co^{2+}<Ni^{2+}<Cu^{2+}>Zn^{2+}}$. Rises with falling radius (electrostatics), plus LFSE beyond $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$; $\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}$ exceeds $\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}$ because of the Jahn–Teller elongation (four strong equatorial bonds).

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> $\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}$ ($d^{10}$) has no LFSE; $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$ ($d^5$ HS) none either.
