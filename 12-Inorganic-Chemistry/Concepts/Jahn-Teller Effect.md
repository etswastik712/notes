# Jahn-Teller Effect

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A pp.481–482, Figs 20.11–20.12.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A non-linear complex with an orbitally degenerate ground state distorts to lift the degeneracy.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Strong for unequal $e_g$ occupation (HS $d^4$, LS $d^7$, $d^9$): $E(Q)=\frac12kQ^2\pm\alpha Q$, minimum at $Q_0=\alpha/k$, $E_{JT}=-\alpha^2/2k$; for $d^9$ the net gain is $\delta_1/2$. Weak for unequal $t_{2g}$ occupation. Elongation (two long bonds) is commoner than compression. Static below 20 K, dynamic above for $[\mathrm{Cu(OH_2)_6}]^{2+}$.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Predicts instability, **not** the direction of the distortion.
