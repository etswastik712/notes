# Autoprotolysis of Water

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=56|slide 56]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Water ionises a little into hydronium and hydroxide, and the amount grows with temperature.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $2\mathrm{H_2O}\rightleftharpoons\mathrm{H_3O^+}+\mathrm{OH^-}$; $\mathrm pK_w=14.95,13.99,13.26,12.70,12.25$ at 0, 25, 50, 75, 100 °C; neutral pH $=\mathrm pK_w/2$ (6.13 at 100 °C). $\Delta H=52.7$ kJ/mol (van't Hoff).

The slide's 'about 1 in $10^7$' is the concentration exponent; the ionised fraction is $1.8\times10^{-9}$.

Used in: [[CH04 Acid-Base Concepts]] §2. Related: [[Arrhenius Acid-Base Definition]] · [[Conjugate Acid-Base Pair]].
