# Steric Number

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** the formula as used on [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=17|slide 17]] for $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{CO_3^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{H_2O}$. · 🔵 **extra · beyond:** the 29-species check.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The steric number is the number of directions in which electron density points from the central atom.

> [!key] Definition
> $Z=\tfrac12\,(V+M+A-C)$ with $V$ valence electrons of the central atom, $M$ monovalent atoms (H, halogens), $A$ anion charge, $C$ cation charge (O counts 0). Lone pairs $=Z-(\text{atoms attached})$.

## Table (computed)
$Z=4$: $\mathrm{CH_4}$, $\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{POCl_3}$ (0 l.p.), $\mathrm{NH_3}$, $\mathrm{SO_3^{2-}}$, $\mathrm{XeO_3}$ (1), $\mathrm{H_2O}$ (2). $Z=5$: $\mathrm{PCl_5}$ (0), $\mathrm{SF_4}$ (1), $\mathrm{ClF_3}$ (2), $\mathrm{XeF_2}$, $\mathrm{I_3^-}$ (3). $Z=6$: $\mathrm{SF_6}$ (0), $\mathrm{BrF_5}$, $\mathrm{XeOF_4}$ (1), $\mathrm{XeF_4}$, $\mathrm{ICl_4^-}$ (2). $Z=7$: $\mathrm{IF_7}$ (0), $\mathrm{XeF_5^-}$ (2). 28 of 29 shapes agree.

> [!pitfall] Odd-electron species
> $\mathrm{NO_2}$ has $Z=2.5$: the formula does not give an integer (the odd electron behaves as a region of reduced repulsion; the O–N–O angle is about 134°, recalled).
