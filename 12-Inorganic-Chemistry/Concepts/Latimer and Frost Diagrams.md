# Latimer and Frost Diagrams

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** S&A §5.12–§5.13 (beyond p.161).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A Latimer diagram lists the potential for each step between oxidation states; a Frost diagram plots $NE^\circ$ against $N$ so that stability can be read off the shape.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Mn in acid: $\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\overset{0.56}\to\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}\overset{2.26}\to\mathrm{MnO_2}\overset{0.95}\to\mathrm{Mn^{3+}}\overset{1.51}\to\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}\overset{-1.18}\to\mathrm{Mn}$; $NE^\circ$: 0, −2.36, −0.85, +0.10, +4.62, +5.18 V.

A species above the line joining its neighbours disproportionates: $\mathrm{Mn^{3+}}$ and $\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}$.

Used in: [[CH09 Redox]] §11. Related: [[Pourbaix Diagram]].
