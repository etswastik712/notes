# Spinel Structure

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A p.483; Huheey pp.411–413, Table 11.8.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Oxide ions in a cubic close packing; the cations sit in 1/8 of the tetrahedral and 1/2 of the octahedral holes.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> **Normal** $(\mathrm A)_t[\mathrm B_2]_o\mathrm O_4$ ($\mathrm{MgAl_2O_4}$, $\mathrm{Co_3O_4}$, $\mathrm{Mn_3O_4}$); **inverse** $(\mathrm B)_t[\mathrm{AB}]_o\mathrm O_4$ ($\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}$, $\mathrm{NiFe_2O_4}$). $\lambda$ = fraction of B in tetrahedral holes (0 normal, 0.5 inverse). Decided by comparing the OSPE of $\mathrm A^{2+}$ and $\mathrm B^{3+}$.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> $\mathrm{Co_3O_4}$ is normal because low-spin $\mathrm{Co^{3+}}$ ($d^6$) strongly prefers octahedral sites.
