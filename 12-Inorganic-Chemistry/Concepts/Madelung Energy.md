# Madelung Energy

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=88|slide 88]]; Tutorial 3 Q12.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The Coulomb attraction between ions, inversely proportional to the distance: small ions bind best.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $E\propto-z_1z_2/d$; the Coulomb pair energy $-1389.35\,z^2/d$ kJ/mol Å: LiF −665, MgO −2621, AgI −415 kJ/mol.

Controls hard–hard interactions; soft–soft interactions are controlled by covalency and London dispersion.

Used in: [[CH06 Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]] §5–§6. Related: [[London Dispersion]] · [[Absolute Hardness]].
