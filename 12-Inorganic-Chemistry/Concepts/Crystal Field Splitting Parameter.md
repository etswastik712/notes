# Crystal Field Splitting Parameter

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A pp.473–475; Huheey p.398.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $\Delta_O$ is the gap between the $e_g$ and $t_{2g}$ sets in an octahedral complex.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\Delta_O=10Dq=E(e_g)-E(t_{2g})$; $e_g=+\frac35\Delta_O=+6Dq$, $t_{2g}=-\frac25\Delta_O=-4Dq$ (barycentre: $2\cdot\frac35=3\cdot\frac25$). For $d^1$ it equals the absorption energy: $[\mathrm{Ti(OH_2)_6}]^{3+}$ 20 300 cm$^{-1}$ = 243 kJ mol$^{-1}$, 493 nm. Depends on ligand ([[Spectrochemical Series]]), metal (rises with charge, $3d<4d<5d$), geometry ($\Delta_T=\frac49\Delta_O$).

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> For $d^n$ with $n>1$ the absorption energies contain electron repulsion, so $\Delta_O$ is not read directly. $1\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}=0.011963\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}$.
