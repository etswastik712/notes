# Phosphorus Oxides and Sulfides

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=105|slides 105–108]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Insert an oxygen or sulfur into each edge of the $\mathrm P_4$ tetrahedron, then add terminal atoms.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{P_4O_6}$ (O in 6 edges, P +3), $\mathrm{P_4O_{10}}$ (+4 terminal O, P +5), $\mathrm{P_4S_3}$ (3 S in basal edges), $\mathrm{P_4S_{10}}$. Masses 219.9, 283.9, 220.1, 444.5 g/mol; $\mathrm{P_4O_{10}+6H_2O\to4H_3PO_4}$ (1.38 g per g).

$\mathrm{P_4S_{10}}$ + anisole → Lawesson's reagent (C=O → C=S).

Used in: [[CH07 Main Group Cages and Clusters]] §5. Related: [[Cyclophosphazene]].
