# Zintl Ion

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=101|slides 101–102]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Main-group atoms with electrons donated by an electropositive metal form cluster ions that follow Wade's rules, each atom carrying one outward lone pair.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Skeletal electrons $=g-2n$. $\mathrm{Sn_5^{2-}}$ 12 (closo), $\mathrm{Sn_9^{4-}}$ 22 (nido), $\mathrm{Bi_8^{2+}}$ 22 (arachno). Extracted from $\mathrm{NaPb_x}$-type solids with cryptand in ethylenediamine or NH$_3$.

Sn is isolobal with BH (4 valence electrons, 2 used externally): Zintl ions are boranes without the hydrogen.

Used in: [[CH07 Main Group Cages and Clusters]] §4. Related: [[Wade's Rules]] · [[Cryptand]].
