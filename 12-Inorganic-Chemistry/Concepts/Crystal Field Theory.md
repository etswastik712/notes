# Crystal Field Theory

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A §20.1 (p.473); Huheey pp.394–397.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Treat each ligand as a point negative charge; the $d$ orbitals pointing at the charges rise, those pointing between fall.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Pure CFT assumes only an **electrostatic** metal–ligand interaction; the five $d$ orbitals split in a way fixed by the geometry (symmetry) while the **barycentre** stays put. The splitting is about 10 % of the bonding energy but fixes colour, spin state, magnetism, geometry and many thermodynamic trends.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> It cannot explain the spectrochemical series, covalency or the nephelauxetic effect: that is **ligand-field theory** (MO-based), outside Quiz 3.
