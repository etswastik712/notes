# Walsh Diagram

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** [[CH03 Molecular Orbital Theory Polyatomics]] §8 (extra).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Follow each orbital's energy as the molecule bends: the shape with the most electrons in the lowest orbitals wins.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> AH$_2$: 4 electrons → linear ($\mathrm{BeH_2}$); 8 electrons → bent (H$_2$O, 104.5°) because the $3a_1$ drops steeply on bending as it gains $s$ character; 5–6 electrons → intermediate (BH$_2$ ≈ 131°).

Complements VSEPR: the same shapes from orbital energies rather than pair repulsion.

Used in: [[CH03 Molecular Orbital Theory Polyatomics]] §8. Related: [[H3 Molecular Orbitals]] · [[VSEPR Model]].
