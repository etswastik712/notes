# Bronsted-Lowry Acids and Bases

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=58|slides 58–60]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Acids give protons and bases take them; it works in any protic solvent, not just water.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Acid$_1$ + Base$_2$ ⇌ Base$_1$ + Acid$_2$, $K=10^{\Delta\mathrm pK_a}$. Acetic acid + ammonia ($\Delta\mathrm pK_a=4.49$): $K=3\times10^4$, not describable by Arrhenius.

Water is amphoteric; in water the strongest acid is $\mathrm{H_3O^+}$ and the strongest base $\mathrm{OH^-}$ (levelling).

Used in: [[CH04 Acid-Base Concepts]] §3. Related: [[Conjugate Acid-Base Pair]] · [[Amphoterism]] · [[Levelling Effect]].
