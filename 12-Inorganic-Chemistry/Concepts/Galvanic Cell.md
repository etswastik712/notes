# Galvanic Cell

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** S&A §5.2 (Fig. 5.1).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Two half-cells joined by a wire and a salt bridge: the spontaneous reaction pushes electrons round the circuit.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Cathode: reduction; anode: oxidation. Daniell: $E^\circ=1.10$ V, $\Delta G=-212$ kJ/mol, $K=10^{37.2}$; H$_2$/O$_2$ fuel cell 1.229 V.

Measure at zero current for reversibility.

Used in: [[CH09 Redox]] §2, §5. Related: [[Standard Reduction Potential]] · [[Faraday Constant]].
