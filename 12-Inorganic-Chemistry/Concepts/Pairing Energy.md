# Pairing Energy

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A p.476; Huheey pp.399–402, Table 11.4.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The price of putting two electrons in the same orbital.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $P=P_{\rm Coulomb}+P_{\rm exchange}$: Coulomb repulsion of the pair (smaller for larger $4d$/$5d$ orbitals) plus the lost exchange stabilisation of parallel spins (largest for $d^5$). Free ions (kJ mol$^{-1}$): $\mathrm{Cr^{2+}}$ 244, $\mathrm{Mn^{2+}}$ 285, $\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}$ 357, $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ 174, $\mathrm{Co^{3+}}$ 229; 15–30 % smaller in complexes.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Compare $P$ with $\Delta_O$ to decide spin state; $\Delta_O>P$ low spin.
