# Amphoterism

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=58|slide 58]]; oxides in [[CH05 Acid Strength Lewis Acidity and Reactions]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A species that can act as either an acid or a base, depending on its partner.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{H_2O}$ (donates to $\mathrm{NH_3}$, accepts from HF); $\mathrm{HCO_3^-}$; $\mathrm{Al_2O_3}$ (dissolves in acid and in base); $\mathrm{Al(OH)_3}$.

Amphoteric oxides lie on the metal/non-metal boundary: B$_2$O$_3$ acidic, Al$_2$O$_3$ amphoteric, Tl$_2$O basic.

Used in: [[CH04 Acid-Base Concepts]] §3. Related: [[Bronsted-Lowry Acids and Bases]] · [[Lux-Flood Definition]].
