# Ambidentate Ligand

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=87|slide 87]]; Tutorial 3 Q11.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A ligand with two possible donor atoms binds through the one that matches the metal's hardness.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{SCN^-}$: N to hard ions ($\mathrm{[Fe(NCS)]^{2+}}$), S to soft ($\mathrm{[Au(SCN)_2]^-}$). Also $\mathrm{NO_2^-}$ (N/O), $\mathrm{CN^-}$ (C/N), $\mathrm{SO_3^{2-}}$ (S/O).

Gives linkage isomers: $\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5(NCS)]^{2+}}$ and $\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5(SCN)]^{2+}}$.

Used in: [[CH06 Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]] §4. Related: [[Linkage Isomerism]] · [[Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]].
