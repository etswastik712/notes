# Inorganic Heterocycle

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=110|slides 109–111]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Take an organic ring and replace every carbon by other $p$-block atoms.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{S_8}$, cyclosilanes $(\mathrm R_2\mathrm{Si})_n$, $(\mathrm{RAs})_n$ (Salvarsan, $n=3,5$), $(\mathrm{NPCl_2})_3$ (1834), $\mathrm{S_4N_4}$ (1835), borazine (1926).

Same electron count as the carbon analogue does not mean the same bonding.

Used in: [[CH08 Main Group Rings and Polymers]] §1. Related: [[Cyclophosphazene]] · [[Iminoborane]].
