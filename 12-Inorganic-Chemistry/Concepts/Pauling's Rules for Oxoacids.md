# Pauling's Rules for Oxoacids

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** [[CH05 Acid Strength Lewis Acidity and Reactions]] §2 (Pauling 1960; not on the slides).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Each extra oxo group lowers the first pKa by about five units, and each successive proton is about five units harder to remove.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm pK_a^{(1)}\approx8-5n$, $\mathrm pK_a^{(k+1)}\approx\mathrm pK_a^{(k)}+5$ ($n$ = number of non-protonated O). $\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$: 2.15, 7.20, 12.35.

$n=0$: ≈8 (HOCl 7.5); $n=1$: ≈3 ($\mathrm{H_3PO_4}$ 2.15); $n=2$: ≈−2 ($\mathrm{HNO_3}$ −1.4); $n=3$: ≈−7 ($\mathrm{HClO_4}$ −8).

Used in: [[CH05 Acid Strength Lewis Acidity and Reactions]] §2. Related: [[Oxo Acid]] · [[Inductive Effect on Acidity]].
