# Photoelectron Spectroscopy

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=44|slide 44]] (NH₃).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Knock an electron out with a photon of known energy and measure its kinetic energy: the difference is how tightly it was bound.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{IE}=h\nu-E_{\rm kin}$; Koopmans: $\mathrm{IE}\approx-\varepsilon_{\rm MO}$. NH$_3$ (He I 21.2 eV): bands at ≈11 eV ($2a_1$, lone pair) and ≈16 eV ($1e$); H$_2$O: 12.6, 14.7, 18.6, 32.2 eV; CH$_4$: ≈14 and ≈23 eV.

Vibrational fine structure shows the change of shape on ionisation (NH$_3^+$ is planar). It is direct evidence that localised 'equivalent' bonds and lone pairs are not energy eigenstates.

Used in: [[CH03 Molecular Orbital Theory Polyatomics]] §4, §6. Related: [[Molecular Orbital Theory]] · [[Lone Pair Stereochemical Activity]].
