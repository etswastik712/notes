# Back-Donation in Boron Halides

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=72|slide 72]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A halogen lone pair donates into the empty boron 2p, partly filling the very orbital that makes BX$_3$ an acid.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Efficient for small, matched $2p$ orbitals (F), poor for large $3p$, $4p$ (Cl, Br). Shortens B–F (≈1.30 Å, recalled) and raises the LUMO.

The same $p\pi$–$p\pi$ idea explains why $\mathrm{B(OMe)_3}$ and $\mathrm{B(NMe_2)_3}$ are weak acids.

Used in: [[CH05 Acid Strength Lewis Acidity and Reactions]] §4.1. Related: [[Lewis Acidity of BX3]] · [[Frontier Orbitals]].
