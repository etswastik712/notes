# Nephelauxetic Effect

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** Huheey p.413, Table 11.10.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Electron–electron repulsion is smaller in a complex than in the free ion.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> "Cloud expanding": the $d$ electrons spread over metal–ligand molecular orbitals, i.e. **indirect evidence for covalency**. Ligand order (increasing effect): $\mathrm{F^-<H_2O<urea<NH_3<en\approx ox<Cl^-<CN^-<Br^-<N_3^-<I^-}$ `[verify]`. It also lowers $P$ by 15–30 %.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> It is a failure of the pure electrostatic model, one of the reasons for ligand-field theory.
