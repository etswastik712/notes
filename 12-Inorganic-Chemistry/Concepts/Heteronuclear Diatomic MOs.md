# Heteronuclear Diatomic MOs

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=36|slides 36–39]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Unequal partners share the electrons unequally: the electronegative atom gets most of the bonding MO and the other atom most of the antibonding one.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> For AO energies $\alpha_A<\alpha_B$ and coupling $\beta$: $c_A^2(\text{bonding})=\tfrac12\big[1+\frac{\Delta/2}{\sqrt{(\Delta/2)^2+\beta^2}}\big]$, $\Delta=|\alpha_A-\alpha_B|$. HF ($\Delta=5.05$ eV, $|\beta|=3$ eV): 82 % F.

HF: 2 electrons in the bond, 6 as non-bonding F pairs; CO: 3σ (HOMO) and 2π (LUMO) both on C.

Used in: [[CH02 Molecular Orbital Theory Diatomics]] §5. Related: [[CO Back-Donation]] · [[Bond Order]].
