# LCAO Approximation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=30|slide 30]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Near each nucleus the electron looks like an atomic orbital, so build the molecular orbital by adding atomic orbitals with weights.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\psi=c_A\chi_A+c_B\chi_B$; $c_A^2$ is the probability of being in $\chi_A$; $\psi^2=c_A^2\chi_A^2+2c_Ac_B\chi_A\chi_B+c_B^2\chi_B^2$ with the cross term the interference. With overlap $S$: $E_\pm=(\alpha\pm\beta)/(1\pm S)$, $\psi_\pm=(\chi_A\pm\chi_B)/\sqrt{2(1\pm S)}$.

For $\mathrm H_2$ at $S=0.7534$ the antibonding level rises $(1+S)/(1-S)=7.1$ times more than the bonding level falls.
