# Cryptand

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=101|slide 101]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A three-dimensional cage ligand that wraps a cation and hides its charge so the anion can be isolated.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{N(C_2H_4OC_2H_4OC_2H_4)_3N}$ encapsulates $\mathrm{Na^+}$ in $[\mathrm{Na(crypt)}]_2\mathrm{Sn_5}$.

Its use makes crystalline Zintl-ion salts possible.

Used in: [[CH07 Main Group Cages and Clusters]] §4. Related: [[Zintl Ion]].
