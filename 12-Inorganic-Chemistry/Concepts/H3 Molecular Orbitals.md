# H3 Molecular Orbitals

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=41|slide 41]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Three hydrogen 1s orbitals make three MOs whose shape depends on whether the atoms form a line or a triangle.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Linear: $E=\alpha\pm\sqrt{2}\beta,\alpha$ with coefficients $(\tfrac12,\tfrac1{\sqrt2},\tfrac12)$. Triangle: $\alpha+2\beta$ and a degenerate pair at $\alpha-\beta$. $\mathrm{H_3^+}$: triangle (4.0 vs 2.83 $|\beta|$); $\mathrm{H_3^-}$: linear.

The first example of a shape decided by electron counting (Walsh).

Used in: [[CH03 Molecular Orbital Theory Polyatomics]] §2. Related: [[Walsh Diagram]] · [[Hueckel Pi Model]].
