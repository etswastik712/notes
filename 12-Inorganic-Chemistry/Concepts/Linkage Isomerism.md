# Linkage Isomerism

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Tutorial 3 Q11; [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=87|slide 87]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Same formula, same ligand, different atom attached to the metal.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5(NCS)]^{2+}}$ ($\kappa N$) versus $\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5(SCN)]^{2+}}$ ($\kappa S$); nitro/nitrito $\mathrm{NO_2^-}$.

The preferred isomer follows HSAB; the less stable one can be trapped kinetically.

Used in: [[CH06 Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]] §4. Related: [[Ambidentate Ligand]].
