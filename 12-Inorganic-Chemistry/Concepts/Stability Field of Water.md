# Stability Field of Water

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** S&A §5.7 (Fig. 5.3).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The strip of potential and pH where water is neither oxidised nor reduced.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Lower line $E=-0.059\,\mathrm{pH}$ ($\mathrm H^+/\mathrm H_2$), upper line $E=1.23-0.059\,\mathrm{pH}$ ($\mathrm{O_2/H_2O}$); at pH 7: −0.41 to +0.82 V; natural waters pH 4–9.

Kinetics can keep couples outside the window alive ($\mathrm{MnO_4^-}$, $\mathrm{Ce^{4+}}$) because O$_2$ evolution needs four electrons and an O–O bond.

Used in: [[CH09 Redox]] §7. Related: [[Pourbaix Diagram]].
