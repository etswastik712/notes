# Hueckel Pi Model

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** Used in [[CH03 Molecular Orbital Theory Polyatomics]] §2 and [[CH08 Main Group Rings and Polymers]] §2 (not on the slides).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Treat only the $\pi$ electrons, give each atom an energy $\alpha$ and each bond a coupling $\beta$, and diagonalise the small matrix.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Benzene: $E=\alpha+2\beta,\alpha+\beta\,(\times2),\alpha-\beta\,(\times2),\alpha-2\beta$, gap $2|\beta|$, π bond order 0.667. Borazine ($h_B=-1,h_N=1.5,k=0.8$): gap 2.97 $|\beta|$, charges B 0.23, N 1.77, bond order 0.44.

It is the matrix version of $\psi=\sum c_i\chi_i$; the same matrix with three atoms gives linear $\mathrm H_3$ and B–H–B.

Used in: [[CH08 Main Group Rings and Polymers]] §2. Related: [[H3 Molecular Orbitals]] · [[Three-Centre Two-Electron Bond]].
