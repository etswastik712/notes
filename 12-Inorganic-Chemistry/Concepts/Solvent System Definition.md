# Solvent System Definition

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=64|slide 64]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> An acid is whatever raises the characteristic cation of the solvent, a base whatever raises its characteristic anion.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Water $\mathrm{H_3O^+/OH^-}$; liquid ammonia $\mathrm{NH_4^+/NH_2^-}$; $\mathrm{BrF_3}$: $\mathrm{BrF_2^+/BrF_4^-}$.

Neutralisation in the solvent is the combination of cation and anion to give the solvent.

Used in: [[CH04 Acid-Base Concepts]] §5. Related: [[Arrhenius Acid-Base Definition]] · [[Lewis Acids and Bases]].
