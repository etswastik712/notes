# Superacid

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=74|slide 74]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A Lewis acid grabs the fluoride from a strong Brønsted acid, leaving a proton with almost no base to hold it.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{SbF_5+2HF\to[SbF_6]^-+[H_2F]^+}$; $\mathrm{SbF_5+HSO_3F}$ ('magic acid') protonates almost any organic compound; $H_0\approx-23$ (recalled).

Superacids are Brønsted acids made by Lewis acids: the two definitions combined.

Used in: [[CH05 Acid Strength Lewis Acidity and Reactions]] §4.2, §6. Related: [[Levelling Effect]] · [[Lewis Acids and Bases]].
