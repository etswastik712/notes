# d Orbitals in Complexes

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A Fig. 20.1; Huheey Figs 11.3–11.6.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Two $d$ orbitals point **at** the octahedral ligands ($e_g$), three point **between** them ($t_{2g}$).

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $e_g=\{d_{z^2},d_{x^2-y^2}\}$ (symmetry $E_g$ in $O_h$), $t_{2g}=\{d_{xy},d_{yz},d_{zx}\}$ ($T_{2g}$). In a tetrahedron the labels become $e$ and $t_2$ (no inversion centre) and the order reverses. $d_{z^2}$ is the combination $d_{2z^2-x^2-y^2}$: lobes on $z$ plus a torus in the $xy$ plane.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> The $e_g$/$t_{2g}$ names come from symmetry (group theory) and say nothing about which is higher; that needs the electrostatics.
