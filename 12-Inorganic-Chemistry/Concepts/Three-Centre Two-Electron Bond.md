# Three-Centre Two-Electron Bond

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=96|slide 96]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Two electrons hold three atoms together when there are not enough electrons for ordinary two-electron bonds.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Three AOs (two B $sp^3$, one H 1s) give a bonding, a non-bonding and an antibonding MO; the 2 electrons fill the bonding one. Hückel levels $\alpha\pm\sqrt2\beta,\alpha$: stabilisation $2.83|\beta|$. Bridge bond order ½; B–H$_b$ 1.31 Å vs B–H$_t$ 1.19 Å.

Also B–B–B 3c-2e bonds in higher boranes, Al–C–Al in $\mathrm{Al_2Me_6}$, Be–H–Be in $\mathrm{BeH_2}$.

Used in: [[CH07 Main Group Cages and Clusters]] §2.2. Related: [[CH07 Main Group Cages and Clusters|Diborane]] · [[Styx Numbers]].
