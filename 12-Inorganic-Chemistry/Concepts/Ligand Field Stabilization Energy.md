# Ligand Field Stabilization Energy

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A pp.475–478, Table 20.2; Huheey pp.399–402, Table 11.3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> How far below the barycentre the occupied $d$ orbitals sit, minus any extra pairing cost.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Octahedral: $\mathrm{LFSE}=(-0.4\,n_{t_{2g}}+0.6\,n_{e_g})\Delta_O+(\text{extra pairs})P$; tetrahedral: $(0.6\,n_e-0.4\,n_{t_2})\Delta_T$ (as a stabilisation, signs reversed). High-spin octahedral: $d^0$–$d^{10}$ → 0, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2, 0.6, 0, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2, 0.6, 0 ($\Delta_O$). Low spin: $d^4$ 1.6−$P$, $d^5$ 2.0−2$P$, $d^6$ 2.4−2$P$, $d^7$ 1.8−$P$.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Count only pairing **in excess of** the spherical-field (Hund) configuration. S&A call it LFSE, Huheey CFSE (strictly CFSE is for ions in crystals).
