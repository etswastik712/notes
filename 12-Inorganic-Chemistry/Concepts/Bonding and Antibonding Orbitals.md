# Bonding and Antibonding Orbitals

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=33|slide 33]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> In phase: waves add between the nuclei and glue them. Out of phase: a node between the nuclei and no glue.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> A bonding MO lowers the energy below the atomic orbitals, an antibonding MO raises it (more), a non-bonding MO keeps it. $\mathrm H_2$: gap 11.4 eV, dissociation 4.5 eV (434 kJ/mol).

Filling an antibonding orbital costs more than filling the corresponding bonding orbital gains: $\mathrm{He_2}$ ($\sigma^2\sigma^{*2}$) does not exist.
