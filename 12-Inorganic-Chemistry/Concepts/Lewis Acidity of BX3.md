# Lewis Acidity of BX3

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=72|slide 72]]; Tutorial 3 Q8.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The most electronegative halide gives the weakest Lewis acid because its lone pairs donate back into boron's empty orbital.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{BF_3<BCl_3<BBr_3}$ (stability of $\mathrm{:NMe_3}$ adducts). Back-donation order $2p$–$2p>2p$–$3p>2p$–$4p$; the cost of pyramidalising BF$_3$ (losing its π donation) adds to the effect.

For $\mathrm{SiX_4}$ the order reverses ($\mathrm{SiI_4<SiF_4}$): no empty $p$ to saturate.

Used in: [[CH05 Acid Strength Lewis Acidity and Reactions]] §4.1. Related: [[Back-Donation in Boron Halides]] · [[Lewis Acids and Bases]].
