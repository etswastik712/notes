# Inductive Effect on Acidity

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=68|slide 68]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Electron-withdrawing atoms near the acidic group stabilise the anion; the effect fades with distance.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> pKa: CH$_3$COOH 4.76, ClCH$_2$COOH 2.86, Cl$_2$CHCOOH 1.29, Cl$_3$CCOOH 0.65: 1.37 units per Cl (×79, ×2951, ×12882 in $K_a$).

Electron donation (+I of CH$_3$, π-donation of NH$_2$ to S) weakens acids.

Used in: [[CH05 Acid Strength Lewis Acidity and Reactions]] §2. Related: [[Oxo Acid]] · [[Pauling's Rules for Oxoacids]].
