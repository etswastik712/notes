# Octahedral Site Preference Energy

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus (Quiz 3)</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught (reading range):** S&A pp.482–483, Fig. 20.13; Huheey pp.411–413, Table 11.9.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The extra LFSE gained by putting an ion in an octahedral rather than a tetrahedral site.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\mathrm{OSPE}=\mathrm{LFSE}_{oct}-\mathrm{LFSE}_{tet}$ with $\Delta_T=\frac49\Delta_O$: $0$ for $d^0$, HS $d^5$, $d^{10}$; $0.133,0.267,0.844,0.422$ $\Delta_O$ for $d^1$–$d^4$ and the same pattern repeated; max $0.844\Delta_O$ at $d^3$, $d^8$. With aqua $\Delta_O$ (kJ mol$^{-1}$): $\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}$ 176, $\mathrm{Mn^{3+}}$ 106, $\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}$ 86, $\mathrm{Co^{2+}}$ 29, $\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}$ ≈15.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Only the *difference* between cations decides spinel inversion; other factors (radius, covalency) can dominate.
