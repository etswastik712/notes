# Pourbaix Diagram

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** S&A §5.14 (beyond the Quiz 2 excerpt).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A map of which species of an element is most stable at each potential and pH.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Lines of slope $-(m/\nu)59$ mV/pH separate regions; the water lines bound the practical region; natural waters sit between pH 4 and 9.

Used to predict corrosion, immunity and passivation of metals.

Used in: [[CH09 Redox]] §7, §11. Related: [[Stability Field of Water]] · [[Latimer and Frost Diagrams]].
