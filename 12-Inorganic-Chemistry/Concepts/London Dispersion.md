# London Dispersion

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=89|slide 89]] (Pitzer).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Instantaneous dipoles in big, floppy electron clouds attract each other, and the effect grows with polarisability.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Dispersion energy increases with the size and polarisability of the interacting atoms, so it stabilises soft–soft more than soft–hard bonds.

A partial reason why AgI and HgS are so insoluble: the Coulomb term of AgI is smaller than that of AgF, yet AgI is less soluble.

Used in: [[CH06 Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]] §5. Related: [[Madelung Energy]] · [[Absolute Hardness]].
