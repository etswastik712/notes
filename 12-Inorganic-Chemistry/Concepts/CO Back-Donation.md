# CO Back-Donation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[chem pre-ms .pdf#page=38|slide 38]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> CO gives a lone pair from carbon to the metal and takes electron density back from the metal into its empty $\pi^*$ orbital: a two-way bond.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> HOMO $3\sigma$ (mostly C 2s) donates to an empty metal orbital; LUMO $2\pi^*$ (C 2p character) accepts from filled metal $d$ orbitals. Bond enthalpy CO 1070 kJ/mol vs N$_2$ 946.

Back-donation weakens the C–O bond (lower $\nu_{CO}$) and strengthens M–C; it is why CO is a soft base ([[CH06 Hard and Soft Acids and Bases]]) and binds through C.

Used in: [[CH02 Molecular Orbital Theory Diatomics]] §5.2. Related: [[Heteronuclear Diatomic MOs]] · [[Frontier Orbitals]].
