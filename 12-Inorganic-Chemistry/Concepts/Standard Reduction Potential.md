# Standard Reduction Potential

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** S&A §5.2 ([[Shriver and Atkins Inorganic Chemistry 5th Ed-174-188.pdf#page=3|pp.149–151]]).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The score of a couple in the competition for electrons, measured against the hydrogen electrode at zero.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\Delta_rG^\circ=-\nu FE^\circ$, $E^\circ_{\rm cell}=E^\circ(\text{red})-E^\circ(\text{ox})$; $E^\circ(\mathrm H^+/\mathrm H_2)=0$; $E^\circ(\mathrm{Zn^{2+}/Zn})=-0.76$ V ($\Delta G=+147$ kJ/mol).

$E^\circ>0\iff K>1$; ±2 V spans 68 decades of $K$.

Used in: [[CH09 Redox]] §2. Related: [[Faraday Constant]] · [[Galvanic Cell]].
