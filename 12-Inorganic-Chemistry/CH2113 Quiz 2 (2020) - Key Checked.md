| 16 | $K_a$ of HW, 0.0100 M, [H₃O⁺] = 2.50 × 10⁻³ | 6.25 × 10⁻⁴ | **[!verify] exact $K_a=\frac{(2.5\times10^{-3})^2}{0.0100-0.0025}=8.33\times10^{-4}$** (option b): 25 % dissociation is too large to neglect; the key's value assumes $[HW]\approx0.0100$ |
| 17 | distilled water in air | acidic; CO₂ + H₂O ⇌ H₂CO₃ | ✓ |

| "15" (2nd) | why ferric nitrate solution is not pH 7 | NO₃⁻ + H₂O ⇌ HNO₃ + OH⁻ | **[!verify] wrong: Fe(OH₂)₆³⁺ + H₂O ⇌ H₃O⁺ + Fe(OH₂)₅OH²⁺** — the small, highly charged Fe³⁺ makes its aqua ligands acidic (p$K_a$ ≈ 2–3); nitrate, the conjugate base of a strong acid, is negligibly basic (and would make the solution *basic*, not acidic).
