# Diffeomorphism

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-27-august-24-28|Aug 24–28 note]] (Prop 8.19: diffeomorphism and $F$-related fields); Thm 4.14 (global rank theorem).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A change of coordinates between manifolds: smooth, invertible, with smooth inverse.

> [!key] Definition
> A smooth bijection $F:M\to N$ with smooth inverse. Equivalent (inverse function theorem): a smooth bijection with invertible $dF_p$ for all $p$; a bijective constant-rank smooth map is a diffeomorphism (Global Rank Theorem, [[DM04 The Tangent Bundle and the Rank Theorem]]).

$x\mapsto x^3$ is a smooth bijection of $\mathbb R$ that is not a diffeomorphism. Lie group isomorphisms are diffeomorphisms ([[DM08 Lie Groups]]); left translations $L_g$ are diffeomorphisms.

Used in: [[DM02 Smooth Maps and Partitions of Unity]]. Related: [[Smooth Map]] · [[Local Diffeomorphism]] · [[Inverse Function Theorem]].
