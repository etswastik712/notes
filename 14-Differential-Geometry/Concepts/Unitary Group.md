# Unitary Group

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-09-11-hw6|HW6]] Q2 ($U(n)$ is an embedded $n^2$-dimensional Lie subgroup of $GL(n,\mathbb C)$).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Complex rotations: matrices preserving the Hermitian inner product; the symmetry group of quantum states.

> [!key] Definition
> $U(n)=\{A\in GL(n,\mathbb C):A^*A=I\}$, the stabiliser of $I$ under $A\cdot X=AXA^*$ (orbit map to Hermitian matrices, dimension $n^2$), an embedded Lie subgroup of dimension $n^2$; $T_IU(n)=\{B+B^*=0\}$. $SU(n)=\ker(\det:U(n)\to U(1))$, $\dim n^2-1$. $SU(2)\cong S^3$ and $SU(2)\to SO(3)$ is a 2-sheeted covering homomorphism.

Spin-½ and the Stern–Gerlach experiment: [[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin]]; fundamental group of $SO(3)$: [[PA15 Covering Spaces and the Fundamental Group]].

Used in: [[DM08 Lie Groups]]. Related: [[Orthogonal Group]] · [[General Linear Group]] · [[Lie Group]].
