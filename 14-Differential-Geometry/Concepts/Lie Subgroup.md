# Lie Subgroup

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slides DG12–DG14 via [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-09-03-august-31-september-4|Aug 31–Sep 4 note]].
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=177|Lee Thm 7.21, p. 159]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A subgroup that is itself a Lie group, sitting in $G$ as an immersed (possibly dense) or embedded submanifold.

> [!key] Definition
> A subgroup $H\le G$ with a topology and smooth structure making it a Lie group and an immersed submanifold. Kernels of homomorphisms and images of injective homomorphisms are Lie subgroups. A Lie subgroup is closed iff embedded; **Closed Subgroup Theorem:** closed subgroups are embedded Lie subgroups ([[DM15 Closed Subgroups and the Lie Correspondence]]).

The dense line on $T^2$ is a Lie subgroup that is not closed. $SL(n)$, $O(n)$, $U(n)$, $SO(n)$ are embedded. An open subgroup is closed.

Used in: [[DM08 Lie Groups]]. Related: [[Immersed Submanifold]] · [[Embedded Submanifold]] · [[Identity Component]].
