# Immersed Submanifold

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slide DG12 (Prop 5.18) via [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-09-03-august-31-september-4|Aug 31–Sep 4 note]].
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=127|Lee Prop. 5.18, p. 109]]; Props. 5.21–5.23, Thm 5.29.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A subset that is a manifold in a topology of its own, finer than the inherited one, so that the inclusion is still an immersion.

> [!key] Definition
> $S\subseteq M$ with a topology and smooth structure making $S\hookrightarrow M$ an immersion. The image of an injective immersion is one (Prop. 5.18). Immersed submanifolds are locally embedded; embedded iff the topology is the subspace topology.

Figure-eight, dense line on $T^2$. Maps into $S$ that are smooth into $M$ need not be continuous into $S$ (Thm 5.29 requires continuity). Lie subgroups are immersed submanifolds ([[DM08 Lie Groups]]).

Used in: [[DM06 Submanifolds]]. Related: [[Embedded Submanifold]] · [[Dense Line on the Torus]] · [[Smooth Embedding]].
