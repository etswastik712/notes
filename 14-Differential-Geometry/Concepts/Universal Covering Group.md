# Universal Covering Group

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=575|Lee Thm 21.32, p. 557]]; covering theory in [[PA15 Covering Spaces and the Fundamental Group]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Unroll the group until it is simply connected; the multiplication lifts and the Lie algebra does not change.

> [!key] Definition
> Every connected Lie group $G$ has a simply connected cover $\tilde G\to G$, a Lie group homomorphism and covering map with discrete central kernel $\Gamma\cong\pi_1(G)$, and $\mathrm{Lie}(\tilde G)=\mathrm{Lie}(G)$. The connected groups with algebra $\mathfrak g$ are exactly the quotients $\tilde G/\Gamma$ (Thm 21.32).

$\mathbb R\to S^1$ (kernel $\mathbb Z$), $\mathbb R^n\to T^n$, $SU(2)\to SO(3)$ (kernel $\{\pm I\}$, $\pi_1(SO(3))=\mathbb Z/2$). The universal cover of $SL(2,\mathbb R)$ has no faithful finite-dimensional representation: not every Lie group is a matrix group.

Used in: [[DM15 Closed Subgroups and the Lie Correspondence]], [[DM08 Lie Groups]]. Related: [[Smooth Covering Map]] · [[Lie Correspondence]] · [[Homomorphism Theorem for Lie Groups]] · [[Lie Group]].
