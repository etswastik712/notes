# Proper Map

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-09-04-hw5|HW5]] Q1(b).
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=629|Lee Thm A.57, p. 611]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Preimages of compact sets are compact: nothing can escape to infinity in the domain while staying bounded in the target.

> [!key] Definition
> $F:X\to Y$ is **proper** if $F^{-1}(K)$ is compact for each compact $K$. A proper continuous map into a locally compact Hausdorff space is **closed**; hence a proper injective immersion is an embedding. Every continuous map from a compact space is proper.

The dense line on the torus is not proper (the image is bounded but the domain is not compact). The closed map lemma is the compact case ([[Closed Map Lemma]]).

Used in: [[DM05 Immersions, Embeddings, Submersions and Coverings]]. Related: [[Smooth Embedding]] · [[Closed Map Lemma]] · [[Locally Compact Space]].
