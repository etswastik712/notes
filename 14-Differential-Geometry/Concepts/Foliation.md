# Foliation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slide DG29 via the [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-11-06-november-2-6|Nov 2–6 note (DG29–30)]].
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=519|Lee p. 501, Ex. 19.18]], [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=520|Prop 19.19, p. 502]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A partition of the manifold into immersed layers that look like parallel slices in suitable charts (but each layer may wind back through a chart infinitely often).

> [!key] Definition
> A **$k$-dimensional foliation** is a collection $\mathcal F$ of disjoint connected nonempty immersed $k$-submanifolds (**leaves**) with union $M$ such that around each point there is a chart **flat for $\mathcal F$**: each leaf meets it in a countable union of $k$-slices. The tangent spaces of the leaves form an involutive distribution (Prop 19.19); conversely an involutive $D$ induces a foliation (**global Frobenius**).

Examples: parallel affine subspaces; rays; spheres; circles $S^1\times\{q\}$ in the torus; slope-$\theta$ lines on $T^2$ (closed leaves if $\theta$ rational, **dense** if irrational; leaf space then indiscrete); components of the fibres of a submersion.

Used in: [[DM17 Foliations, Weakly Embedded Submanifolds and Lie Subgroups]]. Related: [[Global Frobenius Theorem]] · [[Frobenius Theorem]] · [[Dense Line on the Torus]] · [[Foliation by Fibres of a Submersion]] · [[Integral Manifold]].
