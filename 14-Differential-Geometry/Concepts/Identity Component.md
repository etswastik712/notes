# Identity Component

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slide DG13 (Prop 7.15) via [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-09-03-august-31-september-4|Aug 31–Sep 4 note]]; [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=175|Lee Prop. 7.15, p. 157]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The part of the group reachable from the identity by a continuous path; every other component is a translate of it.

> [!key] Definition
> $G_0$ is a **normal, open, connected subgroup**, the only connected open subgroup; the components of $G$ are the cosets $gG_0$. For $W$ a symmetric neighbourhood of $e$: $\langle W\rangle$ is open if $W$ is open, connected if $W$ is connected, $=G$ if $G$ is connected. Open subgroups are closed.

$GL(n,\mathbb R)_0=\{\det>0\}$ (49.9% of random $3\times3$ matrices), $O(n)_0=SO(n)$ (two components, $\det=\pm1$), $\mathbb R^\times_0=(0,\infty)$. $G/G_0$ is discrete.

Used in: [[DM08 Lie Groups]]. Related: [[Lie Group]] · [[Orthogonal Group]] · [[General Linear Group]].
