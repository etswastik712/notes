# Abelian Lie Algebra

A Lie algebra whose Lie bracket vanishes identically for all pairs of its elements.

$$[X, Y] = 0$$

## Links
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/abelian-group]] — An abelian Lie algebra has a trivial bracket [X, Y]=0, mirroring how the commutator trivializes in an abelian group
