# Integral Manifold

An immersed submanifold $S \subset M$ is called an integral manifold of a distribution $\mathcal{D}$ if the tangent space $T_p S$ equals $\mathcal{D}_p$ for every point $p \in S$.

$$T_p S = \mathcal{D}_p \quad \forall p \in S$$
