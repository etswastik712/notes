# Existence of Infinitely Many Smooth Structures

The result establishing that any non-empty smooth manifold of positive dimension admits infinitely many mutually non-diffeomorphic smooth structures on its underlying topological manifold. This is constructed by pulling back standard structures along families of homeomorphisms that fail to be diffeomorphisms.

$$F_n(x) = |x|^{n-1} x$$
