# Antipodal Map on S2

The smooth map on the 2-sphere S^2 sending every point (x, y, z) to its negative (-x, -y, -z), whose smoothness and derivative can be analyzed in local stereographic coordinates.

$$F(x, y, z) = (-x, -y, -z)$$
