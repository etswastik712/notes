# Completely Integrable Distribution

A distribution $\mathcal{D}$ is completely integrable if every point $p \in M$ admits an open coordinate chart that is flat for $\mathcal{D}$.

## Links
- generalises:: [[zettel/integrable-distribution]] — A completely integrable distribution is integrable because the coordinate slices of flat charts provide local integral manifolds
