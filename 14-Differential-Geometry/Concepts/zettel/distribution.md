# Distribution

A rank-k distribution on a smooth manifold assigns to each point a k-dimensional linear subspace of its tangent space, locally spanned by smooth vector fields.

$$\mathcal{D} \subseteq TM$$

$$\mathcal{D}_p \le T_p M, \quad \dim(\mathcal{D}_p) = k$$
