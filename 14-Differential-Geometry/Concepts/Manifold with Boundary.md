# Manifold with Boundary

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=44|Lee Thm 1.37, p. 26 (boundary: pp. 24–29)]]; Tu §22.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A manifold that is allowed an edge: near boundary points it looks like the closed half-space, like a closed disc.

> [!key] Definition
> Locally homeomorphic to open subsets of $\mathbb H^n=\{x^n\ge0\}$. Boundary points are those mapped to $\{x^n=0\}$ (invariantly, Thm 1.37). $\partial M$ is a properly embedded $(n-1)$-manifold (Lee Thm 5.11).

Closed balls, hemispheres, $[0,1]\times M$. Flows and flowouts on manifolds with boundary: [[DM12 Integral Curves, Flows and Flowouts]].

Used in: [[DM01 Smooth Manifolds]]. Related: [[Smooth Manifold]] · [[Topological Manifold]].
