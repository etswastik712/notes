# Smooth Atlas

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-07-hw1|HW1]] Q1 (maximal atlases, compatibility).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A map-book whose overlapping pages agree smoothly; the *maximal* atlas is the book containing every page that agrees with all of them.

> [!key] Definition
> A **smooth atlas** is a family of pairwise smoothly compatible charts covering $M$. It is **maximal** if it contains every chart compatible with all its charts. Every smooth atlas lies in a unique maximal one; two atlases define the same maximal atlas iff their union is a smooth atlas.

[[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=31|Lee Prop. 1.17, p. 13]]. A smooth structure is a maximal atlas ([[Smooth Structure]]).
