# Rank of a Smooth Map

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slide DG11–DG12 via [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-27-august-24-28|Aug 24–28 note]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> How many independent directions survive the map at a point: the rank of the Jacobian in any charts.

> [!key] Definition
> $\operatorname{rank}F|_p=\operatorname{rank}dF_p$. $F$ has **constant rank** $r$ if this equals $r$ at every point; **immersion** ($r=\dim M$), **submersion** ($r=\dim N$) are the extreme cases ([[Immersion and Submersion]]).

Rank can jump: $x^2$ (rank 0 at $0$), the height function $z$ on $S^2$ (rank 0 at the poles, $|\nabla z|=4|w|/(|w|^2+1)^2$ in the stereographic chart). Lie group homomorphisms and equivariant maps have constant rank ([[DM08 Lie Groups]], [[DM09 Group Actions and Semidirect Products]]).

Used in: [[DM04 The Tangent Bundle and the Rank Theorem]]. Related: [[Rank Theorem]] · [[Global Rank Theorem]] · [[Differential of a Smooth Map]].
