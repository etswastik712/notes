# Local Frame

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slide DG9 (local frame, Lie groups have global frames); [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-27-hw4|HW4]] Q4.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A smoothly varying choice of a basis of the tangent space at every point of a region.

> [!key] Definition
> A **local frame** on $U$ is $n$ vector fields $E_1,\dots,E_n$ forming a basis of $T_pM$ at each $p\in U$ (a coordinate frame $\partial/\partial x^i$ is one). A **global frame** exists iff $M$ is **parallelizable**, iff $TM\cong M\times\mathbb R^n$ (HW4 Q4).

Parallelizable: $\mathbb R^n$, $S^1$, $S^3$, $S^7$, every Lie group (left-invariant fields: [[DM11 The Lie Algebra of a Lie Group]]). Not: $S^2$ ([[Hairy Ball Theorem]]).

Used in: [[DM04 The Tangent Bundle and the Rank Theorem]]. Related: [[Tangent Bundle]] · [[Hairy Ball Theorem]].
