# Exotic Smooth Structure

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-07-hw1|HW1]] Q4: infinitely many distinct structures.
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** Milnor 1956, Kervaire–Milnor 1963, Donaldson/Freedman/Taubes (stated, not proved).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A smooth structure is extra data: the same topological space can have several that are not diffeomorphic.

> [!key] Definition
> Distinct structures are cheap (replace a chart $\varphi$ by $F_s\circ\varphi$ with $F_s(x)=|x|^{s-1}x$). The real question is **diffeomorphism classes**: unique in dimensions $\le3$; $S^7$ has 28 oriented classes (exotic spheres); $\mathbb R^n$ has one for $n\ne4$ but uncountably many for $n=4$; $S^4$ is open.

Why the course works with a fixed smooth structure and 'smooth' means smooth with respect to it ([[Smooth Structure]]).

Used in: [[DM01 Smooth Manifolds]]. Related: [[Smooth Structure]] · [[Smooth Manifold]].
