# One-Parameter Subgroup

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slide DG25 via [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-10-24-october-19-24|Oct 19–24 note]]; HW9 Q1–Q2.
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=534|Lee Thm 20.1, p. 516]], [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=536|Lee Prop. 20.3, p. 518]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A copy of the real line (or a circle, or a point) inside $G$: a path through $e$ along which the group law is just addition of times.

> [!key] Definition
> A Lie group homomorphism $\gamma:\mathbb R\to G$. **Thm 20.1:** these are exactly the maximal integral curves from $e$ of left-invariant fields; $\gamma_X'(0)=X_e$. The image is isomorphic to $\mathbb R$, $S^1$ or $\{e\}$ (HW9 Q1). For a subgroup $H$: the one-parameter subgroups of $G$ with velocity in $T_eH$ (Prop 20.3).

$GL(n)$: $e^{tA}$. $T^2$: $t\mapsto(at,bt)$ mod $\mathbb Z^2$: circle if $a/b\in\mathbb Q$, dense line if irrational, point if $(0,0)$. $\mathbb R^n$: $t\mapsto tv$. In physics: the unitary evolution $e^{-iHt/\hbar}$.
