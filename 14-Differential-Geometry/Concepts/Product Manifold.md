# Product Manifold

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-14-hw2|HW2]] Q2(a).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Take coordinates on each factor and use them together; dimensions add.

> [!key] Definition
> If $M^m,N^n$ are smooth manifolds, $M\times N$ (product topology) is a smooth $(m+n)$-manifold with charts $(U\times V,\varphi\times\psi)$; the projections are smooth. $T^n=(S^1)^n$, $S^1\times\mathbb R$, $S^2\times S^2$.

Tangent spaces of a product split: $T_{(p,q)}(M\times N)\cong T_pM\oplus T_qN$ ([[DM03 Tangent Vectors and the Differential]]). Used for $G\times G$ in the definition of a Lie group ([[DM08 Lie Groups]]).

Used in: [[DM01 Smooth Manifolds]]. Related: [[Smooth Manifold]] · [[Product Topology]].
