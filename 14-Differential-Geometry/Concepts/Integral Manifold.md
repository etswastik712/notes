# Integral Manifold

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slide DG29 via the [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-11-06-november-2-6|Nov 2–6 note (DG29–30)]].
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=509|Lee p. 491]], [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=518|Prop 19.16, p. 500]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A surface that is everywhere tangent to the plane field: the plane field is its tangent-plane field.

> [!key] Definition
> A nonempty **immersed** submanifold $N\subseteq M$ with $T_pN=D_p$ for **every** $p\in N$. $D$ is **integrable** if every point lies in an integral manifold. Integral manifolds of an involutive $D$ meet a flat chart in a **countable union of open subsets of slices** (Prop 19.16) and are **weakly embedded** (Thm 19.17).

Integral curves of a nowhere-vanishing field; the planes $z=$ const for $\mathrm{span}\{\partial_x,\partial_y\}$; the graphs $z=xy+c$ for Lee Ex. 19.14; the dense line on $T^2$ (an integral manifold that is not embedded).
