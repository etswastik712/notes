# Left-Invariant Distribution of a Subalgebra

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slide DG30 via the [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-11-06-november-2-6|Nov 2–6 note (DG29–30)]].
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=524|Lee Lemma 19.24, p. 506]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Spread a Lie subalgebra over the whole group by left translation: you get a plane field that is automatically involutive.

> [!key] Definition
> For a Lie subalgebra $\mathfrak h\subseteq\mathrm{Lie}(G)$, $D_g=\{X_g:X\in\mathfrak h\}=dL_g(\mathfrak h_e)$ is a **left-invariant involutive distribution**: a basis of $\mathfrak h$ is a global smooth frame, and $[X_i,X_j]\in\mathfrak h$ (Lemma 19.4).

On $\mathrm{Aff}(1)^+$: $\mathfrak h=\mathrm{span}\{a\partial_a\}$ has leaves $b=$ const, the leaf through $e$ being $\{(a,0)\}$. On $T^2$: $\mathfrak h=\mathrm{span}(1,\sqrt2)$ has the dense lines as leaves.
