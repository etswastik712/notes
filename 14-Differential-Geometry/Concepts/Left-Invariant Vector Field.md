# Left-Invariant Vector Field

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=209|Lee Thm 8.37, p. 191]], [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=210|Lee Cor. 8.38–8.39, p. 192]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Choose a tangent vector at the identity and transport it to every point by left translation: a global field that looks the same everywhere.

> [!key] Definition
> $X$ with $d(L_g)_{g'}X_{g'}=X_{gg'}$. **$\varepsilon:X\mapsto X_e$ is an isomorphism** from the left-invariant fields onto $T_eG$ (Thm 8.37); the field of $v$ is $\tilde v_g=d(L_g)_e v$, smooth because $(\tilde vf)(g)=\frac d{dt}f(g\gamma(t))$ is smooth. A basis gives a **global frame**: Lie groups are parallelizable.

$\mathrm{Aff}(1)$: $X_1=a\partial_b$, $X_2=a\partial_a$, $[X_1,X_2]=-X_1$. $GL(n)$: $\tilde A_X=XA$. $\mathbb R^n$: the constant fields. $S^2$ has no global frame (Hairy Ball), so it is not a Lie group. Fields generated by an action satisfy the brackets with the opposite sign.

Used in: [[DM11 The Lie Algebra of a Lie Group]], [[DM08 Lie Groups]]. Related: [[Lie Algebra of a Lie Group]] · [[Left Translation]] · [[Local Frame]] · [[Vector Field]].
