# Characteristic Property of Surjective Submersions

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=108|Lee Thms 4.29–4.31, p. 90]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A map out of the base of a surjective submersion is smooth exactly when its pull-back to the total space is: smoothness descends.

> [!key] Definition
> For a surjective submersion $\pi:M\to N$: $F:N\to P$ is smooth iff $F\circ\pi$ is smooth; a smooth $G:M\to P$ constant on fibres factors uniquely through a smooth $\widehat G:N\to P$.

Used to show maps induced on quotients ($G/H$, $\mathbb{RP}^n$) are smooth ([[DM09 Group Actions and Semidirect Products]]). Proof via local sections.

Used in: [[DM05 Immersions, Embeddings, Submersions and Coverings]]. Related: [[Local Section Theorem]] · [[Quotient Topology]].
