# Tangent Space

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** week 2 (mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-14-august-10-14); HW2; Test 1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> All the directions in which one can move from $p$, collected in a vector space the same size as the manifold.

> [!key] Definition
> $T_pM$ = the real vector space of derivations at $p$; $\dim T_pM=\dim M=n$ (a chart gives $d\varphi_p:T_pM\cong T_{\varphi(p)}\mathbb R^n$). $T_pU=T_pM$ for $U$ open; $T_aV\cong V$ for a vector space; $T_{(p,q)}(M\times N)\cong T_pM\oplus T_qN$.

For a sphere $T_pS^2\cong p^\perp\subset\mathbb R^3$. All tangent spaces assemble into the tangent bundle ([[DM04 The Tangent Bundle and the Rank Theorem]]). Tangent space of a Lie group at the identity is its Lie algebra ([[DM11 The Lie Algebra of a Lie Group]]).

Used in: [[DM03 Tangent Vectors and the Differential]]. Related: [[Tangent Vector]] · [[Differential of a Smooth Map]] · [[Tangent Space of a Product]].
