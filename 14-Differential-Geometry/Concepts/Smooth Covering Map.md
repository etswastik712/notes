# Smooth Covering Map

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=109|Lee Prop. 4.33, p. 91]], [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=110|Prop. 4.40, p. 92]], [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=112|Cor. 4.43, p. 94]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A covering in which the floors are not just homeomorphic but diffeomorphic to the base.

> [!key] Definition
> A smooth surjection $\pi:E\to M$ of connected manifolds with evenly covered neighbourhoods whose sheets map **diffeomorphically**. Equivalent: a topological covering that is a local diffeomorphism. Smooth coverings are local diffeomorphisms, submersions, open, quotient maps; every connected manifold has a universal covering manifold; lifts of smooth maps are smooth.

$\mathbb R\to S^1$, $z\mapsto z^k$ ($k$ sheets), $S^n\to\mathbb{RP}^n$, $\exp:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C^\times$, $SU(2)\to SO(3)$ (a covering homomorphism, [[DM08 Lie Groups]]). Topological version: [[Covering Space]].

Used in: [[DM05 Immersions, Embeddings, Submersions and Coverings]]. Related: [[Covering Space]] · [[Universal Cover]] · [[Immersion and Submersion]].
