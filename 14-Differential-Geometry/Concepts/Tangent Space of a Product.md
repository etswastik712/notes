# Tangent Space of a Product

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-14-hw2|HW2]] Q2(b); [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=77|Lee Prop. 3.14, p. 59]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A direction in $M\times N$ is a direction in $M$ together with a direction in $N$.

> [!key] Definition
> $\alpha:T_{(p,q)}(M\times N)\to T_pM\oplus T_qN$, $\alpha(v)=(d(\pi_1)v,d(\pi_2)v)$, is an isomorphism (coordinate vectors go to coordinate vectors in the product chart).

Used for the Lie group multiplication $m:G\times G\to G$: $dm_{(e,e)}(X,Y)=X+Y$ (HW4 Q6, [[DM08 Lie Groups]]).

Used in: [[DM03 Tangent Vectors and the Differential]]. Related: [[Product Manifold]] · [[Tangent Space]] · [[Differential of a Smooth Map]].
