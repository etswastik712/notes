# Topological Manifold

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-07-august-3-7|week 1 (DG1–DG3, slides unreadable)]]: manifolds defined.
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=21|Lee Ch. 1, p. 3]]; Tu §5.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A space that near every point looks like an open piece of $\mathbb R^n$, with enough separation (Hausdorff) and smallness (second-countable) to do analysis.

> [!key] Definition
> A **topological $n$-manifold** is a Hausdorff, second-countable space in which every point has a neighbourhood homeomorphic to an open subset of $\mathbb R^n$.

Consequences ([[PA12 Separation, Countability and Local Compactness]]): locally compact, paracompact, countably many components, countable $\pi_1$. Non-examples: [[Line with Two Origins]] (not Hausdorff), the long line (not second-countable). Dimension is well defined (invariance of dimension, Lee Thm 1.2).

Used in: [[DM01 Smooth Manifolds]]. Related: [[Coordinate Chart]] · [[Smooth Manifold]] · [[Hausdorff Space]] · [[Second-Countable Space]].
