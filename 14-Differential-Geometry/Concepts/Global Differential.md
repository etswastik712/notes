# Global Differential

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=86|Lee Prop. 3.21, Cor. 3.22, p. 68]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> All the pointwise differentials $dF_p$ assembled into one smooth map of tangent bundles.

> [!key] Definition
> $dF:TM\to TN$, $dF|_{T_pM}=dF_p$, is smooth: in natural charts $(x;v)\mapsto(\widehat F(x);D\widehat F(x)v)$. $d(G\circ F)=dG\circ dF$ and $d(\mathrm{id})=\mathrm{id}$: $T$ is a functor.

Sends a curve $\gamma$ to its velocity curve $\gamma'$; diffeomorphisms to diffeomorphisms of bundles. Used for $F$-relatedness of vector fields ([[DM10 Vector Fields and Lie Brackets]]).

Used in: [[DM04 The Tangent Bundle and the Rank Theorem]]. Related: [[Tangent Bundle]] · [[Differential of a Smooth Map]].
