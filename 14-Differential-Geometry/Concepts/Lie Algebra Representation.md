# Lie Algebra Representation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** slides DG19–DG20 via [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-10-10-october-5-9|Oct 5–9 note]].
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=217|Lee Thm 8.49, p. 199]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Realise the abstract algebra as honest matrices: a bracket-preserving linear map into $\mathfrak{gl}(V)$.

> [!key] Definition
> A Lie algebra homomorphism $\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak{gl}(V)$; a Lie group representation $\rho:G\to GL(V)$ gives $\rho_*:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak{gl}(V)$. **Ado's theorem** (stated): every finite-dimensional real Lie algebra has a faithful finite-dimensional representation, hence embeds in some $\mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb R)$.

HW7 Q1 realises the 1- and 2-dimensional algebras in $\mathfrak{gl}(2,\mathbb R)$ by hand. Not every Lie group has a faithful representation: the universal cover of $SL(2,\mathbb R)$ has none. Group-theory side: [[GT10 Symmetry Representations and Characters]].
