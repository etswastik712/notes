# Cloud Radiative Effect

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy CERES tutorial, Exercise 4 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Clouds reflect sunlight (cooling) and trap infrared (warming); the net is the difference between the all-sky and clear-sky flux.

> [!key] Definition
> CRE = (net flux, all skies) − (net flux, clear skies), separately SW, LW and total. CERES EBAF 2010–2024 global mean: **SW −45.0, LW +25.4, net −19.6 W m⁻²** (without clouds the Earth would gain +20.9 instead of +1.26). Stratocumulus (low, bright) cool strongly (−69 off Peru); deep convection nearly cancels (SW −59, LW +50, net −9 over the warm pool); over snow clouds warm (up to +39).

Cooling ≈ 15× the planetary imbalance, so small cloud changes matter.
