# Last Glacial Maximum

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 9, p. 24 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The last time the ice sheets were at their greatest extent, about 18,000 years ago.

> [!key] Definition
> Glaciers covered ≈32 % of the land (≈4.8×10⁷ km²), with ice sheets thousands of metres thick over most of Europe and North America.

Followed by the Holocene; the Pleistocene ended 11,700 years ago.

Used in: [[CL17 Orbital Forcing and Glacial Cycles]]. Related: [[Glacial-Interglacial Cycles]] · [[Glacier Mass Balance]].
