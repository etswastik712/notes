# Antarctic Bottom Water

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O5 handout §8 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The densest water in the ocean, made on the Antarctic shelves by cold and brine.

> [!key] Definition
> ≈ 0.4 °C, S ≈ 34.67 (σ ≈ 27.8); Weddell and Ross Seas; katabatic winds, polynyas, brine rejection; flows north on the floor (Atlantic 50°S–30°N) and into the Indian and Pacific basins. Colder and denser than NADW but **not saltier**.

About 10 Sv.
