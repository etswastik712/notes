# Faint Young Sun Paradox

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** entire note (standard result, not in the course)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A 30 % dimmer stove should freeze the house. Yet the geological record shows liquid water, so something else (stronger greenhouse) must have been turning up the thermostat.

> [!key] The puzzle
> Stellar evolution implies the young Sun was ~30 % dimmer ($S\approx0.7\times1361$ W m⁻² 4 Gyr ago). With today's albedo and greenhouse effect Earth would have been frozen, yet there is geological evidence of liquid water by ~3.8 Ga.

Estimate: $T_e\propto S^{1/4}$ gives $T_e=255\times0.7^{1/4}=233$ K and $T_s\approx233+33=266$ K (below freezing).

**Candidate resolutions:** a much stronger greenhouse effect (more CO₂ and/or CH₄; Sagan & Mullen 1972; Kasting), lower albedo (less cloud and land), a faster-rotating Earth, mass loss from the early Sun. The carbonate–silicate cycle provides a negative feedback that can keep CO₂ high when the Sun is faint.
