# Hasselmann Stochastic Climate Model

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy Week 2 deck frames 33–38; O3 handout (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Fast random weather, integrated by a slow slab, makes slow climate wandering with nothing slow driving it.

> [!key] Definition
> For a white push F′ on a slab: power ∝ 1/(ω²C² + λ²). Fast swings: power ∝ 1/C² (mixed layer ≈ 990× below land for the app's numbers); slow swings: λ sets it and C drops out (ratio 0.99 at 100–1000 yr); the bend is at period 2πτ. **Depth sets the timescale, not the size of the slow swings.** A deep ocean calms the slowest swings (≈ ×0.6).

Hasselmann (1976), cited by Joy, not read.
