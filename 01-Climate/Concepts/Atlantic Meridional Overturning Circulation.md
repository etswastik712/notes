# Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O6 deck frames 16–25 (2026); Stewart p. 229 read

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Warm water flows north in the upper Atlantic and returns cold at depth; a mooring array has measured it since 2004.

> [!key] Definition
> RAPID-MOCHA at 26.5°N (since April 2004): mean 17.0 Sv, 10-day values −4 to 32 Sv, yearly means 14.9–19.1 Sv (slide); Stewart's early 24°N array: 18.7 ± 5.6 Sv. IPCC AR6 SPM C.3.4: very likely to weaken this century, medium confidence of no abrupt collapse before 2100; a collapse would shift the tropical rain belt south and weaken the African and Asian monsoons.

Twenty years cannot show a trend in a system with a 1,000-year memory.
