# Sea-Surface Height

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O4 deck frames 25–31 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Ekman convergence piles water 1 m high in the subtropics; geostrophy sends the flow around the hill, not down it.

> [!key] Definition
> Subtropical gyres stand ≈ 0.9 m above the subpolar ocean (N Pacific 0.93, N Atlantic 0.94, S Pacific 1.05 m). Surface geostrophic speed $u=(g/f)\,\text{slope}$: 1 m over 1,500 km ≈ 6.5 cm s⁻¹; 1 m over 100 km ≈ 1 m s⁻¹ (Gulf Stream). Clockwise N, anticlockwise S; fast flow on the west side (Stommel 1948).

Floating debris collects in the middle of the hills (garbage patches).
