# Thermohaline Circulation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 24, p. 2 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The slow, deep circulation driven by density differences set at the surface by heat and fresh-water exchange.

> [!key] Definition
> <1 cm/s, 1000–4500 m, ≈90 % of the ocean, ≈1000-yr cycle, deep water in contact with the atmosphere every ≈600 yr. Roles: heat to the poles, equator–pole gradient, ice and albedo, long-term heat and CO₂ storage, sensitivity to high-latitude salinity.

Wind-driven circulation is the fast surface part.

Used in: [[CL21 Thermohaline Circulation Water Masses and Sea Ice]]. Related: [[Deep Water Formation]] · [[Water Mass and T-S Diagram]] · [[Antarctic Circumpolar Current]].
