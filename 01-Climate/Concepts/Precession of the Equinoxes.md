# Precession of the Equinoxes

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 9, p. 10 (2025)–18.
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** combined period and amplitudes.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The seasons slide around the orbit: northern summer is at aphelion now and was at perihelion half a cycle ago.

> [!key] Definition
> Axis precession 25,700 yr (torque on the equatorial bulge, 21 km); apsidal 112,000 yr; combined ≈20,900 yr. Effect on 65°N June insolation: 33 W m⁻² (6.9 %) at $e=0.0167$, 99 W m⁻² (20 %) at $e=0.05$, 0 at $e=0$.

Every half cycle the hemisphere with the larger seasonal contrast switches.

Used in: [[CL17 Orbital Forcing and Glacial Cycles]]. Related: [[Orbital Eccentricity]] · [[Milankovitch Cycles]] · [[Seasons and Earth's Orbit]].
