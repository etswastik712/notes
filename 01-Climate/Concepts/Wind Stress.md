# Wind Stress

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy Week 2 deck frames 51–53 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The shear force per unit area the wind exerts on the sea; the ocean's input to the momentum ledger.

> [!key] Definition
> τ ≈ 0.1 N m⁻² typical (bulk formula $\tau=\rho_aC_DU_{10}^2$). Trades push the water west, westerlies east, strongest in the Southern Ocean (≈ 50°S). A stress change across a stirred layer is what accelerates it: $F=\tfrac1\rho\partial_z\tau$; τ/(ρH) ≈ 2×10⁻⁶ m s⁻² on 50 m.

Ekman transport is $\tau/\rho f$ per metre of width.
