# Western Boundary Current

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 22, p. 12 (2025); Hartmann pp. 206–216.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A narrow, fast, warm river carrying heat poleward on the western edge of each ocean basin.

> [!key] Definition
> Gulf Stream, Kuroshio, Brazil, East Australian, Agulhas; speeds >1 m/s, ≈10¹⁵ W poleward, steady through the year, breaking into meanders and rings; makes western Europe ≈5 °C warmer than parts of Canada at the same latitude (with atmospheric stationary waves).

Latent heat loss over them exceeds 200 W m⁻².

Used in: [[CL19 Wind-Driven Ocean Circulation]]. Related: [[Subtropical Gyre]] · [[Eastern Boundary Current]] · [[Poleward Energy Transport]].
