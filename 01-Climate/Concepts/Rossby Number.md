# Rossby Number

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra book:** definition $Ro=U/fL$ and the scale argument (M&P §7.1, p.110) · 🔵 **extra:** scale chart across tornado → ocean gyre. (The Suhas pre-midsem table of scales is the 🟢 taught background.)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Whether you notice the Earth's rotation depends on whether you finish before it has turned you. A thrown baseball is over long before rotation matters; a weather system lasts days, and the Earth has time to bend its flow completely.

> [!key] Definition
> $Ro=\dfrac{U^2/L}{fU}=\dfrac{U}{fL}$ compares the acceleration of a parcel ($\sim U^2/L$) with the Coriolis term ($fU$). $Ro\ll1$: rotation dominates (geostrophic balance); $Ro\gtrsim1$: rotation is irrelevant or only one of several terms.

Numbers ($f=1.03\times10^{-4}$ s⁻¹ at 45°): synoptic cyclone $U=10$ m/s, $L=1000$ km → $Ro\approx0.1$; hurricane $U=50$, $L=100$ km → $\approx5$; thunderstorm → $\approx10$; tornado → $\sim5000$; ocean gyre $U=0.1$ m/s, $L=1000$ km → $\sim10^{-3}$. Near the equator $f\to0$ so $Ro$ grows and geostrophy fails.

Named after C.-G. Rossby (1898–1957). See [[Scales of Atmospheric Motion]], [[Geostrophic Balance]], [[CL13 Horizontal Motion - Geostrophic Balance and Friction]], [[Coriolis Parameter]].
