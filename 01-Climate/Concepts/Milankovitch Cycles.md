# Milankovitch Cycles

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 9, p. 6 (2025).
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** exact-insolation checks.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Slow changes in the shape, tilt and orientation of the orbit move sunlight between seasons and latitudes.

> [!key] Definition
> Eccentricity (0.005–0.0607, ~100 kyr), obliquity (22.1°–24.5°, 41 kyr), precession (axis 25.7 kyr, apsides 112 kyr; combined ≈21 kyr). 65°N, 21 June: 477.9 W m⁻² today; 427.7–567.4 W m⁻² for extreme combinations.

They change the annual global mean by ≈0.2 % at most; the effect is redistribution.
