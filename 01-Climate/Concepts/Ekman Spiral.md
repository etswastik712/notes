# Ekman Spiral

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 21, p. 9 (2025).
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** derivation and numbers.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Each layer drags the one below, Coriolis turns the dragged water, so the current weakens and rotates with depth.

> [!key] Definition
> Constant $A_z$: $u+iv=\frac{\sqrt2\tau}{\rho fD_E}e^{z/D_E}e^{i(z/D_E-\pi/4)}$, $D_E=\sqrt{2A_z/|f|}$; surface current 45° right (NH) of the wind, $1/e$ speed at $D_E$, reversed at $\pi D_E$ (4.3 %). $A_z=0.1$, $f=10^{-4}$: $D_E=44.7$ m.

Lecture's 'Ekman depth' conflates $D_E$ and $\pi D_E$ (flagged).

Used in: [[CL19 Wind-Driven Ocean Circulation]]. Related: [[Ekman Transport]] · [[Planetary Boundary Layer and Friction]] · [[Geostrophic Balance]].
