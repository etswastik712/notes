# Mixed Layer

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Hartmann 2016 §7.3 (Joy O3 core) and Neena Lecture 20, p. 18 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The wind-stirred, convectively stirred top of the ocean where temperature and salinity do not change with depth.

> [!key] Definition
> Depth set by buoyancy forcing (cooling, evaporation deepen; heating, rain shallow) against wind and wave stirring: global mean ≈70 m, a few metres in upwelling regions to the full depth in high-latitude sinking regions; thin in late summer, hundreds of metres by late winter; its heat capacity is the ocean's effective capacity on years–decades (≈30 × the atmosphere).

Below lies the seasonal and then the permanent thermocline.

Used in: [[CL18 The Ocean as the Climate System's Heat Reservoir]]. Related: [[Thermocline and Pycnocline]] · [[Ocean Heat Capacity and Seasonal Storage]] · [[Sea Surface Energy Budget]].
