# Brine Rejection

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 24, p. 15 (2025).
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** mass balance.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Freezing leaves the salt behind: the water below gets saltier and heavier.

> [!key] Definition
> $S=(35-fS_\text{ice})/(1-f)$: 10 % frozen with ice salinity 5 gives 38.3 psu (+3.3). Dense plumes sink to form deep and bottom water.

Freezing point ≈ −1.8 °C at 35 psu.
