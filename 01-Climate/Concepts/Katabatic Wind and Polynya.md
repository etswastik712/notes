# Katabatic Wind and Polynya

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O5 handout §7 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Cold dense air pours off the Antarctic ice dome, chills the sea and blows the new ice away, so more ice forms.

> [!key] Definition
> A katabatic wind is dense air flowing downhill off an ice sheet. It cools the water and drives sea ice offshore, leaving open water (**polynyas**) where ice forms fast and rejects brine (Hartmann p. 224, as Joy cites it): the densest water in the ocean forms against the coast.

Can be modelled in a tank with salty coloured water on a ramp.

Used in: [[CL25 The Ocean's Ledger IV - Salt, Density and Water Masses]]. Related: [[Brine Rejection]] · [[Sea Ice]] · [[Antarctic Bottom Water]].
