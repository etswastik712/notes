# Eddy Diffusivity

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy Week 2 deck frames 19–28 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Turbulent eddies mix heat 10⁴–10⁶ times faster than molecules, but stratification cuts it by orders of magnitude below the mixed layer.

> [!key] Definition
> Mixed layer: well mixed to its base. Thermocline: $K\sim10^{-5}$–$10^{-4}$ m² s⁻¹; reach $\sqrt{2K/\omega}$: 1 yr 10/32 m, 10 yr 32/100 m, 100 yr 100/317 m for K = 10⁻⁵/10⁻⁴. No water moves sideways: mixing up and down alone sets the ocean's active thermal inertia.

Tracer-release experiments (Ledwell et al. 1993, as Joy cites) give ≈ 10⁻⁵.
