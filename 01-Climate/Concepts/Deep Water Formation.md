# Deep Water Formation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 24, p. 17 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Cold, salty surface water sinks to the bottom in a few places.

> [!key] Definition
> North Atlantic Deep Water off Greenland; Antarctic Bottom Water on the Weddell, Ross and Adélie shelves; more than half the ocean's volume is cold water from these two sources; Southern Ocean upwelling returns it.

Winds sweep ice away on the Antarctic shelves, favouring more ice and brine.

Used in: [[CL21 Thermohaline Circulation Water Masses and Sea Ice]]. Related: [[Brine Rejection]] · [[Thermohaline Circulation]] · [[Antarctic Circumpolar Current]].
