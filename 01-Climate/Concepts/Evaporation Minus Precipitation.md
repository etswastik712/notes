# Evaporation Minus Precipitation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O5 deck frames 2–8; O5 handout §2–5 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Salt stays when water evaporates or freezes, so surface salinity records E − P.

> [!key] Definition
> Salinity ≈ 35 g kg⁻¹ (mean 34.7). A 50 m layer losing 1 m yr⁻¹ gets saltier by 0.7 g kg⁻¹ yr⁻¹ unless currents carry the salt away. Box means (2010–2020): N Atlantic subtropics +2.8 mm day⁻¹ (S 37.2), S Pacific +3.3 (36.2), E Pacific rain band −3.9 (33.8), W Pacific warm pool −3.6 (34.5), Arabian Sea +2.6 (36.2), Bay of Bengal −1.1 plus rivers (32.9). Atlantic exports 0.32 Sv of vapour (≈ Amazon) to the Pacific: 35.6 vs 34.6.

Subtropics win twice (clear, dry sinking air); ITCZ loses twice.
