# Light Attenuation in the Ocean

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Stewart 2008 §6.10 (Joy core) and Neena Lecture 20, p. 2 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Sunlight fades exponentially with depth, blue-green light lasts longest, infrared is absorbed in the top centimetre.

> [!key] Definition
> $I=I_0e^{-cz}$; 2 % reflected at normal incidence; type I water: 10 % of 465 nm light at 90 m ($c=0.0256$ m⁻¹, 1 % at 180 m); coastal water: a few metres; broadband flux halves within about 1 m; euphotic zone ≈200 m.

Heating is deposited over tens of metres while cooling is at the surface.

Used in: [[CL18 The Ocean as the Climate System's Heat Reservoir]]. Related: [[Mixed Layer]] · [[Radiative Extinction]] · [[Optical Depth]].
