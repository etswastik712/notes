# Daisyworld

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy tutorials 1 and 2 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A planet whose only life, black and white daisies, steers its temperature by changing its colour, without any plan.

> [!key] Definition
> $A=f(a_wA_w+a_bA_b+xA_g)+(1-f)A_o$; $C\,dT/dt=SL(1-A)-\sigma T^4$; patches $T_i=T+q'(A-A_i)$; $da_i/dt=a_i[x\beta(T_i)-\gamma]$. Ocean: **darker** (shifts life's window to dimmer sun), **slower** ($\tau=C/\lambda$), and smaller leverage ($0.5f$). Surviving is not regulating; fast physics plus slow biology gives an overshoot.

With grazers and an ocean's delay the loop becomes a boom–bust cycle (≈ 8 yr).
