# El Nino and La Nina

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 23, p. 8 (2025)–18.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Warm-eastern-Pacific (El Niño) and cool-eastern-Pacific (La Niña) phases of the tropical Pacific.

> [!key] Definition
> El Niño: weaker trades, warm water surges east, thermocline and sea level flatten, upwelling falls, convection and rain move east; La Niña: stronger trades, cooler east. Examples: 1983, 1997–98 (El Niño), 1995–96 (La Niña).

Global impacts by teleconnections.
