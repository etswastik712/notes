# Bulk Aerodynamic Formulas

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Stewart 2008 §5.4 (Joy optional).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Turbulent air–sea fluxes are estimated from the wind speed and the air–sea differences of temperature and humidity.

> [!key] Definition
> $\tau=\rho_aC_DU_{10}^2$, $Q_S=\rho_ac_pC_SU_{10}(t_s-t_a)$, $Q_L=\rho_aL_EC_LU_{10}(q_s-q_a)$; $C_D=(0.50+0.071U_{10})10^{-3}$, $C_S=1.0\times10^{-3}$, $C_L=1.2\times10^{-3}$, $\rho_a=1.3$, $c_p=1030$, $L_E=2.5\times10^6$. Trade wind (8 m/s, 27/25 °C, 80 %): $Q_S=21$, $Q_L=201$ W m⁻² (instantaneous).

$\tau=0.043$ N m⁻² for 6 m/s.
