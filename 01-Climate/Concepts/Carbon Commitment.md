# Carbon Commitment

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O7 notes §9–§10, 'Read this part'

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Stopping emissions does not undo them: the ocean's slowness keeps CO₂ high and warming going for centuries.

> [!key] Definition
> BEAM: stop in 2025 → CO₂ settles at **334 ppm**, not 280. Archer et al. (2009): ocean uptake over **2–20 centuries** (20–35 % stays), sea-floor CaCO₃ over **3000–7000 yr**, rock weathering over **10⁵ yr**. 'The memory that makes El Niño forecastable is the same memory that commits us to the warming.'
