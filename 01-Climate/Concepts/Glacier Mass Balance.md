# Glacier Mass Balance

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 10, p. 15 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> An ice sheet grows if the winter's snow survives the summer.

> [!key] Definition
> Accumulation in winter, ablation in summer; if they balance the mass is constant; cool summers (low summer insolation) leave snow that piles up; warm summers turn precipitation to rain and speed melt. An ice sheet exceeds 50,000 km².

Why high-latitude northern summer insolation, not the annual mean, is the Milankovitch criterion.
