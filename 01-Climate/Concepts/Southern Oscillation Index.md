# Southern Oscillation Index

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 23, p. 19 (2025)–20.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The sea-level pressure difference between Tahiti and Darwin, a gauge of the Walker circulation.

> [!key] Definition
> SOI = SLP(Tahiti) − SLP(Darwin) (normally positive); strongly positive = strong Walker cell (La Niña), negative in El Niño. Niño index = SST anomaly averaged over an eastern-equatorial-Pacific box.

Sir Gilbert Walker, 1920.

Used in: [[CL20 ENSO and the Walker Circulation]]. Related: [[El Nino and La Nina]] · [[Walker Circulation]] · [[ENSO]].
