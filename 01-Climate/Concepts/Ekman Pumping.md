# Ekman Pumping

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Stewart 2008 §9.4 (9.28–9.29).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Where the wind-driven transport converges, water is pushed down; where it diverges, water rises from below.

> [!key] Definition
> $\nabla_H\cdot\mathbf M_E=-\rho w_E(0)$, $w_E=-\mathrm{curl}_z(\boldsymbol\tau/\rho f)$ (top-of-layer convention). A stress curl of $-10^{-7}$ N m⁻³ at 30°N pumps water down at $1.3\times10^{-6}$ m s⁻¹ (≈42 m yr⁻¹).

Why subtropical gyre centres are downwelling 'ocean deserts'.

Used in: [[CL19 Wind-Driven Ocean Circulation]]. Related: [[Ekman Transport]] · [[Subtropical Gyre]] · [[Upwelling]].
