# Subtropical Gyre

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 22, p. 4 (2025)–8.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Ekman transport heaps water in the middle of an ocean basin; the hill drives a clockwise geostrophic flow.

> [!key] Definition
> Anticyclonic: westward equatorial current, poleward western boundary current, equatorward eastern boundary current; sea-level hump (≈1 m) with geostrophic speed $g\Delta\eta/fL$ (5.4 cm/s for 1 m over 2500 km at 30°N); shape set by basin shape, wind variation and $f(\varphi)$; central downwelling gives low productivity.

Subpolar gyres are cyclonic.

Used in: [[CL19 Wind-Driven Ocean Circulation]]. Related: [[Western Boundary Current]] · [[Eastern Boundary Current]] · [[Geostrophic Balance]] · [[Ekman Pumping]].
