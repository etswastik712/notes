# Potential Density

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O5 deck frames 10–13; O5 handout §6 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Density of a water sample brought adiabatically to the surface, quoted as ρ − 1000 so waters from different depths can be compared.

> [!key] Definition
> σ = ρ − 1000 kg m⁻³ (surface ≈ 27). Colder or saltier is denser. At 25 °C, S = 35: ρα = 0.30 kg m⁻³ per K, ρβ = 0.74 per g/kg (cooling 3.3 K ≡ 1.4 g/kg); at 0 °C: 0.054 and 0.80 (18 K ≡ 1.2 g/kg): warm water is ruled by temperature, cold water by salt. Seawater with S > 24.7 has no 4 °C maximum; freezing point −1.92 °C at S = 35.

Hartmann (p. 199) and Stewart ch. 6; values TEOS-10 (Joy), reproduced with EOS-80 in the vault.
