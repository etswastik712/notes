# Sea Surface Energy Budget

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Hartmann 2016 §4.2, §4.9; Stewart 2008 §5.6 (Joy core) and Neena Lecture 20, p. 4 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Absorbed sunlight and back radiation are balanced by infrared emission, evaporation, sensible heat and ocean heat transport divergence.

> [!key] Definition
> $\partial E_s/\partial t=R_s-LE-SH-\Delta F_o$. Global mean (Stewart Fig. 5.6): $168+324=390+78+24=492$ W m⁻². Latent loss is the largest cooling and peaks over western boundary currents (>200 W m⁻²); the ocean gains heat at the equator and eastern margins and loses it at high latitudes.

A ±15 W m⁻² error equals 1.1 K per year in a 100 m column.
