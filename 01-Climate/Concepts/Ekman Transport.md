# Ekman Transport

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 21, p. 11 (2025).
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Stewart 2008 §9.3 (Joy, not covered in class).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The depth-integrated wind-driven flow goes sideways to the wind: right in the northern hemisphere.

> [!key] Definition
> $\mathbf M_E=\hat{\mathbf z}\times\boldsymbol\tau/f$ ($fM_{Ey}=-\tau_x$, $fM_{Ex}=\tau_y$), independent of the viscosity. $1\ \text{Sv}=10^6$ m³ s⁻¹. $\tau=0.1$ at 30°: 1.34 Sv across 1000 km. Atlantic 11°N: 8.8–13.5 Sv.

Basis of equatorial and coastal upwelling and of gyre formation.

Used in: [[CL19 Wind-Driven Ocean Circulation]]. Related: [[Ekman Spiral]] · [[Ekman Pumping]] · [[Upwelling]].
