# Ocean Carbon Sink and Airborne Fraction

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O7 notes §7; carbon-boxes handout §1

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Of every 100 tonnes of carbon we emitted, about 41 are still in the air; the ocean took 28 and the land 25.

> [!key] Definition
> Global Carbon Budget 2025, 1750–2024: 752 GtC emitted (505 fossil + cement, 248 land use); air 312 GtC (**41 %**), ocean 210 (**28 %**), land 190 (**25 %**), ~5 % other. Ocean uptake today ~3.2 GtC/yr. 1 ppm CO₂ = 2.124 GtC.
