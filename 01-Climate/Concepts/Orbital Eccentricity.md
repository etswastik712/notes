# Orbital Eccentricity

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 9, p. 9 (2025).
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** numbers checked.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The orbit is slightly elliptical; the flux at perihelion exceeds that at aphelion by $((1+e)/(1-e))^2-1$.

> [!key] Definition
> $e$ varies 0.005–0.0607 (today 0.0167). Flux ratio: 6.9 % today, 22.2 % at 0.05, 27.5 % at 0.0607 (the slide's 23 % is $e=0.052$). Annual mean ∝ $(1-e^2)^{-1/2}$: +0.18 % over the full range.

Matters through precession: no precession signal when $e=0$.

Used in: [[CL17 Orbital Forcing and Glacial Cycles]]. Related: [[Milankovitch Cycles]] · [[Precession of the Equinoxes]] · [[Seasons and Earth's Orbit]].
