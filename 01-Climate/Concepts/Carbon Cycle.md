# Carbon Cycle

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 6, p. 10 (2025)–16.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Carbon circulates quickly between air, plants and sea, and leaks slowly into rocks and back through volcanoes.

> [!key] Definition
> Inorganic: atmosphere–ocean exchange, carbonate and silicate weathering → carbonate in sea → shells → sediments → subduction → volcanism → CO₂. Organic: photosynthesis, respiration, storage, burial, weathering (needs O₂). Short-term: decades; long-term: organic carbon spends ≈200 Myr in rock. Volcanism balanced by weathering over geological time (thermostat); fossil fuels short-circuit it.

No liquid water on Venus means no weathering thermostat.
