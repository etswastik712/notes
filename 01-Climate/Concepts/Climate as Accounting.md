# Climate as Accounting

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O1 handout §1, §7 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A planet's climate is a set of account books (energy, momentum, mass, salt, carbon); winds and currents are how they are kept balanced.

> [!key] Definition
> Four ledgers, one a week: energy, momentum, mass and salt, carbon. Each with three hats: **accountant** (which budget, what goes in and out), **data scientist** (what does it look like), **physicist/biologist** (why). The budgets are coupled: salt ↔ water ↔ latent heat ↔ momentum.

Joy's thesis: Argha's energy balance and Suhas's circulation are one subject.
