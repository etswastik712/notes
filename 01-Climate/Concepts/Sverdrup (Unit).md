# Sverdrup (Unit)

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O3 handout §7 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> One million cubic metres of water per second: the only sensible unit for ocean currents.

> [!key] Definition
> 1 Sv = 10⁶ m³ s⁻¹ ≈ 10⁹ kg s⁻¹. Gulf Stream off Florida ≈ 30 Sv (100 km × 600 m × 0.5 m s⁻¹); Atlantic deep-water formation 15–20 Sv; Antarctic ≈ 10 Sv; Drake Passage 125 ± 11 Sv (Stewart p. 229); AMOC at 26.5°N ≈ 17 Sv.

Named after Harald Sverdrup.
