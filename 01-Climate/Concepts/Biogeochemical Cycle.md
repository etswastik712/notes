# Biogeochemical Cycle

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 6, p. 8 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Each life element (C, H, O, N, P, S) follows a closed path through rocks, soil, life, sea and air.

> [!key] Definition
> The complete pathway of an element through geosphere, biosphere, atmosphere and hydrosphere: **bio** (life), **geo** (rocks, soils), **chemical** (transformations). Driven by atmospheric and ocean circulation and plate tectonics; strongly coupled to the water cycle.

Fast loops (photosynthesis–respiration) feed slow ones (burial, weathering, subduction).

Used in: [[CL16 Atmospheric Evolution Greenhouse Gases and the Carbon Cycle]]. Related: [[Carbon Cycle]] · [[Earth System]].
