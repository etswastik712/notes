# Water Mass and T-S Diagram

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 24, p. 4 (2025)–8.
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** teaching equation of state.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A body of water with a common birth, recognised by its temperature–salinity signature.

> [!key] Definition
> Surface 0–200 m, intermediate to ≈1500 m, deep to 4000 m, bottom >4000 m. $T$, $S$ quasi-conservative below the mixed layer; ¹⁴C/¹²C gives age; surface density 1020–1030 kg m⁻³. Teaching EOS $\rho=1027[1-2\times10^{-4}(T-10)+7.6\times10^{-4}(S-35)]$: Weddell brine (−1.8 °C, 34.8) 1029.3.

Real equation of state is nonlinear.
