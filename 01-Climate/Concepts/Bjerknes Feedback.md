# Bjerknes Feedback

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 23, p. 5 (2025), 16.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Stronger trades make a bigger SST contrast, which makes stronger trades.

> [!key] Definition
> Easterlies → easterly currents and upwelling → warm west/cool east → convection and low pressure in the west → latent heating → stronger easterlies (positive feedback). In El Niño it runs backwards.

Gives ENSO its instability.

Used in: [[CL20 ENSO and the Walker Circulation]]. Related: [[Walker Circulation]] · [[El Nino and La Nina]] · [[Ice-Albedo Feedback]].
