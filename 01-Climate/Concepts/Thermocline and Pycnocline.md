# Thermocline and Pycnocline

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 20, p. 18 (2025); Hartmann §7.3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The layer where temperature (thermocline), salinity (halocline) or density (pycnocline) changes rapidly with depth, locking the deep ocean away from the surface.

> [!key] Definition
> Below the mixed layer (100–200 m) density rises rapidly to 1–2 km: the pycnocline. Tropics: temperature-controlled, strong thermocline; high latitudes: no permanent thermocline, salinity controls density; the mid-latitude seasonal thermocline forms in summer and is erased by winter convection.

Deep temperatures stay 0–3 °C with little variation.
