# North Atlantic Deep Water

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O5 handout §8; O6 deck (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Salty water cooled in the Nordic and Labrador Seas until it sinks, then spreads south at 2–3 km.

> [!key] Definition
> ≈ 4 °C, S ≈ 35.0 (σ ≈ 27.8); a salinity maximum at 1,750–3,000 m in the 30°W section; formation by **cooling** of salty Gulf Stream water (not brine), 15–20 Sv. Becomes Circumpolar Deep Water in the Southern Ocean, then rises where isopycnals reach the surface near 60°S.

No counterpart in the North Pacific: its surface is 2.3 g/kg fresher.
