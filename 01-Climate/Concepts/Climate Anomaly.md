# Climate Anomaly

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O7 notes §1–§2

> [!intuition] In one picture
> What is unusual: the value minus its normal value for the same place and time of year.

> [!key] Definition
> Anomaly = value − climatological mean (e.g. 1991–2020, same week). Nov 2026: +3.1 °C in Niño-3.4, +5.4 °C off Peru. A **running mean** averages over a sliding window to show the trend under the swings.
