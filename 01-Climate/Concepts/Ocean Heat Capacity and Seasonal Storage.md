# Ocean Heat Capacity and Seasonal Storage

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Hartmann 2016 §4.3; Stewart 2008 §5.1.
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** damped slab model.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The atmosphere equals 2.5 m of water; the top 70 m of the ocean equal 30 atmospheres.

> [!key] Definition
> $C_a=c_pp_s/g=1.04\times10^7$ J m⁻² K⁻¹ (2.46 m of water); 70 m of ocean: 28.5 ×; whole ocean ≈1500 ×; seasonal storage ocean/land ≈83 (Stewart: 100). Damped slab: amplitude $Q_0/\sqrt{(C\omega)^2+\lambda^2}$, lag $\arctan(C\omega/\lambda)$ (50 m: 2.16 K, 64°).

Why oceans moderate the seasons.
