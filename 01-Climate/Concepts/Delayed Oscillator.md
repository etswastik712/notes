# Delayed Oscillator

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O7 notes §6; Stewart §14.2 pp. 244–245 (read)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A fast loop that feeds itself plus a slow loop that undoes it, arriving late, makes a swing of its own.

> [!key] Definition
> Weaker trades launch an eastward equatorial **Kelvin wave** (deepens the eastern warm layer: warming, Bjerknes growth) and westward off-equatorial **Rossby waves** (thin the western layer). The Rossby waves **reflect** off the western boundary and return east with the opposite sign, thinning the eastern layer about **six months** later: a **delayed negative feedback**. Period 2–7 years, mean ~4 (not a clock). Wyrtki: a cycle discharges the tropical Pacific's heat, which the trades must slowly recharge.

Positive feedback + instantaneous negative feedback gives a switch (Stommel); positive + **delayed** negative gives an oscillation (ENSO).
