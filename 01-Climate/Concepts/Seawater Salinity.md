# Seawater Salinity

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 20, p. 10 (2025)–11; Hartmann §1.7.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Grams of salt per kilogram of water: set by evaporation minus precipitation, rivers and ice.

> [!key] Definition
> 33–38 g kg⁻¹ in the open ocean (35: 19.4 chloride, 10.8 sodium); high under subtropical highs ($E>P$), low at the ITCZ and subpolar zones, lowest near big rivers and ice melt; highest in the Red Sea and Mediterranean; measured by conductivity.

Controls density where the water is cold.

Used in: [[CL18 The Ocean as the Climate System's Heat Reservoir]], [[CL21 Thermohaline Circulation Water Masses and Sea Ice]]. Related: [[Thermocline and Pycnocline]] · [[Brine Rejection]].
