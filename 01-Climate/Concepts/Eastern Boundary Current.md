# Eastern Boundary Current

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 22, p. 12 (2025); Hartmann pp. 214–216.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A broad, shallow, cold current flowing towards the equator along the eastern edge of a basin.

> [!key] Definition
> California, Canary, Benguela, Peru/Humboldt; cold SST (below the zonal mean), upwelling, stratocumulus and fog; best developed in northern summer and year-round in the south.

Cool the climate of the coasts they pass.

Used in: [[CL19 Wind-Driven Ocean Circulation]]. Related: [[Upwelling]] · [[Western Boundary Current]] · [[Subtropical Gyre]].
