# Sea Ice

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 24, p. 12 (2025)–16.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Ocean water freezes like fresh water but colder, and stratification sets where.

> [!key] Definition
> Pure water densest at 4 °C; sea water's density maximum lies below its freezing point (≈ −1.8 °C); ≥15 % of the ocean ice-covered at some time; polar stratification by salinity limits convection, so surface freezing starts once a shallow layer is cooled.

Ice insulates the sea from the air.

Used in: [[CL21 Thermohaline Circulation Water Masses and Sea Ice]]. Related: [[Brine Rejection]] · [[Ice-Albedo Feedback]] · [[Deep Water Formation]].
