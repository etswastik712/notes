# Upwelling

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 21, p. 12 (2025)–13; Stewart §9.4.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Where the surface layer is driven away, deeper cold, nutrient-rich water rises to replace it.

> [!key] Definition
> Equatorial (easterlies, divergence), coastal (alongshore wind, offshore Ekman transport), wind-stress curl. Cold SST, fog and little rain onshore, high productivity (Peru, California, Somalia, Morocco, Namibia).

Towards-shore transport gives downwelling.

Used in: [[CL19 Wind-Driven Ocean Circulation]], [[CL20 ENSO and the Walker Circulation]]. Related: [[Ekman Transport]] · [[Eastern Boundary Current]] · [[Walker Circulation]].
