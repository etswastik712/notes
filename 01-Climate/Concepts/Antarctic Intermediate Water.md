# Antarctic Intermediate Water

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O5 handout §8 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Cold fresh surface water of the Southern Ocean, pushed down at the polar front and sent north at 1 km.

> [!key] Definition
> ≈ 3.3 °C, S ≈ 34.15 (σ ≈ 27.2); forms near 50–55°S (rain > evaporation, melting ice); pushed down by Ekman convergence equatorward of the westerlies; salinity minimum 34.2–34.5 at 700–900 m from 40°S to the equator.

Stewart writes AIW.
