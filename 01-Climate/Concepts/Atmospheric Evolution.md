# Atmospheric Evolution

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 4, p. 12 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Earth's air was lost and rebuilt: volcanoes supplied it, the oceans and rocks took out the CO₂, and life added oxygen.

> [!key] Definition
> Primary atmosphere (H, He, lost) → secondary atmosphere from outgassing (today's volcanic gas 80–90 % H₂O, 6–12 % CO₂, 1–2 % SO₂; early gases more reduced, CH₄ the main greenhouse gas) → oceans condense, CO₂ locked into sediments by weathering → cyanobacteria (≈3.8 Ga) produce O₂ → ozone, colonisation of land.

Venus kept CO₂ in the air (96 %, 90 × thicker), Earth in rocks; one-layer model: Venus 276 K vs 735 K observed.
