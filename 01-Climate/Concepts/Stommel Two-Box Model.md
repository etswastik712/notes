# Stommel Two-Box Model

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy O6 deck frames 19–23 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A warm salty box and a cold fresher box: the flow carries salt north, which keeps the north heavy, which keeps the flow going.

> [!key] Definition
> Positive salt feedback; in a nondimensional form $\dot x=1-x(1+|q|)$, $\dot y=F-|q|y$, $q=x-y$: thermal branch $F=q(1-q-q^2)/(1+q)$ with maximum 0.141 at q = 0.30; a reversed state exists for every F > 0; collapse at F_max, recovery only at F < 0. Two stable states for the same forcing, **hysteresis**.

Stommel (1961); Stewart pp. 215–218 (not read). A caricature: shows possibility, not that the ocean has it.
