# Walker Circulation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 23, p. 4 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Air rises over the warm western Pacific and sinks over the cool east, closed by surface easterlies and upper westerlies.

> [!key] Definition
> Zonal overturning circulation of the equatorial Pacific (Bjerknes 1960) driven by the east–west SST/pressure gradient; reinforces the surface easterlies; weakens in El Niño and strengthens in La Niña. Strength measured by the Southern Oscillation Index.

A 0.5 m sea-level slope is held by ≈0.05 N m⁻² of wind stress (my check).

Used in: [[CL20 ENSO and the Walker Circulation]]. Related: [[Bjerknes Feedback]] · [[El Nino and La Nina]] · [[Trade Winds and Westerlies]].
