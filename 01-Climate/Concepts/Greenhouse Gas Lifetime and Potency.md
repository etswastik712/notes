# Greenhouse Gas Lifetime and Potency

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 6, p. 6 (2025)–15: shares, lifetimes, potencies.
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** decay numbers.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A gas's climate impact is its per-molecule absorption (potency) times how long it stays (lifetime) times how much is emitted.

> [!key] Definition
> Water vapour ≈10 d, 60–70 % of the natural effect (a feedback); CO₂ 0.04 %, ≥ a decade (10–100 yr), 20 % natural, 75 % of anthropogenic warming; CH₄ 12 yr, 25–30×, 17 %; N₂O 114 yr, 273×, 6 %. A pulse decays as $e^{-t/\tau}$ (CH₄: 0.050 left after 36 yr).

Vapour pressure rises 6.3 % per kelvin (Clausius–Clapeyron), the strength of the water-vapour feedback.
