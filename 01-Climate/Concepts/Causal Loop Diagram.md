# Causal Loop Diagram

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy Daisyworld part 2 handout §1.2 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Arrows with + or − show how variables push each other; the count of minus signs round a loop tells you if it pushes back.

> [!key] Definition
> An arrow A → B with + means they move together, − opposite. A loop with an odd number of − signs is **balancing** (resists change), even or none is **reinforcing**. ‖ marks a delay. A balancing loop with a long delay overshoots and cycles (the shower with a long pipe).

Daisyworld: B1 white-daisy thermostat, B2 black-daisy thermostat, B3 and B4 food; the path land T → grazers → white daisies → land T has two minus signs: reinforcing.
