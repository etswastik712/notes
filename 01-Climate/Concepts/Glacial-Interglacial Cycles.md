# Glacial-Interglacial Cycles

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 9, p. 19 (2025), Neena Lecture 10, p. 24 (2025).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Ice sheets grew and shrank over about 100,000 years while the planet cooled and warmed by a few kelvin.

> [!key] Definition
> Ice-core records: global mean changes of 4–5 K (up to ~10 K polar) with ≈100-kyr cycles; Pleistocene 2.6 Myr–11.7 kyr ago; ice volume lags the insolation minimum by thousands of years; the present interglacial is expected to be long (≥1.5–2.5 precession cycles) and CO₂ delays the next glaciation.

Evidence: ice-core δ¹⁸O/δD and bubbles, marine cores, ice-rafted debris, glacial landforms.
