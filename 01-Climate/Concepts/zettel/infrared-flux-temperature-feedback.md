# Infrared Flux-Temperature Feedback

Earth's foundational negative climate feedback wherein an increase in surface temperature increases outgoing longwave infrared radiation escaping to space, cooling the surface back toward equilibrium.

## Links
- needs:: [[zettel/stefan-boltzmann-law]] — The increase in outgoing infrared radiation with temperature is governed by the fourth-power temperature dependence in the Stefan-Boltzmann law
- used-in:: [[zettel/planetary-emission-temperature]] — This stabilizing feedback restores planetary radiative equilibrium to yield a steady effective emission temperature
