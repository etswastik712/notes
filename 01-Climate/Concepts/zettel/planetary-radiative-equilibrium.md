# Planetary Radiative Equilibrium

A global zero-dimensional climate energy balance model where absorbed solar shortwave radiation, accounting for planetary albedo and spherical geometry, balances outgoing emitted longwave radiation.

$$SW = \frac{S(1-a)}{4}$$

$$\frac{S(1-a)}{4} = \sigma T^4$$
