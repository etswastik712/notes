# Rayleigh and Mie Scattering

The two dominant atmospheric scattering regimes governed by particle size relative to incident wavelength: Rayleigh scattering occurs for r << lambda with strong 1/lambda^4 wavelength dependence causing blue sky scattering, whereas Mie scattering occurs for r ≈ lambda with weak spectral dependence producing white or gray clouds.
