# Atmospheric Gas Lifetime

The characteristic time required to remove a constituent from the atmosphere, classified as long-lived when the lifetime exceeds inter-hemispheric mixing timescales (1–2 years) leading to global homogeneity, or short-lived when local sinks and reactions cause high spatial and temporal variability.
