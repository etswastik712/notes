# Area-Weighted Mean Temperature

A method to calculate the global mean temperature from heterogeneously distributed observation stations by gridding the globe and weighting each grid box temperature by its surface area. This accounts for planetary surface curvature where polar grid boxes subtend smaller areas than equatorial ones.

$$\overline{T} = \frac{\sum_i T_i A_i}{\sum_i A_i}$$
