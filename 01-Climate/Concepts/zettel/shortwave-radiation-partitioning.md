# Shortwave Radiation Partitioning

The atmospheric gatekeeping of incoming solar irradiance (lambda < 4 micrometers), comprising complete stratospheric photodissociation absorption of UVC (200–290 nm), partial ozone absorption of UVB (290–320 nm), visible transparency with Rayleigh scattering (400–700 nm), and selective tropospheric H2O and CO2 absorption dips in the near-infrared (0.7–3.0 micrometers).
