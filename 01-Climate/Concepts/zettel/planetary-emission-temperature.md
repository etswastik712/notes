# Planetary Emission Temperature

The uniform blackbody temperature at which a planet radiates energy to space across its entire surface area to balance absorbed incident solar power. It is inferred by equating absorbed shortwave radiation to integrated Stefan-Boltzmann longwave emission.

$$T_e = \left(\frac{S_0 (1 - \alpha_p)}{4\sigma}\right)^{1/4}$$

$$4\pi a^2 \sigma T_e^4 = (1 - \alpha_p) S_0 \pi a^2$$
