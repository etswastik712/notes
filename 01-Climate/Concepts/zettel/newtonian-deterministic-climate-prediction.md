# Newtonian Deterministic Climate Prediction

A mechanistic framework attempting to calculate future system states from current initial conditions and known forces via Taylor series integration of conservation equations. In Earth-system science, its practical utility is limited by sparse observations, measurement noise, and nonlinear emergent dynamics.

$$\bar{x}(t) = \bar{x}(t_0) + \left. \frac{\partial \bar{x}}{\partial t} \right|_{t_0} t + \left. \frac{\partial^2 \bar{x}}{\partial t^2} \right|_{t_0} \frac{t^2}{2} + \dots$$

$$m \frac{\partial^2 \bar{x}}{\partial t^2} = \bar{F}(x, t)$$

$$\bar{x}(t) = \bar{x}(t_0) + v(t_0)t + \frac{1}{m} \int_{t_0}^t \bar{F}(x, t') dt'$$
