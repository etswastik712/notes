# State-Space Reduction for Pendulum Simulation

The reformulation of the second-order pendulum differential equation into two coupled first-order ordinary differential equations governing angle and angular velocity, compatible with standard first-order numerical integrators.

$$\frac{d\theta}{dt} = \omega$$

$$\frac{d\omega}{dt} = -\left(\frac{g}{l}\right) \sin(\theta)$$
