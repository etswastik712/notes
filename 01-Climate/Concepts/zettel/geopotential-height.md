# Geopotential Height

A gravity-adjusted vertical coordinate defined as the geopotential divided by standard sea-level gravitational acceleration. It closely approximates geometric height in the lower atmosphere while accounting for gravitational variation with altitude.

$$Z = \frac{\Phi(z)}{g_0} = \frac{1}{g_0} \int_0^z g dz$$
