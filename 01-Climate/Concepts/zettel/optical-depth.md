# Optical Depth

A dimensionless measure of atmospheric opacity along a vertical path, defined such that an optical depth of unity corresponds to an intensity attenuation down to 1/e (~37%) of its incident value.

$$\tau_\lambda = \int \rho r k_\lambda dz$$
