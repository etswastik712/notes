# Atmospheric Angular Momentum Balance

The global balance requiring a net poleward transport of westerly angular momentum from the tropics to midlatitudes. The atmosphere gains angular momentum in tropical regions where surface winds are easterly and loses it to the ground in midlatitudes where surface winds are westerly.
