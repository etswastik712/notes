# Antarctic Circumpolar Current

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 24, p. 19 (2025).
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Hartmann pp. 227–231 (Joy optional).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The only current that circles the globe, driven by the southern westerlies.

> [!key] Definition
> West to east at <20 cm/s, 2000–4000 m deep, up to 2000 km wide, ≈40–60°S; separates the cold Southern Ocean from the Atlantic, Pacific, Indian; the Southern Ocean is where deep water returns to the surface, with vigorous ≈100 km eddies.

Hartmann: Southern Ocean warming lags the globe.

Used in: [[CL21 Thermohaline Circulation Water Masses and Sea Ice]], [[CL19 Wind-Driven Ocean Circulation]]. Related: [[Thermohaline Circulation]] · [[Deep Water Formation]] · [[Western Boundary Current]].
