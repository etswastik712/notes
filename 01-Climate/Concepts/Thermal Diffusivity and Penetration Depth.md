# Thermal Diffusivity and Penetration Depth

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Joy Week 2 deck frames 12–17; O2 handout §5 (2026)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Heat soaks into soil only as deep as √(diffusivity × period): about 10 cm in a day, 2 m in a year.

> [!key] Definition
> $\partial_tT=\kappa\partial_z^2T$, $\kappa=k/\rho c\approx(0.3\text{–}1)\times10^{-6}$ m² s⁻¹. A periodic surface swing gives $T'=Ae^{-z/d}\cos(\omega t-z/d)$, $d=\sqrt{2\kappa/\omega}$: day 9/12/17 cm, year 1.7/2.2/3.2 m for κ = 0.3/0.5/1×10⁻⁶; as a slab $C=\rho c\,d/\sqrt2$ ≈ 3–6 cm and 0.6–1.1 m of water. Still water κ = 1.4×10⁻⁷: 6 cm and 1.2 m.

Hartmann's (4.10) omits the factor 2, so his depth is ours ÷ √2. Reach grows as √period.
