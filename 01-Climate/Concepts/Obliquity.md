# Obliquity

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Neena Lecture 9, p. 13 (2025).
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** latitude dependence.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> More tilt means a bigger summer–winter contrast, mostly near the poles.

> [!key] Definition
> 22.1°–24.5° with period 41 kyr (today 23.44°, decreasing). From 22.1° to 24.5°: annual mean at the pole +16.7, at 65°N +7.1, at the equator −3.5 W m⁻²; 65°N June solstice +39 W m⁻² (8.2 %).

Both hemispheres are affected in the same way.

Used in: [[CL17 Orbital Forcing and Glacial Cycles]]. Related: [[Milankovitch Cycles]] · [[Seasons and Earth's Orbit]] · [[Insolation]].
