# Problem Set I: from the quantum puzzle to the wave function (2026-08-11)

## Covered
Constants given: $h=6.626\times10^{-34}$ J s, $\hbar=1.055\times10^{-34}$ J s, $c=3.00\times10^8$ m/s, $m_e=9.11\times10^{-31}$ kg, $e=1.602\times10^{-19}$ C, $hc\simeq1240$ eV nm. Relations: $E_\gamma=hc/\lambda$, $p_\gamma=h/\lambda$, $\lambda_{dB}=h/p$.

| # | Topic | What it practises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Probabilities do not add; amplitudes do | $P_A=P_B=0.20$; classical sum 0.40; $\psi_B=\sqrt{0.20}e^{i\varphi}$, $P=0.40+0.40\cos\varphi$ (0.80 at $\varphi=0$, 0 at $\varphi=\pi$); interference redistributes particles, it does not change totals |
| 2 | Photoelectric effect | $W=2.0$ eV, 400 nm: photon energy, $K_{\max}$, stopping potential, effect of doubling intensity, threshold wavelength, why classical theory has no threshold |
| 3 | Compton scattering | $\lambda=50$ pm, $\theta=90^\circ$: $\lambda'$ and electron energy in keV; fractional shift for 500 nm; why invisible in optics but unavoidable for X-rays; why Thomson predicts the same frequency |
| 4 | Why a baseball looks classical | $\lambda_{dB}$ of 0.145 kg at 40 m/s; comparison with atomic and nuclear scales; speed for $\lambda=10^{-10}$ m and travel time against age of the universe; the ratio $\lambda_{dB}/d$ |
| 5 | Davisson-Germer | 54 V: $\lambda(\text{Å})\simeq12.27/\sqrt V$, grating $d=0.215$ nm, $d\sin\theta=\lambda$, comparison with 50°, new angle at 100 V |
| 6 | Plane wave is not a particle | $|\psi|^2=|A|^2$, non-normalisability, wavelength is not size, definite $p$ means undefined position |
| 7 | Normalisation and probability density | $\psi=A\sin(\pi x/L)$ in a box: $A$, peak and zeros, probability in $0<x<L/4$ vs classical 0.25, meaning of a single measurement |
| 8 | Conceptual | Roles of $\psi$ and $|\psi|^2$; one localised result per measurement, wave-like distribution from many |
