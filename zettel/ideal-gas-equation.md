# Ideal Gas Equation

An equation of state relating the pressure, volume, mass, and absolute temperature of a gas, obeyed approximately by all gases and assumed exact for atmospheric gases individually or as mixtures.

$$pV = mRT$$

$$p = \rho R T$$

$$p \alpha = RT$$
