# Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation

A major ocean circulation system in the Atlantic driven by density differences that acts as a global conveyor belt transporting heat from southern and tropical latitudes northward. Freshwater influx reduces surface water density, suppressing convective sinking and weakening the circulation.
