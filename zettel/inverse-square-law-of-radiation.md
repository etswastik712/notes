# Inverse-Square Law of Radiation

A geometric conservation law stating that radiative flux intensity decreases inversely with the square of radial distance from a spherical radiating source due to conservation of energy across expanding spherical shells.

$$S = S_0 \left(\frac{r_0}{r}\right)^2$$

$$S = \sigma T_{Sun}^4 \frac{R_{Sun}^2}{R_{S \to E}^2}$$
