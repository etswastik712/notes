# Zero-Dimensional Energy Balance Model

A zero-dimensional planetary climate model that represents a planet as a single spatial point in steady radiative equilibrium, equating globally averaged absorbed solar shortwave radiation to emitted terrestrial longwave radiation.

$$\frac{S}{4}(1 - \alpha) = \sigma T^4$$

$$S \frac{\pi R^2}{4\pi R^2} = \frac{S}{4}$$
