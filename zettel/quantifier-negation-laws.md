# Quantifier Negation Laws

Rules for negating quantified statements and implications by interchanging universal and existential quantifiers and replacing implications with conjunctions.

$$\neg (\forall x, p(x)) \sim \exists x, \neg p(x)$$

$$\neg (\exists y, q(y)) \sim \forall y, \neg q(y)$$

$$\neg (P \implies Q) \sim P \land \neg Q$$
