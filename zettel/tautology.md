# Tautology

A compound statement that is true under every possible valuation of truth values assigned to its constituent propositions.

$$P \lor \neg P$$

## Links
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/contradiction]] — A tautology is invariably true across all truth assignments, whereas a contradiction is invariably false
