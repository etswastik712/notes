# Ring-Opening Polymerization of Hexachlorocyclotriphosphazene

The uncatalyzed thermal conversion of cyclic hexachlorocyclotriphosphazene at 210–250 °C into linear poly(dichlorophosphazene), providing a reactive precursor backbone that readily undergoes macromolecular nucleophilic substitution.
