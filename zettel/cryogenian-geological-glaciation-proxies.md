# Cryogenian Geological Glaciation Proxies

The tripartite geological evidence establishing global Neoproterozoic glaciation (~720–635 Ma): low-latitude glacial deposits (tillites, striations, dropstones within 15° of the equator), cap carbonates overlying glacial strata, and a sharp carbon isotope (δ13C) crash.
