# Island Model of Phosphazenes

A bonding scheme for cyclic phosphazenes wherein phosphorus dxz and dyz orbitals hybridize to form directed orbitals pointing toward adjacent nitrogen atoms, forming three-center pi bonds that limit delocalization to discrete three-atom islands interrupted by nodes at each phosphorus center.
