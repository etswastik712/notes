# Operator Commutator

The algebraic difference between the sequential compositions of two operators in reverse orders, measuring the degree to which order of operation affects the resulting state.

$$[\hat{A}, \hat{B}] = \hat{A}\hat{B} - \hat{B}\hat{A}$$
