# Multi-Scale Temperature Variability

The empirical characterization of atmospheric temperature fluctuations decomposed across nested temporal scales, ranging from diurnal hourly cycles and seasonal day-of-year variations up to multi-decadal trends and paleoclimatic glacial cycles.
