# Potential Energy Zero Gauge Invariance

Shifting the scalar potential energy by an additive constant V0 modifies the solution to the time-dependent Schrödinger equation exclusively by an overall spatial-independent phase factor exp(-i V0 t / hbar), leaving probability densities and expectation values of coordinate observables invariant.

$$V'(x) = V(x) + V_0$$

$$\psi'(x, t) = e^{-iV_0 t/\hbar} \psi(x, t)$$
