# Born Rule and Expansion Postulate

When expanding an arbitrary normalized quantum state in the orthonormal eigenbasis of an observable, the probability of obtaining eigenvalue a upon measurement is given by the squared magnitude of the corresponding expansion coefficient.

$$\Psi = \sum_a c_a \phi_a, \quad \sum_a |c_a|^2 = 1$$

$$P(A = a) = |c_a|^2$$
