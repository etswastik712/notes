# Davisson-Germer Electron Diffraction

The experimental validation of the de Broglie hypothesis through the diffraction of non-relativistic accelerated electrons from a crystalline nickel surface acting as a diffraction grating. Constructive interference maxima occur when the path difference satisfies the grating condition for the de Broglie wavelength corresponding to the accelerating potential.

$$\lambda(\text{\AA}) \simeq \frac{12.27}{\sqrt{V(\text{volt})}}$$

$$d \sin \theta = \lambda$$
