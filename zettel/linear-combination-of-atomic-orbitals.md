# Linear Combination of Atomic Orbitals

An approximation method that constructs delocalized molecular orbitals as linear weighted sums of atomic orbitals contributed by the constituent atoms. The squared expansion coefficients reflect atomic orbital probabilities and their cross-terms represent orbital interference.

$$\psi = C_A \chi_A + C_B \chi_B$$

$$\psi^2 = C_A^2 \chi_A^2 + 2 C_A C_B \chi_A \chi_B + C_B^2 \chi_B^2$$
