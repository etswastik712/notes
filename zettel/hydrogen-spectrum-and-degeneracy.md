# Hydrogen Spectrum and Degeneracy

The discrete negative bound-state energy eigenvalues of the hydrogen atom, which depend purely on the principal quantum number n and exhibit an n^2 spatial degeneracy neglecting electron spin.

$$E_{n,l} = -\frac{m e^4}{2(4\pi\epsilon_0)^2 \hbar^2} \frac{1}{n^2}$$
