# Silicate Weathering Feedback

A long-term geochemical mechanism wherein atmospheric carbon dioxide dissolved in rainwater reacts with silicate rocks, accelerated by biotic acids, sequestering carbon from the atmosphere-ocean reservoir into solid mineral carbonates.
