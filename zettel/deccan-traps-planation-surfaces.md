# Deccan Traps Planation Surfaces

A set of three distinct geomorphic erosion surfaces developed atop the Deccan Plateau hosting mature, meandering river networks. These comprise the Pune-Indore (Upper Plateau) surface at 550±50 m, the Ahmednagar surface at 700±50 m, and the highland surface at 1000–1200 m (Bhimashankar/Mahabaleshwar).
