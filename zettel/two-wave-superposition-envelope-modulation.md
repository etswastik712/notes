# Two-Wave Superposition Envelope Modulation

The linear superposition of two equal-amplitude plane waves with wavevectors k₁ and k₂ forms an oscillatory spatial envelope that forces total wave cancellation at regular periodic intervals. Increasing the wavenumber separation Δk shortens the distance between nodal points, demonstrating that broader spectral mixtures localize destructive interference.

$$\psi(x) = e^{i k_1 x} + e^{i k_2 x} = 2 \cos[(k_1 - k_2)x / 2] \, e^{i (k_1 + k_2)x / 2}$$

$$x_n = (2n + 1)\pi / \Delta k$$
