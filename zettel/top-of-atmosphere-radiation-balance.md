# Top-of-Atmosphere Radiation Balance

The purely radiative energy balance at the top of the atmosphere, defined as absorbed solar radiation minus outgoing longwave radiation. It varies geographically and seasonally, exhibiting positive values in the tropics and negative values near the poles.

$$R_{TOA} = Q_{abs} - OLR$$

$$Q_{abs} = S_{TOA} (1 - \alpha)$$
