# Free Particle Periodic Boundary Condition

A one-dimensional free particle subject to the topological identification x = x + L, which restricts allowed wave numbers to integer multiples of 2pi/L and quantizes the kinetic energy.

$$\psi(x + L) = \psi(x)$$

$$k_n = \frac{2\pi n}{L}$$

$$E_n = \frac{2\pi^2 \hbar^2}{m L^2} n^2$$
