# Homonuclear Diatomic Molecular Orbital Scheme

A molecular orbital framework for symmetric diatomic molecules constructed from four valence atomic orbitals per atom, producing four sigma orbitals (spanning strongly bonding to strongly antibonding) and two degenerate pairs of pi orbitals.
