# Multiple Climate Equilibria in Energy Balance Models

The dynamical emergence of three distinct radiative equilibrium states (a stable snowball planet, an unstable intermediate bifurcation threshold, and a stable warm/ice-free state) resulting from the intersection of non-linear shortwave absorption with quartic longwave emission.
