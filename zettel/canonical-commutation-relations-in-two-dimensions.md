# Canonical Commutation Relations in Two Dimensions

The generalization of Heisenberg commutation brackets to two Cartesian degrees of freedom, asserting that coordinate and conjugate momentum along the same axis satisfy canonical commutation while operators along orthogonal axes commute.

$$[x, p_x] = i\hbar$$

$$[y, p_y] = i\hbar$$

$$[x, p_y] = 0$$

$$[y, p_x] = 0$$
