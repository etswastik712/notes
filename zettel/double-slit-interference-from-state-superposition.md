# Double-Slit Interference from State Superposition

The emergence of an interference cross-term in the spatial probability density resulting from the coherent superposition of two wave amplitudes with a well-defined relative phase. When paths are incoherent or probabilities are added directly, the phase-dependent fringe modulation vanishes.

$$|\Psi|^2 = 2A^2 [1 + \cos(\phi_2 - \phi_1)] = 4A^2 \cos^2\left(\frac{\phi_2 - \phi_1}{2}\right)$$

$$\phi_2 - \phi_1 \simeq \frac{2\pi d}{\lambda} \frac{y}{D}$$

$$|\Psi|^2 = 2A^2 [1 + \cos(\phi_2 - \phi_1 + \alpha)]$$
