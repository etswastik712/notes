# Orbital Approximation

An approximation representing an $N_e$-electron molecular wavefunction as a Hartree product of independent one-electron wavefunctions called molecular orbitals. The probability distribution of each electron is given by the square of its respective one-electron orbital.

$$\psi = \psi(1) \psi(2) \dots \psi(N_e)$$
