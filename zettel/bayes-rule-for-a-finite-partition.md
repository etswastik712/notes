# Bayes' Rule for a Finite Partition

Given a partition of the sample space and the conditional probabilities of an event F given each part, the posterior probability of any part given F is its weighted likelihood divided by the total over all parts.

$$P[E_i \mid F] = \frac{P[F \mid E_i]\,P[E_i]}{\sum_{j=1}^n P[F \mid E_j]\,P[E_j]}$$
