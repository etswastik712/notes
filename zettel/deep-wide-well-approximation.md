# Deep Wide Well Approximation

The limiting case of a finite well where the potential depth and width parameter is very large. The lowest energy eigenvalues approach the energies of an infinite square well of width 2a, while remaining slightly lower.

$$k_n a \approx \frac{n\pi}{2}$$

$$E_n = \frac{n^2 \pi^2 \hbar^2}{8 m a^2}$$
