# Shortwave and Longwave Radiative Boundary

The operational demarcation of planetary radiant energy into shortwave radiation (lambda < 4 micrometers) carrying incoming solar energy and longwave radiation (lambda > 4 micrometers) carrying outgoing terrestrial thermal emission, separated in accordance with Wien's displacement law.

$$\lambda_{max} \approx \frac{2897}{T}$$
