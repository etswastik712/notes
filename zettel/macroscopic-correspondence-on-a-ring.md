# Macroscopic Correspondence on a Ring

The demonstration of the Bohr correspondence principle on a circular ring: as the ring circumference L grows from molecular to macroscopic scales, energy spacing shrinks as 1/L^2, falling orders of magnitude below thermal energy and measurement resolution to recover a continuous classical spectrum.

$$p_n = \frac{2\pi\hbar n}{L}$$

$$E_n = \frac{2\pi^2 \hbar^2 n^2}{m L^2}$$

$$E_1(1\text{ cm}) = E_1(0.83\text{ nm}) \times \left(\frac{0.83\times 10^{-9}}{10^{-2}}\right)^2 \approx 1.5 \times 10^{-14}\text{ eV}$$
