# Compound Hummocky Pāhoehoe

A basalt flow morphology emplaced at low volumetric effusion rates (<10 m^3/s) dominated by endogenous inflation. It is distinguished by knobby micro-relief, lava lobes, toes, inflation clefts, and inflated tumuli.
