# De Morgan's Laws

A pair of logical equivalences establishing that the negation of a disjunction is the conjunction of the negations, and the negation of a conjunction is the disjunction of the negations.

$$\neg(P \lor Q) \sim (\neg P) \land (\neg Q)$$

$$\neg(P \land Q) \sim (\neg P) \lor (\neg Q)$$
