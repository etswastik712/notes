# Arrhenius Acid-Base Concept

A foundational classification defining an acid as a substance that increases the aqueous concentration of hydronium ions (or decreases hydroxide), and a base as one that increases the aqueous concentration of hydroxide ions (or decreases hydronium). The model is limited to aqueous media and fails to explain the behaviour of non-hydrogen acids, non-hydroxide bases, or hydrolysis in certain salt solutions.
