# Field Trip Emergency Response Chain

A tiered escalation procedure specifying the sequential communication pathway to be activated during an on-site crisis or serious injury. It dictates immediate reporting to on-site authority and local civic emergency dispatch before notifying institutional security, medical clinics, and academic coordinators.
