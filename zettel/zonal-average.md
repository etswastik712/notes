# Zonal Average

The mean of an atmospheric field or property evaluated along a full circle of constant latitude over all longitudes from 0 to 2 pi.

$$\overline{X}(\phi, z) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_0^{2\pi} X(\lambda, \phi, z) \, d\lambda$$
