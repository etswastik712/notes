# Bedrock River Erosion Mechanisms

The suite of streampower-dependent physical and chemical processes responsible for bedrock channel degradation. These include mechanical wear by sediment (abrasion), block plucking (quarrying), fluid fracture expansion (hydraulic wedging), and chemical dissolution.
