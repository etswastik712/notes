# Basalt Bedrock Potholes

Enormous cylindrical erosional cavities exceeding 10 meters in diameter carved directly into basaltic river channels by high-energy vortex flow and suspended sediment abrasion. Their presence alongside knickpoint cascades signals active fluvial incision following tectonic rejuvenation.
