# Circular Infinite Well Energy Eigenvalues and Eigenstates

The stationary wavefunctions and quantized energy levels of a particle in an infinite circular well, expressed in terms of Bessel functions of the first kind evaluated at their zeros.

$$R(r) = A J_l(kr) + B Y_l(kr)$$

$$E = \frac{\hbar^2 k_{n,l}^2}{2m}$$

$$\psi(r, \theta) = R(r)\Theta(\theta) = J_l(k_{n,l} r) e^{i l \theta}$$
