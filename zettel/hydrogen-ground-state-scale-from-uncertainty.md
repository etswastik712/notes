# Hydrogen Ground-State Scale from Uncertainty

An order-of-magnitude estimate of the hydrogen atom's ground-state radius and binding energy derived by balancing the localization kinetic energy against the attractive Coulomb potential.

$$E(r) \sim \frac{\hbar^2}{2m_e r^2} - \frac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0 r}$$

$$r_{\min} = \frac{4\pi\epsilon_0 \hbar^2}{m_e e^2} \equiv a_0 = 5.292 \times 10^{-11}\text{ m}$$

$$E_{\min} \sim -2.180 \times 10^{-18}\text{ J} = -13.61\text{ eV}$$
