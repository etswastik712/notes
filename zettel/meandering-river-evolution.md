# Meandering River Evolution

The morphodynamic development of sinuous, single-thread channels driven by coupled bank erosion on outer bends and deposition on inner point bars. This feedback drives planform meander expansion and exhibits geometric self-similarity.

## Links
- needs:: [[zettel/streamflow]] — Channel bend cross-circulation and shear stress patterns driving meander migration require fluid streamflow
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/braided-river]] — Meandering channels evolve via single-thread bend growth and point bar deposition whereas braided channels divide across interlaced mid-channel bars
