# Formal Charge

The apparent electrical charge assigned to an atom in a Lewis formula under the extreme assumption of perfect covalency, where shared bonding pairs are divided equally between bonded atoms and lone pairs belong entirely to their host atom. It acts as an energetic criterion to identify the dominant resonance contributor.

$$f = V - L - \frac{1}{2}P$$

## Links
- used-in:: [[zettel/resonance]] — Minimizing formal charges determines which resonance form contributes predominantly to the resonance hybrid
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/oxidation-number]] — Formal charge exaggerates covalent character by sharing electron pairs equally, whereas oxidation number exaggerates ionic character by assigning electrons entirely to the more electronegative partner
