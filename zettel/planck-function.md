# Planck Function

A fundamental radiation law defining the spectral radiance emitted by an ideal blackbody at thermal equilibrium as a function of wavelength and absolute temperature.

$$I(\lambda, T) = \frac{2hc^2}{\lambda^5 \left(e^{\frac{hc}{\lambda k_B T}} - 1\right)}$$

## Links
- generalises:: [[zettel/wien-s-displacement-law]] — Differentiating the Planck spectral radiance expression with respect to wavelength and finding the extremum yields Wien's displacement relation
- generalises:: [[zettel/stefan-boltzmann-law]] — Integrating the Planck function across all wavelengths over a hemisphere yields the Stefan-Boltzmann fourth-power radiant flux formula
