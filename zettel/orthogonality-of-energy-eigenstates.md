# Orthogonality of Energy Eigenstates

Energy eigenfunctions of a Hamiltonian associated with distinct energy eigenvalues are mutually orthogonal over coordinate space.

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \psi_m^*(x) \psi_n(x) \, dx = 0 \quad (E_n \neq E_m)$$
