# Energy Eigenstate Superposition with Momentum Degeneracy

A quantum state formed by a linear combination of counter-propagating plane waves of equal wavenumber magnitude. It constitutes an exact eigenstate of the free Hamiltonian while possessing non-zero uncertainty and indeterminate outcomes for momentum measurements.

$$\Psi = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} (\psi_+ + \psi_-)$$

$$\hat{p}\psi_\pm = \pm \hbar k \psi_\pm$$

$$E = \frac{p^2}{2m} = \frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m}$$
