# Blackbody

An idealized physical body that completely absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation regardless of frequency or angle of incidence and reradiates thermal energy with maximum theoretical efficiency.

## Links
- used-in:: [[zettel/planck-function]] — The Planck function mathematically prescribes the spectral distribution of energy radiated by an ideal blackbody at absolute temperature T
- used-in:: [[zettel/stefan-boltzmann-law]] — The Stefan-Boltzmann law computes the spectrally integrated total radiant flux emitted per unit surface area of a blackbody
