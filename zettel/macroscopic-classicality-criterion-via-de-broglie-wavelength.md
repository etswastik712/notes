# Macroscopic Classicality Criterion via de Broglie Wavelength

The emergence of classical behaviour in macroscopic objects is governed by the dimensionless ratio of the de Broglie wavelength to the characteristic length scale of the apparatus (\lambda_{\text{dB}}/d), rather than the physical size of the object itself. For macroscopic bodies, this ratio is 19 to 24 orders of magnitude below any atomic or nuclear scale, rendering diffraction physically unobservable.

$$\lambda_{\text{dB}} = \frac{h}{mv}$$

$$v = \frac{h}{m\lambda}$$
