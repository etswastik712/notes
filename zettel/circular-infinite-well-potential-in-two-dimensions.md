# Circular Infinite Well Potential in Two Dimensions

A two-dimensional quantum system confined within a circularly symmetric hard-wall boundary of radius a, inside which the potential vanishes and outside of which it is infinite.

$$V(x, y) = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } x^2 + y^2 < a^2 \\ \infty & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
