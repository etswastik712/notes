# Monsoon Circulation

A large-scale seasonally reversing circulation characterized by low-level flow toward heated continental regions in summer and reversed upper-tropospheric flow toward the winter hemisphere. Precipitation is concentrated around the summer solstice near a continental monsoon trough, modulated by active periods and breaks.
