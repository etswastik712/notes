# Vector Field on S2 with a Single Zero

An explicit construction of a smooth vector field on the 2-sphere S^2 with exactly one singular point, obtained by pushing forward a constant non-vanishing vector field on R^2 via stereographic projection and extending smoothly by zero at the omitted pole.

$$Y := (\phi^{-1})_* (X)$$

$$Y_{(0,0,1)} = 0$$
