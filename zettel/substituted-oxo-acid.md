# Substituted Oxo Acid

A derivative of an oxo acid formed by replacing one or more hydroxyl groups with alternative substituents such as halogens or amino groups. Electron-withdrawing substituents increase central atom positive charge and enhance acidity, whereas pi-donating substituents decrease positive charge and weaken acidity.
