# Semidirect Product of Lie Groups

Given Lie groups N and H and a smooth action \theta of H on N by Lie group automorphisms, the semidirect product is the product manifold N \times H endowed with a group operation twisted by \theta.

$$(n_1, h_1) \cdot (n_2, h_2) = (n_1 \theta(h_1)(n_2), h_1 h_2)$$
