# Snowball Earth Deglaciation via Volcanic Outgassing

The deglaciation mechanism of a globally frozen planet whereby ice shutdown of continental silicate weathering permits continuous volcanic CO2 to accumulate over ~10 million years to ~0.1 bar (~300 times modern), overcoming ice albedo and triggering an abrupt transition to a 50–60°C hothouse.
