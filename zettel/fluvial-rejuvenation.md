# Fluvial Rejuvenation

The renewal of incisional river energy triggered by base-level fall, faulting, or tectonic uplift of plateau blocks. It generates morphologic anomalies including deeply entrenched meanders, hanging tributary valleys, rectilinear fracture-controlled networks, and bedrock cascades.

## Links
- example-of:: [[zettel/basalt-bedrock-potholes]] — Bedrock potholes along linear channels like the Kukdi River represent geomorphic diagnostic markers of stream rejuvenation
- example-of:: [[zettel/entrenched-meander-natural-arch]] — Entrenched meanders that isolate and breach lobes to form natural arches are driven by incisive stream rejuvenation
- used-in:: [[zettel/deccan-traps-planation-surfaces]] — Uplift of geomorphic planation surfaces like the Kanhoor Plateau relative to adjacent river valleys initiates drainage incision
