# Lava Lobe

A frozen discrete volume of basaltic lava exhibiting three fundamental interior layers—crust, core, and base—governed by crystallization relative to a medial cooling isotherm. In two-dimensional field exposures, lobes represent the observable cross-sectional components that build up a complete lava flow.
