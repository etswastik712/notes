# Integrable Distribution

A distribution $\mathcal{D}$ on a manifold $M$ is integrable if through every point of $M$ there passes an integral manifold of $\mathcal{D}$.

- needs:: [[zettel/integral-manifold]] — Integrability requires the existence of an integral manifold containing each point $p \in M$
