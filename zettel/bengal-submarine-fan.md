# Bengal Submarine Fan

The world's largest underwater sediment accumulation fan, fed by Himalayan denudation via the Ganga and Brahmaputra river systems. It spans approximately 3000 km in length, 1430 km in maximum width, 3 million km² in area, and holds 12.5 million km³ of sediment.
