# Associated Legendre Polynomials

The regular solutions to the polar angle differential equation originating from separation of the L^2 operator, bounded when the separation constant equals ħ^2 l(l + 1).

$$-\hbar^2 \left[ \frac{1}{\sin\theta}\frac{d}{d\theta}\left(\sin\theta\frac{d}{d\theta}\right) - \frac{m^2}{\sin^2\theta} \right] \Theta(\theta) = C\Theta(\theta)$$

$$C = \hbar^2 l(l + 1)$$
