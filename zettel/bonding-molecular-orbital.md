# Bonding Molecular Orbital

A molecular orbital formed by constructive interference of adjacent atomic orbitals, resulting in an enhanced electron probability density in the internuclear region and lower energy relative to isolated atomic orbitals.

$$\psi_+ = \chi_A + \chi_B$$
