# Classical Particle in a Box

The classical description of a point particle bouncing back and forth between rigid walls with constant kinetic energy. In this classical regime, any momentum magnitude is permitted, energy is not quantized, and the spatial probability density is uniform across the box.

$$|v| = \frac{p}{m}$$
