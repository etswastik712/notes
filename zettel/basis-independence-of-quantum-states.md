# Basis Independence of Quantum States

The principle that an intrinsic physical state is independent of the eigenbasis selected to represent it, such that expanding an eigenstate of one operator into the eigenbasis of a non-commuting operator reproduces the identical state vector upon operator action.

$$| + x\rangle = \frac{1 - i}{2} | + y\rangle + \frac{1 + i}{2} | - y\rangle$$

$$X | + y\rangle = i | - y\rangle, \quad X | - y\rangle = -i | + y\rangle$$

$$X | + x\rangle = | + x\rangle$$
