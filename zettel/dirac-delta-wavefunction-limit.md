# Dirac Delta Wavefunction Limit

The limiting wave distribution obtained by superposing plane waves across all real wavenumbers with equal weight, resulting in infinite spatial sharpness (Δx = 0) at the cost of completely infinite momentum spread (Δp = ∞). Like the single plane wave, it represents an unphysical idealization at the opposite extreme of the uncertainty trade-off.

$$\frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{ikx} \, dk = \delta(x)$$
