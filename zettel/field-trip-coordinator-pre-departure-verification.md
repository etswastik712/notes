# Field Trip Coordinator Pre-Departure Verification

A mandatory operational readiness checklist completed by trip coordinators prior to embarking. It enforces safety redundancy across transport mechanics, emergency medical logistics, participant subgroup assignments, and the retention of a dedicated logistical coordinator.
