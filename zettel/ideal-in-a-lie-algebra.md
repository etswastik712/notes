# Ideal in a Lie Algebra

A vector subspace $\mathfrak{h}$ of a Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ is an ideal if the Lie bracket $[X, Y]$ belongs to $\mathfrak{h}$ for all $X \in \mathfrak{g}$ and all $Y \in \mathfrak{h}$.

$$[X, Y] \in \mathfrak{h} \quad \forall X \in \mathfrak{g}, Y \in \mathfrak{h}$$
