# Coupon Collector Waiting Time

The discrete random variable T representing the total number of independent draws required to collect at least one coupon of each of N equally likely types from an infinite supply. Its tail distribution P[T > n] is evaluated using the principle of inclusion and exclusion over the events that individual coupon types are omitted in the first n draws.
