# Time-Independent Schrödinger Equation

The eigenvalue differential equation governing the spatial wave function psi(x) for time-independent potentials, derived by separating spatial and temporal variables in the time-dependent Schrödinger equation.

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{d^2 \psi(x)}{dx^2} + V(x)\psi(x) = E\psi(x)$$
