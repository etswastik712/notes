# Amphoteric Lewis Character of Sulfur Dioxide

The dual ability of sulfur dioxide to act as both a Lewis acid and a Lewis base depending on the partner. As a base, it donates via an oxygen lone pair to hard acids such as SbF5 or via sulfur to softer transition metals like Ru(III).
