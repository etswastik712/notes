# Hypsometric Equation

An equation derived from hydrostatic balance and the ideal gas law relating the geopotential thickness between two isobaric surfaces to the layer-mean virtual temperature and the logarithm of their pressure ratio. It demonstrates that the vertical separation of pressure surfaces expands in warm air and contracts in cold air.

$$Z_2 - Z_1 = \frac{R_d \overline{T}_v}{g_0} \ln\left(\frac{p_1}{p_2}\right)$$

$$\overline{T}_v = \frac{\int_{p_2}^{p_1} T_v d(\ln p)}{\ln(p_1 / p_2)}$$
