# Oxygen Isotope Paleothermometry

A paleoclimate proxy method tracking the ratio of heavy oxygen-18 to light oxygen-16 in ice cores and ocean sediment foraminifera shells. Rayleigh distillation during evaporation and precipitation leaves polar ice depleted in oxygen-18 during cold periods while ocean sediments become enriched.

$$\delta^{18}\text{O} = \left( \frac{(^{18}\text{O}/^{16}\text{O})_{\text{sample}}}{(^{18}\text{O}/^{16}\text{O})_{\text{standard}}} - 1 \right) \times 1000\, \text{‰}$$
