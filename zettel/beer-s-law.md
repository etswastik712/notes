# Beer's Law

A radiative transfer law stating that the fractional attenuation of radiation intensity along an optical path is proportional to particle concentration, extinction cross-section, and incremental path length, producing exponential decay with optical depth.

$$dI_\lambda = -I_\lambda K_\lambda N \sigma ds$$

$$I_\lambda = I_{\lambda,0} e^{-\tau_\lambda}$$
