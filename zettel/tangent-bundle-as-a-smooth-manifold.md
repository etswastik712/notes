# Tangent Bundle as a Smooth Manifold

The total space of the tangent bundle TM over an m-dimensional smooth manifold M admits a canonical 2m-dimensional smooth manifold structure making the bundle projection pi from TM to M a smooth map.

$$\pi : TM \to M$$
