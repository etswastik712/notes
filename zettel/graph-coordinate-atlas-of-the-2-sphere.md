# Graph Coordinate Atlas of the 2-Sphere

A smooth atlas on the 2-sphere S^2 consisting of six charts defined on open hemispheres where one Cartesian coordinate has a definite sign. Each chart projects a hemisphere onto the unit open disk in the coordinate plane perpendicular to that axis.

$$U_i^{\pm} = \{(x^1, x^2, x^3) \in S^2 : \pm x^i > 0\}$$

$$\varphi_i^{\pm}(x^1, x^2, x^3) = (x^1, \dots, \widehat{x^i}, \dots, x^3)$$
