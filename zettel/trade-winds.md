# Trade Winds

Persistent low-level surface winds blowing from east to west between roughly thirty degrees latitude and the equator, resulting from equatorward flow deflected westward by rotation.

- used-in:: [[zettel/intertropical-convergence-zone]] — Trade winds converge from both hemispheres into the equatorial low-pressure trough of the ITCZ
- used-in:: [[zettel/atmospheric-angular-momentum-balance]] — Surface easterly trade winds impart angular momentum from the rotating Earth to the atmosphere in the tropics
