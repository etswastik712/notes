# Disjointness or Equality of Equivalence Classes

Any two equivalence classes defined by an equivalence relation on a set are either completely identical or completely disjoint.

## Links
- needs:: [[zettel/equivalence-relation]] — The proof that non-disjoint classes are identical uses symmetry and transitivity of the equivalence relation
