# Classification of Two-Dimensional Non-Abelian Lie Algebras

Every two-dimensional non-abelian Lie algebra possesses a basis where the Lie bracket of the generators equals the second generator, making all such Lie algebras isomorphic to each other.

$$[X, Y] = c[V_1, V_2]$$

$$[W_1, W_2] = W_2$$
