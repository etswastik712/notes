# Ensemble Interpretation of the Wavefunction

The spatial probability density |\psi(x)|^2 describes the statistical distribution of outcomes over an ensemble of identically prepared measurements rather than a physical spatial smearing of a single particle. A single measurement returns a sharp, localized position coordinate, with the wave-like profile emerging solely in the accumulated histogram of repeated detections.
