# Thomson Scattering Classical Limit

A classical electrodynamic model treating radiation scattering as an incident wave driving a charged particle into oscillations at the exact same frequency, reradiating waves without any wavelength shift. It is the leading low-energy, negligible-recoil limit of electromagnetic scattering.

$$\omega' = \omega, \quad \nu' = \nu, \quad \lambda' = \lambda$$

$$\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega} = \frac{r_e^2}{2}(1 + \cos^2\theta)$$

$$r_e = \frac{e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 m_e c^2}$$
