# Finite Well Scattering States

Continuum solutions for energy above the well depth where wavefunctions represent incident, reflected, and transmitted plane waves. Continuity of the wavefunction and its first derivative at both boundaries determines transmission and reflection amplitudes.

$$\psi_I(x) = A e^{ikx} + B e^{-ikx}$$

$$\psi_{II}(x) = C e^{ik_0 x} + D e^{-ik_0 x}$$

$$\psi_{III}(x) = F e^{ikx}$$
