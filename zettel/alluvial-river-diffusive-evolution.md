# Alluvial River Diffusive Evolution

A continuum geomorphic model representing the long-term longitudinal profile grading of transport-limited alluvial rivers through a 1D diffusion-type transport equation.

## Links
- needs:: [[zettel/hack-s-rule]] — Longitudinal profile diffusion equations depend on along-stream distance and contributing area scaling relationships
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/bedrock-river]] — Transport-limited alluvial river profiles evolve via diffusive sediment transport dynamics, whereas bedrock rivers evolve via detachment-limited streampower incision
