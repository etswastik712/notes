# Atmospheric Thermal Stratification

The vertical subdivision of Earth's atmosphere into distinct layers based on the sign of the temperature lapse rate with altitude. The troposphere exhibits a decrease in temperature with height due to surface heating, whereas the stratosphere exhibits a temperature inversion driven by ozone UV absorption.
