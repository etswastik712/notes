# Discrete Uniform Distribution

A discrete distribution over a finite, non-empty sample space where every elementary outcome has an equal probability of occurrence, given by the reciprocal of the sample space cardinality.

$$P[X = x] = \frac{1}{|\Omega|} \quad \text{for each } x \in \Omega$$

$$P[X = i] = \frac{1}{n} \quad \text{for all } i \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}$$
