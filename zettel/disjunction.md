# Disjunction

A compound mathematical statement formed by connecting two statements, which is true whenever at least one of the component statements is true.

$$P \lor Q$$

## Links
- used-in:: [[zettel/de-morgan-s-laws]] — De Morgan's laws relate the negation of a disjunction to the conjunction of negated terms
