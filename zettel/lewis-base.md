# Lewis Base

An electron pair donor that supplies an electron pair to an acceptor species to form a coordination bond or adduct.

## Links
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/lewis-acid]] — Lewis bases supply the electron pair accepted by Lewis acids in coordinate complexation
- generalises:: [[zettel/hard-base]] — Hard bases are a subclass of Lewis bases characterized by high electronegativity and low polarizability
- generalises:: [[zettel/soft-base]] — Soft bases are a subclass of Lewis bases with highly polarizable donor atoms
