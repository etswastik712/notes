# Lie Algebra Ideal and Normal Subgroup Correspondence

For a connected Lie group $G$ and a connected Lie subgroup $H$, $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$ if and only if its Lie algebra $\mathfrak{h}$ is an ideal in the Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$.

$$[X, Y] \in \mathfrak{h} \quad \forall X \in \mathfrak{g}, Y \in \mathfrak{h}$$
