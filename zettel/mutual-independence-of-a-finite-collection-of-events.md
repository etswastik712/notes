# Mutual Independence of a Finite Collection of Events

A finite collection of events is independent when the probability of the intersection of any non-empty subset equals the product of the individual probabilities. This requires the product condition for every subset, not only pairs.

$$P\left[\bigcap_{j=1}^s E_{i_j}\right] = \prod_{j=1}^s P[E_{i_j}]$$
