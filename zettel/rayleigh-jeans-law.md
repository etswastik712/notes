# Rayleigh-Jeans Law

A classical approximation of blackbody spectral radiance valid at long wavelengths and high temperatures, scaling inversely with the fourth power of wavelength.

$$B_\lambda(T) \approx \frac{2 c k T}{\lambda^4}$$

## Links
- special-case-of:: [[zettel/planck-function]] — It is obtained as the first-order Taylor expansion of the exponential term in Planck's denominator for hc / (lambda k T) << 1
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/wien-s-displacement-law]] — Rayleigh-Jeans applies at long wavelengths and causes the ultraviolet catastrophe at short wavelengths, where Wien's approximation instead succeeds
