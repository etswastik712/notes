# Sahyadri Group Stratigraphy

The formal stratigraphic scheme established for the Western Deccan Volcanic Province, historically considered the type section for over 3,000 meters of composite flood basalt sequence. It reconciles vertical geochemical trends with field lithostratigraphy marked by regional flow morphologies and GPB horizons.
