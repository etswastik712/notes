# Simple Greenhouse Model

An idealized planar radiative equilibrium model where the atmosphere is represented by an isothermal single layer completely transparent to incoming shortwave solar flux and completely opaque to outgoing longwave terrestrial flux.

$$\sigma T_a^4 = \frac{1}{4}(1 - \alpha_p) S_0 = \sigma T_e^4$$

$$\sigma T_s^4 = 2\sigma T_e^4$$

$$T_s = 2^{1/4} T_e$$
