# Negation of a Conditional

The logical negation of a conditional statement P implies Q is logically equivalent to the conjunction of the premise P and the negation of the conclusion Q. It asserts that the condition holds while the consequence fails.

$$\neg (P \implies Q) \sim (P \land \neg Q)$$
