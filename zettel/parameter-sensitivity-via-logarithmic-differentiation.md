# Parameter Sensitivity via Logarithmic Differentiation

A perturbation method applying logarithmic differentiation to the planetary radiative equilibrium equation to relate relative fractional changes in solar constant and planetary co-albedo directly to fractional changes in equilibrium surface temperature.

$$\frac{\Delta S}{S} - \frac{\Delta \alpha}{1 - \alpha} = 4 \frac{\Delta T_s}{T_s}$$

$$-\frac{\Delta \alpha}{1 - \alpha} \approx 4 \frac{\Delta T_s}{T_s}$$

$$4 \frac{\Delta T_s}{T_s} \approx \frac{\Delta S}{S}$$
