# Hydrostatic Balance

The vertical mechanical equilibrium in a fluid column where the downward gravitational weight of a slab of air is balanced precisely by the net upward pressure gradient force.

$$\frac{dp}{dz} = -\rho g$$

$$(p)dA - (p + dp)dA = \rho g dA dz$$
