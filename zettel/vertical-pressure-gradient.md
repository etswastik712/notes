# Vertical Pressure Gradient

The steep decrease of atmospheric pressure with increasing altitude, which exceeds typical horizontal synoptic pressure gradients by four to five orders of magnitude.

## Links
- used-in:: [[zettel/hydrostatic-balance]] — The extreme vertical gradient of ~125 hPa/km is balanced almost entirely by gravity, validating hydrostatic balance
- special-case-of:: [[zettel/pressure-gradient-force]] — It represents the vertical component dp/dz of the full three-dimensional pressure gradient force
