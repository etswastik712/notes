# Addition Rule for Two Events

The probability of the union of two events equals the sum of their individual probabilities minus the probability of their intersection. It adjusts for the double counting of outcomes lying in both events.

$$P(E \cup F) = P(E) + P(F) - P(E \cap F)$$
