# Intertropical Convergence Zone

The low-pressure belt situated near the equator where trade winds converge and heated air ascends within the tropical overturning cells, migrating latitudinally across seasons.

## Links

- used-in:: [[zettel/monsoon-circulation]] — Seasonal migration of the ITCZ and continental heating drives the reversal of monsoon circulations
