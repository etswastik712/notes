# Angular Wavefunction Representations in Circular Wells

The selection between complex exponential basis states and standing trigonometric wave superpositions for the degenerate +/- l energy levels in a circularly symmetric potential. Complex exponentials yield circularly symmetric ring probability densities with definite angular momentum, whereas trigonometric linear combinations break angular rotational invariance into 2l spatial lobes.

$$|e^{il\theta}|^2 = 1$$

$$\psi(r,\theta) \propto J_l(k_{n,l}r)\,\sqrt{2}\cos(l\theta)$$
