# Sum of Independent Bernoulli Trials Representation of Binomial

A Binomial(n, p) random variable can be represented as the sum of n mutually independent Bernoulli(p) indicator random variables corresponding to the individual trial outcomes.

$$X = \sum_{i=1}^n X_i$$
