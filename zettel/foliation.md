# Foliation

A $k$-dimensional foliation $\mathcal{F}$ of a manifold $M$ is a decomposition of $M$ into a disjoint union of connected, immersed $k$-dimensional submanifolds called leaves, such that $M$ is covered by smooth flat charts whose intersections with each leaf correspond to constant slices.
