# Robertson Uncertainty Relation for Pauli Matrices

The explicit verification of the commutator uncertainty relation Delta X Delta Y >= (1/2)|<[X, Y]>| for two-level systems, saturating the inequality in eigenstates of the commutator operator Z while yielding a trivial lower bound of zero in eigenstates of X.

$$[X, Y] = 2iZ$$

$$\Delta X \Delta Y = 1, \quad \frac{1}{2} |\langle [X, Y] \rangle| = \frac{1}{2} |2i| = 1$$

$$\Delta X = 0, \quad \langle Z \rangle = 0$$
