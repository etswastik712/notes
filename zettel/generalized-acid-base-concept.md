# Generalized Acid-Base Concept

A unifying framework defining acidity as the positive character of a chemical species that decreases upon reaction with a base, and basicity as the negative character decreased upon reaction with an acid. This generalizes all definitions into donating positive species (or accepting negative species) for acids, and donating negative species (or accepting positive species) for bases.
