# Lewis Metathesis Reaction

A bimolecular reaction in which partners are mutually exchanged between acid-base adducts or salts, driven by differences in leaving group ability, halide affinities, or precipitation of insoluble salts.

$$\text{Me}_3\text{Si-I} + \text{AgBr} \rightarrow \text{Me}_3\text{Si-Br} + \text{AgI}$$
