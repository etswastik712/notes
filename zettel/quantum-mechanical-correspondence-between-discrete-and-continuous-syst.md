# Quantum-Mechanical Correspondence Between Discrete and Continuous Systems

The structural isomorphism mapping discrete vectors, Hermitian matrices, and summations in finite dimensions to wavefunctions, differential operators, and integrals in continuous wave mechanics.

$$|\psi\rangle = \sum_{n} c_n |a_n\rangle \longleftrightarrow \psi(x) = \sum_n c_n \phi_n(x)$$

$$\langle \psi | A | \psi \rangle \longleftrightarrow \int dx \, \psi^*(x) \hat{A} \psi(x)$$
