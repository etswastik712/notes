# Basic Principle of Counting

If two experiments are performed such that the first yields m possible outcomes and the second yields n outcomes, the total number of composite outcome pairs is mn. More generally, performing r successive experiments with m_i outcomes each produces m_1 m_2 ... m_r possible r-tuples.

$$m_1 m_2 \dots m_r$$
