# Free Particle on an Infinite Line

A quantum particle in zero potential across the entire real axis whose solutions are plane waves with unrestricted real wave numbers, generating a continuous non-negative energy spectrum.

$$k^2 = \frac{2mE}{\hbar^2}$$

$$\psi(x) = A e^{ikx} + B e^{-ikx}$$

$$E = \frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m} = \frac{p^2}{2m}$$
