# Principle of Inclusion and Exclusion for Cardinalities

The combinatorial identity computing the cardinality of the union of three finite sets by alternating sums of intersection cardinalities, applied here to demonstrate an inconsistency in reported survey data exceeding total population size.

$$|P \cup M \cup C| = |P| + |M| + |C| - |P \cap C| - |M \cap C| - |M \cap P| + |P \cap M \cap C| = 1057$$
