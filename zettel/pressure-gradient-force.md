# Pressure Gradient Force

A net surface force per unit mass on a fluid parcel arising from spatial differences in pressure across its opposing boundary faces, directed toward lower pressure.

$$\frac{F_x}{m} = -\frac{1}{\rho} \frac{\partial p}{\partial x}$$

$$\frac{\mathbf{F}}{m} = -\frac{1}{\rho} \nabla p$$
