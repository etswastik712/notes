# Valence Bond Theory

A quantum mechanical bonding model that expresses Lewis pair-sharing concepts using atomic orbital wavefunctions and spin pairing. Covalent bonds form when overlapping atomic orbitals with appropriate symmetry share paired electrons.

$$\psi = \chi_A(1) \chi_B(2) + \chi_A(2) \chi_B(1)$$
