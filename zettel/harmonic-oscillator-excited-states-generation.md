# Harmonic Oscillator Excited States Generation

The algebraic construction of the n-th energy eigenstate by repeated application of the raising operator to the ground state. A factorial normalization prefactor ensures unit normalization of the resulting state.

$$\psi_n(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n!}} (â_+)^n \psi_0(x)$$
