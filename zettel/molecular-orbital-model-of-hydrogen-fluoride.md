# Molecular Orbital Model of Hydrogen Fluoride

The molecular orbital configuration of hydrogen fluoride comprising three sigma-symmetry orbitals from H 1s, F 2s, and F 2pz, and two unperturbed non-bonding F 2px/2py pi orbitals. The bonding 2sigma orbital and non-bonding pairs localize predominantly on fluorine, explaining molecular polarity.
