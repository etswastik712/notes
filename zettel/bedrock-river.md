# Bedrock River

A river channel incising directly into exposed rock rather than an alluvial sediment bed, where incision is driven by streampower-dependent erosion mechanisms. Its morphology is shaped by processes like abrasion, quarrying, hydraulic wedging, and knickpoint migration.
