# Wave Function Boundary Matching across Discontinuities

The boundary conditions imposed on energy eigenfunctions by integrating the time-independent Schrödinger equation across a point of potential variation: a finite jump leaves the first derivative continuous, whereas a delta function induces a finite discontinuity in the first derivative proportional to the delta strength.

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \psi'' + V\psi = E\psi$$

$$\psi'(x_0^+) - \psi'(x_0^-) = \frac{2m\lambda}{\hbar^2} \psi(x_0)$$
