**Problem.** A fair die is rolled twice, with outcomes $X$ for the first roll and $Y$ for the second. Find the moment generating function $M_{X+Y}(t)$ of $X+Y$; the answer should be a function of $t$ and can contain unsimplified finite sums.

What it practises: the mgf $M_W(t)=E[e^{tW}]$ of a sum of two independent rolls (compare the joint pmf and independence of two dice in [[introduction-to-probability/2026-09-02-class-notes-2nd-september-2026]]).
