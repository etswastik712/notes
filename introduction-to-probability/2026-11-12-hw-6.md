## Covered
The file hw6_edited.pdf is handwritten (1 page); it was read from the page image, so formulas should be checked against the file. Three problems, no solutions given.

**Q1: orthogonal diagonalisation of a multivariate normal.** Let $X_i\sim N(\mu_i,\sigma_i^2)$ and let $\Sigma_{ij}=\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)$ be positive definite (symmetric, all eigenvalues $>0$). The non-degenerate multivariate normal distribution of $\vec X=(X_1,\dots,X_n)$ has pdf
$$f_{\vec X}(\vec x)=\frac{\exp\!\big(-\tfrac12(\vec x-\vec\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(\vec x-\vec\mu)\big)}{\sqrt{2\pi}^{\,n}\sqrt{\det\Sigma}}.$$
Let $\vec Y=O\vec X$, where the $n\times n$ matrix $O$ satisfies $O^{-1}=O^T$ and $O\Sigma O^T=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1'^2,\dots,\sigma_n'^2)$ (the sheet writes the diagonal entries as $\sigma_1^2,\dots,\sigma_n^2$; such an $O$ exists by a theorem from linear algebra, the spectral theorem). Show that $Y_i\sim N(\mu_i',\sigma_i'^2)$ with $\vec\mu'=O\vec\mu$, and that $Y_1,\dots,Y_n$ are independent. (The reading of the primes on $\sigma$ is uncertain; check the file.)

**Q2: transformations of uniforms.** Let $U_1,\dots,U_n$ be i.i.d. $\text{Unif}(0,1)$ and $X_j=-\ln U_j$.
- (a) Find the distribution of $X_j$ and name it.
- (b) Find the distribution of $U=U_1U_2\cdots U_n$ (hint on the sheet: pass to $V=-\log U$).

**Q3: tangent line and Jensen.** Related to Jensen's inequality $E[g(X)]\ge g(E[X])$ for $g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ with $g''(t)>0$. Show that the tangent line to the graph of $g$ lies below the graph (this fact is used in the proof of Jensen's inequality).

What the problems practise: change of variables for joint densities, and independence from a diagonal covariance in the Gaussian case (Q1); transformation of a single variable and sums of i.i.d. exponentials (Q2); the convexity argument behind Jensen (Q3).
