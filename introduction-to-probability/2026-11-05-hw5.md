# HW5

## Covered
The file hw5.pdf is handwritten (2 pages) and was read from page images, so formulas should be checked against the file. It has three problems; no solutions are given.

**Problem 1: introduction to order statistics.** Let $X_1,X_2$ be i.i.d. with pdf $f(x)$, and $Y_1=\min\{X_1,X_2\}$, $Y_2=\max\{X_1,X_2\}$. Prove that for $y_1<y_2$
$$f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)=2f(y_1)f(y_2).$$
The sheet gives a schematic diagram for the proof: in the $(x_1,x_2)$-plane the small square at $(y_1,y_2)$ and the one at $(y_2,y_1)$ both map to the same small square of sides $dy_1,dy_2$ in the $(y_1,y_2)$-plane (two preimages, hence the factor 2). More generally, if $X_1,\dots,X_n$ are i.i.d. with density $f$ and $Y_1,\dots,Y_n$ are the $X_i$ sorted from min to max, then
$$f_{Y_1,\dots,Y_n}(y_1,\dots,y_n)=n!\,f(y_1)\cdots f(y_n)\quad\text{for }y_1<y_2<\cdots<y_n.$$

**Problem 2: exponential order statistics.** Let $X_1,X_2\sim\text{Expo}(\lambda)$ be independent, and $Y_1,Y_2$ as above.
- (a) Show $Y_1\sim\text{Expo}(2\lambda)$ (done in class).
- (b) Using the change of variables $(Y_1,Y_2)\to(Y_1,Y_2-Y_1)$, find the pdf of $(Y_1,Y_2-Y_1)$.
- (c) Find the marginal distribution of $Y_2-Y_1$ from (b) and hence show $Y_2-Y_1\sim\text{Expo}(\lambda)$.
- (d) Again using (b) and (c), show that $Y_2-Y_1$ and $Y_1$ are independent.
- (e) Show $\operatorname{Var}(Y_1)=\dfrac{5}{4\lambda^2}$ and that $\operatorname{Corr}(Y_1,Y_2)=1/\sqrt5$ (marked "done in class"). As read from the handwriting; check against the file, since $Y_1\sim\text{Expo}(2\lambda)$ would give $\operatorname{Var}(Y_1)=1/(4\lambda^2)$, so one of the two readings may be off.

**Problem 3: multinomial moments.** Let $(X_1,\dots,X_k)\sim\text{Mult}_k(n,\vec p)$ with $\vec p=(p_1,\dots,p_k)$, $p_1+\cdots+p_k=1$. Let $Y_{ij}=1$ if the $i$-th outcome appears in the $j$-th trial, else $0$.
- (a) Show $E(Y_{ij})=p_i$ and
$$\operatorname{Cov}(Y_{ij},Y_{kj})=\begin{cases}-p_ip_k&i\ne k\\ p_i(1-p_i)&i=k.\end{cases}$$
- (b) Show $X_i=Y_{i1}+\cdots+Y_{in}$ and hence $E(X_i)=np_i$, $\operatorname{Var}(X_i)=np_i(1-p_i)$, and for $i\ne k$, $\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_k)=-np_ip_k$. The covariance matrix of $(X_1,\dots,X_k)$ is hence $n\,\operatorname{diag}(p_1,\dots,p_k)-n\,\vec p\,\vec p^{\,T}$.

What the problems practise: change of variables for joint densities (Problems 1, 2), order statistics of exponentials and independence of $Y_1$ and $Y_2-Y_1$ (Problem 2), covariance and correlation of dependent variables via indicators (Problems 2e, 3).
