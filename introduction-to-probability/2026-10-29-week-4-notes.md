**Problem.** A fair die is rolled twice, with outcomes $X$ for the first roll and $Y$ for the second. Find the moment generating function $M_{X+Y}(t)$ of $X+Y$; the answer should be a function of $t$ and can contain unsimplified finite sums (Classroom: Week4_notes.pdf). This is HW3 problem 4, which HW3 had postponed; the same problem is in [[introduction-to-probability/2026-10-29-hw4]].

What it practises: the mgf $M_W(t)=E[e^{tW}]$ of a sum of two independent rolls. Rule of 2026-09-17: the mgf of any known distribution used in a solution must be derived first, otherwise points are lost.
