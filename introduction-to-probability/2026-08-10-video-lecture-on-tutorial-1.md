# Video Lecture on Tutorial 1

## Covered
A recorded video of solutions to the Tutorial 1 problems ([[introduction-to-probability/2026-08-06-practice-problems-7th-august-2026]]), made at the request of students in the remedial class. The video itself was not watched; only the post text is summarised.

Correction stated in the post: in question 6 (round-robin with $n$ players), the total number of outcomes is $2^{n(n-1)/2}$, since there are $\binom n2=n(n-1)/2$ games and 2 possibilities for the winner of each game (Classroom: Video Lecture on Tutorial 1, 2026-08-10).
