# Tutorial: CERES, Earth's energy budget (self-study, posted 2026-10-31)

## Covered
Only the tutorial's landing page was readable; the handout PDF and the data app themselves were not opened (see "Files"). What the page states (Classroom announcement and course website, EC2113 Module 3, Joy Monteiro):
- The tutorial is in the ocean module and works with real data on Earth's energy budget from CERES (Clouds and the Earth's Radiant Energy System), with a handout of exercises.
- The guiding question: where does Earth take in more energy than it gives back, and where less, and how do clouds, the atmosphere and El Niño change the books?
- The data can be downloaded in CSV format for your own explorations on a computer.
- It is purely for self-study: no TA sessions.
- The course website also lists lecture "O1: Climate science is accounting" (28 October 2026): a planet's energy books must balance, no single place on Earth is in balance, and the atmosphere and ocean move energy, mass and momentum to keep them balanced.
