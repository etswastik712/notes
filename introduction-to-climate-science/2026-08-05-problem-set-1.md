# Problem set 1 (Rahul M & Argha B)

Based on Lectures 1 and 2. Posted 2026-08-05; no due date given. The post says to attempt the problems and bring doubts to the Friday tutorial.

## Covered
| Q | Task | Practises |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | Total weight of the atmosphere with sea-level pressure $10^5$ Pa | Pressure as column weight per area: $W=pA$ with $A=4\pi R^2$ |
| 1b | Ratio of atmosphere thickness (100 km) to Earth's radius; argue $g$ is constant | Scale separation, constant $g$ in climate problems |
| 1c | Mass of the atmosphere (100 km slab of constant density) | Order-of-magnitude estimation |
| 2a | Total solar power received by Earth ($S\approx1400$ W m$^{-2}$) | Intercepted power $S\pi R^2$ |
| 2b | Compare the solar constant with human basal metabolic rate (W m$^{-2}$) | Unit conversion and comparison |
| 2c | Compare total solar input with humankind's energy needs (W) | Scale of human energy use |
| 2d | Fraction of solar energy fixed by vegetation; share appropriated by humans; is the planetary boundary crossed? | Link to planetary boundaries (HANPP boundary <10 %, now ~30 %) |
| 3 | Mercury thermometer: bulb radius 0.50 cm, bulb length 1 cm, $\beta=1.8\times10^{-4}$ K$^{-1}$, glass expansion negligible, cylindrical bulb and capillary | Thermal expansion, instrument design |
| 3a | Capillary radius for a 0.1 °C least count with a 1 mm scale | $\Delta V=\beta V\Delta T$ spread along the capillary |
| 3b | Calibration constant in °C/mm | Inverse of the sensitivity |
| 3c | Constriction case: object at 100 °C, mercury in the capillary cools by 10 °C while the bulb stays isolated; estimate the reading error and comment | Error estimation |
| 4 | $\delta^{18}$O of continental ice varies −57 ‰ to −51 ‰ over the ~100 ky cycle: percentage change; sea level amplitude 100 m, mean ocean depth 4000 m: estimate the seawater $\delta^{18}$O change, match with the slide 20 graph, discuss any discrepancy | Isotopic thermometers, mass balance of ocean and ice |
