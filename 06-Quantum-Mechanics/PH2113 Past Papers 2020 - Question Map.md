> [!warning] Scope then and now
> The 2020 course spent its second half on **spin, Stern–Gerlach, qubits and circuits** (Quiz-2 and a third of the final). In 2026 the lectures went instead to the finite well, barriers, Ehrenfest, **2D problems and hydrogen** ([[QM11 Two-Dimensional Problems Angular Momentum and the Circular Well]], [[QM12 The Hydrogen Atom]]); the vault covers spin only as an extra ([[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin]]). Use the 2020 spin questions as optional practice unless this year's instructor says otherwise.
