# Hydrogen Atom Spectrum

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Hydrogen_atom.pdf#page=5|hydrogen notes eq. 26]]
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** lines and reduced mass, QM12 §5

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Bound levels at −13.6 eV/n², crowding toward zero, with all l from 0 to n−1 at the same energy.

> [!key] Definition
> $E_n=-\frac{me^4}{2(4\pi\epsilon_0)^2\hbar^2}\frac1{n^2}=-\frac{13.606\ \text{eV}}{n^2}$; $l=0..n-1$, $m=-l..l$: degeneracy $n^2$ ($2n^2$ with spin). Lyman α 121.5 nm, Balmer α (H-α) 656.1 nm, Balmer β 486.0 nm. Reduced mass: ×0.999456.
