# Logarithmic Derivative Matching

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Griffiths §2.6; the lecture does the same division on [[Finite_well.pdf#page=3|p.3]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Instead of matching two numbers at the wall (height and slope) separately, match their *ratio* $\psi'/\psi$: it removes the unknown amplitudes in one step.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> At a boundary $x_0$ where $\psi,\psi'$ are continuous, $\dfrac{\psi'}{\psi}\Big|_{x_0^-}=\dfrac{\psi'}{\psi}\Big|_{x_0^+}$. Finite well, even state: $-k_0\tan k_0a=-\kappa$.

The logarithmic derivative $(\ln\psi)'$ is what the interior must supply to look like a decaying exponential outside. For $\psi'/\psi=-\kappa$ outside, quantisation is *"find $E$ such that the interior ends with slope $-\kappa$"*.

Used in: [[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §3, §9. Related: [[Transcendental Equation]] · [[Boundary Conditions and Quantisation]].
