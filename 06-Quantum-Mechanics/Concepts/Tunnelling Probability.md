# Tunnelling Probability

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** QM07 §5.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The share of a bound state's probability that sits outside the classical turning points.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Even state: $P_{\rm out}=\dfrac{\cos^2z/\kappa a}{1+\sin2z/2z+\cos^2z/\kappa a}$; odd: $\dfrac{\sin^2z/\kappa a}{1-\sin2z/2z+\sin^2z/\kappa a}$. Reference: $z_0=5$: 1.17 %, 5.11 %, 14.0 %, 49.0 % for the four states.

It is largest for the highest bound state (small $\kappa$) and tends to 100 % as $z_0\to0$. Not to be confused with the *transmission* coefficient through a barrier ([[Transmission Coefficient]]).

Used in: [[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §5, §6. Related: [[Penetration Depth]] · [[Classically Forbidden Region]].
