# Associated Legendre Polynomials

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Hydrogen_atom.pdf#page=3|hydrogen notes eqs. 16–22]]

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The θ-dependence of angular-momentum eigenstates: well-behaved at both poles only for C = ħ²l(l+1).

> [!key] Definition
> Solve $\frac1{\sin\theta}\frac{d}{d\theta}(\sin\theta\,\Theta')-\frac{m^2}{\sin^2\theta}\Theta=-l(l+1)\Theta$. $P_0^0=1$, $P_1^0=x$, $P_1^1=-\sqrt{1-x^2}$, $P_2^0=\frac12(3x^2-1)$, $P_2^1=-3x\sqrt{1-x^2}$, $P_2^2=3(1-x^2)$, $x=\cos\theta$ (Condon–Shortley sign).

Used in: [[QM12 The Hydrogen Atom]]. Related: [[Spherical Harmonics]] · [[Orthogonal Polynomials]].
