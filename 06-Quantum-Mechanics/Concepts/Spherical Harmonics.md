# Spherical Harmonics

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Hydrogen_atom.pdf#page=3|hydrogen notes pp.3–4]]

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The standing-wave patterns on a sphere: simultaneous eigenfunctions of the size and z-component of angular momentum.

> [!key] Definition
> $Y_{lm}(\theta,\varphi)\propto P_l^m(\cos\theta)e^{im\varphi}$, $l=0,1,2,\dots$, $m=-l..l$; $\hat L^2Y_{lm}=\hbar^2l(l+1)Y_{lm}$, $\hat L_zY_{lm}=\hbar mY_{lm}$. Orthonormal and complete on the sphere. The angular part of every central-potential eigenfunction.

Used in: [[QM12 The Hydrogen Atom]]. Related: [[Associated Legendre Polynomials]] · [[Angular Momentum Algebra]] · [[Laplacian in Polar and Spherical Coordinates]].
