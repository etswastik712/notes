# Flavour Oscillation

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** the two-level mapping ([[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §12.2); constants recalled `[verify]`.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Neutrinos fly as a superposition of two mass states; the phase between them rotates the 'flavour spin' on a Bloch sphere.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $H=\frac{\Delta m^2}{4E}\begin{pmatrix}-\cos2\theta&\sin2\theta\\\sin2\theta&\cos2\theta\end{pmatrix}$ so $\Omega=\frac{\Delta m^2}{2E}\sin2\theta$, $\Delta=-\frac{\Delta m^2}{2E}\cos2\theta$, and Rabi's formula gives $P=\sin^22\theta\,\sin^2\big(1.267\,\Delta m^2[\mathrm{eV}^2]\,L[\mathrm{km}]/E[\mathrm{GeV}]\big)$.

$1.267=1/(4\hbar c)$ in these units. $\Delta m^2=2.5\times10^{-3}$ eV$^2$: first maximum at $L/E=496$ km/GeV; a 295 km, $0.6$ GeV beam has phase $1.557$ rad, $\sin^2=0.9998$. Neutral kaons $K^0$–$\bar K^0$ are the same mathematics.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> The $\sin^22\theta$ amplitude is $\Omega^2/(\Omega^2+\Delta^2)$: maximal mixing needs $\Delta\to0$.
