# Rectangular Barrier

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Griffiths §2.5–§2.6; [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A wall of finite thickness: the wave leaks through to the other side.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $E<V_0$: $T=\big[1+\frac{V_0^2\sinh^2\kappa L}{4E(V_0-E)}\big]^{-1}\simeq16\frac E{V_0}\big(1-\frac E{V_0}\big)e^{-2\kappa L}$ for $\kappa L\gg1$. Electron, $E=1$, $V_0=2$ eV, $L=0.5$ nm: $T=0.0235$.

It is the finite well with $k_0\to i\kappa$ ([[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §11 continued analytically).

Used in: [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §3. Related: [[Quantum Tunnelling]] · [[Step Potential]] · [[Transmission Coefficient]] · [[Delta Function Potential]].
