# Quantum Zeno Effect

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** the chain of [[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §5.6; Misra and Sudarshan (1977) recalled.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Measure a changing state often enough and it stops changing: or, with a slowly rotating axis, measure it into a new state.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Survival after $N$ equally spaced measurements of a spin precessing by $\Delta\phi$: $P=\cos^{2N}(\Delta\phi/2N)\to1-\Delta\phi^2/4N\to1$. Dragging: $+z\to-z$ through $N$ analysers turned by $\pi/N$: $P_N=\cos^{2N}(\pi/2N)$: $0.25$ ($N=2$), $0.78$ (10), $0.976$ (100), $0.9975$ (1000).

Same formula as the chain of polarisers; the short-time quadratic decay $1-(\Delta\phi/2)^2$ is what makes frequent measurement freeze motion.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Not magic: each measurement is a projection that resets the clock; with finite measurement times the effect fades.

Used in: [[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §5.6, §12.1. Related: [[Sequential Stern-Gerlach Experiments]] · [[Measurement Postulate]] · [[Larmor Precession]] · [[Photon Polarisation]].
