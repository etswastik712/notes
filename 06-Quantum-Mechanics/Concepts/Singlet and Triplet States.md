# Singlet and Triplet States

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Sakurai §3.8 ([[modern-quantum-mechanics-3nbsped-9781108473224-1108473229_compress.pdf#page=225|PDF p.225 ff.]]); Griffiths §4.4.3 ([[Griffiths - Introduction to quantum mechanics.pdf#page=180|p.165 ff.]]). 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** the rotation-invariance argument ([[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §10).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Two spin-½'s combine into total spin 0 (one state, antisymmetric) or 1 (three states, symmetric).

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $|0,0\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2}(|{\uparrow\downarrow}\rangle-|{\downarrow\uparrow}\rangle)$; $|1,1\rangle=|{\uparrow\uparrow}\rangle$, $|1,0\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt2}(|{\uparrow\downarrow}\rangle+|{\downarrow\uparrow}\rangle)$, $|1,-1\rangle=|{\downarrow\downarrow}\rangle$. $\mathbf S_1\cdot\mathbf S_2=-\frac34\hbar^2$ (singlet), $+\frac14\hbar^2$ (triplet).

$(U\otimes U)|0,0\rangle=\det U\,|0,0\rangle=|0,0\rangle$: the singlet has no preferred direction. Pauli principle: spin triplet $\leftrightarrow$ antisymmetric spatial part, lower energy (helium $2^3S_1$ below $2^1S_0$; Hund's rule; $\mathrm{O_2}$ triplet). Hyperfine: [[Hydrogen 21 cm Line]].

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Each spin of a singlet is completely unpolarised ($\rho=\frac12\mathbf1$) even though the pair is pure.

Used in: [[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §10–§11. Related: [[Entanglement]] · [[Hydrogen 21 cm Line]] · [[Angular Momentum Algebra]] · [[Bell Inequality and CHSH]].
