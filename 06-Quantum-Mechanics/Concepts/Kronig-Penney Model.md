# Kronig-Penney Model

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §8.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The simplest solvable crystal: a row of thin barriers; bands appear, one edge at each box level.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\cos qa=\cos ka+\frac{m\alpha}{\hbar^2k}\sin ka$, $P=m\alpha a/\hbar^2$. Band edges at $ka=n\pi$. For $P=3$ the first three bands (units $\hbar^2\pi^2/2ma^2$): $[0.40,1.00]$, $[1.92,4.00]$, $[5.09,9.00]$.

$P\to0$: free particle; $P\to\infty$: isolated boxes (tight binding).

Used in: [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §8. Related: [[Bloch Theorem]] · [[Delta Function Potential]] · [[Transfer Matrix]].
