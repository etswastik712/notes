# Bohr Radius and Atomic Units

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Hydrogen_atom.pdf#page=1|hydrogen notes p.1]] (dimensional analysis)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The only length and energy one can build from m_e, e²/4πε₀ and ħ.

> [!key] Definition
> $a=\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0\hbar^2}{m_ee^2}=0.52918$ Å; $\varepsilon=\frac{m_e}{\hbar^2}(\frac{e^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0})^2=27.211$ eV $=2\times13.606$ eV (the Hartree). In units $\hbar=m_e=e^2/4\pi\epsilon_0=1$, $E_n=-1/2n^2$.

Used in: [[QM12 The Hydrogen Atom]], [[QM01 From Waves to Probabilities]]. Related: [[Hydrogen Atom Spectrum]] · [[Hydrogen Atom Stability]].
