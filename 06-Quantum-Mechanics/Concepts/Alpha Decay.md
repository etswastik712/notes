# Alpha Decay

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond** (application of [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] and [[Gamow Factor]]; numbers in [[LSX1 Clocks in Rocks]] §3).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The alpha particle rattles inside the nucleus and leaks out through the Coulomb barrier by tunnelling: a tiny chance per hit, a huge number of hits.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\lambda\approx f\,e^{-2G}$, $f\sim v/2R\sim10^{21}$ s$^{-1}$, $G=\hbar^{-1}\int_R^b\sqrt{2\mu(2Z_de^2/4\pi\varepsilon_0r-Q)}\,dr$. Computed without fitting: $^{238}$U 4.27 MeV, $2G=88$, $t_{1/2}=1.5\times10^9$ yr (observed $4.5\times10^9$); $^{212}$Po 8.95 MeV, $2.8\times10^{-8}$ s (observed $3\times10^{-7}$); a 10 % rise of $Q$ shortens $^{238}$U by $\sim2300$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> The exponential sensitivity means small errors in $Q$ or $R$ give big errors in $t_{1/2}$: the model is good to a factor $\sim3$–10, not better.
