# Effective Well Width

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Griffiths §2.6; QM07 §5.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A finite well behaves like a longer box because the wave spills into the walls, so it has lower energy.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $2a_{\rm eff}=2a+2/\kappa$, giving $E_n\simeq E_n^{\rm box}\big(1+\tfrac{1}{\kappa a}\big)^{-2}\approx E_n^{\rm box}(1-2/z_0)$ for deep wells.

Check at $z_0=50$: exact ground state $E/E^{\rm box}=0.96116$, formula $0.96$.

Used in: [[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §5, §12. Related: [[Penetration Depth]] · [[Particle in a Box]] · [[Zero-Point Energy]].
