# Transmission Resonance

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** the lecture's $T(E)$ sketch on [[Finite_well.pdf#page=13|p.13]]: $2k_0a=n\pi$.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A thin film is transparent when a whole number of half-wavelengths fits inside: reflections from its two faces cancel.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Perfect transmission, $T=1$, when $\sin(2k_0a)=0$, i.e. $E_n=n^2\pi^2\hbar^2/2m(2a)^2$ (the *unshifted* levels of a rigid box of width $2a$), for those $E_n>V_0$. At threshold $T\to0$ unless $z_0$ is a multiple of $\pi/2$.

The same mechanism makes a double barrier transparent at its quasi-bound level ([[Resonant Tunnelling]]).

Used in: [[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §11. Related: [[Transmission Coefficient]] · [[Scattering State]] · [[Resonant Tunnelling]].
