# Transmission Coefficient

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** the lecture: $T=|F/A|^2$ ([[Finite_well.pdf#page=11|p.11]]).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Of the current that arrives, the fraction that gets through.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $T=j_{\rm trans}/j_{\rm inc}=\dfrac{k'}{k}|t|^2$. Finite well: $T=\big[1+\frac{V_0^2}{4E(E-V_0)}\sin^2(2k_0a)\big]^{-1}$. Barrier ($E<V_0$): $T=\big[1+\frac{V_0^2\sinh^2\kappa L}{4E(V_0-E)}\big]^{-1}$.

Always use the *current* ratio: the step at $E=2V_0$ has $|t|^2=1.373$ but $T=0.971$.

Used in: [[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §11, [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §1–§3. Related: [[Reflection Coefficient]] · [[Probability Current]] · [[Quantum Tunnelling]].
