# Resonant Tunnelling

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §7.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Two opaque barriers can be perfectly transparent together: they form a cavity that fills when the energy matches a quasi-bound level.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> At $E_r$: $T=1$ (symmetric barriers); near it $T\approx\Gamma_L\Gamma_R/[(E-E_r)^2+(\Gamma/2)^2]$; lifetime $\hbar/\Gamma$. Example (units $\hbar^2/2m=1$): $E_r/V_0=0.5616$.

Off resonance the pair transmits *less* than one barrier ($10^{-3}$ against 0.23 at $E=0.1V_0$).

Used in: [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §7. Related: [[Transmission Resonance]] · [[Rectangular Barrier]] · [[Transfer Matrix]].
