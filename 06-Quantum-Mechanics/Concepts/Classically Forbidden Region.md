# Classically Forbidden Region

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Griffiths §2.6; QM05 §9 (oscillator).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The places where a classical particle with energy $E$ would need negative kinetic energy; a quantum wave still has a tail there.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Where $V(x)>E$. In it $\psi''=\kappa^2\psi$ with $\kappa^2>0$, so $\psi\propto e^{\pm\kappa x}$ and only the decaying one is allowed. The probability of finding the particle there is $P_{\rm out}>0$: 0.83 % for the ground state of a 5 eV, 1 nm well; 63.8 % for the deuteron.

No measurement ever finds a negative kinetic energy: localising the particle inside gives it a momentum spread large enough that the average is positive.

Used in: [[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §5, [[QM05 The Harmonic Oscillator]] §9. Related: [[Penetration Depth]] · [[Tunnelling Probability]] · [[Quantum Tunnelling]].
