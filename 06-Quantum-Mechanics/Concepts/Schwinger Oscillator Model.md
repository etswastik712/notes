# Schwinger Oscillator Model

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Sakurai §3.9 ([[modern-quantum-mechanics-3nbsped-9781108473224-1108473229_compress.pdf#page=238|PDF p.238 ff.]]). 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** the link to light and to QM05–QM06 ([[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §9.5).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Angular momentum as the way two oscillators trade quanta: moving a quantum from $b$ to $a$ raises $J_z$.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $J_+=\hbar a^\dagger b$, $J_-=\hbar b^\dagger a$, $J_z=\frac\hbar2(a^\dagger a-b^\dagger b)$; $[J_+,J_-]=2\hbar J_z$; $\mathbf J^2=\hbar^2\frac N2(\frac N2+1)$, $N=n_a+n_b$. $|n_a,n_b\rangle=|j,m\rangle$ with $j=\frac{n_a+n_b}2$, $m=\frac{n_a-n_b}2$.

One quantum shared between two modes is spin-½ (for light: $H$ and $V$ polarisation, with the Stokes parameters as $J_x,J_y,J_z$). Two quanta: spin 1. Checked numerically on a truncated two-mode Fock space.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> $N$ is conserved; $J_\pm$ never changes it, so each $j$ is a separate representation.

Used in: [[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §9.5. Related: [[Ladder Operators]] · [[Number Operator]] · [[Angular Momentum Algebra]] · [[Photon Polarisation]] · [[QM06 The Oscillator Toolbox]].
