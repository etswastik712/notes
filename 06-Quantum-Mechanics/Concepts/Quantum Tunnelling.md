# Quantum Tunnelling

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Lecture 9 announced it; no lecture source; [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A wave decays *inside* a barrier but does not stop; if the barrier is thin the amplitude that is left comes out on the other side.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $T\sim e^{-2\kappa L}$, $\kappa=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$: exponentially sensitive to width and to $\sqrt{\rm mass}$. Examples: $\alpha$ decay of $^{238}$U ($2G=88$), the STM (factor 7.8 per 0.1 nm), ammonia inversion (23.87 GHz).

Not an energy loss: the energy is unchanged; the particle emerges with the same $E$.

Used in: [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]]. Related: [[Rectangular Barrier]] · [[WKB Approximation]] · [[Gamow Factor]] · [[Evanescent Wave]].
