# Magnetic Resonance

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Sakurai §5.5.4 ([[modern-quantum-mechanics-3nbsped-9781108473224-1108473229_compress.pdf#page=345|PDF p.345]]) incl. the Nobel Prizes (Rabi 1944, Bloch and Purcell 1952). 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** the Boltzmann-polarisation numbers ([[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §6, §7.5).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Shine radio waves at exactly the Larmor frequency of the spins and they flip: Rabi's method turned into NMR, ESR and MRI.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Resonance when $\omega=|\gamma|B_0$. In the rotating frame the effective field is $B_0+\omega/\gamma$, zero on resonance. Proton: $63.87$ MHz at 1.5 T, $127.7$ MHz at 3 T; electron: $28.03$ GHz at 1 T. Signal $\propto$ polarisation $P=\tanh(\mu B/k_BT)$.

Protons in a 1.5 T scanner at 300 K have $P=5.1\times10^{-6}$ (50 % would need 2.8 mK): magnetic resonance is a ppm-level effect built on a Boltzmann factor. An oscillating field is a rotating plus a counter-rotating one; only the resonant one matters.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> $\gamma_e/\gamma_p=658$: ESR is GHz, NMR is MHz at the same field.
