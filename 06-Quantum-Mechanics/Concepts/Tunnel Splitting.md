# Tunnel Splitting

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** 🔵 **extra · beyond:** [[QM10 The Finite Barrier and Tutorial V]] §6 (checked against exact roots).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Coupling two identical wells through a barrier turns one level into an even (lower) and an odd (higher) level.

> [!key] Definition
> $E_{\rm odd}-E_{\rm even}\propto e^{-\kappa d}$, $\kappa=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$. Example $V_0=20$, $w=1$ ($\hbar^2/2m=1$): $d=1$: $0.0942$; $d=3$: $3.67\times10^{-5}$; slope of $\ln\Delta E$ vs $d$ is $-3.9256=-\kappa$.

Used in: [[QM10 The Finite Barrier and Tutorial V]]. Related: [[Double Well Potential]] · [[Quantum Tunnelling]] · [[Resonant Tunnelling]].
