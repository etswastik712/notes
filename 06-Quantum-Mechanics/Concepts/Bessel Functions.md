# Bessel Functions

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Angular_momentum (2).pdf]] eqs. 7–9
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** integral representation and zeros, QM11 §5.1

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The radial standing waves of anything circular — a drum, a light beam in a fibre, a particle in a disc.

> [!key] Definition
> Solutions of $R''+\frac1rR'+(k^2-\frac{l^2}{r^2})R=0$: $J_l(kr)$ (finite at 0) and $Y_l(kr)$ (diverges at 0). $J_l(x)=\frac1\pi\int_0^\pi\cos(l\tau-x\sin\tau)\,d\tau$; near 0, $J_l\sim x^l$; for large $x$, $J_l\approx\sqrt{2/\pi x}\cos(x-l\pi/2-\pi/4)$. First zeros: $J_0$: 2.405, 5.520, 8.654; $J_1$: 3.832, 7.016; $J_2$: 5.136, 8.417.

Used in: [[QM11 Two-Dimensional Problems Angular Momentum and the Circular Well]]. Related: [[Circular Infinite Well]] · [[Orthogonal Polynomials]].
