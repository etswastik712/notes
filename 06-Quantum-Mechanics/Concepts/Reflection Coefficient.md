# Reflection Coefficient

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** the lecture: $R=|B/A|^2$ and $T+R=1$ ([[Finite_well.pdf#page=11|p.11]]).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Of the current that arrives, the fraction that bounces back.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $R=|r|^2$ with $r=B/A$; $R=1-T$. Step: $R=\big(\frac{k_1-k_2}{k_1+k_2}\big)^2$. For a symmetric potential $r/t$ is purely imaginary (unitarity).

Above a *lower* region there is still reflection (quantum): the step at $E=2V_0$ reflects 2.9 %.

Used in: [[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §11, [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §1–§2, §9. Related: [[Transmission Coefficient]] · [[Probability Current]].
