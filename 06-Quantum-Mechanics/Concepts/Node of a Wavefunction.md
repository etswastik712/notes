# Node of a Wavefunction

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Particle_in_a_box.pdf#page=7|box lecture p.7]]. · 🟡 **extra · book:** the oscillation theorem (ground state has no nodes).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A point where the probability is exactly zero; more nodes means shorter wavelengths and higher energy.

> [!key] Definition
> A **node** is an interior zero of $\psi$. Box $\psi_n$: $n-1$ nodes. Oscillator $\psi_n$: $n$ nodes ($\psi_2$ at $\xi=\pm1/\sqrt2$). The ground state has none.

## Use
More nodes raise $\langle p^2\rangle$ and the energy. A nodeless ground state is unique ([[Ground State Uniqueness]]). A node is where the density vanishes: for $n=2$ the box density at $a/2$ is zero, yet $\langle x\rangle=a/2$.

> [!pitfall] The wall zeros are not nodes
> The zeros at $x=0,a$ are boundary conditions, not interior nodes.
