# Circular Infinite Well

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Angular_momentum (2).pdf]] (typed note); `circular_well_density.ipynb`

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A particle confined to a disc: Bessel-function standing waves whose allowed wavenumbers are the zeros of J_l.

> [!key] Definition
> $\psi=N J_l(k_{n,l}r)e^{il\theta}$, $k_{n,l}a=j_{l,n}$ ($n$-th zero of $J_l$), $E_{n,l}=\hbar^2j_{l,n}^2/2ma^2$: 5.783, 14.682 (×2), 26.375 (×2), 30.471, 40.706 (×2) … in units $\hbar^2/2ma^2$. $|N|^2=1/\pi a^2J_{l+1}^2(j_{l,n})$. $e^{il\theta}$ states have ring densities; $\cos l\theta$ combinations have $2l$ lobes.

Tool: `qm_circular_well`.

Used in: [[QM11 Two-Dimensional Problems Angular Momentum and the Circular Well]]. Related: [[Bessel Functions]] · [[Particle in a Box]] · [[Particle on a Ring]] · [[Degeneracy]].
