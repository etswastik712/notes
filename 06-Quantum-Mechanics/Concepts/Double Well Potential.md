# Double Well Potential

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** 🟢 **taught:** [[Tutorial5.pdf|Tutorial V]] Q2; [[QM10 The Finite Barrier and Tutorial V]] §6.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Two wells share one barrier: thin barrier gives one wide well with a dip, thick barrier gives two nearly independent wells.

> [!key] Definition
> Ground state is even (no node). $d\ll w$: single wide-well hump with a central dip. $d\gg w$: two equal lobes; odd partner has a node at the centre. Levels split by $\Delta E\propto e^{-\kappa d}$.

Used in: [[QM10 The Finite Barrier and Tutorial V]]. Related: [[Tunnel Splitting]] · [[Bound State]] · [[Parity Theorem]] · [[Rectangular Barrier]].
