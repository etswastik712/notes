# Hydrogen 21 cm Line

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Griffiths hyperfine splitting (`[verify]` section); Sakurai §3.8. 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** the numbers ([[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §10, §13).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The electron and proton spins of hydrogen flip relative to each other and emit a radio photon: a *spin-flip* seen across the galaxy.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $H_{\rm hf}=(A/\hbar^2)\,\mathbf I\cdot\mathbf S$: triplet ($F=1$) above singlet ($F=0$) by $A$. $\nu=1420.405752$ MHz, $\Delta E=5.874\ \mu$eV, $\lambda=21.106$ cm, $h\nu/k_B=68.2$ mK. Contact-interaction estimate $\frac43g_p\frac{m_e}{m_p}\alpha^4m_ec^2=5.877\ \mu$eV ($0.05\,\%$ off).

In the interstellar medium $k_BT\gg h\nu$: populations $3:1$ by weight (at 100 K, $2.998:1$), so emission $\propto n_{\rm H}$. Spontaneous rate $\approx2.9\times10^{-15}$ s$^{-1}$ ($\approx10^7$ yr, recalled): slow, but there is a great deal of hydrogen.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> The splitting is internal, not a Zeeman effect: it exists in zero field.

Used in: [[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §10, §13. Related: [[Singlet and Triplet States]] · [[Zeeman Effect]] · [[Two-Level System]] · [[Partition Function]].
