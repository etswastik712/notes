# Sequential Stern-Gerlach Experiments

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Sakurai §1.1.2 ([[modern-quantum-mechanics-3nbsped-9781108473224-1108473229_compress.pdf#page=24|p.4 ff.]]). 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** the general-angle formula, recombination phase, filter chain ([[QM09 The Stern-Gerlach Experiment and Spin|QM09]] §5).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Filters are questions: a question about a new axis does not add to the old answer, it **replaces** it.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $z\to z$: repeatable. $z\to x$: 50:50. $z\to x\to z$: the blocked $z-$ beam **reappears**, $\tfrac18I_0$ in each final port. At a general angle $\theta$ for the middle magnet: $P(z-)=\cos^2\frac\theta2\sin^2\frac\theta2=\tfrac14\sin^2\theta$. Recombining the middle beams with relative phase $\phi$: $P(z-)=\sin^2\frac\phi2$ (zero when untouched).

No list of definite $(S_z,S_x)$ values can explain it: after the $x+$ filter such a list would still say $z+$, observed 50:50. A chain of $N$ analysers turned by $\pi/N$ each turns $+z$ into $-z$ with probability $\cos^{2N}(\pi/2N)\to1$ ([[Quantum Zeno Effect]]). Same arithmetic as three polarisers ([[Photon Polarisation]]).

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Blocking a beam is not recombining it: which-path information (any tag orthogonal on the two arms) turns $\sin^2(\phi/2)$ into $\tfrac12$ for every $\phi$.
