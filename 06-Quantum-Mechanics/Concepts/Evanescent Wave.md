# Evanescent Wave

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §2; the optics analogue is frustrated total internal reflection.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A wave that is only present as a non-propagating tail that decays exponentially with distance from the interface.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\psi\propto e^{-\kappa x}$, real, carries no current ($\mathrm{Im}\,\psi^*\psi'=0$). In the step with $E<V_0$ the density is $\frac{4E}{V_0}e^{-2\kappa x}$.

Becomes a propagating wave again if a second medium is brought within $\sim1/\kappa$ (tunnelling).

Used in: [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §2. Related: [[Penetration Depth]] · [[Step Potential]] · [[Quantum Tunnelling]].
