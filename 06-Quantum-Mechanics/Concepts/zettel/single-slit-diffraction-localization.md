# Single-Slit Diffraction Localization

A physical mechanism confining a spatial plane wave through an aperture of width a to bound position uncertainty to Δx ≈ a. Geometrical wave diffraction across the aperture spreads transverse wavevectors, inducing a transverse momentum spread Δp_x ≈ h/a that enforces the reciprocal product Δx Δp_x ≈ h.

$$\Delta x \approx a$$

$$\sin\theta = \lambda / a$$

$$\Delta p_x \approx p \sin\theta = (h/\lambda)(\lambda/a) = h/a$$

$$\Delta x \Delta p_x \approx a \cdot (h/a) = h$$
