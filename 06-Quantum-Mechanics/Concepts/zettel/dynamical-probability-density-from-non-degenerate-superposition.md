# Dynamical Probability Density from Non-Degenerate Superposition

A superposition of states with different energy eigenvalues generates an evolving spatial probability density due to time-dependent cross terms. For a ring state composed of ground and first excited states, this manifests as a propagating cosine probability wave.

$$\Psi(x, t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2L}} [1 + e^{i(kx-\omega t)}]$$

$$\omega = \frac{E_1}{\hbar}$$

$$|\Psi(x, t)|^2 = \frac{1}{L} [1 + \cos(kx - \omega t)]$$
