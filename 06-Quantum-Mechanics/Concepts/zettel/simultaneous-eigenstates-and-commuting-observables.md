# Simultaneous Eigenstates and Commuting Observables

The condition that two observables commute ([A, B] = 0) implies the existence of a mutual eigenbasis, but does not imply that an arbitrary quantum state will have vanishing dispersion in both observables simultaneously.

$$[H, p] = 0$$

$$\Delta H \Delta p = 0 \ge \frac{1}{2} |\langle [H, p] \rangle| = 0$$

$$\Delta H = 0, \quad \Delta p = \hbar k$$
