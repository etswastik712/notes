# Compton Scattering Momentum and Energy Transfer

The relativistic collision kinematics between a photon and an electron, where conservation of total energy and vector momentum dictates that a larger deflection angle of the photon induces a greater momentum change, imparting more kinetic energy to the recoiling electron and shifting the scattered photon to a longer wavelength.

$$E_\gamma = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$$
