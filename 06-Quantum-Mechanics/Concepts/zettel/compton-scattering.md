# Compton Scattering

The scattering of high-energy electromagnetic radiation by a charged particle in which energy and vector momentum are conserved between a discrete photon and the recoiling particle, producing an angle-dependent shift to longer wavelengths.

$$\Delta\lambda = \lambda' - \lambda = \frac{h}{m_e c} (1 - \cos\theta) = \lambda_C (1 - \cos\theta)$$

$$K_e = E_\gamma - E_\gamma' = hc\left(\frac{1}{\lambda} - \frac{1}{\lambda'}\right)$$
