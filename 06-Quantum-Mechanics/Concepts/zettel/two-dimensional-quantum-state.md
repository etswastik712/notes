# Two-Dimensional Quantum State

A state of a two-dimensional quantum system represented by a two-component complex column vector normalized such that the sum of the squared moduli of its components equals one.

$$|\psi\rangle = \begin{pmatrix} a \\ b \end{pmatrix}, \quad |a|^2 + |b|^2 = 1$$
