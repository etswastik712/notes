# Free Particle Dispersion Relation in Schrödinger Theory

The specific application of the time-dependent Schrödinger equation to a vanishing potential, demonstrating that plane wave solutions satisfy the quadratic non-relativistic energy-momentum relation.

$$i\hbar \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t} = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{\partial^2 \psi}{\partial x^2}$$

$$\hbar \omega = \frac{\hbar^2 k^2}{2m}$$

$$E = \frac{p^2}{2m}$$
