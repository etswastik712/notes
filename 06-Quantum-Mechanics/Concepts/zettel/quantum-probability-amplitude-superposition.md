# Quantum Probability Amplitude Superposition

The quantum mechanical principle stating that for indistinguishable physical alternatives, probability amplitudes are added first and then squared in magnitude to yield the observable probability distribution.

$$p_{AB}(x) = |\psi_A(x) + \psi_B(x)|^2$$

$$|\psi_A + \psi_B|^2 = |\psi_A|^2 + |\psi_B|^2 + 2\operatorname{Re}(\psi_A^* \psi_B)$$
