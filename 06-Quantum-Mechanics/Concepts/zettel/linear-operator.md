# Linear Operator

A mathematical mapping acting on quantum states that satisfies linearity over linear combinations of states, associated with observable physical quantities such as position, momentum, and total energy.

$$\hat{x} = x, \quad \hat{p} = -i\hbar \frac{d}{dx}, \quad \hat{H} = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2}{dx^2} + V(x)$$

$$\hat{A}(c_1 \psi_1 + c_2 \psi_2) = c_1 \hat{A}\psi_1 + c_2 \hat{A}\psi_2$$
