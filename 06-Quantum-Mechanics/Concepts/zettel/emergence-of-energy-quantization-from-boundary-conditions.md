# Emergence of Energy Quantization from Boundary Conditions

The principle that the Schrödinger differential equation does not intrinsically enforce discrete energies; quantization arises only when global boundary conditions, single-valuedness, or square-integrability filter the continuous family of solutions.

$$e^{i 2\pi \alpha} = 1 \implies \alpha = n$$
