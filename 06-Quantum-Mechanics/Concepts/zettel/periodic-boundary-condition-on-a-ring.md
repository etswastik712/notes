# Periodic Boundary Condition on a Ring

The requirement that a wavefunction traversing a closed loop must be single-valued and join smoothly onto itself with the same value and phase after a full rotation. This restricts the allowed wavelengths to those where an integer number of full wavelengths fits the circumference, quantizing momentum.

$$L = n\lambda, \quad n \in \mathbb{Z}$$
