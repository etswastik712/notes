# Canonical Commutation Relation Position-Momentum

The fundamental commutation relation between position and momentum operators resulting from the derivative product rule acting on wavefunctions in coordinate space.

$$[\hat{x}, \hat{p}] = i\hbar$$

$$\Delta x \Delta p \ge \frac{\hbar}{2}$$
