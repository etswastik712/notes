# Fourier Band-Limited Wave Packet Localization

The superposition of plane waves with uniform weight across a finite symmetrical band [-K, K] in k-space, producing a central spatial peak governed by a sinc profile sin(Kx)/(πx). The product of the spatial peak width and wavenumber band is strictly constant (Δx Δk ≈ π), proving that narrowing spatial localization demands an equal factor expansion in wavenumber content.

$$\psi(x) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-K}^{K} e^{ikx} \, dk = \frac{\sin(Kx)}{\pi x}$$

$$\Delta x \Delta k \approx \pi$$
