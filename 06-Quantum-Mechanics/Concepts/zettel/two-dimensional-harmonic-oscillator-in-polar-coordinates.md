# Two-Dimensional Harmonic Oscillator in Polar Coordinates

A two-dimensional quantum mechanical harmonic oscillator governed by a circularly symmetric quadratic potential V(r) = (1/2)mω^2 r^2. Separation of variables yields angular momentum eigenstates Θ(θ) = e^{i l θ} and a differential equation for the radial function R(r).

$$V(x, y) = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 (x^2 + y^2)$$

$$\Theta(\theta) = e^{il\theta}$$

$$\frac{d^2 R}{dr^2} + \frac{1}{r} \frac{dR}{dr} + \left[ \frac{2m}{\hbar^2} \left( E - \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 r^2 \right) - \frac{l^2}{r^2} \right] R = 0$$
