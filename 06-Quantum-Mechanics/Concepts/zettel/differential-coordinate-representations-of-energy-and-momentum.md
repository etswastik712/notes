# Differential Coordinate Representations of Energy and Momentum

The identification of energy and momentum observables with differential operators in coordinate space, originating from the action of time and space derivatives extracting frequency and wavenumber from wave functions via the Planck-de Broglie relations.

$$E \longleftrightarrow i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t}$$

$$p \longleftrightarrow -i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial x}$$

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{\partial^2 \psi}{\partial x^2} = \frac{p^2}{2m}\psi$$
