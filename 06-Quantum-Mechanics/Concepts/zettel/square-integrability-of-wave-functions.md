# Square-Integrability of Wave Functions

The requirement that the integral of the squared absolute value of a wave function over the entire space converges to a finite value, allowing normalization to unity to represent a valid probability distribution.

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} |\psi(x, t)|^2 dx < \infty$$

$$\int |\psi|^2 dx = 1$$
