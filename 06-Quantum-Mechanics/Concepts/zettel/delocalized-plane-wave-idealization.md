# Delocalized Plane Wave Idealization

An idealized, non-square-integrable quantum state possessing a single exact wavenumber and momentum, resulting in total spatial delocalization across the real line.

$$\psi(x) = A e^{ikx}$$

$$|\psi|^2 = |A|^2$$
