# Penetration Depth

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Griffiths §2.6; QM07 §5.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> How far the wave leaks into the wall before dying to $1/e$: short for strongly bound states, long for barely bound ones.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $d_{\rm pen}=\dfrac1\kappa=\dfrac{\hbar}{\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}}$. Electron bound by 1 eV: 0.195 nm; by 0.01 eV: 1.95 nm.

The finite well behaves like a box of width $2a+2d_{\rm pen}$, which is why its energies lie below the box levels ([[Effective Well Width]]).

Used in: [[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §5, [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §2. Related: [[Evanescent Wave]] · [[Tunnelling Probability]] · [[Classically Forbidden Region]].
