# Gamow Factor

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §5 (derived; decay data recalled).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The tunnelling probability of an $\alpha$ particle through the Coulomb wall of its nucleus.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $G=\frac{\sqrt{2\mu E}}{\hbar}R_c\big[\arccos\sqrt x-\sqrt{x(1-x)}\big]$, $x=R/R_c$, $T=e^{-2G}$. $^{238}$U: $2G=88.0$, $T_{1/2}\approx4.9\times10^9$ yr (measured $4.47\times10^9$).

$2G\propto Z/\sqrt E$ explains why $T_{1/2}$ runs from $3\times10^{-7}$ s ($^{212}$Po) to $10^{17}$ s ($^{238}$U).

Used in: [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §5. Related: [[WKB Approximation]] · [[Quantum Tunnelling]].
