# Scattering State

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** the lecture's $E>V_0$ section, [[Finite_well.pdf#page=7|pp.7–13]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A wave that comes in from far away, interacts with the potential, and leaves again: it is never localised, so it cannot be normalised in the usual way.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> For $E$ above the asymptotic potential the solutions are plane waves at $\pm\infty$; the spectrum is **continuous** and each energy is allowed. They are labelled by an incident amplitude and give the **reflection** and **transmission** coefficients $R,T$ with $R+T=1$.

They are non-normalisable in $L^2$ but are $\delta$-normalisable ([[Delta-Function Normalisation]]). Wave packets built from them are normalisable and describe real scattering experiments.

Used in: [[QM07 The Finite Square Well]] §11, [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]] §1. Related: [[Bound State]] · [[Transmission Coefficient]] · [[Reflection Coefficient]] · [[Probability Current]] · [[Transmission Resonance]].
