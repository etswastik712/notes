# Radial Probability Density

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[tutorial7.pdf|Tutorial VII]] Q3; [[Hydrogen_atom.pdf#page=6|hydrogen notes eq. 33]]

> [!intuition] In one picture
> How likely the particle is at a given distance, not at a given point: the density times the size of the shell.

> [!key] Definition
> $P(r)=\int_0^{2\pi}|\psi|^2r\,d\theta$ (2D), $\int|\psi|^2r^2\sin\theta\,d\theta d\varphi$ (3D); for angle-independent $\psi$: $2\pi r|\psi|^2$, $4\pi r^2|\psi|^2$; with normalised $Y_{lm}$, $P=r^2R_{nl}^2$. Hydrogen 1s: peak at $r=a$, $\langle r\rangle=1.5a$; 2D oscillator ground state: peak at $1/\sqrt2$ oscillator lengths.

Used in: [[QM11 Two-Dimensional Problems Angular Momentum and the Circular Well]], [[QM12 The Hydrogen Atom]]. Related: [[Born Rule]] · [[Normalisation of a Wavefunction]] · [[Laplacian in Polar and Spherical Coordinates]].
