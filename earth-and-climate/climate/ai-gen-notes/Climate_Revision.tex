\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=NavyBlue!4,colframe=NavyBlue!70!black,title={How to use these notes}]
Written in the same way as the eco-evo notes: for MCQs that test \emph{reasoning} (``which statement follows'',
``A because B'') and for long written answers. Each section is an argument rather than a list:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Core idea} boxes give the one thing to understand;
\item \textbf{Reasoning chain} boxes go from assumptions to result, saying why each step follows;
\item \textbf{Statement check} boxes list plausible claims with a verdict and the reason (cover the verdict and decide first);
\item \textbf{Trap} boxes name the slips that distractors are built from;
\item \textbf{Long-answer skeleton} boxes give the structure of a full answer;
\item \textbf{Numbers and facts} boxes hold the values worth memorising.
\end{itemize}
Section~\ref{sec:toolkit} lists the reasoning moves that recur across the course. Section~\ref{sec:examples} catalogues every
example, data set and case (where it comes from, what is seen, what it shows). The glossary and the equation sheet follow
(Sections~\ref{sec:glossary} and~\ref{sec:equations}), then a mixed drill.

\textbf{Scope.} Instructors this year: Argha (L1--L6), Suhas (pre- and post-midsem atmosphere), Joy (the ocean, O1--O7 and
tutorials). Last year's course (Neena) is included where it adds material (Part V). Classroom, 19 Nov: ``the syllabus will be
everything. However, the exam will likely test your grasp of the post mid-sem material in greater depth.''
\end{tcolorbox}
