# Advanced Group Theory

> [!summary] What this folder is
> The parts of Kurzweil and Stellmacher chapter 6 that go beyond a first course: **§6.3–6.4 radical, residue and $\pi$-separable groups (Hall subgroups)**, **§6.5 components and the generalised Fitting subgroup**, **§6.6 primitive maximal subgroups**. They are written as **guides**: definitions, statements, worked examples (all computed) and the short proofs that are within reach; the long proofs are referenced. None of the main chapters ([[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]]–[[GT10 Symmetry Representations and Characters]]) depends on this folder. *K–S chapter 6 was not read in detail; the titles come from its table of contents (PDF pp.141–158).*
