# Composition Series

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT04 Commutators Simple Groups and Composition Series]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A chain $1=G_0<\dots<G_r=G$ with each term normal in the next and simple quotients.

> [!key] Key facts
> Exists for every finite group (take a normal subgroup of maximal order); Jordan–Hölder: factors are unique. $S_4$: factors $C_2,C_2,C_3,C_2$.
