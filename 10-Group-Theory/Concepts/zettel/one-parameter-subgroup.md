# One-Parameter Subgroup

A one-parameter subgroup of a Lie group G is a Lie group homomorphism from (\mathbb{R}, +) into G. These correspond precisely to the maximal integral curves of left-invariant vector fields passing through the identity at time zero.

$$\gamma: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow G$$
