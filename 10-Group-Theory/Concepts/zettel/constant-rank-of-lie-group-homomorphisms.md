# Constant Rank of Lie Group Homomorphisms

Every smooth Lie group homomorphism has constant rank throughout its domain, equalling the rank of its differential at the identity element.

$$\operatorname{rank}(dF_g) = \operatorname{rank}(dF_e)$$
