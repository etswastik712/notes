# Locality of Derivations

A tangent vector at a point p on a smooth manifold acts locally as a derivation, meaning its value on a smooth function depends only on the germ of the function at p. If two smooth functions agree on an open neighbourhood of p, any tangent vector evaluates to the same value on both.

$$v(f) = v(g)$$
