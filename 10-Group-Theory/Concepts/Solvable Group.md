# Solvable Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A group whose derived series reaches 1; equivalently all composition factors have prime order.

> [!key] Key facts
> Closed under subgroups, quotients and extensions. $S_4$ solvable (derived length 3), $A_5$ not. Burnside $p^aq^b$, Feit–Thompson (odd order).
