# Lagrange's Theorem

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The order of a subgroup divides the order of the group: $|G|=|U||G:U|$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Consequences: $o(g)\mid|G|$, $g^{|G|}=1$ (Fermat, Euler), prime order $\Rightarrow$ cyclic.
> - The converse is false: $A_4$ has no subgroup of order 6.
> - Converse holds for prime-power orders (Sylow).

Used in: [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]]. Related: [[Coset]] · [[Sylow Theorems]].
