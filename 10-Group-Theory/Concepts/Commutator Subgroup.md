# Commutator Subgroup

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $G'=\langle x^{-1}y^{-1}xy\rangle$; $G/G'$ is the largest Abelian quotient.

> [!key] Key facts
> - $S_n'=A_n$ (checked for $n=3,4$).
> - Perfect group: $G=G'$ (e.g. $A_n$, $n\ge5$).

Used in: [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]]. Related: [[Quotient Group]] · [[Simple Group]].
