# Subgroup

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A subset that is itself a group; a nonempty finite subset closed under the product is a subgroup.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Finite one-step test (K–S 1.1.3).
> - $\langle X\rangle$ = smallest subgroup containing $X$.
> - $AB\le G$ iff $AB=BA$; $|AB|=|A||B|/|A\cap B|$.

Used in: [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]]. Related: [[Coset]] · [[Lagrange's Theorem]].
