# Isomorphism Theorems

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $G/\operatorname{Ker}\varphi\cong\operatorname{Im}\varphi$; $UN/N\cong U/(U\cap N)$; $(G/N)/(M/N)\cong G/M$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Proofs: apply the homomorphism theorem to $U\to G/N$ and $G/N\to G/M$.
> - Counting form: $|G|=|\operatorname{Ker}||\operatorname{Im}|$.

Used in: [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]]. Related: [[Quotient Group]] · [[Homomorphism]].
