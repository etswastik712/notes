# Orbit-Stabiliser Theorem

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT03 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $|\omega^G|=|G:G_\omega|$, so $|G|=|\text{orbit}|\cdot|\text{stabiliser}|$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Proof: $G_\omega g\mapsto\omega^g$ is a bijection onto the orbit.
> - Example: $D_4$ on square vertices: $8=4\cdot2$.

Used in: [[GT03 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]]. Related: [[Group Action]] · [[Class Equation]].
