# Burnside Fusion Lemma

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT09 Transfer and Normal p-Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> If $P$ is an Abelian Sylow $p$-subgroup and $x,y\in P$ are conjugate in $G$, they are conjugate in $N_G(P)$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Proof: $P$ and $P^g$ are Sylow in $C_G(y)$, hence conjugate there.


Used in: [[GT09 Transfer and Normal p-Complements]]. Related: [[Burnside Normal p-Complement Theorem]].
