# Subnormal Subgroup

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A subgroup reached by a chain of subgroups each normal in the next.

> [!key] Key facts
> Intersections of subnormal subgroups are subnormal; in nilpotent groups every subgroup is subnormal; $\langle(12)(34)\rangle$ is subnormal in $S_4$, $\langle(12)\rangle$ is not in $S_3$.
