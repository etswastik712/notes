# Automorphism Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT03 Cyclic and Abelian Groups and Direct Products]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The group of isomorphisms $G\to G$ under composition; inner automorphisms (conjugations) form $\operatorname{Inn}G\cong G/Z(G)$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\operatorname{Aut}(C_n)\cong(\mathbb Z/n)^\times$; $\operatorname{Aut}(C_2\times C_2)\cong S_3$; a semidirect product $N\rtimes_\varphi H$ needs a homomorphism $H\to\operatorname{Aut}N$.
