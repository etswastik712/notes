# Burnside's Lemma

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The number of orbits is the average number of fixed points: $\frac1{|G|}\sum_g|\mathrm{Fix}(g)|$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Necklaces: 4 beads, 2 colours: 6; 3 colours: 24; 6 beads, 2 colours: 14.
> - Proof: count pairs $(g,\omega)$ with $\omega^g=\omega$ two ways.

Used in: [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]]. Related: [[Group Action]] · [[Orbit-Stabiliser Theorem]].
