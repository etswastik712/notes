# Minimal Normal Subgroup

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT04 Commutators Simple Groups and Composition Series]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A non-trivial normal subgroup with no smaller non-trivial normal subgroup inside.

> [!key] Key facts
> Abelian case: elementary Abelian $p$-group (the elements of order $p$ form a characteristic subgroup); non-Abelian case: a direct power of a simple group. $S_4$: $V_4$; $D_4$: $Z(D_4)$.

## In this vault
Used in the Hall and Schur–Zassenhaus inductions.
