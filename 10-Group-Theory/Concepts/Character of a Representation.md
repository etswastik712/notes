# Character of a Representation

> [!info] Source
> written up in [[GT10 Symmetry Representations and Characters]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $\chi(g)=\operatorname{tr}\rho(g)$: a class function with $\chi(1)=\dim V$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - For permutation-like bases, $\chi$ = number of basis functions left unmoved.
> - Characters determine the representation.
