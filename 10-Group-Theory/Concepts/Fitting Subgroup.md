# Fitting Subgroup

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The largest nilpotent normal subgroup; $F(G)=\prod_pO_p(G)$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $F(S_4)=V_4$, $F(S_3)=A_3$, $F(A_5)=1$. For solvable $G$: $C_G(F)\le F$.


Used in: [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]]. Related: [[Nilpotent Group]] · [[Generalised Fitting Subgroup]] · [[Radical of a Group]].
