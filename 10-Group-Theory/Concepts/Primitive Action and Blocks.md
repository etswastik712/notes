# Primitive Action and Blocks

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT06 Permutation Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A transitive action is primitive if it preserves no non-trivial partition (blocks) of the set.

> [!key] Key facts
> Primitive $\iff$ point stabiliser maximal. $D_4$ on the square's vertices is imprimitive (diagonals), $S_4$ on 4 points is primitive.
