# Representation of a Group

> [!info] Source
> written up in [[GT10 Symmetry Representations and Characters]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A homomorphism $\rho:G\to GL(V)$; reducible if there is an invariant subspace.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Maschke: representations of finite groups decompose into irreps.
> - Basis functions (orbitals, displacements) give representations.
