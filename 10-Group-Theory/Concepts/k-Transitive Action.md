# k-Transitive Action

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT06 Permutation Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Any $k$ distinct points can be sent to any other $k$ distinct points.

> [!key] Key facts
> $n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1)$ divides $|G|$; 2-transitive $\Rightarrow$ primitive; $A_5$ is sharply 3-transitive on 5 points.
