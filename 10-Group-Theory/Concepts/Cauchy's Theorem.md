# Cauchy's Theorem

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> If a prime $p$ divides $|G|$, then $G$ has an element of order $p$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - McKay's proof: $\mathbb Z_p$ acts on $p$-tuples with product 1 by shifts.
> - First step towards Sylow.

Used in: [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]]. Related: [[Sylow Theorems]] · [[Group Action]].
