# Direct Product of Groups

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $G\times H$ with componentwise product; internal form $G=A\times B$ with $A,B$ normal, $A\cap B=1$, $AB=G$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Order $|G||H|$.
> - $C_m\times C_n\cong C_{mn}$ iff coprime.
> - $D_6\cong D_3\times\mathbb Z_2$; $D_4$ is not a product.

Used in: [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]]. Related: [[Cyclic Group]] · [[Normal Subgroup]].
