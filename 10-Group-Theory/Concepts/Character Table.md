# Character Table

> [!info] Source
> written up in [[GT10 Symmetry Representations and Characters]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Rows = irreps, columns = classes, entries = characters; rows and columns are orthogonal.

> [!key] Key facts
> - $C_{3v}$: $A_1=(1,1,1)$, $A_2=(1,1,-1)$, $E=(2,-1,0)$ over $(E,2C_3,3\sigma_v)$.
> - Right-hand columns list $x,y,z$ and quadratics: IR and Raman selection rules.

Used in: [[GT10 Symmetry Representations and Characters]]. Related: [[Reduction Formula]] · [[Point Group]].
