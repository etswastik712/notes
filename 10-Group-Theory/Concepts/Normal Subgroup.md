# Normal Subgroup

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A subgroup stable under conjugation: $xNx^{-1}=N$; exactly the kernels of homomorphisms.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Equivalent: $xN=Nx$.
> - Index-2 subgroups are normal; the centre and $G'$ are normal.
> - Normal is not transitive ($\langle(12)(34)\rangle\trianglelefteq V_4\trianglelefteq S_4$).

Used in: [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]]. Related: [[Quotient Group]] · [[Simple Group]].
