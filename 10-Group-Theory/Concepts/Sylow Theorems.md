# Sylow Theorems

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Existence, conjugacy and counting ($n_p\equiv1\bmod p$, $n_p\mid m$) of subgroups of order $p^e$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - $n_p=|G:N_G(P)|$.
> - Order 15 cyclic; order 12 has a normal Sylow subgroup; order 56 not simple.
> - $S_4$: $n_2=3,n_3=4$; $A_5$: $n_2=5,n_3=10,n_5=6$.

Used in: [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]]. Related: [[Cauchy's Theorem]] · [[Lagrange's Theorem]].
