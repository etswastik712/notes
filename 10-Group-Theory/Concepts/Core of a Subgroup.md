# Core of a Subgroup

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT-A3 Primitive Maximal Subgroups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $M_G=\bigcap g^{-1}Mg$: the largest normal subgroup of $G$ inside $M$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Kernel of the action of $G$ on the cosets of $M$; maximal subgroups of $S_4$ have cores $1,V_4,A_4$.


Used in: [[GT-A3 Primitive Maximal Subgroups]]. Related: [[Primitive Action and Blocks]] · [[Affine Group]].
