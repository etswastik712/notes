# Quaternion Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $Q_8=\{\pm1,\pm i,\pm j,\pm k\}$ with $i^2=j^2=k^2=ijk=-1$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Non-Abelian of order 8, a unique element of order 2, six of order 4; class 2; centre $\{\pm1\}$, $Q_8/Z\cong V_4$. Not a semidirect product over any proper normal subgroup.

## In this vault
Unit quaternions and spin: $Q_8\subset SU(2)$ (see [[Heisenberg Group]] for another class-2 group).

Used in: [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]]. Related: [[Nilpotent Group]] · [[Dihedral Group]].
