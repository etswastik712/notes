# Radical of a Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT-A1 Radical Residue and Hall Subgroups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The largest normal $\pi$-subgroup $O_\pi(G)$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $O_p(G)=\bigcap$ Sylow $p$-subgroups: $O_2(S_4)=V_4$, $O_3(S_4)=1$; $F(G)=\prod O_p(G)$.


Used in: [[GT-A1 Radical Residue and Hall Subgroups]]. Related: [[Hall Subgroup]] · [[Fitting Subgroup]].
