# Semidirect Product

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $N\rtimes_\varphi H$: pairs with $(n_1,h_1)(n_2,h_2)=(n_1\varphi(h_1)(n_2),h_1h_2)$, $\varphi:H\to\operatorname{Aut}N$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Recognition: $N\trianglelefteq G$, $H$ a complement $\Rightarrow G\cong N\rtimes H$. $D_n=C_n\rtimes C_2$, $S_n=A_n\rtimes C_2$, $A_4=V_4\rtimes C_3$, $S_4=V_4\rtimes S_3$.

## In this vault
Space groups of crystals $\mathbb Z^3\rtimes P$; Euclidean group of motions ([[Euclidean Group]]); the Lie-group version is [[Semidirect Product of Lie Groups]] ($O(n)=SO(n)\rtimes O(1)$, $GL(n,\mathbb C)=SL(n,\mathbb C)\rtimes\mathbb C^\times$).

Used in: [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]]. Related: [[Complement of a Normal Subgroup]] · [[Automorphism Group]] · [[Frobenius Group]].
