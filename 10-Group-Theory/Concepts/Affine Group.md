# Affine Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT-A3 Primitive Maximal Subgroups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $V\rtimes GL(V)$ acting on the vector space $V$: translations and linear maps.

> [!key] Key facts
> $AGL(1,5)=F_{20}$; solvable primitive groups are affine ($V\rtimes H$, $H$ irreducible on $V$); crystal space groups $\mathbb Z^3\rtimes P$.


Used in: [[GT-A3 Primitive Maximal Subgroups]]. Related: [[Frobenius Group]] · [[Core of a Subgroup]] · [[Semidirect Product]].
