# Jordan-Holder Theorem

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT04 Commutators Simple Groups and Composition Series]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Any two composition series of a finite group have the same length and the same factors up to isomorphism and order.

> [!key] Key facts
> Proof by induction via the 'diamond': $G/A\cong B/(A\cap B)$, $G/B\cong A/(A\cap B)$. Same atoms do not mean the same group ($C_6$ versus $S_3$).
