# Reduction Formula

> [!info] Source
> written up in [[GT10 Symmetry Representations and Characters]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $n_i=\frac1{|G|}\sum_C|C|\chi_i(C)^*\chi(C)$: how many copies of $\Gamma_i$ a representation contains.

> [!key] Key facts
> - NH$_3$ H 1s: $A_1+E$; $\Gamma_{vib}=2A_1+2E$.
> - d orbitals in $O$: $E+T_2$; octahedral $\sigma$ set: $A_1+E+T_1$.
