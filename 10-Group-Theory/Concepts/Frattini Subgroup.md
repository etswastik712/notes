# Frattini Subgroup

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The intersection of all maximal subgroups; the set of non-generators.

> [!key] Key facts
> $G=\langle X,\Phi\rangle\Rightarrow G=\langle X\rangle$. For a $p$-group $P/\Phi(P)$ is elementary Abelian (Burnside basis theorem). $\Phi(D_4)=\langle r^2\rangle$.


Used in: [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]]. Related: [[Burnside Basis Theorem]] · [[Nilpotent Group]].
