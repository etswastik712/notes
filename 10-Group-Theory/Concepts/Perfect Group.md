# Perfect Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT04 Commutators Simple Groups and Composition Series]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A group equal to its own commutator subgroup: no non-trivial Abelian quotient.

> [!key] Key facts
> $A_n$ ($n\ge5$) and $SL_n$ (mostly) are perfect; a perfect group is never solvable (unless trivial); the derived series of $A_5$ is stuck at $A_5$.
