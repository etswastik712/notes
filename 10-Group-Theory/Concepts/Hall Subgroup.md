# Hall Subgroup

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT-A1 Radical Residue and Hall Subgroups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A subgroup whose order is exactly the $\pi$-part of $|G|$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Solvable groups have Hall $\pi$-subgroups for all $\pi$ (all conjugate); conversely if all exist the group is solvable. $A_5$ has a Hall $\{2,3\}$ ($A_4$) but no Hall $\{2,5\}$ or $\{3,5\}$.


Used in: [[GT-A1 Radical Residue and Hall Subgroups]]. Related: [[Radical of a Group]] · [[Solvable Group]].
