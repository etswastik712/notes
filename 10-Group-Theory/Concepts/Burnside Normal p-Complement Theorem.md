# Burnside Normal p-Complement Theorem

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT09 Transfer and Normal p-Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> If $P\le Z(N_G(P))$ for a Sylow $p$-subgroup $P$, then $G$ has a normal $p$-complement.

> [!key] Key facts
> Proof: transfer into $P$, conjugate elements of $P$ are equal, so $V(x)=x^{|G:P|}$ is a bijection of $P$; $\ker V$ is the complement.


Used in: [[GT09 Transfer and Normal p-Complements]]. Related: [[Burnside Fusion Lemma]] · [[Transfer Homomorphism]].
