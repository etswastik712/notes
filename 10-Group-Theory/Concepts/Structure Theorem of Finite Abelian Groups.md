# Structure Theorem of Finite Abelian Groups

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT03 Cyclic and Abelian Groups and Direct Products]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Every finite Abelian group is a product of cyclic groups of prime-power order, uniquely.

> [!key] Key facts
> $G\cong\prod C_{p_i^{e_i}}$; proof: split into $p$-parts, then a cyclic subgroup of maximal order has a complement; uniqueness by counting solutions of $x^{p^k}=1$. Number of Abelian groups of order $\prod p_i^{a_i}$ is $\prod p(a_i)$ (order 72: 6).
