# Burnside Basis Theorem

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A minimal generating set of a $p$-group has exactly $d=\log_p|P/\Phi(P)|$ elements.

> [!key] Key facts
> $D_4$ needs 2 generators; $C_2^3$ needs 3; $Q_8$ needs 2.


Used in: [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]]. Related: [[Frattini Subgroup]].
