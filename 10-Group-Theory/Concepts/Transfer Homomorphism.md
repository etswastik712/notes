# Transfer Homomorphism

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT09 Transfer and Normal p-Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $V:G\to H/H'$, $V(g)=\prod h_i(g)H'$, built from how $g$ permutes the cosets of $H$ and the $H$-twists.

> [!key] Key facts
> Well defined and a homomorphism; cycle formula $V(g)=\prod t g^f t^{-1}$; $S_3\to C_2$ is the sign; $A_4\to V_4$ is trivial.


Used in: [[GT09 Transfer and Normal p-Complements]]. Related: [[Normal p-Complement]] · [[Burnside Normal p-Complement Theorem]].
