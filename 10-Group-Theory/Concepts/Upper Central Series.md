# Upper Central Series

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $1=Z_0\le Z(G)=Z_1\le Z_2\le\cdots$ with $Z_{i+1}/Z_i=Z(G/Z_i)$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Reaches $G$ iff $G$ is nilpotent; $D_4$: orders $1,2,8$; $S_3$ stuck at 1.


Used in: [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]]. Related: [[Nilpotent Group]].
