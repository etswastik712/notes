# Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A set with an associative product, an identity and inverses; the algebra of symmetries.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Axioms: associativity, left identity, left inverse; these imply a two-sided identity and inverse, both unique, plus cancellation and $(xy)^{-1}=y^{-1}x^{-1}$.
> - Order $|G|$; element order $o(g)$.
> - Examples: $\mathbb Z_n$, $S_n$, $D_n$, $GL_n$.

Used in: [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]]. Related: [[Subgroup]] · [[Cyclic Group]] · [[Symmetric Group]].
