# Simple Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A non-trivial group whose only normal subgroups are $1$ and itself.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Abelian simple groups are exactly $C_p$.
> - $A_n$ is simple for $n\ge5$; $A_5$ (order 60) is the smallest non-Abelian one.
> - Composition factors of any finite group are simple (Jordan–Hölder).

Used in: [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]]. Related: [[Alternating Group]] · [[Normal Subgroup]].
