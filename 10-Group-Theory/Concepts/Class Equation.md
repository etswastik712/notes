# Class Equation

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $|G|=|Z(G)|+\sum|G:C_G(x_i)|$ over non-central classes.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Gives $Z(P)\ne1$ for $p$-groups, so order $p^2$ is Abelian.
> - Used in the induction proof of Sylow existence.

Used in: [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]]. Related: [[Conjugacy Class]] · [[Sylow Theorems]].
