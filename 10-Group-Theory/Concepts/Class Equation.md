# Class Equation

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT03 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $|G|=|Z(G)|+\sum|G:C_G(x_i)|$ over non-central classes.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Gives $Z(P)\ne1$ for $p$-groups, so order $p^2$ is Abelian.
> - Used in the induction proof of Sylow existence.

Used in: [[GT03 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]]. Related: [[Conjugacy Class]] · [[Sylow Theorems]].
