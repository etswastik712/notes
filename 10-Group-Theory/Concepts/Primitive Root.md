# Primitive Root

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT03 Cyclic and Abelian Groups and Direct Products]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A generator of the cyclic group $(\mathbb Z/p)^\times$ of order $p-1$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $(\mathbb Z/p)^\times$ is cyclic: elements of order $d$ number $0$ or $\varphi(d)$ and $\sum\varphi(d)=p-1$. Example: $2$ mod 11. $(\mathbb Z/n)^\times$ is not cyclic for $n=8,12$.

## In this vault
Discrete logarithms, pseudo-random generators.
