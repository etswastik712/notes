# Quasisimple Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT-A2 Components and the Generalised Fitting Subgroup]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A perfect group $L$ with $L/Z(L)$ simple.

> [!key] Key facts
> $A_5$, $SL_2(5)$; components are subnormal quasisimple subgroups; layer $E(G)$ is their product.


Used in: [[GT-A2 Components and the Generalised Fitting Subgroup]]. Related: [[Generalised Fitting Subgroup]] · [[Simple Group]].
