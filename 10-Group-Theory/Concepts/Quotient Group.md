# Quotient Group

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The cosets of a normal subgroup with product $(xN)(yN)=xyN$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Well defined exactly when $N$ is normal.
> - $S_4/V_4\cong S_3$ (6 cosets of 4).
> - Subgroups of $G/N$ correspond to subgroups containing $N$.

Used in: [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]]. Related: [[Isomorphism Theorems]] · [[Normal Subgroup]].
