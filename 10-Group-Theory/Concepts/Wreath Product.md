# Wreath Product

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT06 Permutation Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $H\wr K=H^m\rtimes K$: independent copies of $H$ on $m$ blocks, with $K$ permuting the blocks.

> [!key] Key facts
> Order $|H|^m|K|$; $D_4=C_2\wr C_2$; stabiliser of a block partition in $S_{rm}$ is $S_r\wr S_m$; Sylow $p$ of $S_{p^2}$ is $C_p\wr C_p$; Rubik's cube lives in $(C_3\wr S_8)\times(C_2\wr S_{12})$.


Used in: [[GT06 Permutation Groups]]. Related: [[Primitive Action and Blocks]] · [[Semidirect Product]].
