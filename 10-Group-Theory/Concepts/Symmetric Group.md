# Symmetric Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> All permutations of $n$ points; conjugacy classes = cycle types.

> [!key] Key facts
> - $|S_n|=n!$; Abelian only for $n\le2$.
> - Sign homomorphism with kernel $A_n$.
> - Cayley: every finite group embeds in some $S_n$.

Used in: [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]]. Related: [[Alternating Group]] · [[Conjugacy Class]].
