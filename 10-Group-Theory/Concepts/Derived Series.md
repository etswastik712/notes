# Derived Series

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $G\ge G'\ge G''\ge\cdots$, each the commutator subgroup of the previous.

> [!key] Key facts
> Reaches 1 iff $G$ is solvable; $S_4>A_4>V_4>1$; stuck at $A_5$ for $A_5$.


Used in: [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]]. Related: [[Commutator Subgroup]] · [[Solvable Group]].
