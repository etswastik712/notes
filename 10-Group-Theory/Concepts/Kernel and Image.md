# Kernel and Image

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $\operatorname{Ker}\varphi=\varphi^{-1}(1)$ is a normal subgroup; $\operatorname{Im}\varphi\le H$; $|G|=|\operatorname{Ker}||\operatorname{Im}|$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - $\varphi$ injective iff kernel trivial.
> - Every normal subgroup is a kernel (of $G\to G/N$).

Used in: [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]]. Related: [[Homomorphism]] · [[Normal Subgroup]].
