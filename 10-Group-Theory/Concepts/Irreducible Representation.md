# Irreducible Representation

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond:** written up in [[GT04 Symmetry Representations and Characters]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A representation with no proper invariant subspace; labels $A,B$ (1-dim), $E$ (2-dim), $T$ (3-dim).

> [!key] Key facts
> - #irreps = #classes; $\sum(\dim)^2=|G|$.
> - $C_{3v}$: $A_1,A_2,E$ ($1+1+4=6$); $O$: $A_1,A_2,E,T_1,T_2$ ($1+1+4+9+9=24$).
