# Nilpotent Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A group whose upper central series reaches $G$; for finite groups, the direct product of its Sylow subgroups.

> [!key] Key facts
> Equivalent: normalisers grow; maximal subgroups normal; Sylow subgroups normal. $D_4$ class 2, $D_8$ class 3; $S_3,A_4,D_6$ not nilpotent.
