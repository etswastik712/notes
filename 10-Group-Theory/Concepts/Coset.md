# Coset

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Translate a subgroup: $gU$; cosets partition the group into equal pieces.

> [!key] Key facts
> - $gU=g'U$ iff $g^{-1}g'\in U$.
> - All cosets have $|U|$ elements; number of cosets = index $|G:U|$.
> - Normal subgroup iff left cosets = right cosets.

Used in: [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]]. Related: [[Lagrange's Theorem]] · [[Normal Subgroup]].
