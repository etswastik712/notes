# Roots of Unity

> [!info] Source
> [[Assignment-10.pdf]] Problem 1 (statement); written up in [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]] (Practice 7–8).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The $n$ corners of a regular $n$-gon inscribed in the unit circle, with $1$ as one corner: multiplying two corners adds their angles, so you land on another corner.

> [!key] Key facts
> - $\mu_n=\{z\in\mathbb C:z^n=1\}=\{e^{2\pi ik/n}:k=0,\dots,n-1\}$, so $|\mu_n|=n$.
> - $(\mu_n,\cdot)$ is an **abelian group**: $(zw)^n=z^nw^n=1$, $z^{-1}=\bar z$, and commutativity and associativity come from $\mathbb C$.
> - It is **cyclic**, generated by $e^{2\pi i/n}$, so $\mu_n\cong\mathbb Z_n$ ($\mu_4=\langle i\rangle$, $o(i)=4$).
> - $\mu_n\subset S^1=\{|z|=1\}$; $S^1$ is infinite, so it is not any $\mu_n$.
