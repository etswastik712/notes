# Complement of a Normal Subgroup

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A subgroup $H$ with $NH=G$ and $N\cap H=1$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Not every normal subgroup has one ($C_2\le C_4$, $C_4\le Q_8$); exists when $\gcd(|N|,|G:N|)=1$ (Schur–Zassenhaus).


Used in: [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]]. Related: [[Semidirect Product]] · [[Schur-Zassenhaus Theorem]].
