# Heisenberg Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT07 p-Groups and Nilpotent Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The group generated by displacements in $x$ and $p$ with the central commutator $[x,p]=i\hbar$: nilpotent of class 2.

> [!key] Key facts
> Over $\mathbb F_p$: order $p^3$, one of the five groups of order $p^3$ ($D_4$ for $p=2$). BCH series terminates after the commutator.
