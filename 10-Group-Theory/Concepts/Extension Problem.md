# Extension Problem

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Listing all groups $G$ with a given normal subgroup $N$ and quotient $G/N$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Split extensions are semidirect products; non-split: $C_4$ and $Q_8$. $C_6$ and $S_3$ are the two ways of gluing $C_3$ and $C_2$.


Used in: [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]]. Related: [[Semidirect Product]] · [[Jordan-Holder Theorem]].
