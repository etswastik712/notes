# Cyclic Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A group generated by one element; $C_n\cong\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - One subgroup for each divisor of $n$; $\varphi(n)$ generators.
> - $C_m\times C_n\cong C_{mn}$ iff $\gcd(m,n)=1$.
> - Only Abelian simple groups are $C_p$.

Used in: [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]]. Related: [[Group]] · [[Simple Group]].
