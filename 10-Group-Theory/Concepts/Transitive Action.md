# Transitive Action

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT06 Permutation Groups]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> An action with a single orbit; regular if the point stabiliser is trivial.

> [!key] Key facts
> $|G|=n\,|G_\alpha|$; $S_n$ is $n$-transitive, $A_n$ is $(n-2)$-transitive.

## In this vault
Equivalent atoms of a molecule are one orbit of the point group.

Used in: [[GT06 Permutation Groups]]. Related: [[k-Transitive Action]] · [[Primitive Action and Blocks]].
