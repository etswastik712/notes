# Group Action

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A homomorphism $G\to S_\Omega$: each element permutes a set.

> [!key] Key facts
> - Orbits partition $\Omega$; stabilisers $G_\omega$.
> - Regular, coset and conjugation actions are the standard three.
> - Kernel of the action $=\bigcap G_\omega$.

Used in: [[GT05 Actions Conjugation and Sylow Theorems]]. Related: [[Orbit-Stabiliser Theorem]] · [[Conjugacy Class]].
