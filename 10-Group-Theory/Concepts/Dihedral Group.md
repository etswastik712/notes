# Dihedral Group

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The symmetries of a regular $n$-gon: $n$ rotations and $n$ reflections, order $2n$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - $r^n=s^2=1$, $srs=r^{-1}$.
> - $D_3\cong S_3$; $D_4$ has 10 subgroups and centre $\langle r^2\rangle$.
> - Point groups $C_{nv}$ and $D_n$ are built from it.

Used in: [[GT01 Groups Subgroups and Cosets]]. Related: [[Symmetric Group]] · [[Point Group]].
