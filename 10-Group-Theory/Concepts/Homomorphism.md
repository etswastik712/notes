# Homomorphism

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A map preserving the product, $\varphi(xy)=\varphi(x)\varphi(y)$.

> [!key] Key facts
> - $\varphi(1)=1$, $\varphi(x^{-1})=\varphi(x)^{-1}$.
> - Fibres are cosets of the kernel.
> - Examples: det, sign, reduction mod $n$, exp.

Used in: [[GT02 Homomorphisms Normal Subgroups and Quotients]]. Related: [[Kernel and Image]] · [[Quotient Group]].
