# Generalised Fitting Subgroup

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT-A2 Components and the Generalised Fitting Subgroup]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $F^*(G)=F(G)E(G)$ with $C_G(F^*)\le F^*$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $F^*(S_5)=A_5$, $F^*(S_4)=V_4$; proved for solvable $G$: $C_G(F)\le F$.


Used in: [[GT-A2 Components and the Generalised Fitting Subgroup]]. Related: [[Fitting Subgroup]] · [[Quasisimple Group]].
