# Schur-Zassenhaus Theorem

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> If $\gcd(|N|,|G:N|)=1$ then $N\trianglelefteq G$ has a complement (unique up to conjugacy).

> [!key] Key facts
> Proof for Abelian $N$: $N$ acts regularly on classes of transversals via $d(T,T')$ and $n\mapsto n^m$ is bijective; general case by Sylow/Frattini reduction. $A_4\supset V_4$ has 4 complements.


Used in: [[GT08 Solvable Groups and Complements]]. Related: [[Complement of a Normal Subgroup]] · [[Semidirect Product]].
