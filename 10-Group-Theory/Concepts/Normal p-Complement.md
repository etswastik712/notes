# Normal p-Complement

> [!info] Source
> Kurzweil–Stellmacher; written up in [[GT09 Transfer and Normal p-Complements]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A normal subgroup $K$ with $G=K\rtimes P$, $P$ a Sylow $p$-subgroup.

> [!key] Key facts
> Exists for cyclic Sylow $p$ at the smallest prime $p$; Cayley: cyclic Sylow 2 gives an odd-order normal subgroup.


Used in: [[GT09 Transfer and Normal p-Complements]]. Related: [[Burnside Normal p-Complement Theorem]] · [[Transfer Homomorphism]].
