# Project groups and brief (2026-08-14)

## Covered
- Course project done in groups of four, with at most two auditing students per group.
- Group details go into a shared sheet by the end of the following week. The problem column may stay empty if there is no problem in mind yet.
- Discussion of potential problems is encouraged with the teacher, anyone else, and group members. Time is set aside in the next tutorial, with group presentations in the tutorial after that. At least two rounds of presentations are planned to sharpen the problems (these became the 5 and 6 Oct presentations, see [[maps/courses/landscape-evolution]]).
- What makes a good project: look at a piece of landscape and ask one interesting question that can be addressed by (A) freely available data (satellite data, DEM, ...) or by generating your own data, (B) some simple model runs, or (C) both.
- Your group's row in the sheet (as of the export) listed no problem in the problem column; the sheet's other rows are not copied here.
