# Field trip on 7 Nov: safety protocols and declaration

## Covered
Logistics, not lecture material. The IISER Pune "Safety Protocols, Itinerary & Indemnity Document" (applies to all field trips) says:
- Participants must carry: IISER ID card plus a government-issued identity proof, sufficient water, proper clothing and footwear (full pants, shoes, a change of clothes if needed).
- Participants must: stay with the group, know where first-aid kits and contact persons are, tell the nearest coordinator at once if any issue arises. A mandatory safety briefing is held just before bus departure.
- Take part only if physically and mentally fit and the parent/guardian has been informed.
- Students with asthma, allergies, epilepsy or other conditions tell the coordinators confidentially and carry their own medication.
- Head counts: at the start, on reaching the destination, at major movement points, before returning, and finally before the bus leaves for campus.
- Safety tips: closed shoes with good grip (no slippers); careful on rocky or slippery surfaces; no cliff climbing, risky selfies or standing near edges; stay hydrated; carry all waste back; respect environment and heritage sites; follow the coordinator at all times.
- Fire and environment: no flammable items (lighters, matchboxes, aerosols, liquid fuel); no fires near dry grass or forest; avoid isolated trees and water bodies in thunderstorms; no feeding wildlife; stay on marked paths; no pranks with safety equipment.
- Heat: carry ORS, wear a cap or hat; suspected heat stroke means shade, ORS, wet cloth, call 112 if serious.
- Emergency chain: on-site authority, then 100/101/108/112, then IISER Security Supervisor or Wellness Clinic, then the faculty coordinator, then an email incident report. The sheet ends with a page of IISER and nearby hospital emergency numbers (in the PDF).
- The last pages are an Indemnity & Declaration form: date, name, roll number, own and guardian contact, blood group, then a declaration (own responsibility for safety, guardian informed, emergency contact given, club/IISER not liable, fit to join, will report emergencies or feeling unwell, will follow coordinators), and a signature.
