# Linearity of Expectation

The expectation of a finite sum of random variables equals the sum of their individual expectations, holding regardless of whether individual variables are continuous or discrete.

$$E\left[\sum_{i=1}^n X_i\right] = \sum_{i=1}^n E[X_i]$$
