# Sequential Question Answering Optimization

A two-stage decision model where a contestant chooses an ordering of two questions to attempt sequentially, earning prize Vi with success probability pi for question i, with the second question accessible only if the first is answered correctly. The optimal ordering is chosen to maximize the overall expected monetary reward.
