# Axiomatic Definition of Probability

A real-valued function on events satisfying non-negativity and boundedness between 0 and 1, unit measure on the sample space, and countable additivity over pairwise mutually exclusive events.

$$0 \le P(E) \le 1$$

$$P(S) = 1$$

$$P\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^\infty E_i\right) = \sum_{i=1}^\infty P(E_i)$$
