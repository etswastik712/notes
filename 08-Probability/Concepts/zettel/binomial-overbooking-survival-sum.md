# Binomial Overbooking Survival Sum

A model computing the probability that capacity constraints are respected when individuals independently show up with fixed probability, expressed as a sum of binomial probabilities up to the total seat count.

$$P[S \leqslant C] = \sum_{k=0}^C \binom{N}{k} p^k (1-p)^{N-k}$$
