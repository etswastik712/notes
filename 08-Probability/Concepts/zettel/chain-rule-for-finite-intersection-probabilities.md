# Chain Rule for Finite Intersection Probabilities

The expansion of the joint probability of a finite intersection of independent events into the product of their individual probabilities via conditional probabilities.

$$P\left[\bigcap_{i=1}^n E_i\right] = \prod_{i=1}^n P[E_i]$$
