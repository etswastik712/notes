# Permutations of Distinguishable Objects

The total number of ordered arrangements or permutations of n distinct, distinguishable items is given by n factorial.

$$n!$$

## Links
- used-in:: [[zettel/combinations]] — Choosing r objects from n is obtained by dividing the number of r-permutations by the r! permutations within the chosen group
