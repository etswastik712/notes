# Central Moment

The expected value of the n-th power of the deviation of a random variable X from its mean E[X], for any n in N. It can be represented in terms of raw moments up to order n using the binomial expansion.

$$E[(X - E[X])^n]$$

## Links
- needs:: [[zettel/raw-moment]] — The central moment measures deviations from the first raw moment E[X] and expands into raw moments via the binomial formula
- generalises:: [[zettel/variance]] — Variance is specifically defined as the second central moment E[(X - E[X])^2]
