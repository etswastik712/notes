# Order Statistic of First Rank in Uniform Random Permutations

The distribution of the minimum index assigned to elements of a marked subset within a uniformly random permutation of an ordered collection of distinct ranks.

$$P[X = k] = \frac{\binom{N - k}{n - 1}}{\binom{N}{n}}$$
