# Banach Matchbox Problem

A classical probability problem evaluating the probability that, when one of two matchboxes containing N matches each is discovered to be empty, precisely k matches remain in the other box. The occurrences of draws from either pocket are modeled as independent Bernoulli trials of equal probability.

$$P[E] = \binom{2N - k}{N} \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2N - k + 1}$$

$$P[E \cup F] = \binom{2N - k}{N} \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2N - k}$$
