\begin{keybox}[Sign convention]
MIT (this sheet): $\dd E=\bar{\dd}Q+\bar{\dd}W$, $\bar{\dd}W=\sum J_i\dd x_i$, fluid $-P\dd V$ (work \emph{on} the system). Zemansky and the climate notes: $\dd U=\bar{\dd}Q-\bar{\dd}W$, $W_{\rm by}=\int P\dd V$. $Q$ is heat added in both.
\end{keybox}
