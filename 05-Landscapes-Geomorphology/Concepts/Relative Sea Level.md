# Relative Sea Level

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 16, printed pp.504-508 ([[LS26 Sea Level and Ocean Waves]] §1).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> What the coast feels is the sea's rise minus the land's rise.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $RSL=SL-R$ ($R>0$ uplift). $RSL<0$: emerging, rocky, terraces; $RSL>0$: drowning, sandy, barrier islands. LGM $-120$ m; melt-water pulse 1A: 25 m in 500 yr $=5$ cm/yr $=6\times10^{5}$ m³/s $=3$ Amazons; present 2.9 mm/yr (steric about half); steric $\Delta h=\alpha H\Delta T$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> A tide gauge measures *relative* sea level, including the land's own motion (glacial isostatic adjustment is $\sim1$ mm/yr at New York).

Used in: [[LS26 Sea Level and Ocean Waves]], [[LS27 Coasts Beaches Deltas and Rocky Shores]]. Related: [[Marine Terrace]] · [[Storm Surge]].
