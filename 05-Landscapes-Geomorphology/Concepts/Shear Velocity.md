# Shear Velocity

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 12 ([[LS21 River Hydraulics and Hydraulic Geometry]] §3).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The speed scale of the turbulent eddies near the bed; it is what the bed stress looks like as a velocity.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $u_*=\sqrt{\tau_b/\rho}=\sqrt{gHS}$ for steady uniform flow. Typical values 0.05–0.5 m/s. Threshold of grain motion $u_{*c}\propto\sqrt D$; Rouse number $P=w_s/\kappa u_*$; abrasion $\propto U^3\propto u_*^3$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> $u_*$ is not a flow speed; the mean speed is $\sim(10$–$20)\,u_*$.

Used in: [[LS21 River Hydraulics and Hydraulic Geometry]], [[LS24 Sediment Transport Mechanics]]. Related: [[Law of the Wall]] · [[Shields Criterion]] · [[Rouse Profile]] · [[Saltation]].
