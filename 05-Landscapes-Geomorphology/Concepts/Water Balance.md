# Water Balance

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 11, printed pp.352–353 ([[LS25 Water in the Landscape]] §2).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Whatever rain does not leave as river flow or vapour must go into storage.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $dS/dt=AR-Q_s-Q_{gw}-A\,ET$; over a year $R-ET\approx Q/A$ (effective precipitation = runoff depth). Coos Bay: $R=2$ m/yr, $ET=0.6$ m/yr, runoff ratio $0.70$, mean discharge of the 860 m² hollow $0.038$ L/s. Over a storm the storage term cannot be dropped.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Groundwater export and storage change are small only over long periods and for closed basins.
