# Braided Rivers

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 12, printed pp.400–401 ([[LS22 Floods Planform and River Profiles]] §3, [[LS21 River Hydraulics and Hydraulic Geometry]] §7.3).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> With banks that cannot hold together, the river can only get deeper until the bed just moves; extra water goes into extra threads.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Depth pinned: $\rho gHS=\tau_c\Rightarrow H=\tau_{*c}(s-1)D/S$; so $Q\propto W_{eff}$ (a satellite measures the width). Mechanism: divergence of the bedload flux builds mid-channel bars (Exner). Needs non-cohesive banks, bedload and flashy flow (glacial outwash).

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> More slope means a shallower threshold flow, hence wider and more braided, not narrower.

Used in: [[LS22 Floods Planform and River Profiles]], [[LS21 River Hydraulics and Hydraulic Geometry]]. Related: [[Meandering and Point Bars]] · [[Shields Criterion]] · [[Hydraulic Geometry]].
