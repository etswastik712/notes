> [!intuition] In one picture
> Deeply incised bedrock channels whose meanders are locked in trenches, evidence of vertical incision into rising basalt.

![[dec_031.jpg|360]] ![[dec_032.jpg|360]]

> [!key] Key facts
> - River Mula near Bota and a tributary on the Sakur plateau (slide 31).
> - A natural arch 4 m high and 12 m wide at the head of a knickpoint on a Kukdi tributary at Ane Ghat (slide 32).
> - Knickpoints retreat upstream by undercutting the soft layer under hard cores.
