# Saltation

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 14, printed pp.462–468 ([[LS24 Sediment Transport Mechanics]] §4); used for wind in [[LS17 Aeolian Landscapes]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Grains hop along the bed; in air each landing splashes new grains up, and the wind loses the momentum the grains take.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Ballistic hop: $h=w_0^2/2g$, $L=v_0^2\sin2\alpha/g$; real paths launch at $\sim45^\circ$ and land at $10$–$15^\circ$. Splash: rebound at about half the impact speed, ejecta at a tenth. Flux $q=mNL\propto u_*\cdot u_*^2=u_*^3$; Meyer-Peter–Müller in water $q_*=8(\tau_*-\tau_{*c})^{3/2}$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> In water the splash is negligible and the fluid alone controls the flux.
