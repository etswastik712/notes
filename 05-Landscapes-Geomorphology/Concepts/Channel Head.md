# Channel Head

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 11, printed pp.370-376 ([[LS25 Water in the Landscape]] §8).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Channels begin where convergent flow becomes strong enough to cut: by overland flow, by saturation of a hollow, by a landslide, or by groundwater seeping out.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Saturation: $A/c\ge T S/q_r$, i.e. $a/S\ge T/q_r$ (4 m for till, 300 m for loam, 3000 m for Coos Bay soil at 20 mm/d). Landslide-controlled heads migrate over $\sim30$ m. Sapping: tip speed $v=\alpha A$, new tips at $dN/dt=\beta A$; blunt amphitheatre heads. The head position sets $D_d$, hence hillslope length and the basin's response time.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Blunt-headed valleys do not prove groundwater sapping (hard-over-soft stratigraphy gives them too).
