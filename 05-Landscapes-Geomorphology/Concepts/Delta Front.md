# Delta Front

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 16, printed pp.515-520 ([[LS27 Coasts Beaches Deltas and Rocky Shores]] §5).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A delta advances like a conveyor: flat top, steep front, gentle toe, with the front smoothed by slope-driven slumping.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Gilbert's topset, foreset and bottomset beds; river = planar turbulent jet (speed $\propto x^{-1/2}$) giving reverse-graded deposits. Front: $z=z_0e^{-u_0x/K}$, length $K/u_0$ ($K=2.4\times10^4$ Fraser to $5.6\times10^5$ m²/yr Mississippi; 0.5 to 11 km at $u_0=50$ m/yr). Sediment to keep pace with RSL: $M=A_d\dot{RSL}\rho_b$ (390 Mt/yr for $3\times10^4$ km², 10 mm/yr). Subsidence 5-10 mm/yr (compaction), slope $10^{-4}$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Compaction and dam-trapped sediment, not only sea-level rise, drown deltas.

Used in: [[LS27 Coasts Beaches Deltas and Rocky Shores]]. Related: [[Hyperpycnal Flow]] · [[Littoral Cell Budget]] · [[Storm Surge]].
