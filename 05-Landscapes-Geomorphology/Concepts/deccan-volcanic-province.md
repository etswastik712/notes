# Deccan Volcanic Province

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus
> 🟢 **taught:** slides 2, 5 of [[Geomorphic Features of the Deccan Traps Terrain.pdf]]; written up in [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> One of the largest Large Igneous Provinces on Earth, erupted about 66 Ma and a leading suspect for the Cretaceous–Palaeogene mass extinction.

![[dec_002.jpg|360]] ![[dec_005.jpg|360]]

> [!key] Key facts
> - Area ≈380,000 km² (main) to ≈500,000 km² (with outliers); onland erupted volume ≈617,700 km³ (average thickness 1.2–1.6 km); onland composite thickness ≈3000 m; highest continuous stack 1642 m (Mt. Kalsubai).
> - ≈90 % of the volume is tholeiitic continental flood basalt; the rest are tholeiitic–silicic volcanics, pillow basalts, alkaline complexes and dyke swarms.
> - Cause: the Réunion mantle plume beneath the northward-moving Indian plate ([[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]] slide 4).
