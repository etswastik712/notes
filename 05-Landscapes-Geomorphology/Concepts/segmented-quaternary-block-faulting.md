# Segmented Quaternary Block Faulting

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus
> 🟢 **taught:** slides 30 of [[Geomorphic Features of the Deccan Traps Terrain.pdf]]; written up in [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Quaternary faults cut the plateau into blocks that have moved vertically relative to each other.

![[dec_030.jpg|360]]

> [!key] Key facts
> - Planation surfaces at the same original level now sit at different elevations in different blocks (slide 30).
> - Faulting also diverts rivers and controls the position of gorges.
> - One of the four factors that govern planation surfaces (slide 34).
