# Intertrappean Beds

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus
> 🟢 **taught:** slides 24 of [[Geomorphic Features of the Deccan Traps Terrain.pdf]]; written up in [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Thin sedimentary layers, 1 m to more than 20 m thick, sandwiched between basalt flows; they record quiet intervals and preserve fossils.

![[dec_024.jpg|360]]

> [!key] Key facts
> - Mapped across the province (Malwa, Mandla, Saurashtra, Sahyadri groups; slide 24, Wilson Mantilla et al. 2022).
> - Link volcanism to the K–Pg boundary through fossils (eggshells, plants, frogs).
> - A kind of interflow horizon.

Used in: [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]]. Related: [[interflow-horizons]] · [[deccan-volcanic-province]].
