# Manning Equation

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 12, printed pp.389–391 ([[LS21 River Hydraulics and Hydraulic Geometry]] §5).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Three bookkeeping schemes for the same law $\langle U\rangle\propto\sqrt{gR_hS}$.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Manning $\langle U\rangle=R_h^{2/3}S^{1/2}/n$; Chézy $C\sqrt{R_hS}$; Darcy–Weisbach $\sqrt{8gR_hS/f}$. $f=8(u_*/\langle U\rangle)^2$, $C=\sqrt{8g/f}$, $n=R_h^{1/6}/C$. Natural $n\approx0.02$–$0.08$. The log law reproduces Manning's $H^{2/3}$ exactly at $H/z_0=e^{7}\approx1100$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Chézy and Manning coefficients contain $g$ and are not dimensionless: they do not transfer to other planets; $f$ does.
