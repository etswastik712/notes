# Marine Terrace

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 16, printed pp.507-508 ([[LS26 Sea Level and Ocean Waves]] §1.3).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> An old shoreline is a level line scribed in the rock; how high it now stands, and how old it is, gives the uplift rate.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $U=(z-z_{sl})/T$: a terrace at 30 m formed at 125 ka (sea level $+6$ m) gives 0.19 mm/yr. Huon Peninsula (1-3 mm/yr): the 125 ka terrace stands 130-380 m up. Corals ($Acropora$, depth range 5 m) are dated by U/Th. Spacing of terraces from successive highstands $\approx U\times$ cycle length.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Sea level at the time must be subtracted; river terraces are poorer gauges because their initial slope is unknown.

Used in: [[LS26 Sea Level and Ocean Waves]], [[LS27 Coasts Beaches Deltas and Rocky Shores]]. Related: [[Relative Sea Level]] · [[Rocky Coast Erosion]].
