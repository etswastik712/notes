# Thermochronology

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 6, printed pp.146–157 ([[LSX1 Clocks in Rocks]] §5).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Cooling ages record when a rock climbed through the temperature at which a mineral starts to remember; with a geotherm that is a depth, hence an exhumation rate.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\varepsilon\approx z_c/t=(T_c-T_s)/(G\,t)$: 4 Ma, $T_c=70\,^\circ$C, $G=25$ °C/km gives 0.6 mm/yr. Topography bends isotherms (compressed under valleys, expanded under ridges, to depths of $\sim2\times$ relief); advection matters for $Pe=\varepsilon L/\kappa_T\gtrsim1$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> A cooling age is not an exhumation rate until a thermal model has turned temperature into depth.
