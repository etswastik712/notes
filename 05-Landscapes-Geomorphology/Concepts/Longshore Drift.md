# Longshore Drift

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 16, printed pp.512-513, 521 ([[LS27 Coasts Beaches Deltas and Rocky Shores]] §1).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Waves hitting the beach at an angle push sand along it, a river of sand running parallel to the shore.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $P_\ell=(EC_g)_b\sin\alpha_b\cos\alpha_b=0.0707\rho g^{3/2}H_b^{5/2}\sin2\alpha_b$ (breaker index 0.78); $Q=KP_\ell/[(\rho_s-\rho)g(1-p)]$, $K\approx0.39$. $H_b=1$ m, $10^\circ$: 0.98 Mm³/yr. Doubling $H_b$ multiplies $Q$ by 5.7; maximum at $45^\circ$. Net drift is a small difference of opposing large fluxes.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Net and gross drift differ, and the big, rare waves carry a large share of the gross.
