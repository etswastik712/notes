# Compound Hummocky Pahoehoe

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus
> 🟢 **taught:** slides 11, 14, 15 of [[Geomorphic Features of the Deccan Traps Terrain.pdf]]; written up in [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Low-effusion-rate lava built of many small inflated lobes, giving knobby, undulating micro-relief with tumuli.

![[dec_011.jpg|360]] ![[dec_014.jpg|360]] ![[dec_015.jpg|360]]

> [!key] Key facts
> - Rate < 10 m³/s; lobes a few m to 100s of m long and <10s of m thick (slide 15).
> - Features: tumuli with axial clefts, lava lobes, toes and break-outs; inflation by injection under a rigid crust (slide 14).
> - Example: Bhuleshwar Ghat section, SE of Pune.

Used in: [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]]. Related: [[massive-sheet-flow]] · [[lava-flow-internal-structure]].
