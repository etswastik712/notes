# Littoral Cell Budget

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 16, printed pp.521-524 ([[LS27 Coasts Beaches Deltas and Rocky Shores]] §2, §4).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Count the sand a stretch of coast gains from cliffs and rivers and loses to the shelf and canyons; what is left is carried along the beach.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\partial_tA=-\partial_xQ_{litt}+\dot e f H_{cliff}-q_{shelf}+Q_{river}\delta(x-x_r)$; steady: $Q=(\dot efH-q_{shelf})x$ plus steps at river mouths. Example: 1.5 m³/yr/m gain over 30 km, river 8e4: $Q=1.25\times10^{5}$ m³/yr at the downcoast end. Equilibrium (constant $Q$) bays are log spirals $r=r_0e^{\theta\cot\alpha}$, $\alpha=$ angle between ray and shoreline.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Only grains coarser than the cutoff ($\sim0.2$ mm) stay in the littoral system.
