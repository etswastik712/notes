# Strath Terrace

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 13, printed pp.425, 442–445 ([[LS23 Bedrock Channels and River Incision]] §2, §8).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A bedrock bench cut sideways while the river was blocked by its own gravel, left high when it cut down again.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Sediment cover thicker than the scour depth stops vertical incision; lateral planation widens a strath; thinning of the cover restarts incision and abandons the bench. Terraces lag the forcing. Wind River: 20, 120, 600 kyr.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> A strath terrace is bedrock with a thin cover; a *fill* terrace is all sediment.
