# Base Level

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **In the slides** ([[LS16 Fluvial Landscapes]]) and 🟡 **extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 12, printed pp.405–411 ([[LS22 Floods Planform and River Profiles]] §5–§6).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The level a river grades to; change it and the river readjusts, diffusively if alluvial and as a wave if bedrock.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Alluvial: $\partial_tz=\kappa\,\partial_x^2z$, $\kappa=\alpha Q/\rho_b$; a drop $h$ is felt as $z=-h\operatorname{erfc}(x/2\sqrt{\kappa t})$ (23 km after 1 kyr, 74 km after 10 kyr for $\kappa=10^5$ m²/yr). Bedrock: kinematic wave. Backwater length $\sim H/S$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Relative sea level (including land motion) is what matters; the boundary moves horizontally too.

Used in: [[LS22 Floods Planform and River Profiles]], [[LS23 Bedrock Channels and River Incision]]. Related: [[Knickpoint]] · [[Meandering and Point Bars]].
