# Shields Criterion

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **In the slides** (outline: [[LS16 Fluvial Landscapes]]) and 🟡 **extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 14, printed pp.455–461 ([[LS24 Sediment Transport Mechanics]] §2).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A grain moves when the fluid's torque beats its weight: the critical stress grows linearly with grain size.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\tau_c=\tau_{*c}(\rho_s-\rho)gD$, $\tau_{*c}\approx0.03$–$0.06$ (torque balance predicts 0.08); minimum $0.032$ at $u_*D/\nu\approx10$ (Brownlie fit); easiest grain $\approx0.1$ mm in air and water. In air $u_{*t}=0.1\sqrt{(\rho_s/\rho_a)gD}$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Thresholds are statistical: marginal transport occurs below the mean-stress threshold because of sweeps, bursts and favourable pockets.
