# Rouse Profile

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 14, printed pp.468–473 ([[LS24 Sediment Transport Mechanics]] §5).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Settling pulls grains down, turbulence mixes them up; the balance is a power-law profile whose exponent is the ratio of the two speeds.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $w_sC+\varepsilon\,dC/dz=0$ with $\varepsilon=\kappa u_*z(1-z/H)$ gives $C/C_a=\big[\frac{H-z}{z}\frac a{H-a}\big]^P$, $P=w_s/\kappa u_*$. $P>2.5$ bedload, $<0.8$ fully suspended (convention).

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> The reference concentration $C_a$ is not given by the profile; it must come from an entrainment law.

Used in: [[LS24 Sediment Transport Mechanics]], [[LS22 Floods Planform and River Profiles]] (levees). Related: [[Law of the Wall]] · [[Shear Velocity]] · [[Saltation]].
