# Radioactive Decay Law

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 6, printed pp.131–133 ([[LSX1 Clocks in Rocks]] §2).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Each unstable atom has a fixed chance per unit time of decaying; the population shrinks exponentially.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $N=N_0e^{-\lambda t}$, $t_{1/2}=\tau\ln2=0.693\tau$ (half-life is 69 % of the mean life), age $t=\tau\ln(N_0/N)$, $\delta t=\tau\,\delta N/N$. $^{14}$C: $t_{1/2}=5730$ yr, $\tau=8267$ yr, 13.8 decays/min per gram of modern carbon; limit $\sim50$ kyr (9 half-lives).

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> $\lambda$ is a property of the nucleus (see [[Alpha Decay]]); chemical state and temperature do not change it.

Used in: [[LSX1 Clocks in Rocks]]. Related: [[Cosmogenic Nuclides]] · [[Burial Dating]] · [[Alpha Decay]].
