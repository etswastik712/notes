# Pothole Networks

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus
> 🟢 **taught:** slides 33 of [[Geomorphic Features of the Deccan Traps Terrain.pdf]]; written up in [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Dense networks of circular holes drilled in a river bed by pebbles whirled in eddies.

![[dec_033.jpg|360]]

> [!key] Key facts
> - Large network in the linear fracture-controlled channel of the Kukadi river near Nighoj, north of Pune (slide 33).
> - Abrasion by bedload in a high-power, narrow reach.
> - Often drawn as the 'Who made these holes?' puzzle on the slide.

Used in: [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]]. Related: [[fracture-bound-rock-trench]].
