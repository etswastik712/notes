# Effective Discharge

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 12, printed pp.396–399, Box 12.3 ([[LS22 Floods Planform and River Profiles]] §1).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The flow that moves the most sediment over the long run: neither the commonest nor the biggest, but a moderate flood near bankfull.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $Q_e=\arg\max\,Q^jp(Q)$; for lognormal daily flows ($\sigma$) and $q_s\propto Q^j$, $Q_e=Q_{med}e^{j\sigma^2}$ (a few days a year). Gumbel annual maxima: the mean annual flood has $T=2.33$ yr. Bankfull recurrence 1.5–2.5 yr.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> A threshold of motion shifts $Q_e$ to rarer flows (0.6 d/yr for $j=2.5$, $Q_c=3Q_{med}$).

Used in: [[LS22 Floods Planform and River Profiles]], [[LS24 Sediment Transport Mechanics]] (the same argument for wind). Related: [[Hydraulic Geometry]] · [[Braided Rivers]] · [[Shields Criterion]].
