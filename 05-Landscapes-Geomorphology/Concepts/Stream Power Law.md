# Stream Power Law

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **In the slides** (outline: [[LS16 Fluvial Landscapes]]) and 🟡 **extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 13, printed pp.428–429 ([[LS23 Bedrock Channels and River Incision]] §3).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A river lowers its bed at a rate set by the power it spends per unit bed area.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $\omega=\rho gqS=\tau_bU$ (W/m²); $E=k\omega$ with $k=\alpha d/(BE_b)$ (work of fracture, efficiency $\sim10^{-11}$); general $E=KA^mS^n$. Steady state $S=(U/K)^{1/n}A^{-m/n}$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> The law is an energy budget, not a process: it does not say whether rock is removed by abrasion, quarrying or dissolution.
