# Cosmogenic Erosion Rate

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 6, printed pp.139–142 ([[LSX1 Clocks in Rocks]] §4.2).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A rock being lowered steadily carries a fixed concentration of nuclides; the faster the lowering, the fewer it holds.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $N=P/(\lambda+\rho\varepsilon/\Lambda)$ $\Rightarrow$ $\varepsilon=(\Lambda/\rho)(P/N-\lambda)$; $N=10^5$ atoms/g ($P=5$) means 29 m/Myr averaged over $z_*/\varepsilon=20$ kyr. River sand gives the catchment average.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> The same $N$ is an age (no erosion) or a rate (steady erosion): you must know which applies.
