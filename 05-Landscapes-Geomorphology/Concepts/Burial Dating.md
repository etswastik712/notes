# Burial Dating

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 13, printed p.425 and Ch. 6 ([[LS23 Bedrock Channels and River Incision]] §2.1, [[LSX1 Clocks in Rocks]] §4.3).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Bury quartz deep and the cosmic-ray clock stops; $^{26}$Al then decays faster than $^{10}$Be, and their ratio is a stopwatch.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $R=R_0e^{-t/\tau}$, $1/\tau=1/\tau_{26}-1/\tau_{10}$, $\tau=2.07$ Myr, $R_0\approx6.75$; $t=\tau\ln(R_0/R)$; range to $\sim5$ Myr.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Needs burial deep enough ($\gtrsim5$ m) to stop production, and no later exposure.
