# Closure Temperature

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book** (the idea, A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 6, printed pp.146–152) and 🔵 **extra · beyond** (Dodson's equation, [[LSX1 Clocks in Rocks]] §5.1).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A mineral keeps its daughter atoms only when it is cold enough that diffusion out of the grain is slower than the cooling.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $T_c=\dfrac{E/R}{\ln(A\tau_cD_0/a^2)}$, $\tau_c=RT_c^2/(E|\dot T|)$, $A=55$ (sphere); logarithmic in the cooling rate (16 K per decade for apatite He). Apatite fission tracks $\approx110\,^\circ$C, apatite (U–Th)/He $\approx75\,^\circ$C (A&A).

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> The age is the time since cooling through $T_c$, not the formation age.

Used in: [[LSX1 Clocks in Rocks]]. Related: [[Thermochronology]] · [[Radioactive Decay Law]].
