# Green-Ampt Infiltration

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 11, printed pp.355–359 ([[LS25 Water in the Landscape]] §4).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Water soaks in behind a sharp wetting front; early on the suction gradient is steep and the soil drinks fast, later only gravity and $K_s$ are left.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $f=K_s(1+\psi_f\Delta\theta/F)$; $F-\psi_f\Delta\theta\ln(1+F/\psi_f\Delta\theta)=K_st$. Ponding when $f=R$: $F_p=\psi_f\Delta\theta K_s/(R-K_s)$, $t_p=F_p/R$. Example ($K_s=3.6$ mm/h, $\psi_f\Delta\theta=0.06$ m, rain 20 mm/h): $F_p=13.2$ mm, $t_p=39.5$ min; in 3 h 40.9 of 60 mm infiltrates (runoff ratio 0.32).

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Infiltration capacity is not a constant of the soil: it falls with time and depends on antecedent moisture.
