# Cosmogenic Nuclides

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 6, printed pp.136–145 ([[LSX1 Clocks in Rocks]] §4).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Cosmic rays make rare isotopes inside the top metre of rock; how many have built up tells how long the rock has been there.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $P(z)=P_0e^{-\rho z/\Lambda}$, $\Lambda\approx160$ g/cm² ($z_*=0.59$ m in rock); $\dot N=P-\lambda N-(\rho\varepsilon/\Lambda)N$; exposure age $N/P$; secular equilibrium $P/\lambda$ ($10^7$ atoms/g for $^{10}$Be, 6 Myr to 95 %). Water shields: $e^{-h/1.6\,\mathrm m}$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Inheritance: a surface exposed before the event carries extra nuclides, so its age is too old.
