# Columnar Jointing

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus
> 🟢 **taught:** slides 17 of [[Geomorphic Features of the Deccan Traps Terrain.pdf]]; written up in [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Polygonal (mostly hexagonal) shrinkage cracks that split cooling basalt into prismatic columns.

![[dec_017.jpg|360]]

> [!key] Key facts
> - Cause: thermal contraction; a fully constrained basalt cooling by 900 K would reach ≈670 MPa, ≈50 × its tensile strength, so it fractures (`scripts/ls_numbers.py`).
> - Colonnade = tall regular columns formed by slow cooling; entablature = narrower curved columns formed by faster/wetter cooling (Singhania, Madhya Pradesh).
> - Controls how cliffs collapse, how scarps retreat and how vertical faces persist.

Used in: [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]]. Related: [[lava-flow-internal-structure]] · [[massive-sheet-flow]].
