# Froude Number and Hydraulic Jump

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book** (the Froude number, A&A appendix, printed pp.414–419) and 🔵 **extra · beyond** (the jump, [[LS21 River Hydraulics and Hydraulic Geometry]] §6).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The ratio of flow speed to the speed of a surface gravity wave; above 1 the flow outruns the waves and cannot be influenced from downstream.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $Fr=U/\sqrt{gH}$. $Fr<1$ subcritical (lowland rivers, 0.2–0.6), $Fr>1$ supercritical (mountain steps, chutes). Jump: $H_2/H_1=\frac12(\sqrt{1+8Fr_1^2}-1)$, head lost $(H_2-H_1)^3/4H_1H_2$; $Fr_1=3$: ratio 3.77, 26 % of the head lost. Backwater length $\sim H/S$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Momentum (not energy) is conserved across the jump; energy is dissipated.

Used in: [[LS21 River Hydraulics and Hydraulic Geometry]], [[LS22 Floods Planform and River Profiles]] (backwater, avulsion). Related: [[Manning Equation]] · [[Hydraulic Geometry]].
