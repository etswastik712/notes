# Planation Surfaces

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus
> 🟢 **taught:** slides 34, 35 of [[Geomorphic Features of the Deccan Traps Terrain.pdf]]; written up in [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Multi-tiered flat tablelands at distinct elevations, remnants of ancient erosion surfaces.

![[dec_034.jpg|360]] ![[dec_035.jpg|360]]

> [!key] Key facts
> - Elevations ≈1,330, 1,225, 1,000, 920, 700, 600 m (slide 34).
> - Governed by etchplanation (tropical weathering), pediplanation (scarp retreat), lithological differential erosion and Quaternary block displacement.
> - Periodic uplift re-incises older surfaces and leaves them as tablelands.

Used in: [[LS18 Deccan Traps Terrain]]. Related: [[laterite-cappings]] · [[segmented-quaternary-block-faulting]].
