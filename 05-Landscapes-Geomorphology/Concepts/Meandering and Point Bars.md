# Meandering and Point Bars

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 12, printed pp.401–405 ([[LS22 Floods Planform and River Profiles]] §4).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Water in a bend is flung outward at the top and returns inward along the bed, carrying sand to the inside bank.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> Super-elevation $\Delta h=U^2W/gR$; wavelength $\approx11W$; sinuosity of a sine-generated curve $1/J_0(\omega)$; point-bar deposit fines upward; cutoffs (neck, chute) make oxbow lakes and cap the sinuosity.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> A meander does not 'erode' only on the outside: the inside builds a point bar at the same rate.
