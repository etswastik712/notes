# Hydraulic Geometry

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **Extra · book:** A&A = Anderson and Anderson, *Geomorphology* (PDF page = printed page + 18). Ch. 12, printed pp.395–396 ([[LS21 River Hydraulics and Hydraulic Geometry]] §8).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> When a river carries more water it widens and deepens in roughly equal parts and speeds up only a little.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $W=aQ^b$, $H=cQ^f$, $U=kQ^m$ with $b+f+m=1$. Downstream $0.5,\,0.4,\,0.1$; at a station $0.26,\,0.40,\,0.34$. Braided rivers: depth pinned at the threshold, $b\to1$.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> The exponents are empirical averages, not laws; individual rivers scatter.
