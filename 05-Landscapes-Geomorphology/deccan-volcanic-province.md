# Deccan Volcanic Province

A major continental Large Igneous Province formed primarily by stacked, horizontal tholeiitic basalt flows associated with the Cretaceous-Paleogene boundary. Differential weathering and erosion of these stacked flows produce characteristic step-like slopes ('traps').
