# Generalised Force and Displacement

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[0d0ec730e8e02ed0399d39ff05054a72_MIT8_333F13_Lec1.pdf#page=5|MIT L1 Table 1]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Every way of doing work is a push (intensive) times a move (extensive).

> [!key] Definition
> $\bar\mathrm dW=\sum_iJ_i\,\mathrm dx_i$ with $J$ intensive and $x$ extensive: wire $(F,L)$, film $(S,A)$, fluid $(-P,V)$, magnet $(H,M)$, dielectric $(E,P)$, chemical $(\mu,N)$. With heat added: $\mathrm dE=T\mathrm dS+\sum J_i\mathrm dx_i$ ($T,S$ are another pair).

## Note
$n$ pairs plus $(T,S)$ need $n+1$ independent coordinates. The **minus sign** in $-P\,\mathrm dV$ comes from defining $P$ as the outward force of the gas on the walls.

> [!pitfall] Conventions
> MIT counts work **on** the system; Zemansky and the climate notes count work **by** it ([[TH02 Work Heat and the First Law]] §2).
