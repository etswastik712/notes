# Refrigerator and Heat Pump

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[1a131e225246d9749a298f49675c5fe5_MIT8_333F13_Lec2.pdf#page=1|MIT L2 eq. I.17]]. · 🔵 **extra · beyond:** the COP formulas for Carnot machines.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> An engine run backwards: spend work to move heat uphill.

> [!key] Definition
> Refrigerator $\omega=Q_C/W=Q_C/(Q_H-Q_C)\le T_C/(T_H-T_C)$; heat pump $\mathrm{COP}=Q_H/W\le T_H/(T_H-T_C)$. $Q_H=Q_C+W$.

## Numbers
$500$/$300$ K: refrigerator $1.5$, heat pump $2.5$. Fridge at $270$ K in a $300$ K room removing $1$ kW: $\omega=9$, minimum power $111$ W, $1111$ W to the room.

> [!pitfall] COP can exceed 1
> Unlike efficiency, $\omega>1$ is normal: it moves heat rather than creating it.
