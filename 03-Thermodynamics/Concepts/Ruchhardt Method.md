# Ruchhardt Method

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Zemansky §5.6 ([[Heat and thermodynamics an intermediate textbook (Mark Waldo Zemansky Richard Dittman) (z-library.sk, 1lib.sk, z-lib.sk).pdf#page=138|PDF p.138]]).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A ball bobbing on a gas spring: the stiffer the gas, the faster the period.

> [!key] Definition
> Ball of mass $m$ in a tube of area $A$ on a volume $V$ at pressure $P$: $T=2\pi\sqrt{mV/\gamma PA^2}$, so $\gamma=4\pi^2mV/(PA^2T^2)$.

## Numbers
$m=20$ g, $V=10$ L, $A=1$ cm$^2$, $P=1$ atm: $T=2.36$ s for $\gamma=1.40$; argon would give $2.16$ s.

> [!pitfall] Use the gas pressure
> $P$ is atmospheric plus $mg/A$ in the gas vessel, and the motion must be fast enough to be adiabatic.
