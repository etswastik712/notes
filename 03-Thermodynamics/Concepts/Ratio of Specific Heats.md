# Ratio of Specific Heats

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** adiabat exponent in [[1a131e225246d9749a298f49675c5fe5_MIT8_333F13_Lec2.pdf#page=2|MIT L2 eq. I.19]]. · 🔵 **extra · beyond:** the degrees-of-freedom values.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Bigger molecules have more ways to store energy, so heating them at constant volume wastes more, and $\gamma$ falls.

> [!key] Definition
> $\gamma=C_P/C_V=1+2/f$ for an ideal gas with $f$ active quadratic degrees of freedom: $5/3$ (monatomic), $7/5$ (diatomic, air), $4/3$ (non-linear polyatomic).

## Uses
Adiabat $PV^\gamma=$ const; sound speed $\sqrt{\gamma RT/M}$; $T^\gamma P^{1-\gamma}$ const; Rüchhardt period. Also $\gamma=\kappa_T/\kappa_S$.

> [!pitfall] Frozen modes
> $\gamma=1.40$ for air because N$_2$ and O$_2$ vibrations are frozen ($\hbar\omega\gg k_BT$); CO$_2$ at 288 K is partly excited and has $\gamma\approx1.30$ (recalled).
