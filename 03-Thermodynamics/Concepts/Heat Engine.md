# Heat Engine

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[1a131e225246d9749a298f49675c5fe5_MIT8_333F13_Lec2.pdf#page=1|MIT L2 §I.D, eq. I.16]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A device that takes heat from a hot source, turns part of it into work and must dump the rest cold.

> [!key] Definition
> Efficiency $\eta=W/Q_H=(Q_H-Q_C)/Q_H\le1$; maximal value $\eta=1-T_C/T_H$ (Carnot). Perpetual motion of the first kind violates the first law, of the second kind the second law.

## Numbers
$500$ K / $300$ K: $\eta_{\max}=0.40$. Power plant $800$ K / $300$ K: $0.625$ (real $0.33$–$0.40$). Rectangular $PV$ loop of $1013$ J per cycle ($1$ to $2$ atm, $10$ to $20$ L) must run clockwise to be an engine.

> [!pitfall] 100 % is impossible
> Even without friction a cyclic engine must reject heat; the limit is set by the two temperatures, not by engineering.
