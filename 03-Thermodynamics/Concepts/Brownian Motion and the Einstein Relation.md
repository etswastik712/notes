# Brownian Motion and the Einstein Relation

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **Extra · beyond** ([[THX1 Diffusion Random Walks and the Heat Equation]] §5; builds on [[TH06 From Kinetic Theory to Statistical Mechanics]]).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A big particle is kicked by invisible molecules and random-walks; the same friction that slows it drives the kicks.

> [!key] Definition and key results
> $D=\mu k_BT$ (from $J=-Dc'+\mu Fc=0$ with $c\propto e^{-U/k_BT}$); Stokes–Einstein $D=k_BT/6\pi\eta a$: 1 µm sphere in water, $D=2.1\times10^{-13}$ m²/s, 0.66 µm in 1 s.

> [!pitfall] Pitfall
> Mobility and diffusivity are not independent: measuring one gives the other (Perrin used it to find Avogadro's number).
