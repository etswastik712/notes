# Langmuir Isotherm

> [!info] Source
> Langmuir (1918), Schroeder §7.1; [[SM05 The Grand Canonical Ensemble and Chemical Equilibrium]] §6.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Independent sites, each empty or singly occupied, in equilibrium with a gas: the coverage is a Fermi–Dirac function of the gas's chemical potential.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\theta=\dfrac{P}{P+P_0(T)}$, $P_0=\dfrac{k_BT}{\lambda^3}e^{-\epsilon/k_BT}$ ($\epsilon$ the binding energy). $P_0$ is the half-coverage pressure; coverage falls with $T$ at fixed $P$.

> [!example] Numbers (recomputed, N$_2$-mass molecule)
> $\epsilon=0.5$ eV: $P_0=2.4\times10^3$ (300 K), $6.2\times10^5$ (400 K), $2.1\times10^8$ Pa (600 K); coverage at 1 bar $0.977,\ 0.139,\ 5\times10^{-4}$. $\epsilon=0.1$ eV at 77 K: $P_0=5.7\times10^3$ Pa, $\theta=0.946$.
