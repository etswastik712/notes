# Fluctuation-Response Relation

> [!info] Source
> Standard; [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]] §2.6.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> How much a quantity changes when you push its conjugate field equals how much it fluctuates by itself: response is fluctuation.

> [!key] Key facts
> $H=H_0-\lambda B$: $\partial\langle B\rangle/\partial\lambda=\beta\,\mathrm{Var}(B)\ge0$. Gives $C_V=\mathrm{Var}(E)/k_BT^2$, $\chi=\beta\mathrm{Var}(M)/V$, $\kappa_T=\mathrm{Var}(V)/k_BT\langle V\rangle$ and hence **stability** ($C_V,\chi,\kappa_T\ge0$).

> [!example] Paramagnet (recomputed)
> $\partial M/\partial B=\beta N\mu^2\mathrm{sech}^2x=\beta\,\mathrm{Var}(M)$; $\mathrm{sech}^2(0.5)=0.7864$. Time-dependent generalisation: fluctuation–dissipation theorem (SM25, planned).

Used in: [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]]. Related: [[Response Functions]] · [[Thermodynamic Stability]] · [[Heat Capacity]] · [[Curie Law]].
