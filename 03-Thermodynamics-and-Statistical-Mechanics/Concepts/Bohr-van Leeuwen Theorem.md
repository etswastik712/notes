# Bohr-van Leeuwen Theorem

> [!info] Source
> Bohr (1911), van Leeuwen (1919); Pathria Problem 3.43; Huang §11.5; [[SM07 Equipartition the Virial Theorem and Magnetic Systems]] §5.3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Magnetic forces do no work, so they cannot change Boltzmann weights: classical charges have no magnetism at all.

> [!key] Key facts
> With $\mathcal H=\sum(\mathbf p-q\mathbf A)^2/2m+U$, shift $\mathbf p'=\mathbf p-q\mathbf A(\mathbf r)$ (Jacobian 1): $Z$ is independent of $\mathbf B$, so $F$ is, and $M=-\partial_BF=0$. Bulk (diamagnetic) and boundary (skipping) orbits cancel exactly. Escape: quantum spectra depend on $B$.

> [!connection] Consequence
> Orbital magnetism is quantum; the classical Langevin paramagnet assumes fixed intrinsic moments (spins).
