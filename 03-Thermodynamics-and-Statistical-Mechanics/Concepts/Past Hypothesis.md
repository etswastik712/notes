# Past Hypothesis

> [!info] Source
> Boltzmann, Feynman, Penrose, Albert (recalled); [[SM06 Entropy Information and the Arrow of Time]] §7.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The laws are time-symmetric; the direction of time comes from the Universe starting in a very special, very low-entropy state.

> [!key] Key facts
> Without it, the second law predicts entropy increase toward the past too (a macrostate drawn uniformly is a fluctuation in both directions). With it, all irreversibility is the relaxation of the initial atypicality. Gravitationally smooth early Universe $=$ low entropy; black holes $=$ very high entropy.

> [!example] Scales (recomputed)
> CMB photons in the observable Universe: $5.3\times10^{89}k_B$; Sgr A* ($4.3\times10^6M_\odot$): $1.9\times10^{90}k_B$; one solar mass: $1.05\times10^{77}k_B$.
