# Scattering Cross Section

> [!info] Source
> Reif §12.2 (pp. 468–470); Kardar §3.4; [[SM04 Kinetic Theory and the Boltzmann Equation]] §4.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The effective target area a molecule presents: the area through which an incoming particle must pass to be deflected.

> [!key] Key facts
> Differential: $\mathrm d\sigma/\mathrm d\Omega$ with $\mathrm d^2b=\frac{\mathrm d\sigma}{\mathrm d\Omega}\mathrm d\Omega$ ($\mathbf b$ the impact parameter). Total, identical hard spheres of diameter $d$: $\sigma=\pi d^2$. Collision rate per molecule $\sqrt2\,n\sigma\bar v$; mean free path $\lambda=1/\sqrt2n\sigma$; the $\sqrt2$ is $\langle|\mathbf v_{12}|\rangle/\bar v$.

> [!example] Air (recomputed)
> $d=3.7$ Å: $\sigma=4.3\times10^{-19}$ m$^2$; $\lambda=67$ nm; collision rate $7.1\times10^9$ s$^{-1}$; $8.7\times10^{34}$ collisions s$^{-1}$m$^{-3}$.
