# Mean Free Path

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** `KTG_Chapter.pdf` §1.7. · 🔵 **extra · beyond:** numbers for air.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> How far a molecule typically flies before bumping into another.

> [!key] Definition
> $\lambda=1/(\sqrt2\,n\sigma)=k_BT/(\sqrt2\pi d^2P)$, $\sigma=\pi d^2$; collision rate $\langle v\rangle/\lambda$.

## Numbers ($d=3.7$ Å)
Air, 1 atm, 300 K: $\lambda=67$ nm ($\approx180\,d$), rate $7.1\times10^9$ s$^{-1}$; at 10 km: $191$ nm; at 1 Pa: $6.8$ mm. Graham effusion: rates $\propto1/\sqrt m$.

> [!pitfall] Viscosity independent of density
> $\eta\propto\lambda\langle v\rangle n m\propto$ const, as long as $\lambda$ is small compared with the container.
