# Entropy of Radiation

> [!info] Source
> [[SM06 Entropy Information and the Arrow of Time]] §4; from $u=aT^4$, $P=u/3$.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A photon gas has entropy $\tfrac43$ of its energy per kelvin; each photon carries $3.6\,k_B$, whatever its energy.

> [!key] Key facts
> $s=\tfrac43aT^3=\tfrac43u/T$; $s/n_\gamma=\frac{2\pi^4}{45\zeta(3)}k_B=3.602\,k_B$, energy per photon $2.701\,k_BT$. Surface emission: energy flux $\sigma T^4$, entropy flux $\tfrac43\sigma T^3$; dilute blackbody beam from a source at $T_\odot$: entropy flux $\tfrac43J/T_\odot$.

> [!example] The Earth (recomputed)
> $J=238.2$ W m$^{-2}$, $T_e=254.6$ K: entropy flux in $0.055$, out $1.247$ W m$^{-2}$K$^{-1}$, production $1.19$ ($6.1\times10^{14}$ W K$^{-1}$); one solar photon becomes $22.7$ infrared photons.
