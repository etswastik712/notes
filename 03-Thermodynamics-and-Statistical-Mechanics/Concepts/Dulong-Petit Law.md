# Dulong-Petit Law

> [!info] Source
> Dulong and Petit (1819), Einstein (1907); [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]] §4.3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Every oscillator carries $k_BT$ classically, so a crystal holds $3k_BT$ per atom whatever it is made of, until quantum freezing sets in.

> [!key] Key facts
> $C_V=3Nk_B=3R=24.94$ J mol$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$ (classical). Einstein: $C/3Nk_B=x^2e^x/(e^x-1)^2$, $x=\theta_E/T$; $0.171$ at $T=\theta_E/5$, $0.724$ at $\theta_E/2$, $0.921$ at $\theta_E$; $\propto e^{-\theta_E/T}$ as $T\to0$.

> [!example] Diamond (recomputed; fit)
> $\theta_E\approx1300$ K: $C(298\ \mathrm K)=6.2$ vs $6.1$ J mol$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$ measured (recalled).
