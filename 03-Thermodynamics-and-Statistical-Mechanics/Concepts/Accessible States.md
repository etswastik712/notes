# Accessible States

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Reif §2.3; Koks §2.1 and §2.5; Schroeder §2.1.
> 🟢 **taught (pointer only):** [[TH09 Microstates Macrostates and Multiplicity]] §1.3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The microstates an isolated system can be in, given its constraints: fixed energy (in a window), volume, particle number and any conserved quantity.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\Omega(E,V,N)$ counts the states with $E\le E_i\le E+\delta E$; $\ln\Omega$ is insensitive to $\delta E$ for macroscopic systems. Lifting a constraint adds accessible states: that is the origin of spontaneous change.

> [!example] A removed partition
> Doubling the volume doubles each molecule's accessible positions: $\Omega\to2^N\Omega$, $\Delta S=Nk_B\ln2$ ($5.763$ J K$^{-1}$ per mole).
