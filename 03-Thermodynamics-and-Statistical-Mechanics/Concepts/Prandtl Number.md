# Prandtl Number

> [!info] Source
> [[SM04 Kinetic Theory and the Boltzmann Equation]] §10; the ratio of momentum to thermal diffusivity.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> How quickly momentum diffuses compared with heat: a single number for the fluid.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\mathrm{Pr}=\frac{\nu}{D_T}=\frac{c_p\eta}\kappa$. Single-time model: $1$. Exact monatomic gas: $\tfrac23$ (He $0.662$, Ne $0.664$, Ar $0.667$, Kr $0.669$, Xe $0.646$). Air: $0.71$; water $\approx7$; liquid metals $\ll1$.

> [!pitfall] Pr is not a geometry parameter
> It depends only on the fluid; it sets the relative thickness of the thermal and velocity boundary layers.
