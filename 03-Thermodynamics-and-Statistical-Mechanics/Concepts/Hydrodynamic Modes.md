# Hydrodynamic Modes

> [!info] Source
> Kardar §3.8–3.9 (pp. 83–87); [[SM04 Kinetic Theory and the Boltzmann Equation]] §9, §11.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A conserved quantity cannot be destroyed locally, only moved: perturbations of the five conserved densities relax slowly, by sound, or by diffusion at rate $\propto k^2$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Zeroth order: sound $\omega=\pm ck$; shear and heat $\omega=0$. First order: shear $\omega=-i\nu k^2$ ($\nu=\eta/mn$); heat $\omega=-iD_Tk^2$ ($D_T=\frac{2\kappa}{5nk_B}=\frac\kappa{\rho c_p}$); sound $\omega=\pm ck-i\Gamma k^2$, $\Gamma=\frac{2\eta}{3mn}+\frac{2\kappa}{15nk_B}$.

> [!example] Air (recomputed)
> 1 cm: shear decays in $0.16$ s, heat in $0.11$ s; 1 m: $1.6\times10^3$ s and $1.1\times10^3$ s. Classical sound absorption at 1 kHz $=1.3\times10^{-4}$ dB m$^{-1}$ (measured $\sim5\times10^{-3}$: molecular relaxation).
