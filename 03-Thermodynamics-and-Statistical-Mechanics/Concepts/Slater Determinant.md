# Slater Determinant

> [!info] Source
> Kardar §7.1 (pp. 183–184, eq. 7.6); [[SM08 Quantum Ensembles and Identical Particles]] §4.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The antisymmetrised product of one-particle orbitals: a determinant that vanishes whenever two rows (or two orbitals) coincide.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\Psi(x_1..x_N)=\frac1{\sqrt{N!}}\det[\varphi_{k_j}(x_i)]$. Swapping two particles swaps two rows (sign change); two equal orbitals make two columns equal (zero): Pauli exclusion. Two particles: $\frac1{\sqrt2}[\varphi_a(1)\varphi_b(2)-\varphi_b(1)\varphi_a(2)]$, probability density vanishing at $x_1=x_2$.

> [!example] Exchange hole
> Same-spin plane waves: $g(r)=1-e^{-2\pi r^2/\lambda^2}$ (thermal), $g(0)=0$; spin-averaged $g(0)=\tfrac12$ in an unpolarised gas.
