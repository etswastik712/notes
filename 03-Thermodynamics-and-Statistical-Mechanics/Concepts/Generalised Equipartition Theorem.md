# Generalised Equipartition Theorem

> [!info] Source
> Pathria §3.7 (pp. 61–62); Huang §6.4 (p. 136); Reif §7.5; [[SM07 Equipartition the Virial Theorem and Magnetic Systems]] §1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> One integration by parts on the Boltzmann factor shows that every coordinate carries $k_BT$ of 'virial' with its own conjugate force.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\langle x_i\,\partial\mathcal H/\partial x_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}k_BT$ ($x_ie^{-\beta\mathcal H}\to0$ at the ends). Quadratic term: $\tfrac12k_BT$; $V=a|q|^s$: $\langle V\rangle=k_BT/s$; gravity: $\langle z\rangle=k_BT/mg$; ultrarelativistic: $\langle\varepsilon\rangle=3k_BT$.

> [!example] Checks (recomputed)
> $s=1,2,4,6$: $\langle V\rangle/kT=1,\ 0.5,\ 0.25,\ 0.1667$; air at 288.15 K: $\langle z\rangle=8434.6$ m.
