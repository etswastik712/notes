# Adiabatic Demagnetisation

> [!info] Source
> [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]] §5 (the physics is the $B/T$ dependence of the paramagnet's entropy).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Magnetise isothermally to squeeze out entropy, insulate, then lower the field: the entropy cannot change, so $T$ must fall with $B$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $S=S(B/T)$ for non-interacting moments, so an isentropic change keeps $B/T$ fixed: $T_f=T_iB_f/B_i$. Cooling stops when the moments' own interactions (internal field $B_{\rm int}$) become comparable to $B_f$.

> [!example] Order of magnitude
> From 1 K and 1 T to 1 mT would reach $1$ mK for ideal moments; electronic paramagnetic salts reach $\sim$ mK, nuclear spins $\sim\mu$K. It is the experimental face of the third law ($S\to0$ needs $B/T\to\infty$).
