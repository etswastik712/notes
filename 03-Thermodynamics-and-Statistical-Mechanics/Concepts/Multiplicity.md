# Multiplicity

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Schroeder Ch. 2 (§2.1–2.6); Koks §2.5 and §3.8.
> 🟢 **taught (pointer only):** [[TH09 Microstates Macrostates and Multiplicity]] §1.1 and §3; MIT L4 §I.J writes the same quantity as $g$.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The number of microstates $\Omega$ compatible with a macrostate; entropy is its logarithm.

> [!key] Key facts
> Two-state: $\binom Nk$; Einstein solid $\binom{q+N-1}q$; ideal gas $\propto V^NU^{3N/2}$; independent parts multiply, $\Omega=\Omega_A\Omega_B$, so $S=k_B\ln\Omega$ adds. $\Omega(50\text{ of }100)=1.0089\times10^{29}$.

> [!example] Two solids (recomputed)
> $N_A=300$, $N_B=200$, $q=100$: peak at $q_A=60$ with $\ln\Omega_{\rm tot}=267.02$, $\ln\Omega_{\rm peak}=264.42$, $\sigma=5.36$.
