# Szilard Engine

> [!info] Source
> Szilard (1929); [[SM06 Entropy Information and the Arrow of Time]] §3.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> One molecule, one bit of knowledge about which half it is in, and an isothermal expansion that turns that bit into work.

> [!key] Key facts
> $W=\int_{V/2}^Vk_BT\,\mathrm dV'/V'=k_BT\ln2$ per cycle; with the measurement information $I=\ln2$: $W\le-\Delta F+k_BT\,I$. Net zero once the memory is erased (Landauer).

> [!example] Numbers (recomputed)
> $k_BT\ln2=2.87\times10^{-21}$ J at 300 K; $4.0\times10^{-23}$ J at 4.2 K.

Used in: [[SM06 Entropy Information and the Arrow of Time]]. Related: [[Landauer Principle]] · [[Maxwell's Demon]] · [[Exergy and Availability]] · [[Mutual Information]].
