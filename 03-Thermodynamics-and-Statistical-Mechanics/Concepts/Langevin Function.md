# Langevin Function

> [!info] Source
> Langevin (1905), recalled; [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]] §5.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Classical magnetic moments free to point anywhere: thermal disorder against alignment, with the orientation integral over the sphere.

> [!key] Key facts
> $L(y)=\coth y-\frac1y\simeq\frac y3-\frac{y^3}{45}$; $Z_1=4\pi\sinh y/y$; $M=N\mu L(\mu B/k_BT)$; $\chi=N\mu^2/3k_BT$. The $J\to\infty$ limit of the Brillouin function.

> [!example] Check (recomputed)
> $L(0.3)=0.09941$ vs the series $0.09940$.
