# Grand Potential

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[df0c4e121858c0be246b496dd8c39d2c_MIT8_333F13_Lec3.pdf#page=3|MIT L3 eqs. I.40–I.41]]. · 🔵 **extra · beyond:** the statistical form $-k_BT\ln Z_G$.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The potential for systems that exchange particles with a reservoir at fixed chemical potential.

> [!key] Definition
> $\tilde G=E-TS-\mu\!\cdot\!N$; $\mathrm d\tilde G=-S\mathrm dT-J\!\cdot\!\mathrm dx-N\!\cdot\!\mathrm d\mu$; minimal in chemical equilibrium at fixed $T,\mu,x$. For a uniform fluid $\tilde G=-PV$.

## Note
The lecture writes it with a tilde to distinguish it from the Gibbs free energy. It is the thermodynamic limit of the grand-canonical ensemble.

> [!pitfall] Different from $G$
> $G(T,J,N)$ keeps $N$ fixed; $\tilde G(T,x,\mu)$ fixes $\mu$ and lets $N$ fluctuate.
