# Number Fluctuations

> [!info] Source
> Huang §7.4 (p. 152); Pathria §4.5; [[SM05 The Grand Canonical Ensemble and Chemical Equilibrium]] §3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> How much the number of molecules in an open region wanders: set by how squashable the fluid is.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\mathrm{Var}N=k_BT(\partial N/\partial\mu)_{T,V}=\langle N\rangle\,nk_BT\kappa_T$. Ideal gas: $\langle N\rangle$ (Poisson). Water at 20 °C: $0.062\langle N\rangle$. At the critical point $\kappa_T\to\infty$ and the fluctuations become macroscopic (critical opalescence). Derived from Gibbs–Duhem: $(\partial n/\partial\mu)_T=n^2\kappa_T$.

> [!example] Water (recomputed)
> $(1\ \mathrm{nm})^3$: $\langle N\rangle=33.4$, $\sigma=1.4$ (ideal gas 5.8); $(10\ \mathrm{nm})^3$: $\sigma/\langle N\rangle=1.4\times10^{-3}$. Light scattering by a fluid $\propto nk_BT\kappa_T$.
