# Enthalpy

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[df0c4e121858c0be246b496dd8c39d2c_MIT8_333F13_Lec3.pdf#page=1|MIT L3 §I.G, eqs. I.30–I.33]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The energy bookkeeping for processes at constant pressure: heat added equals the change in $H$.

> [!key] Definition
> $H=E-J\cdot x$ (fluid: $H=E+PV$); $\mathrm dH=T\mathrm dS-x\,\mathrm dJ$ (fluid $T\mathrm dS+V\mathrm dP$); $C_P=(\partial H/\partial T)_P$; $\delta H\le0$ at $\bar\mathrm dQ=0$ and constant forces.

## Use
At constant $P$, $Q=\Delta H$ (e.g. $2910$ J for 1 mol N$_2$, 300 → 400 K). Atmosphere: dry static energy $c_pT+gz$ is conserved in adiabatic ascent.

> [!pitfall] Different constraint, different potential
> Minimising $H$ requires no heat exchange; with $T$ fixed use $G$ ([[Gibbs Free Energy]]).
