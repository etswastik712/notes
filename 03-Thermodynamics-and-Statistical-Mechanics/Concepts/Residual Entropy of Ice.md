# Residual Entropy of Ice

> [!info] Source
> Pauling (1935), recalled; [[SM02 Phase Space Liouville and the Microcanonical Ensemble]] §4.4.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A crystal that cannot choose a unique proton arrangement keeps a finite entropy per molecule down to $T=0$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $W=(3/2)^N$ from $2^{2N}$ bond placements times the fraction $(6/16)^N$ obeying 'two near, two far'; $S_0=R\ln\frac32=3.371$ J mol$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$ (calorimetric $\approx3.4$).

> [!example] The third law
> $S(0)=k_B\ln g_0$: zero only for a non-degenerate ground state; frozen disorder (ice, CO, glasses) leaves a residue ([[Third Law of Thermodynamics]]).
