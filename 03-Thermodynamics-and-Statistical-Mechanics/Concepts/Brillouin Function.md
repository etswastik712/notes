# Brillouin Function

> [!info] Source
> Kardar §4.7, Pathria §3.9; derived in [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]] §5.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The magnetisation curve of $2J+1$ equally spaced levels: $\tanh$ at $J=\tfrac12$, the Langevin function at $J\to\infty$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $M=Ng\mu_BJ\,\mathcal B_J(y)$, $y=gJ\mu_BB/k_BT$, $\mathcal B_J(y)=\frac{2J+1}{2J}\coth\frac{(2J+1)y}{2J}-\frac1{2J}\coth\frac y{2J}$. Small $y$: $\frac{J+1}{3J}y$ (Curie constant $\propto g^2J(J+1)$). $Z_1=\sinh\frac{(2J+1)y}{2J}/\sinh\frac y{2J}$.

> [!example] Gd$^{3+}$ (recomputed)
> $J=7/2$, $g=2$, 5 T, 2 K: $y=11.76$, $M/M_{\rm sat}=0.990$ (Langevin: $0.915$); Curie constant $63$ vs $3$ for spin ½.
