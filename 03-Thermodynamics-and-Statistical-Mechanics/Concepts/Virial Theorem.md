# Virial Theorem

> [!info] Source
> Clausius (1870); Pathria §3.7 (pp. 63–65); [[SM07 Equipartition the Virial Theorem and Magnetic Systems]] §3–§4.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> For bound motion the average of $\sum\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf p$ cannot drift, so twice the kinetic energy balances the virial of the forces.

> [!key] Key facts
> $2\langle K\rangle=-\langle\sum\mathbf q_i\cdot\mathbf F_i\rangle=s\langle V\rangle$ for $V$ homogeneous of degree $s$. Thermal form: $\langle\sum\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf F\rangle=-3Nk_BT$; wall virial $-3PV$: $PV=Nk_BT-\tfrac13\langle\sum r_{ij}u'(r_{ij})\rangle$.

> [!example] Atoms and stars (recomputed)
> Hydrogen: $K=13.606$, $V=-27.21$, $E=-13.606$ eV. Sun (uniform sphere): $\bar T=GM\mu m_H/5kR=2.8\times10^6$ K; $t_{KH}=3.1\times10^7$ yr.
