# Maxwell's Demon

> [!info] Source
> Maxwell (1867); Blundell §14.7 (p. 149); [[SM06 Entropy Information and the Arrow of Time]] §3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A being that sorts molecules without doing work would break the second law; it does not, because its memory must eventually be erased.

> [!key] Key facts
> Resolution (Bennett 1982): measurement can be reversible; **erasure cannot**, costing $\ge k_BT\ln2$ per bit. Reversing the Joule expansion of one mole needs $N_A$ bits: erasure costs $RT\ln2=1.72$ kJ at 298 K, $=T\times R\ln2$, exactly the entropy decrease achieved.

> [!pitfall] The popular wrong answer
> 'The demon needs light to see the molecules': observation can be done with arbitrarily small dissipation (Blundell, p. 150).

Used in: [[SM06 Entropy Information and the Arrow of Time]]. Related: [[Landauer Principle]] · [[Szilard Engine]] · [[Gibbs Paradox]] · [[Second Law of Thermodynamics]].
