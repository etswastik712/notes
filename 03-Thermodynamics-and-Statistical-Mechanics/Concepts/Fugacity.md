# Fugacity

> [!info] Source
> [[SM05 The Grand Canonical Ensemble and Chemical Equilibrium]] §1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The chemical potential on an exponential scale: the weight of each extra particle.

> [!key] Key facts
> $z=e^{\beta\mu}$; $\mathcal Z=\sum_Nz^NZ_N$; ideal gas: $z=n\lambda^3$ ($=1.69\times10^{-7}$ for N$_2$ at 300 K, 1 atm). Dilute gases have $z\ll1$ (expansion in powers of $z$: the cluster expansion); $z\sim1$ marks quantum degeneracy; condensing Bose gases approach $z\to1$.

> [!pitfall] Not the same as chemical-engineering 'fugacity'
> The thermodynamic fugacity $f$ of a real gas is $P$ corrected so that $\mu=\mu^\circ+k_BT\ln(f/P^\circ)$; for an ideal gas $f=P$ and $z=f\lambda^3/k_BT$.
