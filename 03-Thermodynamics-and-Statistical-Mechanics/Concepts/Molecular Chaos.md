# Molecular Chaos

> [!info] Source
> Kardar §3.4 (p. 71, eq. 3.40); Huang §3.3; [[SM04 Kinetic Theory and the Boltzmann Equation]] §4.2.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Two molecules that are about to collide have not met before, so their momenta are statistically independent: the number of pairs is the product of the numbers.

> [!key] Key facts
> $f_2(\mathbf p_1,\mathbf q;\mathbf p_2,\mathbf q)\to f_1(\mathbf p_1,\mathbf q)f_1(\mathbf p_2,\mathbf q)$ for **incoming** pairs; outgoing pairs are correlated by the collision (constant along the two-body trajectory). This is the only place where the time-asymmetry enters the Boltzmann equation.

> [!pitfall] Before, not after
> Assuming independence *after* collisions gives the same equation with reversed sign: $H$ would increase. The assumption holds for ordinary initial states and fails for the velocity-reversed state of Loschmidt ([[Loschmidt and Zermelo Paradoxes]]); the [[Kac Ring]] makes it exactly testable.

Used in: [[SM04 Kinetic Theory and the Boltzmann Equation]]. Related: [[Boltzmann Equation]] · [[BBGKY Hierarchy]] · [[Irreversibility]] · [[H-Theorem]].
