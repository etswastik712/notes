# Exchange Correlation of the Ideal Gas

> [!info] Source
> Kardar §7.2 (pp. 185–186); Pathria §5.5; [[SM08 Quantum Ensembles and Identical Particles]] §5.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A perfect gas of identical particles is not uncorrelated: bosons come in pairs, same-spin fermions avoid each other, over a thermal wavelength.

> [!key] Key facts
> $g(r)=1\pm e^{-2\pi r^2/\lambda^2}$ ($+$ bosons, $-$ same-spin fermions). Equivalent statistical potential $u_{\rm eff}=-k_BT\ln(1\pm e^{-2\pi r^2/\lambda^2})$. $B_2=-\tfrac12\int(g-1)\mathrm d^3r=\mp\lambda^3/2^{5/2}$ ($\mp\lambda^3/(g_s2^{5/2})$ with spin degeneracy); $P=nk_BT[1\mp n\lambda^3/(g_s2^{5/2})]$.

> [!example] Numbers (recomputed)
> $r=\lambda/2$: $g=1.208$ (bosons), $0.792$ (fermions). Air at 300 K: mean spacing $181\lambda$, exchange factor $\sim10^{-89\,000}$. He-4 at 4.2 K, 1 atm: $P/nkT=0.976$; He-3: $1.018$. Copper electrons: spacing $0.053\lambda$.
