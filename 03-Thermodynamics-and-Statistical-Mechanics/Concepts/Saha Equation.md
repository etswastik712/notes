# Saha Equation

> [!info] Source
> Schroeder §5.6 (pp. 218–219); Pathria §6.6; [[SM05 The Grand Canonical Ensemble and Chemical Equilibrium]] §7.3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Thermal ionisation is a chemical equilibrium: the free electron's huge phase-space volume pays for the ionisation energy at $k_BT$ far below $I$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\dfrac{n_en_p}{n_{\rm H}}=\Big(\dfrac{2\pi m_ek_BT}{h^2}\Big)^{3/2}e^{-I/k_BT}$ ($g_eg_p/g_{\rm H}=1$); ionised fraction $x=2/(1+\sqrt{1+4n/K})$. Photosphere (5800 K): $K=1.6\times10^{15}$ m$^{-3}$; half-ionised at $14\,000$ K ($n=10^{23}$), $9000$ K ($10^{20}$), $4000$ K ($10^{10}$).

> [!example] The Universe (recomputed)
> $n_B=\eta n_\gamma(T)$, $\eta=6.1\times10^{-10}$: Saha half-recombination at $T=3760$ K, $z=1380$ (observed last scattering $z\approx1090$: recombination lags equilibrium).
