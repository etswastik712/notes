# Mayer Relation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[0d0ec730e8e02ed0399d39ff05054a72_MIT8_333F13_Lec1.pdf#page=6|MIT L1 eq. I.15]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Heating at constant pressure also pays for pushing the walls back.

> [!key] Definition
> Ideal gas: $C_P-C_V=P(\partial V/\partial T)_P=PV\alpha_P=Nk_B$ ($R$ per mole). General: $C_P-C_V=TV\alpha_P^2/\kappa_T\ge0$.

## Numbers
Air: $R_d=c_p-c_v=1004.5-717.5=287.0$ J kg$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$. Liquid water: $0.49$ J mol$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$, $<1\%$ of $C_P$ (nearly incompressible).

> [!pitfall] Only ideal gas has $=Nk_B$
> The general expression involves $\alpha_P^2/\kappa_T$; solids and liquids have $C_P\approx C_V$.

Used in: [[TH02 Work Heat and the First Law]], [[TH03 The Ideal Gas and Adiabatic Processes]]. Related: [[Heat Capacity]] · [[Ratio of Specific Heats]] · [[Response Functions]] · [[Joule Expansion]].
