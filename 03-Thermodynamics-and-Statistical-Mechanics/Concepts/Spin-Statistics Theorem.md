# Spin-Statistics Theorem

> [!info] Source
> Stated in [[SM08 Quantum Ensembles and Identical Particles]] §4.1; Pauli (1940); proof from relativistic QFT (not given here).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Particles of integer spin are bosons and those of half-integer spin are fermions; in three dimensions no other exchange phase is possible.

> [!key] Key facts
> Exchange is a rotation by $\pi$ in the centre-of-mass frame; the phase $(-1)^{2s}$ follows from relativistic invariance, positivity of energy and causality (recalled). In two dimensions the exchange phase $e^{\mathrm i\theta}$ is free: **anyons** (fractional statistics).

> [!pitfall] A theorem of field theory
> In non-relativistic quantum mechanics the symmetry type is an independent postulate (Kardar §7.1); composite particles: an even/odd number of fermions makes a boson/fermion ($^4$He boson, $^3$He fermion).

Used in: [[SM08 Quantum Ensembles and Identical Particles]]. Related: [[Identical Particles]] · [[Pauli Exclusion Principle]] · [[Fermi-Dirac Distribution]] · [[Bose-Einstein Distribution]].
