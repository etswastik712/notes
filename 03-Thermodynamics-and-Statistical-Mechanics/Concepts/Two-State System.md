# Two-State System

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Schroeder §2.1 (p. 49); Koks Ch. 1 (the binomial).
> 🟢 **taught (pointer only):** [[TH09 Microstates Macrostates and Multiplicity]] §3.1; the thermodynamics of the same system is in [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]] §5.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $N$ objects each with two states: the simplest system with a macrostate (the number up) whose multiplicity is a binomial.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\Omega=\binom Nk$, $S/Nk_B=-f\ln f-(1-f)\ln(1-f)$, peak at $f=\tfrac12$ with width $\sigma_k=\sqrt N/2$ ($\sigma_f=1/2\sqrt N$). For spins in a field $U=-(N_\uparrow-N_\downarrow)\mu B$, and $1/T=\partial S/\partial U$ gives $T<0$ when more than half are up ([[Negative Temperature]]).

> [!example] 100 coins
> $\Omega(50)=1.0089\times10^{29}$, $P(50)=0.0796$, $P(\ge60)=0.0284$, $P(100)=7.9\times10^{-31}$.
