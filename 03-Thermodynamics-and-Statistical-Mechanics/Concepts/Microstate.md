# Microstate

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Koks §2.1 (p. 83); Reif §2.1; Blundell §4.3 (p. 35).
> 🟢 **taught (pointer only):** [[97dcd8dee3d3301cd7c27745b17b10b5_MIT8_333F13_Lec4.pdf#page=5|MIT L4 §I.J]] for $S=k_B\ln g$; [[TH09 Microstates Macrostates and Multiplicity]] §1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A complete specification of the system at the finest level physics allows: one energy eigenstate, or one cell of phase space.

> [!key] Key facts
> Examples: the ordered pair of two dice; which of 100 coins are heads ($2^{100}=1.27\times10^{30}$); the quanta on each Einstein oscillator ($\binom{q+N-1}{q}$ in all); a cell $h^{3N}$ of phase space. A microstate of an isolated system in equilibrium is equally likely as any other ([[Fundamental Postulate of Statistical Mechanics]]).

> [!pitfall] Every microstate is equally unlikely
> The specific sequence of 100 heads has probability $2^{-100}$, the same as any other specific sequence; the macrostate "100 heads" is rare because it contains one of them.
