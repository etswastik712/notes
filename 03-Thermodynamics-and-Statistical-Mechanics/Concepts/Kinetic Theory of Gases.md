# Kinetic Theory of Gases

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** `KTG_Chapter.pdf` §1.1–1.2 (AI-written; re-derived). · 🔵 **extra · beyond:** the numerical examples.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A gas is a swarm of tiny elastic balls; pressure is their bombardment of the walls.

> [!key] Definition
> $P=\tfrac13nm\langle v^2\rangle$, so $\langle\tfrac12mv^2\rangle=\tfrac32k_BT$; $E=\tfrac32Nk_BT$ for a monatomic gas. Valid for dilute gases ($\lambda\gg d$).

## Numbers
N$_2$, 300 K: $n=2.45\times10^{25}$ m$^{-3}$, $v_{rms}=517$ m s$^{-1}$, $\tfrac32k_BT=38.8$ meV; check $P=nk_BT=101\,325$ Pa.

> [!pitfall] Temperature is a mean
> $T$ is defined by the mean translational energy of a large sample; it has no meaning for one molecule.
