# Kelvin-Helmholtz Time

> [!info] Source
> [[SM07 Equipartition the Virial Theorem and Magnetic Systems]] §4.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> How long a star could shine by contracting alone: its gravitational energy divided by its luminosity.

> [!key] Key facts
> $t_{KH}\sim GM^2/(RL)$; Sun: $9.9\times10^{14}$ s $=3.1\times10^7$ yr ($1.6\times10^7$ yr if half the released energy is radiated, by the virial split), against an age of 4.6 Gyr: contraction cannot power the Sun; nuclear fusion does.

> [!connection] History
> Kelvin used it to bound the age of the Sun and Earth (tens of Myr), in conflict with geology and Darwin (recalled).
