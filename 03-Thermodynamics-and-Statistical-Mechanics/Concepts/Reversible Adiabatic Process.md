# Reversible Adiabatic Process

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[1a131e225246d9749a298f49675c5fe5_MIT8_333F13_Lec2.pdf#page=2|MIT L2 eqs. I.18–I.19]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> No heat in or out and no entropy created: the gas does or receives work at the expense of its internal energy.

> [!key] Definition
> $\bar\mathrm dQ=0$ along a quasi-static path: $PV^\gamma=$ const, $TV^{\gamma-1}=$ const, $T^\gamma P^{1-\gamma}=$ const (ideal gas, constant $\gamma$); $S$ is constant. $W_{\rm by}=(P_1V_1-P_2V_2)/(\gamma-1)=C_V(T_1-T_2)$.

## Numbers
Air compressed by a volume ratio 10 from 300 K: $754$ K, $25.1$ atm; ratio 15: $886$ K, $44.3$ atm. Dry atmosphere: $\mathrm dT/\mathrm dz=-g/c_p=-9.77$ K km$^{-1}$ ([[Adiabatic Process and Potential Temperature]]).

> [!pitfall] Adiabatic does not imply isentropic
> A free expansion is adiabatic but $S$ rises; only the reversible adiabat is an isentrope.
