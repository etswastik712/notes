# Schottky Anomaly

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** derived and computed here.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A two-level system soaks up heat only when the temperature is comparable to its gap: a bump in $C(T)$.

> [!key] Definition
> Two-level, spacing $\varepsilon$, $x=\varepsilon/k_BT$: $C/k_B=x^2e^x/(e^x+1)^2$, peak $0.439$ at $k_BT=0.417\varepsilon$; $S\to0$ as $T\to0$ and $\to k_B\ln2$ per particle at high $T$.

## Values
At $k_BT=\varepsilon$: upper population $0.269$, $S=0.582k_B$, $C=0.197k_B$. The vanishing of $C$ at both ends is the third-law behaviour.

> [!pitfall] Bounded spectrum
> $\langle E\rangle$ saturates at $\varepsilon/2$ per particle: the system cannot absorb arbitrarily much heat, unlike an oscillator or a gas.
