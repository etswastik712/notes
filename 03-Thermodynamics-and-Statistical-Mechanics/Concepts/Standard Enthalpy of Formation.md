# Standard Enthalpy of Formation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** Basis of [[Thermodynamics_Tutorial_2_Questions.pdf#page=1|Tutorial 2]], Q4 (data not supplied in the tutorial); [[TH07 Enthalpy Thermochemistry and Calorimetry]] §2.1.
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Schroeder, data table pp. 404–405 (298 K, 1 bar; data from Atkins 1998, Lide 1994, Anderson 1996).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The enthalpy change of making one mole of a compound from its elements in their most stable forms, all at 1 bar: elements are zero by definition.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\Delta_rH^\circ=\sum\nu\Delta_fH^\circ$. Values (kJ mol$^{-1}$): CO$_2$(g) $-393.51$; H$_2$O(l) $-285.83$; H$_2$O(g) $-241.82$; CO(g) $-110.53$; CH$_4$(g) $-74.81$; NH$_3$(g) $-46.11$; C(diamond) $+1.895$.

> [!example] Methane burning
> $-393.51+2(-285.83)-(-74.81)=-890.36$ kJ mol$^{-1}$ ($55.5$ kJ g$^{-1}$).

Used in: [[TH07 Enthalpy Thermochemistry and Calorimetry]]; [[TH08 Gibbs Energy Equilibrium Constants and Phase Boundaries]] (the same table gives $\Delta_fG^\circ$ and $S^\circ$).. Related: [[Hess's Law]] · [[Reaction Enthalpy]] · [[Gibbs Free Energy]] · [[Third Law of Thermodynamics]].
