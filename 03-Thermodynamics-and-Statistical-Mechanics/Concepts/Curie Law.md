# Curie Law

> [!info] Source
> Kardar §4.7; Pathria §3.9–3.10; [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]] §5.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Thermal agitation fights the field's alignment: magnetisation is $\propto B/T$ at high $T$ and saturates at low $T$.

> [!key] Key facts
> $M=N\mu_B\tanh x$, $x=\mu_BB/k_BT$; $S/Nk=\ln(2\cosh x)-x\tanh x$; $C/Nk=x^2\operatorname{sech}^2x$ (Schottky peak $0.4392$ at $\varepsilon/k_BT=2.399$, $\varepsilon=2\mu_BB$). High $T$: $M=N\mu_B^2B/k_BT$ ($\chi\propto1/T$).

> [!example] Numbers (recomputed)
> $B=1$ T: $\tanh x=0.00224$ (300 K), $0.1586$ (4.2 K), $0.586$ (1 K), $\approx1$ (0.1 K). Curie constant per mole $\mu_0N_A\mu_B^2/k_B=4.714\times10^{-6}$ K m$^3$mol$^{-1}=0.3751$ emu K mol$^{-1}$; $N_A\mu_B=5.585$ J T$^{-1}$mol$^{-1}$.
