# Langevin Diamagnetism

> [!info] Source
> Schwabl §6.2 (pp. 278–279, Table 6.1); [[SM07 Equipartition the Virial Theorem and Magnetic Systems]] §5.4.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A closed-shell atom has no moment of its own, but the field induces a small current whose moment opposes it (Lenz).

> [!key] Key facts
> $E_1=\frac{e^2B^2}{12m}\langle\sum r_i^2\rangle$; $\chi_{\rm mol}=-N_Ar_e\langle\sum r_i^2\rangle/6$ (CGS, $r_e=2.818\times10^{-13}$ cm). Measured $\chi_{\rm mol}$ ($10^{-6}$ cm$^3$mol$^{-1}$): He $-1.9$, Ne $-7.2$, Ar $-15.4$, Kr $-28.0$, Xe $-43.0$.

> [!example] Helium (recomputed)
> $\langle r^2\rangle=1.1935a_0^2$ per electron: $\chi_{\rm mol}=-1.89\times10^{-6}$ cm$^3$mol$^{-1}$ (0.5 % from the measured value); 661 times smaller in magnitude than the spin-½ Curie paramagnetism at 300 K.

Used in: [[SM07 Equipartition the Virial Theorem and Magnetic Systems]]. Related: [[Bohr-van Leeuwen Theorem]] · [[Curie Law]] · [[Adiabatic Demagnetisation]].
