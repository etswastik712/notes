# Rigid Rotor

> [!info] Source
> [[SM07 Equipartition the Virial Theorem and Magnetic Systems]] §2; Pathria §6.5 (planned SM09).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A diatomic molecule's rotation switches on around $T\sim\theta_r$ and overshoots equipartition on the way.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\epsilon_J=k_B\theta_rJ(J+1)$, $g_J=2J+1$; $C_{\rm rot}/k_B=\mathrm{Var}(\epsilon)/(k_BT)^2$; $0.794$ at $0.5\theta_r$, maximum $1.098$ at $0.81\theta_r$, $\to1+\frac1{45}(\theta_r/T)^2+\frac{16}{945}(\theta_r/T)^3$. $\theta_r$: H$_2$ 85 K, N$_2$ 2.9 K, O$_2$ 2.1 K, CO 2.8 K.

> [!pitfall] Homonuclear molecules
> H$_2$ and N$_2$ have ortho/para spin-statistics restrictions on $J$ (SM09, planned).
