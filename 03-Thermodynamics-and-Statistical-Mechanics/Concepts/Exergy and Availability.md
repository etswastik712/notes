# Exergy and Availability

> [!info] Source
> [[SM06 Entropy Information and the Arrow of Time]] §6; from [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]] §2.2.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The most work you can still get out of a non-equilibrium state: the free-energy distance to equilibrium.

> [!key] Key facts
> $W_{\rm avail}=F[p]-F_{\rm eq}=k_BT\,D(p\|p_{\rm eq})$; entropy produced $=k_BD(p\|p_{\rm eq})$. Heat flow: $\dot S=\dot Q(1/T_c-1/T_h)$, lost work $=T_0\dot S$ (Gouy–Stodola). Radiation: exergy flux $J-T_es_{\rm in}$.

> [!example] Numbers (recomputed)
> $N_A$ molecules in half a box at 298 K: $RT\ln2=1.72$ kJ. 100 W from 500 K to 300 K: $\dot S=0.133$ W/K, lost work $40$ W (= Carnot). Sunlight absorbed by the Earth: $224$ W m$^{-2}$ of exergy.
