# Thermal Wavelength

> [!info] Source
> Kardar §4.4; Pathria §1.4; [[SM02 Phase Space Liouville and the Microcanonical Ensemble]] §6 and [[SM03 The Canonical Ensemble]] §4.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The de Broglie wavelength of a particle with thermal momentum: when it is comparable to the spacing between particles, quantum statistics takes over.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\lambda=h/\sqrt{2\pi mk_BT}$. Classical regime: $n\lambda^3\ll1$. Ideal gas: $Z=(V/\lambda^3)^N/N!$, $\mu=k_BT\ln n\lambda^3$, $S=Nk_B[\ln(V/N\lambda^3)+\tfrac52]$.

> [!example] Values (recomputed)
> | system | $\lambda$ (nm) | $n\lambda^3$ |
> |---|---|---|
> | N$_2$, 300 K, 1 atm | 0.0190 | $1.7\times10^{-7}$ |
> | He gas, 300 K | 0.0504 | $3.1\times10^{-6}$ |
> | He gas, 4.2 K | 0.426 | $0.13$ |
> | liquid He-4, 2.17 K | 0.592 | $4.5$ |
> | electrons in Cu, 300 K | 4.30 | $6.8\times10^{3}$ |
