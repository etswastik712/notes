# Pauli Exclusion Principle

> [!info] Source
> [[SM08 Quantum Ensembles and Identical Particles]] §4.1 and §5.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> No two identical fermions can occupy the same one-particle state: the antisymmetrised state with a repeated orbital is the zero vector.

> [!key] Key facts
> $n_k\in\{0,1\}$; $\langle n_k\rangle=1/(e^{\beta(\varepsilon_k-\mu)}+1)\le1$; correlation $g(0)=0$ for same-spin fermions; pressure raised by the exchange term, $P=nk_BT[1+n\lambda^3/(g_s2^{5/2})]$ at low density and $P=\tfrac25nE_F$ at $T=0$ (SM12).

> [!connection] Consequences
> Atomic shells and the periodic table; the stability of matter; white dwarfs and neutron stars (degeneracy pressure); the specific heat of metals (only electrons within $k_BT$ of $E_F$ respond).
