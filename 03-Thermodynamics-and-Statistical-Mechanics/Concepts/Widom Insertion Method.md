# Widom Insertion Method

> [!info] Source
> Widom (1963), recalled; derived in [[SM05 The Grand Canonical Ensemble and Chemical Equilibrium]] §9.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The chemical potential is the free energy of adding one molecule: insert a ghost at random and average its Boltzmann factor.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\mu=\mu_{\rm id}+\mu_{\rm ex}$, $\mu_{\rm ex}=-k_BT\ln\langle e^{-\beta\Delta U}\rangle$ (average over random insertions in the $N$-particle ensemble). Ideal gas: $0$. Hard spheres: $-k_BT\ln p_{\rm fit}$.

> [!pitfall] Dense systems
> $e^{-\beta\Delta U}$ is dominated by rare cavities: the average converges slowly at high density.

Used in: [[SM05 The Grand Canonical Ensemble and Chemical Equilibrium]]. Related: [[Chemical Potential]] · [[Grand Canonical Ensemble]] · [[Helmholtz Free Energy]].
