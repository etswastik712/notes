# Negative Heat Capacity

> [!info] Source
> [[SM07 Equipartition the Virial Theorem and Magnetic Systems]] §4; Lynden-Bell and Wood (1968), recalled.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A bound gravitating system heats up when it loses energy: it contracts, and the virial theorem converts half of the released potential energy into heat.

> [!key] Key facts
> $E=-K=-\tfrac32Nk_B\bar T\Rightarrow C=\mathrm dE/\mathrm d\bar T=-\tfrac32Nk_B$. Impossible in the canonical ensemble ($C=\mathrm{Var}E/kT^2\ge0$; the partition function diverges); allowed in the microcanonical: the ensembles are inequivalent for long-range forces.

> [!example] The Sun (recomputed)
> $C=-4.1\times10^{34}$ J K$^{-1}$.
