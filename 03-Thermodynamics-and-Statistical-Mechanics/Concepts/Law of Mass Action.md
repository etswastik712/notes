# Law of Mass Action

> [!info] Source
> Pathria §6.6 (pp. 170–178); Huang §7.5; Schroeder Ch. 5; [[SM05 The Grand Canonical Ensemble and Chemical Equilibrium]] §7.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> At chemical equilibrium the products of densities, each raised to its stoichiometric power, equal a function of temperature built from partition functions.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\sum\nu_i\mu_i=0\ \Rightarrow\ \prod_in_i^{\nu_i}=\prod_i(z_i^{\rm int}/\lambda_i^3)^{\nu_i}=K_c(T)$; $K_p=K_c(k_BT/P^\circ)^{\sum\nu_i}=e^{-\Delta_rG^\circ/RT}$. Energies in $z^{\rm int}$ measured from a common zero.

> [!example] N$_2$O$_4\rightleftharpoons2$NO$_2$ (recomputed)
> $K_p=0.148$ at 298 K; dissociation $\alpha=\sqrt{K_p/(K_p+4P)}=0.061,\ 0.189,\ 0.52,\ 0.89$ at $10,\ 1,\ 0.1,\ 0.01$ bar.
