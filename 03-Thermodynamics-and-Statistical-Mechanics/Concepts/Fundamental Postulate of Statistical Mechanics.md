# Fundamental Postulate of Statistical Mechanics

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Reif §2.3 (p. 58, djvu printed + 17); Koks §2.1 (p. 85); Schroeder §2.1; [[SM02 Phase Space Liouville and the Microcanonical Ensemble]] §3 for the rigorous discussion.
> 🟢 **taught (pointer only):** [[TH09 Microstates Macrostates and Multiplicity]] §2 (pointer only: MIT L4 §I.J).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> An isolated system in equilibrium is equally likely to be in any accessible microstate; everything else is counting.

> [!key] Key facts
> $P(M)=\Omega(M)/\Omega_{\rm tot}$. Justifications: no state favoured; the uniform density is stationary (Liouville; $\hat\rho=\Pi/\Omega$ commutes with $\hat H$); ergodicity; maximum entropy (least bias). It says nothing about *how fast* equilibrium is reached.

> [!pitfall] Where it fails
> Integrable or not-yet-equilibrated systems, glasses, and any non-isolated system (there the weights are Boltzmann, [[Canonical Ensemble]]).
