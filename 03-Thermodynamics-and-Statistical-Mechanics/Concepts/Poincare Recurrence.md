# Poincare Recurrence

> [!info] Source
> [[SM02 Phase Space Liouville and the Microcanonical Ensemble]] §2.3; Poincaré (1890), Kac (1947), recalled.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A bounded, volume-preserving system must come back; how long it takes is the whole point.

> [!key] Key facts
> Theorem: for $\mu(A)>0$, almost every point of $A$ returns to $A$ infinitely often. Kac: mean return time $=\tau\,\mu(\Gamma)/\mu(A)$ (ergodic case). All molecules in the left half: $2^N\tau$.

> [!example] Numbers (recomputed)
> $\tau=10^{-13}$ s: $N=50$: $113$ s; $N=100$: $1.3\times10^{17}$ s ($0.29$ age of the Universe); $N=200$: $1.6\times10^{47}$ s. Kac ring: return after exactly $N$ steps.
