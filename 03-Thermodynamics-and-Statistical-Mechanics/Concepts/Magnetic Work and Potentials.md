# Magnetic Work and Potentials

> [!info] Source
> Pathria §3.10; Kardar §4.7; [[SM07 Equipartition the Virial Theorem and Magnetic Systems]] §5.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Thermodynamics with $B$ as the intensive variable and $M$ as the extensive one: $F(T,B)$ is the Gibbs-like potential.

> [!key] Key facts
> $\mathcal H=\mathcal H_0-MB$; $\mathrm dF=-S\,\mathrm dT-M\,\mathrm dB$; $M=-\partial_BF$; $(\partial S/\partial B)_T=(\partial M/\partial T)_B$; $\mathrm d\langle\mathcal H_0\rangle=T\mathrm dS+B\mathrm dM$; $\mathrm d\langle\mathcal H\rangle=T\mathrm dS-M\mathrm dB$. Curie: $M=CB/T$.

> [!pitfall] Signs
> $\pm B\,\mathrm dM$ vs $\mp M\,\mathrm dB$ depends on whether the field energy is counted with the sample; the Maxwell relation does not.
