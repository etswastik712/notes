# HW4 (2026-08-27)

## Covered
Homework 4 covers lectures up to August 27th. $M$ is an $m$-dimensional and $N$ an $n$-dimensional smooth manifold; $G$ is a Lie group.

Problems and what each practises:
1. $F: M\to N$ smooth, $q\in N$, $S=F^{-1}(q)$, $dF_p$ surjective for all $p\in S$. Use the rank theorem to prove $S$ is a submanifold of dimension $m-n$, and $T_p S=\ker dF_p$. Practises: rank theorem, preimage of a regular value, slice charts. See [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-08-27-august-24-28]].
2. Given $X_p\in T_pM$, prove there is $X\in\mathfrak X(M)$ with $X(p)=X_p$. Practises: extending a vector to a global field with a chart and a bump function.
3. $M=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2: x,y>0\}$, $F(x,y)=(xy,y/x)$. Show $F$ is a diffeomorphism and find $Y\in\mathfrak X(M)$ that is $F$-related to $X=x\,\partial_x+y\,\partial_y$. Practises: Prop 8.19, $Y=dF\circ X\circ F^{-1}$.
4. If $M$ has a global frame then $TM\cong M\times\mathbb R^m$. Practises: frames and trivialisation of the tangent bundle.
5. Show multiplication $m: G\times G\to G$ is a submersion. Hint: with $a_g(h)=(g,h)$, $m\circ a_g=L_g$.
6. (a) Identify $T_{(e,e)}(G\times G)$ with $T_eG\oplus T_eG$ as in Homework 2; show $dm_{(e,e)}(X,Y)=X+Y$ (hint: compute $dm(X,0)$ and $dm(0,Y)$ separately). (b) Show $di_e(X)=-X$ for the inversion $i$. Practises: differentials of group operations.
