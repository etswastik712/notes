# Test - I (2026-09-03)

## Covered
Test 1 was based on Homework 1, 2 and 3. Each question was worth 5 points; the suggested time was 10-15 minutes per question.

Questions and what each practises:
1. $f,g\in C^\infty(M)$, $p\in M$, $U$ a neighbourhood of $p$ with $f|_U=g|_U$. Show $Xf=Xg$ for every $X\in T_pM$. Practises: tangent vectors are local; proof with a bump function (the solution takes $h=f-g\equiv0$ on $U$, a bump function $\psi$ with $\psi(p)=1$ and $\mathrm{supp}\,\psi\subset U$, so $\psi h=0$ and $0=X(\psi h)=\psi(p)Xh+h(p)X\psi=Xh$).
2. $F: S^2\to S^3$, $F(x,y,z)=(x^2+y^2,\,z^2,\,\sqrt2xz,\,\sqrt2yz)$. Choose atlases and compute $dF$ at $(\tfrac12,\tfrac12,\tfrac1{\sqrt2})$ in local coordinates (hint: choose charts that simplify the calculation). Practises: differential in coordinates.
3. $M=S^2\times S^2\subset\mathbb R^3\times\mathbb R^3$ with the product atlas; $\alpha:(-\tfrac14,\tfrac14)\to M$, $\alpha(t)=(t,\sqrt2t,\sqrt{1-3t^2},\,3t,t,\sqrt{1-10t^2})$ (as printed in the OCR; coefficients may be misread). Compute $\alpha'(0)$ in local coordinates. Practises: velocity of a curve in a product chart.
4. On $S^2$ with the stereographic atlas $A_s$ and the graph-coordinate atlas $A_g$: a vector $X=(1,2)$ at $p=(0,0,1)$ is given in a chart of $A_s$; express it in a chart of $A_g$. Practises: change of coordinates for tangent vectors (Jacobian of the transition map).

A solutions file (Test1 Solutions.pdf) was posted too. Its OCR is poor beyond Q1, so only the Q1 approach is recorded above.
