# HW5 (2026-09-04)

## Covered
Homework 5 relates to lectures up to September 3rd.

Problems and what each practises:
1. $S\subset M$ an immersed submanifold. Show $S$ is embedded if (a) $\dim S=\dim M$; (b) the inclusion $S\subset M$ is proper (preimages of compact sets are compact); (c) $S$ is compact. Practises: immersion vs embedding; open maps, proper maps are closed.
2. $G$ a smooth manifold with a group structure whose multiplication $m$ is smooth. Show $G$ is a Lie group, i.e. smoothness of inversion is redundant. Hint: $F(g,h)=(g,gh)$ is a bijective local diffeomorphism of $G\times G$. Practises: inverse function theorem, rank theorem.
3. With $\det: GL(n,\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R$: (a) for $A\in M(n,\mathbb R)$, $\frac{d}{dt}\big|_{t=0}\det(I_n+tA)=\mathrm{tr}(A)$ (hint: coefficient of $t$ in the polynomial $\det(I+tA)$); (b) for $X\in GL(n,\mathbb R)$ and $B\in T_XGL(n,\mathbb R)\simeq M(n,\mathbb R)$, $d(\det)_X(B)=\det(X)\,\mathrm{tr}(X^{-1}B)$ (hint: $\det(X+tB)=\det X\det(I+tX^{-1}B)$). Practises: differentials of matrix functions.
4. $U$ a neighbourhood of $e\in G$. Show there is a neighbourhood $V$ of $e$ with $V\subset U$ and $gh^{-1}\in U$ whenever $g,h\in V$. Practises: continuity of $(g,h)\mapsto gh^{-1}$ at $(e,e)$.
