# HW10 (2026-11-06)

## Covered
Homework 10 relates to lectures up to November 6th. Problems and what each practises:
1. $G$ connected Lie group, $H$ any Lie group, $\Phi,\Psi:G\to H$ Lie group homomorphisms with $\Phi_*=\Psi_*$. Prove $\Phi=\Psi$. Practises: naturality $\Phi\circ\exp=\exp\circ\Phi_*$ and the fact that $\exp(\mathfrak g)$ generates a connected group ([[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-11-06-october-26-30]]).
2. For a foliation $\mathcal F$ on $M$, the collection of tangent spaces to the leaves is an involutive distribution. Practises: flat charts, Prop 19.3-style bracket argument ([[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-11-06-november-2-6]]).
3. For a diffeomorphism $\Phi:M\to M$ and involutive $D$ with foliation $\mathcal F$: $D$ is $\Phi$-invariant iff $\mathcal F$ is $\Phi$-invariant. Practises: Prop 19.23 (left as homework in class), maximal integral manifolds.
4. Existence and uniqueness of a connected Lie subgroup with a given Lie subalgebra $\mathfrak h$. The post says this theorem is worked out in the notes (Thm 19.26). Practises: Lemma 19.24, Frobenius, weak embeddedness.
5. $F:M\to N$ a smooth submersion; $\mathcal F$ = the connected components of the fibres $F^{-1}(q)$. Show $\mathcal F$ is a foliation of $M$. Practises: Rank Theorem / submersion charts as flat charts.
