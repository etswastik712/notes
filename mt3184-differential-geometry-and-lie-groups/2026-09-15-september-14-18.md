**Lie groups and Lie algebras: the correspondence (DG18)**
- Goal: relate simply connected Lie groups to finite-dimensional Lie algebras via $G\mapsto \mathrm{Lie}(G)$.
- $\mathrm{Lie}(G)$ is the subalgebra of $\mathfrak X(G)$ of left-invariant vector fields; it is well defined and is a Lie algebra.
- The evaluation map $\varepsilon:\mathrm{Lie}(G)\to T_eG$, $X\mapsto X_e$, is a vector space isomorphism (Thm 8.37, see [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-09-10-september-7-12]]).
- If $F:G\to H$ is a Lie group homomorphism, then $F_*:\mathrm{Lie}(G)\to\mathrm{Lie}(H)$ is a Lie algebra homomorphism (Thm 8.44).
- Corollary: if $F:G\to H$ is a Lie group isomorphism, then $F_*$ is a Lie algebra isomorphism.
- Homomorphism Theorem (the slide numbers it "Thm 20.19" by OCR; check): if $\varphi:\mathrm{Lie}(G)\to\mathrm{Lie}(H)$ is a Lie algebra homomorphism and $G$ is simply connected, then there is a unique Lie group homomorphism $F:G\to H$ with $F_*=\varphi$. This makes the map $G\mapsto\mathrm{Lie}(G)$ injective on simply connected groups (isomorphic Lie algebras give isomorphic groups).
- Summary table drawn on the slide: simply connected Lie groups $\to$ finite-dimensional Lie algebras, $G\mapsto\mathrm{Lie}(G)$; well defined; injective by the Homomorphism Theorem; surjective by Lie's Third Theorem (see [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-10-10-october-5-9]]).
