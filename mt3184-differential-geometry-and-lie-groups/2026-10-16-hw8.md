# HW8 (2026-10-16)

## Covered
Homework 8 relates to lectures up to October 16th (the PDF's own title line reads "HOMEWORK 7", which is an error in the file). Problems and what each practises: (Classroom: HW8)
1. For an integral curve $\gamma:J\to M$ of $V$ and $a\in\mathbb R$, $\tilde\gamma(t)=\gamma(at)$ is an integral curve of $aV$ on $\tilde J=\{t:at\in J\}$. Practises: chain rule for curves.
2. Every compactly supported vector field is complete. Practises: Uniform Time Lemma 9.15 ([[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-10-16-october-12-16]]).
3. If $\gamma:(-\infty,b)\to M$ is a maximal integral curve of $V$, then for any $t_0<b$, $\gamma([t_0,b))$ is not contained in any compact subset of $M$ (escape lemma). Practises: contradiction with maximality via the uniform time on a compact set.
4. For a compact submanifold $S$ and $V$ nowhere tangent to $S$, there is $\varepsilon>0$ such that the flow restricts to a smooth embedding $\Phi:(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\times S\to M$. Practises: Flowout Theorem 9.20, compactness to take a uniform $\varepsilon$.
5. For $v\in\mathbb R^n$ and $W\in\mathfrak X(\mathbb R^n)$, with $V=\sum_iv^i\partial/\partial x^i$, show $D_vW(p)=(\mathcal L_VW)_p$. Practises: computing the Lie derivative from the flow $\theta_t(p)=p+tv$.
6. Give smooth fields $V,\tilde V,W$ on $\mathbb R^2$ with $V=\tilde V=\partial/\partial x$ along the $x$-axis but $\mathcal L_VW\neq\mathcal L_{\tilde V}W$ at the origin. Practises: the Lie derivative depends on the derivative of $V$ transverse to its integral curves, not only on $V$ along a curve.
