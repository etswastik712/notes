# HW1 (2026-08-07)

## Covered
Homework 1 relates to lectures up to August 6th and is not graded. $M$ is an $m$-dimensional and $N$ an $n$-dimensional smooth manifold. (The PDF's own title says "MT3128".)

Problems and what each practises:
1. (a) Every smooth atlas is contained in a maximal smooth atlas. (b) For smooth atlases $\mathcal A_1,\mathcal A_2$ of $M$: they induce the same smooth structure iff $\mathcal A_1\cup\mathcal A_2$ is a smooth atlas. Practises: maximal atlases and compatibility of charts.
2. $\mathcal A_g=\{(U_i^\pm,\varphi_i^\pm)\}_{i=1}^3$ is the graph atlas of $S^2$, with $U_i^\pm=\{x\in S^2:\pm x^i>0\}$ and $\varphi_i^\pm$ dropping the $i$-th coordinate. $\mathcal A_s=\{(V_1,\psi_1),(V_2,\psi_2)\}$ is the stereographic atlas, with $V_1=S^2\setminus(0,0,1)$ and $V_2=S^2\setminus(0,0,-1)$; $\psi_1$ (resp. $\psi_2$) sends $p$ to the point where the line through $(0,0,1)$ (resp. $(0,0,-1)$) and $p$ meets the plane $z=0$.
 (a) Show $\mathcal A_g$ is an atlas by checking the transition map between $U_1^+$ and $U_3^+$ is smooth (the others are similar).
 (b) Give formulas for $\psi_1,\psi_2$ by Euclidean geometry and show $\mathcal A_s$ is an atlas.
 (c) Show $\mathcal A_s$ and $\mathcal A_g$ induce the same smooth structure (check compatibility for one pair of charts).
 Practises: transition maps, computing stereographic projection.
3. $F: M\to N$ continuous. Show $F$ is smooth at $p$ if there are charts $(U,\varphi)\ni p$, $(V,\psi)\ni F(p)$ with $\psi\circ F\circ\varphi^{-1}$ smooth on $\varphi(U\cap F^{-1}(V))$. Practises: the definition of smoothness in local coordinates.
4. $M$ of dimension $m>0$ has infinitely many distinct smooth structures. Hint: show $F_n(x)=|x|^{n-1}x$ is a homeomorphism of the ball $B^n$ to itself, a diffeomorphism iff $n=1$. Practises: distinct smooth structures versus diffeomorphic ones.
