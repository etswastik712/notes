# HW7 (2026-10-10)

## Covered
Homework 7 relates to lectures up to October 9th. Problems and what each practises: (Classroom: HW7)
1. Up to isomorphism there is exactly one 1-dimensional and two 2-dimensional (real) Lie algebras; show all three are isomorphic to Lie subalgebras of $\mathfrak{gl}(2,\mathbb R)$ without Ado's theorem. Practises: classifying low-dimensional Lie algebras (bracket determined by $[e_1,e_2]$), explicit matrix realisations.
2. For a Lie group homomorphism $F:G\to H$, show $\ker(F_*:\mathrm{Lie}(G)\to\mathrm{Lie}(H))$ is the Lie algebra of the Lie subgroup $\ker F$. Practises: constant rank, $T_e\ker F=\ker dF_e$, Thm 8.44.
3. (a) $\mathrm{Lie}(SO(n))=\{A\in\mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb R): A^T+A=0\}$. (b) $\mathrm{Lie}(SO(3))\cong\mathbb R^3$ with the cross product as bracket. Practises: equivariant rank argument for $A\mapsto AA^T$ ([[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-10-10-october-5-9]]), computing brackets of skew matrices.
4. For a maximal integral curve $\gamma$ of $X\in\mathfrak X(M)$, show $\gamma$ is constant, injective, or non-constant periodic ($\exists T>0$ with $\gamma(t+T)=\gamma(t)$ for all $t$). Practises: uniqueness of maximal integral curves, flow group law.
5. Compute the flow of $V=y\,\partial_x+\partial_y$ on $\mathbb R^2$. Practises: solving $\dot x=y,\ \dot y=1$.
6. If $M$ is a compact manifold admitting a nowhere-vanishing vector field, show there is a smooth $F:M\to M$ homotopic to the identity with no fixed points. Practises: compactness gives a global flow, $F=\theta_t$ for small $t$, regular points have no fixed points for small times (uniform time lemma).
