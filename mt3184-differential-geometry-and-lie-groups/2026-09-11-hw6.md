# HW6 (2026-09-11)

## Covered
Homework 6 relates to lectures up to September 12th.

Problems and what each practises:
1. $\theta$ an action of $G$ on $M$. Show the stabiliser $G_p$ of $p\in M$ is an embedded Lie subgroup of $G$. Practises: equivariant rank theorem, orbit map ([[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-09-10-september-7-12]]).
2. For each $n\ge1$ prove $U(n)$ is an embedded $n^2$-dimensional Lie subgroup of $GL(n,\mathbb C)$. Practises: stabiliser / regular-level-set argument.
3. After reading the notes on semidirect products: (a) $G\cong N\rtimes H$ iff there are Lie group homomorphisms $\varphi: G\to H$ and $\psi: H\to G$ with $\varphi\circ\psi=\mathrm{Id}_H$ and $\ker\varphi\cong N$. (b) $O(n)\cong SO(n)\rtimes O(1)$. (c) $GL(n,\mathbb C)\cong SL(n,\mathbb C)\rtimes\mathbb C^*$. Practises: split exact sequences, recognising semidirect products.
4. For $X,Y\in\mathfrak X(M)$ prove $[X,Y]=XY-YX\in\mathfrak X(M)$. Practises: derivations (the commutator of two derivations is a derivation).
5. For $X=x\,\partial_y-y\,\partial_x$ and $Y=y\,\partial_z-z\,\partial_y$ in $\mathfrak X(\mathbb R^3)$, compute $[X,Y]$. Practises: bracket calculation in coordinates.
6. If $G$ is an abelian Lie group, show $\mathrm{Lie}(G)$ is abelian, $[X,Y]=0$. Hint: inversion is a group homomorphism.
7. Treat $\det: GL(n,\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R^*$ as a Lie group homomorphism and show the induced Lie algebra homomorphism is $\mathrm{tr}:\mathfrak{gl}(n,\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R$. Practises: induced Lie algebra map, with HW5 Q3.

**Semidirect product notes (file "DG Semidirect products.pdf")**
- For a smooth action $\theta: H\times N\to N$ with each $\theta_h: N\to N$ a group isomorphism (automorphism), $N\rtimes_\theta H$ is the Lie group with smooth structure of $N\times H$ and group operation $(n_1,h_1)(n_2,h_2)=(n_1\,\theta_{h_1}(n_2),\,h_1h_2)$.
