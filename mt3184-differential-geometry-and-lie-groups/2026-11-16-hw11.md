# HW11 (2026-11-15)

## Covered
Homework 11 relates to lectures up to November 13th. Problems and what each practises:
1. For a smooth right action $\theta$ of $G$ on $M$ with infinitesimal generator $\hat\theta:\mathrm{Lie}(G)\to\mathfrak X(M)$, show $X$ and $\hat\theta(X)$ are $\theta^{(p)}$-related for $X\in\mathrm{Lie}(G)$, $p\in M$. The post says this is worked out in the notes. Practises: orbit map, $\theta^{(p)}$-relatedness (Thm 20.15, [[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-11-16-november-9-13]]).
2. Show $\mathrm{ad}:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak{gl}(\mathfrak g)$, $\mathrm{ad}(X)Y=[X,Y]$, is a Lie algebra representation. Practises: Jacobi identity, definition of a representation (linear, bracket-preserving).
3. Show $\det e^A=e^{\mathrm{tr}A}$. Practises: matrix exponential (Prop 20.2), $\exp$ of matrices and its naturality ([[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-10-24-october-19-24]]).
4. Show the infinitesimal generator of the action of $G$ on itself by right translation is the inclusion $\mathrm{Lie}(G)\hookrightarrow\mathfrak X(G)$. Practises: definition $\hat\theta(X)_g=\frac{d}{dt}|_0\,g\exp(tX)$, left-invariant fields and their flows.
5. $G$ connected: show $\ker(\mathrm{Ad}:G\to GL(\mathfrak g))$ is the center $Z(G)$. Practises: $\mathrm{Ad}(g)=(C_g)_*$, and that homomorphisms from a connected group with equal differentials agree ([[mt3184-differential-geometry-and-lie-groups/2026-11-06-hw10]] Q1).
6. Center of a Lie algebra: $Z(\mathfrak g)=\{X:[X,Y]=0\ \forall Y\}$. For $G$ connected, show $Z(\mathrm{Lie}(G))=\mathrm{Lie}(Z(G))$. Practises: $\mathrm{Ad}_*=\mathrm{ad}$ (Thm 20.27), kernels and Lie algebras, Q5.

## From the post
This homework deals with infinitesimal generators of group actions, and the Lie correspondence between simply connected Lie groups and finite dimensional Lie algebras.
