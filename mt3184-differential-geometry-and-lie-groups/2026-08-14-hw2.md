# HW2 (2026-08-14)

## Covered
Homework 2 relates to lectures up to August 13th. $M$ is an $m$-dimensional and $N$ an $n$-dimensional smooth manifold, and $S^2\subset\mathbb R^3$ is the unit sphere. (The PDF's own title says "MT3128".)

Problems and what each practises:
1. $p\in M$, $v\in T_pM$, and $f,g\in C^\infty(M)$ agree on a neighbourhood of $p$. Show $v(f)=v(g)$. Practises: locality of tangent vectors (derivations).
2. For $(p,q)\in M\times N$ with projections $\pi_1,\pi_2$: (a) show $M\times N$ is an $(m+n)$-dimensional smooth manifold; (b) show $\alpha: T_{(p,q)}(M\times N)\to T_pM\oplus T_qN$, $\alpha(v)=(d(\pi_1)_{(p,q)}v,\,d(\pi_2)_{(p,q)}v)$ is an isomorphism. Practises: product manifolds and their tangent spaces. This identification is used later in HW4 Q6.
3. On the stereographic atlas $\{(U,\varphi),(V,\psi)\}$ of $S^2$, with $p=(\sqrt3/2,\,1/2,\,0)$, $X=(1,2)$ a tangent vector at $p$ in the basis from $(U,\varphi)$, and $f$ given in $(U,\varphi)$ coordinates by $f(u^1,u^2)=u^1+2(u^2)^2$: (a) compute $Xf$; (b) express $X$ in the basis from $(V,\psi)$. Practises: tangent vectors in coordinates and change of basis under a change of chart.
4. $F: S^2\to S^2$, $F(x,y,z)=(-x,-y,-z)$, at $p=(0,0,1)$: (a) write $F$ in local coordinates (choose suitable charts); (b) show $F$ is smooth at $p$ and find its derivative there in local coordinates. Practises: local expressions of maps and the differential as a Jacobian.
5. The basis of $T_x\mathbb R$ is $\frac{d}{dt}\big|_x$. For $v\in T_pM$, show there are $\varepsilon>0$ and a curve $\alpha:(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\to M$ with $\alpha(0)=p$ and $d\alpha_p(\frac{d}{dt}|_0)=v$. Practises: tangent vectors as velocities of curves.
