# Dispersion Relation

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The relation $\omega(k)$ between frequency and wavenumber of the modes of a linear equation; it contains all the physics.

> [!key] Key facts
> Wave: $\omega=ck$; deep water $\sqrt{gk}$; free quantum particle $\hbar k^2/2m$; diffusion $-iDk^2$. Obtained by inserting $e^{i(kx-\omega t)}$.

## In this vault
Waves, [[Gaussian Wave Packet]] spreading, diffusion in [[LS12 Hillslope Transport and the Diffusion Equation]].

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Curvature of $\omega(k)$ means dispersion (shape change).

Used in: [[Fourier and Laplace Transforms]]. Related: [[Phase and Group Velocity]] · [[Fourier Transform]].
