# Phase and Group Velocity

> [!intuition] In one picture
> $v_p=\omega/k$ is the speed of the crests; $v_g=\mathrm d\omega/\mathrm dk$ is the speed of the envelope (energy).

> [!key] Key facts
> Deep water $v_g=v_p/2$ (100 m wave: 12.5 and 6.25 m/s); free quantum particle $v_g=2v_p$ ($\hbar k/m$). Envelope spreads if $\omega''\ne0$.

## In this vault
Swell arrives sorted by period; electron packets spread in $3\times10^{-14}$ s from 1 nm.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Information does not travel at $v_p$ in general.
