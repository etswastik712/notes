# Uncertainty Principle (Fourier)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A function and its Fourier transform cannot both be narrow: $\Delta x\,\Delta k\ge\frac12$.

> [!key] Key facts
> Proof by Cauchy–Schwarz and $\int xf\bar f'=-\frac12$; equality only for Gaussians. Numbers: Gaussian $0.500$, sech $\pi/6$, triangle $0.548$, $e^{-\lvert x\rvert}$ $0.707$. Times $\hbar$: $\Delta x\Delta p\ge\hbar/2$.

## In this vault
QM: [[Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle]], Robertson in [[Dirac Algebra]] §8. Signals: $\Delta t\,\Delta\omega\ge1/2$.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> A statement about spreads of $\lvert f\rvert^2$ and $\lvert\tilde f\rvert^2$, not about measurement disturbance.
