# Critical Slowing Down

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Near a tipping point the dominant pole approaches zero, so recovery from perturbations becomes ever slower.

> [!key] Key facts
> Relaxation time $\tau_0/(1-r)$: $2\tau_0$ at $1-r=0.5$, $10\tau_0$ at $0.1$, $100\tau_0$ at $0.01$. Signals: rising autocorrelation and variance.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Needs a time series long and stationary enough to estimate recovery rates.
