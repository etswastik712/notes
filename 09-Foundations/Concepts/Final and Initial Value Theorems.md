# Final and Initial Value Theorems

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Read $f(0^+)$ and $f(\infty)$ directly from $F(s)$ without inverting.

> [!key] Key facts
> $f(0^+)=\lim_{s\to\infty}sF(s)$; $f(\infty)=\lim_{s\to0}sF(s)$ if all poles of $sF$ lie in the left half-plane (e.g. $F=1/s(s+2)\to1/2$).

## In this vault
Steady-state gain of a relaxing system.

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Final value theorem fails with right-half-plane or imaginary-axis poles.
