# Convolution Theorem

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Convolution in one domain is multiplication in the other.

> [!key] Key facts
> Fourier (vault convention): $\widetilde{f*g}=\sqrt{2\pi}\tilde f\tilde g$ (no $\sqrt{2\pi}$ in the $\omega$ convention). Laplace: $\mathcal L\{f*g\}=FG$. Proof: Fubini and a shift.

## In this vault
Why a linear time-invariant system is a transfer function. Why $\varphi_{X+Y}=\varphi_X\varphi_Y$ for independent variables (basis of the CLT).

> [!pitfall] Watch out
> Check which convolution (linear or cyclic).
