# Foundations: Course Map

Support material shared by several subjects: calculus-flavoured tools, integral transforms, stability and dynamics, Dirac's formalism. Nothing here is a course of its own; each note is linked from the subjects that use it.

## Chapters
- [[Fourier and Laplace Transforms]]: Fourier series and transform (properties, uncertainty, distributions, sampling, DFT/FFT, PDEs, dispersion), Laplace transform (initial-value problems, transfer functions, poles and stability, critical slowing down), characteristic functions and the CLT, the DFT as the character table of $\mathbb Z_N$; used by QM, Waves, Probability, Thermodynamics, Climate, Landscapes, Group Theory.
- [[Dirac Algebra]]: kets, bras, operators, completeness, $x$ and $p$ representations, translation, Robertson, time evolution (Sakurai Ch. 1); used by [[Quantum Mechanics - Course Map]] and [[QM08 Quantum Tunnelling]].
