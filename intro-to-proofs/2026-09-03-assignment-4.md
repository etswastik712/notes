# Assignment-4

## Covered
Problem sheet (9 questions) on posets, extremal elements, equivalence relations and partitions (Classroom: Assignment-4.pdf).

| Q | Problem | Practises |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $P=\{1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24\}$ ordered by divisibility ($a\le b\iff a\mid b$), $S=\{2,3,4,6\}$: minimal/maximal elements, minimum/maximum, lower/upper bounds in $P$, glb/lub | extremal elements vs. bounds in a divisibility poset |
| 2 | $(\wp(X),\subseteq)$, $X=\{1,2,3,4\}$, $S=\{\{1\},\{2\},\{1,2\},\{1,3\},\{2,3\}\}$: same four parts | same, in a power-set poset |
| 3 | $\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z$ with product order $(a,b)\le(c,d)\iff a\le c,\ b\le d$; $S=\{(0,1),(1,0),(1,2),(2,1)\}$: same four parts (bounds in $\mathbb Z^2$) | same, in a product order |
| 4 | All subspaces of $\mathbb R^3$ ordered by inclusion; $U=\mathrm{span}\{(1,0,0),(0,1,0)\}$, $V=\mathrm{span}\{(0,1,0),(0,0,1)\}$, $S=\{U,V\}$: minimal/maximal, min/max, bounds, glb and lub | glb = intersection, lub = sum of subspaces |
| 5 | Decide whether each is an equivalence relation and find the classes: (a) in $\mathbb R^2$, $P\sim Q$ means $P,Q$ lie on the same line; (b) on $\mathbb Z$, $x\sim y\iff x\mid y$; (c) on $\mathbb Z$, $x\sim y\iff n\mid x-y$ for a fixed $n$ | checking reflexive / symmetric / transitive; finding classes |
| 6 | Example of a relation that is reflexive and transitive but not symmetric | building examples with prescribed properties |
| 7 | Example of a relation that is not reflexive, not symmetric, but transitive | same |
| 8 | Prove: (a) a minimum of a poset, if it exists, is unique; (b) every nonempty finite poset has a maximal element, and likewise a minimal element; (c) two equivalence classes are equal or disjoint | proofs: antisymmetry, finiteness argument, class argument |
| 9 | Prove that an equivalence relation $\sim$ on $A$ gives a partition $P_\sim$; conversely a partition $P$ gives an equivalence relation $\sim_P$ | equivalence relations $\leftrightarrow$ partitions |
