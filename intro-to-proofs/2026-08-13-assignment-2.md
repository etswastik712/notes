# Assignment-2

## Covered
Topics: conditionals, quantifiers and negation.

Logical facts given on the sheet:
- $\lnot(P\Rightarrow Q)\sim(P\land\lnot Q)$
- $\lnot(\forall x,\,P(x))\sim(\exists x,\,\lnot P(x))$
- $\lnot(\exists x,\,P(x))\sim(\forall x,\,\lnot P(x))$

Problems and what each practises:
1. $P$: "$n$ divisible by 6", $Q$: "$n$ divisible by 3". Write $P\Rightarrow Q$ as if-then, "only if", "is sufficient for", "is necessary for"; give converse and contrapositive in English and say which is equivalent to the original: forms of the conditional.
2. Negate conditionals so the English says exactly how they fail: (a) $x>2\Rightarrow x^2>4$; (b) $n$ prime $\Rightarrow n$ odd; (c) $a,b\in\mathbb R$, $ab=0\Rightarrow a=0$ or $b=0$: negating $P\Rightarrow Q$ as $P\land\lnot Q$.
3. Symbolise with domains: every integer has an additive inverse in $\mathbb Z$; some rational has square 2; no real has square $-1$; not every real is greater than its square.
4. Negate and give a further natural English phrasing: every student submitted every assignment; some integer is greater than every natural number; at least one real satisfies $x^2+x+1=0$.
5. Universe $\mathbb R$: for (a) $\forall x\,\exists y\,(x+y=0)$, (b) $\exists y\,\forall x\,(x+y=0)$, (c) $\forall x\,\forall y\,(x<y\lor y\le x)$, (d) $\exists x\,\exists y\,(x^2+y^2<0)$: English sentence, negation in symbols and English, truth values of both.
6. Compare $A:\forall x\in\mathbb R\,\exists y\in\mathbb R\,(y>x)$ and $B:\exists y\in\mathbb R\,\forall x\in\mathbb R\,(y>x)$: translate, truth values, negations, and explain why quantifier order matters.
7. Predicate $L(x,y)$: "student $x$ likes book $y$". Symbolise and negate: every student likes at least one book; a book every student likes; some student likes no book; no student likes every book; say which pairs are negations of each other.
8. Push negation through quantifiers and connectives, then state in English: (a) $\lnot(\forall x\in\mathbb Z,\exists y\in\mathbb Z;(x<y\land y\le x+5))$; (b) $\lnot(\exists a\in\mathbb R,\forall b\in\mathbb Z;(b<a\Rightarrow b^2<a^2))$; (c) $\lnot(\forall m\in\mathbb Z,\forall n\in\mathbb Z;(m\mid n\Rightarrow m\le|n|))$.
