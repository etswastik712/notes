# Allele and Genotype Frequencies

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[09_Hardy-Weinberg.pdf#page=5|EE09 slide 5]]; Templeton p. 30

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Count gene copies, not individuals: each homozygote has two copies of its allele, each heterozygote one of each.

> [!key] Definition
> With genotype frequencies $P,Q,R$: $p=P+Q/2$, $q=R+Q/2$ — true for **any** population. For $k$ alleles: $p_i=$ (homozygote $i$) + ½ Σ (heterozygotes containing $i$). For a recessive trait with only two visible classes, $\hat q=\sqrt{\text{freq(aa)}}$ **assumes** HWE (0 degrees of freedom, untestable).

Pueblo MN 83/46/11: $p=0.757$. Acid phosphatase: $p_A=0.3515$, $p_B=0.6095$, $p_C=0.039$.
