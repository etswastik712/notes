# Heritability

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span> <span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Freeman & Herron pp. 84–85 (Computing Consequences 3.1)
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** breeder's equation example, EE10 §2

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The share of trait variation that is passed from parents to offspring; without it, selection changes nothing in the next generation.

> [!key] Definition
> Estimated as the slope of mid-offspring on mid-parent values (0 = no resemblance, 1 = perfect). Pitfalls: misidentified (extra-pair) paternity and nest parasitism bias it **down**; shared environments and maternal effects bias it **up**. Daphne Major beak depth: **$h^2=0.65\pm0.15$** after excluding the 20 % extra-pair chicks (Keller et al.). Response to selection: $R=h^2S$.

Used in: [[EE10 Natural Selection]]. Related: [[Natural Selection]] · [[Darwin's Finches on Daphne Major]].
