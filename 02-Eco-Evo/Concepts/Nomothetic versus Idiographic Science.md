# Nomothetic versus Idiographic Science

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Pigliucci (2002) and Mayr's *detailed readings* ([[Pigliucci_Philosophy of Ecology.pdf#page=2|Pigliucci, p.88]]; [[Extract from Growth of Biological Thought_Mayr.pdf#page=51|Mayr, PDF p.51]]). Not examinable, but it is the reasoning behind the slide "Why is there no apparent structure to the subject?" ([[01-IntroToBiology_-07-Aug-2026.pdf#page=1|slide 1]]).

> [!intuition] A physics lab and a detective
> A physicist splits an atom on Monday or on Friday, in Delhi or in Oslo, and gets the same answer: the atom has no history. A detective reconstructs one unrepeatable crime from traces. Ecology and evolution are a mixture: their organisms are the outcome of a long history (detective work), yet they also obey regularities (physics-like).

> [!key] Definitions (Windelband 1894)
> **Nomothetic:** seeks general *laws* (physics, chemistry). **Idiographic:** describes the *unique and individual* and reconstructs events as a coherent narrative (history, palaeontology). Pigliucci's thesis: ecology and evolutionary biology sit **between** the two, and feeling like a "soft science" comes from not appreciating that.

- Consequences of historicity: generalisations have **short half-lives** (Pigliucci); context changes; generalisations are probabilistic, and Mayr quotes the wit that "all biological laws have exceptions". Physics is not free of the problem either: the three-body problem has no closed-form solution because of its sensitivity to initial conditions.
- **Meehl's list** (Pigliucci Box 1) of shared difficulties with psychology includes: non-linear divergent causality, contingency, unknown critical events, feedback loops, sheer number of variables, limited correspondence of lab and field results, inconsistent results across labs, importance of outliers ([[Pigliucci_Philosophy of Ecology.pdf#page=4|PDF p.4]]).
- Mayr: explanation in evolutionary biology often takes the form of a **historical narrative**, where earlier events make a causal contribution to later ones (the end-Cretaceous extinction vacated niches for the mammalian radiation).

> [!connection] Climate and Earth history
> Earth's own history is idiographic, while the energy balance is nomothetic: the same blend runs through [[CL04 Feedback and Multiple Steady States]] (Snowball Earth is a one-off event explained with general physics) and [[Evolution of Earth and Life - Course Map]].
