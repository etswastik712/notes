# Extended Evolutionary Synthesis

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Evolution_06-Oct-2026.pdf#page=59|EE08 slides 59–85]]
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Pigliucci 2009 (abstract and introduction)

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The proposal that heredity is wider than genes (epigenome, horizontal transfer), so the Modern Synthesis needs widening, not replacing.

> [!key] Definition
> Three MS concepts questioned: (1) features chiefly set by genes → gene × epigenome, environment alters the epigenome; (2) acquired traits not inherited → some are (transgenerational epigenetic inheritance); (3) information passes only within species → horizontal gene transfer, lineages blurred. Pigliucci: an **expansion** of the MS (evo-devo, expanded heredity, evolvability, levels of selection), not a paradigm shift.

The slides' *Elysia chlorotica* HGT example is marked 'recent evidences suggest that no HGT has happened'. No observation contradicts the MS (slide 58): the debate is about scope.
