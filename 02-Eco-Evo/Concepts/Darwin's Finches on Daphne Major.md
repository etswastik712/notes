# Darwin's Finches on Daphne Major

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span> <span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Selection_Class Lecture.pdf#page=3|EE10 slides 2–3]]
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Freeman & Herron §3.4, pp. 81–90

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A tiny island where a drought selected for deep beaks and the next generation inherited them: evolution watched in real time.

> [!key] Definition
> *Geospiza fortis*, ~1200 birds, monitored since 1973 by P. & R. Grant. **1977 drought**: 24 mm rain (usual 130), 84 % died; only deep-beaked birds could crack *Tribulus*; 1978 chicks deeper-beaked. **1983 El Niño**: 1359 mm, small birds favoured. **2003–04 drought** with *G. magnirostris* present: selection for smaller beaks. Lessons: evolution can be rapid; selection shifts and reverses; zig-zags look gradual in fossils.
