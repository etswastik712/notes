# Connected Space

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[proofs-prems-topo.pdf#page=78|AI notes ch. 18.5]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> It cannot be split into two separated open pieces.

> [!key] Definition
> Disconnected if $X=U\cup V$ with $U,V$ disjoint, non-empty, open (equivalently, a proper non-empty subset is open and closed). $A\subseteq\mathbb R$ is connected iff it is an interval. Continuous images of connected sets are connected.

$\mathbb Z$, $\mathbb Q$ (split at $\sqrt2$), $[0,1]\cup[2,3]$, $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$ are disconnected; $\mathbb R$, intervals connected. Discrete spaces with $\ge2$ points are disconnected.

Used in: [[PA07 Metric and Topological Spaces]]. Related: [[Intermediate Value Theorem]] · [[Continuous Function (Topological)]] · [[Open and Closed Sets]].
