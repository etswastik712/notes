# Zorn's Lemma

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Zorn's_lemma.pdf#page=1|instructor's note]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> If every chain can be capped, something is at the very top.

> [!key] Definition
> A **non-empty** poset in which every chain has an upper bound has a maximal element. Equivalent to the Axiom of Choice.

**Pattern:** (1) poset of partial objects ordered by inclusion; (2) non-empty; (3) a chain's union is an upper bound; (4) a maximal element has the property. Finite case: a finite non-empty poset has a maximal element (follow a strictly increasing sequence). $(\mathbb N,\le)$ is a chain with no upper bound and no maximal element.

Used in: [[PA03 Relations Equivalence and Order]]. Related: [[Basis of a Vector Space]] · [[Axiom of Choice]] · [[Partially Ordered Set]].
