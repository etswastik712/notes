# Basis for a Topology

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=614|Lee App. A, p. 596]]; Munkres §13.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A menu of 'round' open sets: every open set is a union of menu items, and any two items can be refined at a common point.

> [!key] Definition
> A family $\mathcal B$ of subsets of $X$ is a **basis** if it covers $X$ and, for $x\in B_1\cap B_2$, some $B_3\in\mathcal B$ has $x\in B_3\subseteq B_1\cap B_2$. It generates the topology whose open sets are the unions of basis sets.

Open balls (metric space), intervals with rational endpoints ($\mathbb R$ is second-countable), boxes $U\times V$ (product topology). Continuity of $f:X\to Y$ can be checked on a basis of $Y$.

Used in: [[PA11 Subspaces, Products and Quotients]]. Related: [[Topological Space]] · [[Product Topology]] · [[Second-Countable Space]].
