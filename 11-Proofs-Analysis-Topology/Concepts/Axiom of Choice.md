# Axiom of Choice

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[proofs-prems-topo.pdf#page=28|AI notes ch. 4.3]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> You may pick one element from each of any family of non-empty sets, even infinitely many, without a rule.

> [!key] Definition
> Every family of non-empty, pairwise disjoint sets has a **choice set** meeting each member in exactly one point. Equivalent to Zorn's lemma and to the well-ordering theorem.

Consequences: every vector space has a basis; non-constructive objects such as a Hamel basis of $\mathbb R$ over $\mathbb Q$; Banach–Tarski. It is independent of the other ZF axioms.

Used in: [[PA02 Sets Families and the ZFC Foundations]], [[PA03 Relations Equivalence and Order]]. Related: [[Zorn's Lemma]] · [[ZFC Axioms]] · [[Basis of a Vector Space]].
