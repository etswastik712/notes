# Universal Cover

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=634|Lee Prop. A.80, p. 616]]; Hatcher §1.3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The most unwrapped version of a space: all its loops cut open, so the cover is simply connected.

> [!key] Definition
> A covering $\widetilde X\to X$ with $\widetilde X$ simply connected. It exists for connected, locally path-connected, semilocally simply connected $X$ (every manifold), is unique up to isomorphism, and $\operatorname{Deck}(\widetilde X\to X)\cong\pi_1(X)$. The universal cover of a connected Lie group is a Lie group.

$\mathbb R\to S^1$, $\mathbb R^n\to T^n$, $S^n\to\mathbb{RP}^n$, $SU(2)\to SO(3)$ (verified: 2-sheeted homomorphism, kernel $\pm1$), $\widetilde{SL}(2,\mathbb R)\to SL(2,\mathbb R)$ (no faithful finite-dimensional representation).

Used in: [[PA15 Covering Spaces and the Fundamental Group]]. Related: [[Covering Space]] · [[Deck Transformation]] · [[Fundamental Group]].
