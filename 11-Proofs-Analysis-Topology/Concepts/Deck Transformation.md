# Deck Transformation

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Hatcher §1.3.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A symmetry of the covering space that shuffles the floors without changing what lies below.

> [!key] Definition
> A homeomorphism $\varphi:E\to E$ with $\pi\circ\varphi=\pi$. They form a group; for a universal covering it is isomorphic to $\pi_1(X)$. A free properly discontinuous action of a discrete group $\Gamma$ has $X\to X/\Gamma$ as covering with deck group $\Gamma$.

$\mathbb R\to S^1$: translations by $\mathbb Z$; $S^n\to\mathbb{RP}^n$: antipodal map, $\mathbb Z/2$; $z\mapsto z^k$: $\mathbb Z/k$.

Used in: [[PA15 Covering Spaces and the Fundamental Group]]. Related: [[Universal Cover]] · [[Covering Space]] · [[Fundamental Group]].
