# Closed Map Lemma

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=628|Lee Lemma A.52, p. 610]]; Munkres Thm 26.6.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Compactness upstairs plus separation downstairs leaves no room for a continuous map to misbehave at the edges.

> [!key] Definition
> A continuous map from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is closed. Hence a continuous bijection $X\to Y$ (compact $\to$ Hausdorff) is a homeomorphism, and a continuous injection is an embedding.

Closed $C\subseteq X$ is compact, $f(C)$ is compact in a Hausdorff space, hence closed. It identifies $[0,1]/(0\sim1)$ with $S^1$ and shows compact submanifolds are embedded.

Used in: [[PA11 Subspaces, Products and Quotients]]. Related: [[Homeomorphism and Embedding]] · [[Compact Space]] · [[Quotient Topology]].
