# Cartesian Product

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Assignment-prems_merged.pdf#page=3|Assignment 3, Q6]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> All ordered pairs with first entry from $A$ and second from $B$.

> [!key] Definition
> $A\times B=\{(a,b):a\in A,b\in B\}$, with $(a,b)$ the [[Ordered Pair]]. $|A\times B|=|A||B|$ for finite sets. $T\subseteq A\Rightarrow T\times B\subseteq A\times B$; $A\times(B\cup C)=(A\times B)\cup(A\times C)$.

Relations on $A$ are subsets of $A\times A$ and functions $A\to B$ are certain subsets of $A\times B$. $\mathbb N\times\mathbb N$ is countable (anti-diagonals); $\mathbb R^2=\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$.

Used in: [[PA02 Sets Families and the ZFC Foundations]]. Related: [[Ordered Pair]] · [[Binary Relation]] · [[Indexed Family of Sets]].
