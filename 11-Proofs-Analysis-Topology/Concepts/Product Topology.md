# Product Topology

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=621|Lee Prop. A.23, p. 603]]; Munkres §§15, 19.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Open means: pin down finitely many coordinates approximately, and ignore the rest.

> [!key] Definition
> The product topology on $X\times Y$ has the boxes $U\times V$ as a basis. The projections are continuous and open, and $f:Z\to X\times Y$ is continuous iff both components are.

$\mathbb R^n$ is the $n$-fold product of $\mathbb R$; $T^n=(S^1)^n$; $GL(n,\mathbb R)$ is a topological group ([[DM08 Lie Groups]]). $X$ is Hausdorff iff the diagonal is closed in $X\times X$. Compact $\times$ compact is compact ([[Tube Lemma]]).

Used in: [[PA11 Subspaces, Products and Quotients]]. Related: [[Subspace Topology]] · [[Tube Lemma]] · [[Basis for a Topology]].
