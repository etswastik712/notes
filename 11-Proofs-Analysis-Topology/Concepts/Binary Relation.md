# Binary Relation

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Assignment-prems_merged.pdf#page=1|Assignment 4, Q5–Q7]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A rule saying which pairs are related: a subset of $A\times A$.

> [!key] Definition
> $R\subseteq A\times A$; **reflexive** ($xRx$), **symmetric**, **antisymmetric**, **transitive**. Symmetric + transitive does **not** imply reflexive ($\{(1,1)\}$ on $\{1,2\}$).

Counts on an $n$-set: $2^{n^2}$ relations; $n=3$: 64 reflexive, 64 symmetric, 5 equivalence, 19 partial orders, 29 preorders; $n=4$: 4096, 1024, 15, 219, 355. $\le$: reflexive, transitive, not symmetric; $<$: transitive only.

Used in: [[PA03 Relations Equivalence and Order]]. Related: [[Equivalence Relation]] · [[Partially Ordered Set]] · [[Cartesian Product]].
