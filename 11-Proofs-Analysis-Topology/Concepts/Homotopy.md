# Homotopy

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=631|Lee Props. A.62, Thm A.71, pp. 613–615]]; Hatcher §0.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A continuous deformation of one map into another, played as a movie parametrised by $t\in[0,1]$.

> [!key] Definition
> $f_0\simeq f_1$ if there is a continuous $H:X\times[0,1]\to Y$ with $H_0=f_0$, $H_1=f_1$. A **path homotopy** fixes the endpoints. A homotopy equivalence has a homotopy inverse; a space homotopy equivalent to a point is **contractible**.

Convex sets are contractible (straight-line homotopy). Homotopy equivalences induce isomorphisms on $\pi_1$ ([[Fundamental Group]]) and, for smooth manifolds, on de Rham cohomology.

Used in: [[PA15 Covering Spaces and the Fundamental Group]]. Related: [[Fundamental Group]] · [[Winding Number]].
