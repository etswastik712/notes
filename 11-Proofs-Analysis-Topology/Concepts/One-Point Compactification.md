# One-Point Compactification

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** Munkres §29.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Add a single point at infinity whose neighbourhoods are complements of compact sets: the plane becomes a sphere, an open interval a circle.

> [!key] Definition
> For $X$ locally compact Hausdorff, $X^*=X\cup\{\infty\}$ with open sets the open sets of $X$ and the sets $X^*\setminus K$, $K\subseteq X$ compact, is compact Hausdorff.

$(\mathbb R^n)^*\cong S^n$ by stereographic projection: $|\sigma^{-1}(w)-N|=2/\sqrt{1+|w|^2}$ (for $|w|=1000$: $2.0\times10^{-3}$). $(0,1)^*\cong S^1$; $\mathbb N^*$ is a convergent sequence.

Used in: [[PA12 Separation, Countability and Local Compactness]]. Related: [[Locally Compact Space]] · [[Compact Space]].
