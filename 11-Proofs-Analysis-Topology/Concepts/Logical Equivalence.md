# Logical Equivalence

<span class="scope-taught">taught · in syllabus</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟢 **taught:** [[Assignment-prems_merged.pdf#page=7|Assignment 1, Q3]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Two forms are equivalent when no assignment of truth values can tell them apart.

> [!key] Definition
> $\Phi\sim\Psi$ iff they have the same truth value under every assignment, iff $\Phi\Leftrightarrow\Psi$ is a tautology. Laws: De Morgan, $(P\Rightarrow Q)\sim\neg P\vee Q\sim\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$, $\neg(P\Rightarrow Q)\sim P\wedge\neg Q$, distributivity, exportation.

Use $\sim$ or $\equiv$ for equivalence of statements and $=$ only for objects. All laws in this note were verified over all assignments (11 laws, 2–3 variables). The set-theoretic images of these laws are the identities of [[Set Operations and De Morgan's Laws]].

Used in: [[PA01 Logic and Methods of Proof]]. Related: [[Tautology]] · [[Conditional Statement]] · [[Set Operations and De Morgan's Laws]].
