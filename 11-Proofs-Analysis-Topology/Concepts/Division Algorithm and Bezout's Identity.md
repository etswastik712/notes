# Division Algorithm and Bezout's Identity

<span class="scope-extra-beyond">extra · beyond</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🔵 **extra · beyond:** [[PA10 Well-Ordering and Well-Ordered Pairs]] §3 (arithmetic from a least element). Compare [[Congruence Modulo n]] (taught, Assignment 5).

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Remainders shrink and a strictly shrinking sequence of naturals must stop; the smallest positive combination of $a$ and $b$ is their gcd.

> [!key] Statements
> **Division:** for $b\in\mathbb N$, $a=bq+r$ with unique $0\le r<b$ (least element of $\{a-bq\ge0\}$). **Bézout:** the least positive $ax+by$ is $\gcd(a,b)$ ($21=7\cdot462-3\cdot1071$). **Euclid's lemma:** $p\mid ab\Rightarrow p\mid a$ or $p\mid b$. **Unique factorisation** by strong induction. **Euclid's algorithm** $(a,b)\to(b,a\bmod b)$ stops.
