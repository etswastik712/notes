# Locally Compact Space

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=630|Lee App. A, p. 612]]; Munkres §29.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Every point has a small closed neighbourhood that is compact, like closed balls in $\mathbb R^n$.

> [!key] Definition
> $X$ is **locally compact** if every point has a neighbourhood contained in a compact set. For Hausdorff $X$ this means: a basis of open sets with compact closure (**precompact** open sets).

$\mathbb R^n$ and all manifolds are; $\mathbb Q$ and infinite-dimensional normed spaces are not. Open and closed subsets of a locally compact Hausdorff space are locally compact.

Used in: [[PA12 Separation, Countability and Local Compactness]]. Related: [[One-Point Compactification]] · [[Compact Exhaustion]] · [[Compact Space]].
