# Fundamental Group

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=632|Lee App. A, Props. A.64–A.69, p. 614]]; Hatcher §1.1.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The group of loops at a point that cannot be pulled tight, multiplied by going around one after the other.

> [!key] Definition
> $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ = path-homotopy classes of loops at $x_0$, product = concatenation. $X$ is **simply connected** if path-connected with $\pi_1=1$. A map $f$ induces $f_*:\pi_1(X)\to\pi_1(Y)$, functorially.

$\pi_1(\mathbb R^n)=1$, $\pi_1(S^1)=\mathbb Z$, $\pi_1(S^n)=1$ ($n\ge2$), $\pi_1(T^n)=\mathbb Z^n$, $\pi_1(\mathbb{RP}^n)=\mathbb Z/2$ ($n\ge2$), $\pi_1(SO(3))=\mathbb Z/2$, $\pi_1(SL(2,\mathbb R))=\mathbb Z$. Simply connected Lie groups are the domain of the Lie correspondence ([[DM15 Closed Subgroups and the Lie Correspondence]]).

Used in: [[PA15 Covering Spaces and the Fundamental Group]]. Related: [[Homotopy]] · [[Covering Space]] · [[Universal Cover]] · [[Winding Number]].
