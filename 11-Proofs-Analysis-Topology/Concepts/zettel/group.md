# Group

A group is a pair of a non-empty set and a binary operation satisfying associativity, the existence of a unique identity element, and the existence of a unique inverse for every element.

$$(a * b) * c = a * (b * c)$$

$$e * a = a * e = a$$

$$a * b = b * a = e$$
