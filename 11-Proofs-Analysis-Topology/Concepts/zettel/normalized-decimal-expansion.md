# Normalized Decimal Expansion

A decimal representation of a real number in (0, 1) that avoids non-uniqueness by forbidding trailing infinite tails of 9s, providing a unique canonical representation for each real number.

$$x = 0.a_1 a_2 a_3 \dots = \frac{a_1}{10} + \frac{a_2}{100} + \frac{a_3}{1000} + \dots$$
