# Conditional Statement

A compound proposition asserting that a conclusion statement holds whenever a hypothesis statement holds, evaluating to false only when the hypothesis is true and the conclusion is false.

$$P \Rightarrow Q$$

$$(P \Rightarrow Q) \sim (\neg P \lor Q)$$
