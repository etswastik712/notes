# Method of Minimal Counterexample

A proof technique where one assumes the set of counterexamples to a proposition is non-empty, invokes the Well-Ordering Principle to choose its minimal element, and produces a strictly smaller counterexample to achieve a contradiction.

$$S = \{n \in \mathbb{N} : P(n) \text{ is false}\}, \quad m = \min S$$

- needs:: [[zettel/well-ordering-principle]] — The method directly relies on the Well-Ordering Principle to guarantee the existence of min S for a non-empty set of counterexamples
