# Greatest Lower Bound

The largest element among all lower bounds of a subset in a partially ordered set, if it exists.

## Links
- special-case-of:: [[zettel/lower-bound-in-a-poset]] — glb(S) is an element belonging to the collection of lower bounds of S that bounds all other lower bounds from above
- contrasts-with:: [[zettel/least-upper-bound]] — glb is the infimum of lower bounds, whereas lub is the supremum of upper bounds
