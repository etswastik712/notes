# Abelian Group

An abelian or commutative group is a group whose binary operation satisfies commutativity for every pair of elements in the set.

$$a * b = b * a$$

## Links
- special-case-of:: [[zettel/group]] — Abelian groups are groups that impose the additional constraint that all elements commute under the group operation
