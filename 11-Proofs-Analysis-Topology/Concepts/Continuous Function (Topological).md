# Continuous Function (Topological)

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[proofs-prems-topo.pdf#page=74|AI notes ch. 17.2 and 18.2]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> Nearby inputs go to nearby outputs; equivalently preimages of open sets are open.

> [!key] Definition
> For metric spaces TFAE: (a) $\varepsilon$–$\delta$ continuity; (b) $f^{-1}(V)$ open for all open $V$; (c) $x_n\to x\Rightarrow f(x_n)\to f(x)$. In topological spaces (b) is the definition; compositions of continuous maps are continuous.

Continuous images of compact sets are compact and of connected sets connected, giving the extreme value and intermediate value theorems. A cdf is right-continuous ([[Cumulative Distribution Function]]).

Used in: [[PA07 Metric and Topological Spaces]]. Related: [[Image and Preimage]] · [[Compact Space]] · [[Connected Space]].
