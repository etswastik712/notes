# ZFC Axioms

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[proofs-prems-topo.pdf#page=27|AI notes ch. 4–5]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> The rules for building sets: enough to do all of mathematics, restricted enough to avoid Russell.

> [!key] Definition
> Extensionality, Separation, Pairing, Union, Power set, Infinity, Replacement, Regularity, Choice. Everything else (ordered pairs, functions, $\mathbb N$, $\mathbb R$) is a set built from these.

Separation gives $\{x\in A:\varphi(x)\}$, hence $\varnothing$, $A\cap B$, $A\setminus B$; Pairing + Union give $A\cup B$; Infinity gives $\mathbb N$; Regularity gives $A\notin A$ and rules out membership cycles. Gödel (1940) and Cohen (1963): the Continuum Hypothesis is independent of ZFC ([[Continuum Hypothesis]]).

Used in: [[PA02 Sets Families and the ZFC Foundations]]. Related: [[Russell's Paradox]] · [[Axiom of Choice]] · [[Ordered Pair]].
