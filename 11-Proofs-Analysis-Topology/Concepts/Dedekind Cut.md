# Dedekind Cut

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[proofs-prems-topo.pdf#page=66|AI notes ch. 15.2]].

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A real number is the set of all rationals to its left.

> [!key] Definition
> A cut is $\alpha\subsetneq\mathbb Q$, $\alpha\ne\varnothing$, closed downward, with no greatest element. $\mathbb R$ = cuts, ordered by $\subsetneq$; $\sup S=\bigcup S$; $q^*=\{p<q\}$; $\sqrt2=\{p\le0\}\cup\{p:p^2<2\}$.

Addition $\alpha+\beta=\{r+s\}$; zero $0^*$; the field axioms and the embedding $q\mapsto q^*$ are routine but long (Rudin, Appendix to Ch. 1).

Used in: [[PA06 The Real Numbers and Sequences]]. Related: [[Least Upper Bound Property]] · [[Construction of Integers and Rationals]] · [[Archimedean Property]].
