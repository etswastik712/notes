# Second-Countable Space

<span class="scope-extra-book">extra · book</span>

> [!syllabus] In the syllabus vs extra
> 🟡 **extra · book:** [[John M. Lee - Introduction to Smooth Manifolds.pdf#page=619|Lee Prop. A.16, p. 601]]; Munkres §30.

> [!intuition] In one picture
> A countable list of open sets describes the whole topology (rational balls in $\mathbb R^n$).

> [!key] Definition
> $X$ is **second-countable** if the topology has a countable basis. Then $X$ is first-countable, separable and **Lindelöf**: every open cover of every open subset has a countable subcover.

$\mathbb R^n$ is; an uncountable discrete space, $\mathbb R_\ell$ and the long line are not. A second-countable locally connected space has countably many components. The manifold definition requires it ([[DM01 Smooth Manifolds]]): it gives a countable atlas, exhaustions and partitions of unity.

Used in: [[PA12 Separation, Countability and Local Compactness]]. Related: [[First-Countable Space]] · [[Compact Exhaustion]] · [[Paracompact Space]] · [[Basis for a Topology]].
